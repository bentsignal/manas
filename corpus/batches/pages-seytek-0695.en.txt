The peoples of the world had been destroyed.
My renowned father was a lion,
Favored by perfect fortune.
As for the offspring born from him,
Unable to overcome Sarybai,
What sort of descendants could they be called?
Our first ancestor was Tügöl Khan;
You made all his line lions.
Yet unable to overcome this accursed one,
Allah Most High, one God,
Why did You make us so deficient?
All my forefathers
You made lions, one God.
Yet unable to overcome this perishing dog,
Why did You make their descendants
So deficient, one God?
What inheritance can we attain
If we remain alive without dying?
Let not one of us remain;
Let us all perish in massacre.
Külchoro standing over there
Was a lion inscribed upon our destiny,
Favored by perfect fortune.
He was a soul of terrible majesty;
Not merely Sarybai, but the whole world,
If anger chanced to seize him,
He was one who could destroy it.
If I am parted from my uncle,
What inheritance could I attain?
Let not one lone soul remain;
Let me perish in the massacre.
My uncle, a true falcon,
Was forged crimson iron.
Rather than let it be said
That the infidel son of a monster killed him,
Let not one lone soul remain;
Let me perish in the massacre.
If I am parted from my uncle,
Will the people not settle along the river?
With all the nation slaughtered,
Their whole bodies drained of blood.
Seeing this, brave Semetey
Was seized by terrible fury.
When the boy saw his khanly uncle,
He no longer cared
Whether his poor soul lived;
He cast that precious life aside.
Slung with his long-barreled gun,
He prayed to the Creator,
From within the infidel Er Sarybai’s
Unbreakable iron net,
From the dreadful pit he had dug,
Offering this prayer to God:
“All my earliest forefathers were khans;
You created them all as khans.
Before one wrathful infidel’s armies,
Why have You made them bow so low?
You made my father, lion Manas,
A hero upheld by the saints.
You fulfilled all he desired.
God who created all,
Before one peerless dog,
Why have You brought such disgrace?
While my mighty father lived,
Whenever fury roused him,
He brought massacre upon the Chinese.
Of infidels such as this,
He scattered all four kinds of livestock
And trod upon their heads.
He ranged along the waters of Sombuk,
And those who fought him crawled away.
When he charged in wrath,
None who resisted him prospered.
When true fury seized him,
No adversary remained standing.
When he gathered himself and charged,
No man could withstand him.
When they saw my true father,
All the people of Chinmachin wept.
