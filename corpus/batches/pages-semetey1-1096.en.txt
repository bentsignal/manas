The Kyrgyz of Zheti-Suu,
With your skill you made into a people.
Of surging hell,
You have seen its very eye, brave Koshoy;
Of the teeming Black Chinese,
You have seen their very eye, brave Koshoy.
Of the entrenched foe,
You have seen his stronghold, brave Koshoy.
Infidels and Muslims,
Clans where peoples gathered—
You have seen most of them, brave Koshoy.
Relentless havoc
You have wrought, brave Koshoy.
When the Chinese seized
Aykozho's Bilerik,
You fought without rest for six months
And brought her back, brave Koshoy.
For the paradise that had been sealed,
You became a source of aid, brave Koshoy.
At my noble father's memorial feast,
Be my support, brave Koshoy.
The red banner with its golden finial—
Raise it for me, brave Koshoy.
To my lion father's memorial feast,
Go for me, brave Koshoy.
Let your white beard stream,
And wave it there, brave Koshoy.
Infidels and Muslims
Will crowd there in multitudes;
You have gone forth sowing havoc—
Go for me, brave Koshoy.
Accept these words of mine;
Go and oversee the feast.
Bloodthirsty Manas the leopard
Does not count me equal to his boot heel;
He too is a resounding saint of yours.
I will not go to your Manas;
I will not seek his counsel.
Apart from you,
Even if I die, I will heed no one.
I will not go to overbearing Manas
To say I will hold the feast;
If I die, then let me die,
But I will not heed Manas's words.
I will make Kökötöy's feast spread like a lake;
Kökötöy's great wealth
I will make multiply like spring grass.
I have broad Samarkand and Alay;
If I tell it, I have wealth in plenty.
I have sheep filling the four directions;
If all the eighteen thousand worlds
Come crowding together,
I mean to feed them full.
On this side lies the Syr River;
I have camels filling all of it.
To herd the camels there,
I have giants in abundance;
To lead and distribute the camels,
I have many servants on foot.
For every camel set aside,
I have herds of horses worth
Nine hills of grazing.
I will gather this people in surging crowds;
Mountain-like Koshoy, hear me.
I have the knowledge to load the baggage;
I have gold coins to load on the wise.
My wealth is inexhaustible;
My breast holds many sorrows.
To bring in firewood,
I have many mighty wrestlers.
For the Black Kalmyks and Manchus,
I will make a whole ox their slaughter beast.
The Kalmyks who come teeming,
I will subdue with frozen meat.
People will come in abundance;
A surging flood will come.
Calling it the brow of the great gathering,
Calling it Khan Kökötöy's memorial feast—
