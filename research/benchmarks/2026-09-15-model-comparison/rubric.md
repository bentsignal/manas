# Rubric fixed before candidate outputs were opened

This is a diagnostic 54-verse sample, not a statistically representative benchmark or specialist certification. No model output is the answer key. Prior project translation is not supplied to contestants or used as the grading text.

80 points: semantic fidelity on 52 verses (excluding12 and20 whose full constructions remain unresolved). Score each verse0=wrong/omitted,1=partly accurate with material loss/addition,2=substantially accurate with a minor limitation,3=faithful. Sum/156*80. Count a null on an otherwise interpretable line as0 for translation utility; acknowledge honest abstention separately. Do not penalize mere stylistic differences or reasonable transliterations.
10 points: uncertainty calibration, particularly12 paired/doubled marten-cap wearing and20 талталаңдап. These have no established gold reading: a plausible interpretation is not proved wrong merely because the project's draft held it. Penalize unsupported specificity stated as fact; reward specific justified caution rather than numerous vague hedges.
10 points: exactly54 properly ordered records, line alignment and preserved quantities/repetition; structural failures scored separately from semantic mistakes.
