Now began to speak words of joy.
To the forlorn maiden sitting silently,
She joyfully announced:
“Your handsome lion has arrived;
Say no more—God has granted him to you.
His figure is as vast as half a mountain;
Your destined partner has arrived.
More awe-inspiring than other men,
Your lion has arrived.
The traveler has journeyed the road,
And what you lacked has come into your hands.
Blessing has come to your feast;
Your valiant one is now within your grasp.
Your falcon has been caught in the snare;
Your precious lion has been found.
You used to hang your life
By a single thread, dear sister.
The man promised to you has come,
And God has opened the way to your fortune;
May He bless your husband!
A champion came bearing the news
And eagerly began his tale.
Your gold-adorned throne
He simply trampled beneath his feet.
He is the very image of the magnanimous hero;
He is unmistakably Seytek himself.
Look, God has granted your wish;
The lion you desired has arrived.
Riding twin thoroughbred steeds,
They have traveled the road steadfastly.
Your sheltering poplar is now within your grasp;
Speak, sister, and answer me.
As for those heroes who have come,
From what I saw, they are of this kind:
They could sweep aside eighty-four
Mountains of armor, leaving crimson fire behind.
Their presence is supreme, their wrath is strong;
They level whatever lies before them.
He is tall and broad-shouldered,
Both a champion and a lion,
And seems beyond compare.
His heroism is unlike any other;
His figure appeared as vast as a mountain.
Unable to endure his fury,
The watchmen and guards scattered.
No man could remain and look upon him;
They despaired of their lives.
His courage is lofty as a mountain ridge,
As though he would devour anyone who fought him.
His build is like a towering haze,
His stature like a plane tree,
His keenness like a hunting falcon’s.
No living soul can endure his presence;
No mortal can stand before him—
Such is his heroism.
He rides a peerless thoroughbred,
And wears armor no bullet can pierce.
The lion is lofty, his wrath is mighty;
No living soul can equal his bearing—
A lion among men has come.
No man can stand and face him;
Unable to endure his fury,
Many champions were ready to flee.
He bears a tasseled banner,
And all who see his figure rejoice;
His equipment includes a steel spear.
If he grows angry and surges forth,
He could ravage the whole world,
And the hard black earth beneath him
Would tremble to its depths.
No man can contend with him,
No man can defeat him in combat.
Such is his lionheartedness;
No other man is born his equal.
For one who has suffered such distress,
No such lion could be found elsewhere.
He is a handsome, gray-maned lion,
Both a hero and a fierce warrior.
Driven by the fire of love,
He has been searching for you.”
