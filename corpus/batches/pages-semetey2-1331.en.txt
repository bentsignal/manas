When he reaches twelve,
He will roam your hills and raise dust;
He will lay low anyone he grapples.
His pounding tread will raise dust,
And bring renown to your mighty people.
Do not boast of strength, Kiyaz:
If you wrestle him, he will bloody your head!
He will dig your grave layer by layer
And make your misery boil;
Before your very eyes he will show you
A marvel no man has ever wrought.
He will take a spear and fight the host,
Demanding, “Find my father for me!”
He will quarrel not only with you, Kiyaz,
But with the whole face of the earth.
If it happens that you come to blows,
If by chance she bears a son,
He will dig your deep grave;
If you swarm him, he will slaughter your host.
Before your very eyes he will show you
A wonder dreadful for man to behold.
That is my counsel; understand it.
Be wary, Kiyaz, and heed my words!
All his earliest forefathers were khans,
Blessed by Kydyr from their very roots;
All his chief ancestors were khans.
He is the offspring of calamity,
Blessed by Kydyr from the beginning.
His forefather through his father—
The son who remains after him—
Will grow to manhood as an orphan
And somehow take his place among the people.
Just look at his maturity:
When he reaches twelve,
He will break a fortress and take plunder,
And embroil mighty peoples in strife.
Just look at his devastation!
When he reaches thirteen,
He will draw Zulpukor and wield it;
The sullen foe will vanish,
And he will rise boldly and prevail.
Before the offspring of this troublemaker,
Every man who faces him will vanish.
He will spare no hero;
No opponent will remain.
The offspring of such an infidel—
His lashes are flame and his eyes are embers;
Among men there is no such scourge.
His brow is high and his gaze direct;
Provoke him, and his harsh words strike true.
A gray tiger will walk before him;
In all the world there will be no such hero.
He is the son of suspect kin—
Know that the child will be an enemy;
If the child lives and grows,
He will come after all of us
And become our greatest foe.
Wise Kiyaz, heed my words:
Though his outward form is different,
At heart he will bear us enmity;
That child is not one of our people at root.
When the child is born,
If by chance it is a son,
Be sure to post guards outside!
Find a way to steal him,
And devise the means with care.
My husband Kiyaz, think;
I tell you often—please heed my words.
Put aside your worry until the birth.
If this Chörök bears a son,
Find a way to dispose of her child.
That is how matters stand with the child;
Heroic Kiyaz, listen,
And know that I have warned you hoarse!
Ah, my dear Kiyaz, heed my words:
If it is a girl, leave her alive;
But if the child is a boy,
Find the means somehow to slaughter him.
