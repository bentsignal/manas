His mountain ear was keen and true.
The tongue that spoke was tightly bound.
A divided age came crashing down.
Dark night descended upon repentance.
Leaving him unable to find a refuge,
Of the forked one who fell into the grave,
An encircling host rose toward God.
Blessing boiled, yet the spirit suffered.
An iron net reached toward heaven.
Unable to find a shelter for his breast,
He descended from an ocean of souls.
That road led among the irons.
There was no battle cry and no people.
In the clan, hero Kenen stood alone.
He had neither son nor household.
There was no racehorse to ride into war.
They spoke his name over and over.
He stood between me and death.
The wicked enemy knew no mercy.
From the swarming Chinese host,
From its unbreakable iron net,
He rose from his misery to the height.
He seated Aitai above the rest.
His life was being carried away.
There was no immense, mighty hero.
My strength, dearer than life itself,
I reach out my hand and implore.
Chengker, the shriveled old woman,
Became bronze in China.
She was separated from China.
The remnant left to wander,
Saying, “Now I shall seize him,”
Chengker, who boasted of heroism,
Had bound them all.
She herself twisted his mouth shut.
