I will make him weary of life.
In Bakay’s old age
I myself will settle his account;
I will beat out his ancient demon.
I will devise a superior stratagem
And somehow take the head
Of arrogant, deathless Bakay.
I will heap humiliation upon him like sheep;
I will gather without exception
All the descendants and people of Manas,
Grind them to stone dust,
And do as I please.
Massed black Jediger,
Come, every last one!” he shouted,
Calling their fiercest men.
Then Kanchoro,
Summoning them all to come, said:
“Brave Kyyaz of the Jediger,
You, their hero, stand here;
You resourceful, wise
Champion stand here;
You who boldly enter battle,
Khan of the massed black Jediger,
You stand here.
All you seasoned men who know the people,
All of you stand here;
All you farsighted men,
You long-eared listeners,
You strong men who know the distant future,
You strong men who know what is near;
You broad-chested lions,
You heroes who do not grieve
Though both feet burn in fire—
Turn and come here!
You unshaken lions,
You fortunate heroes on the road,
You mighty black Jediger,
My tigers, come here!
Believe the words of Kanchoro Khan,
All you people;
Your khan will speak now—
People, now behold!
Kanchoro who governs the people—
You know, great nation,
Kanchoro’s renown
And fierce Aychürök’s quarrel.
Will Kyyaz Khan lie still
Without taking fierce Aychürök,
That woman Aychürök,
That glinting infidel,
She who is famed as beautiful?
Did the settled Kyrgyz not witness
The strife she caused?
Toltoy Khan was to take her,
But the madman burned to death
And met his end through Külchoro.
Because of that former affair,
Because of fierce Aychürök,
Many khojas and khans died;
All who grappled with them died,
And many humble souls died too.
Spears were bundled and heaped;
Beginning with old Tölök,
How many were slaughtered together
And scattered abroad!
The camps of the Jediger people
Were broken and scattered;
Their wives were left widows.
No trace remained,
No tidings of the Jediger;
Only their willows remained upon the land,
Their children left as orphans.
How many of their khans were killed!
When you met face to face,
Many of your lives were destroyed.
Seeking to take fierce Chürök,
All of you were massacred.
