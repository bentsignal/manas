One was Jamgyrchy's Jainake.
The three senior wives went together,
To intervene between them,
From the spoiled woman
To obtain an answer.
Chachykei's white palace,
Its door of iron and solid tin,
The spoiled daughter-in-law's
Wealth was exceedingly great.
People did not venture near;
Anyone who approached was astonished.
People did not pass to and fro;
Anyone who looked was astonished.
Into Chachykei's court,
As mediators then,
The three senior wives entered,
About the falcon that had escaped,
To learn what had happened.
