# Current 146 page 79 — bounded exact overlap audit

## Result

The audit finds **15 secure one-to-one, globally ordered counterparts**, mapping manuscript rows 24–38 to the printed continuation on page 330. Rows 1–23 remain an unmapped manuscript expansion describing Chyng Tengir's mobilization, tower, and enormous war drum.

Cross-witness review exposed one print-induced transcription error in the initial reviewed file. Manuscript row 29 reads provisionally `Олбуп минер ат кайдан,` (“Where could one find a horse fit to mount proudly?”), while the print has `Олбуп минер ат камдап,` (“Preparing a horse fit to mount proudly”). The corrected full-page witness is `page-079.overlap-audit.reviewed.jsonl`. This lexical variant is preserved rather than normalized to print.

## Unmapped opening expansion

Rows 1–23 have no secure one-to-one positions in this printed run. They describe Chyng Tengir raging, preparing his hosts, and sounding an immense drum from a tower as the sign that the enemy has come. The print moves directly from its preceding threats into the dispatch to the Chinese, Manchu, Shibe, Torgout, Solon, Kalmyk, and Kara-Khitai. These manuscript rows remain additions and must not be excluded as overlap.

## Editorial decision

For release deduplication, rows 24–38 should be classified as secure printed-edition overlap. Rows 1–23 should remain manuscript additions. Integration should use `page-079.overlap-audit.reviewed.jsonl`, which differs from the initial reviewed file only in row 29's manuscript reading, English translation, and uncertainty note.
