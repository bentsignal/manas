The handwriting and text differ between pages 110 and 111, and neither is
another photograph of folio 2424. Thus the **108/109 duplicate occurs once**
in the physical sequence, followed by two distinct leaves on pages 110 and
111. The earlier duplicate screen correctly compared PDF pages, even though
its extracted files are numbered one lower.

These margin numbers are manuscript annotations, **not yet independently
verified physical-baseline totals**. I did not complete a line-by-line count or
word-level transcription of either leaf, so this note does not add 46 or 48
rows to the release and does not assert an exact printed-edition crosswalk.

photos as folios 2424 and 2425, respectively, in their draft records. Those
labels each lag the photographed folio by one here; do not treat the
draft records as verified transcriptions of pages 110–111.
