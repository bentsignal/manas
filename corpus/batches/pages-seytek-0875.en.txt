Surpassed all the world,
Such was that infidel's courage,
Breaking away from the host,
He appeared like Ala-Too,
He rode out raging to single combat,
The madman who scorched the earth
Charged possessed onto the battlefield,
As though to suck dry whoever crossed him,
As though to slaughter all who opposed him,
Like a mighty, merciless wrestler,
As though to tear any soul he met into ten pieces,
And mangle it again and again,
Such was his appearance,
For single combat in the great battle,
Sarybay rode out alone,
His menace unmatched, his venom strong,
“If you see the infidel, I am dead,” they said,
“Abandon hope for your precious life!”
His balefulness was unlike any other,
This terror surpassed them all,
At the ground where battle would be waged,
At the place where he would ride out alone,
He surged and thundered in frenzy,
Behind him the wrestlers
Beat their drums,
And pounded their kettledrums,
The earth split and dust arose,
Strength deserted humankind,
The countless army dared not advance,
No mortal dared show his face,
His nature strange, his venom strong—
Such was the swine Sarybay,
The scheming madman
Took up a strange long spear,
Buckled his sword diagonally,
And readied all his arms,
Like a cloud driven by a gale,
His wrath wheeled across his face,
He was a giant of mighty spirit,
Shallow in reason and without sense,
An arrogant brute,
A master of swift assaults,
A stalwart who ravaged the earth,
The wrathful infidel,
The hero of potent venom,
Rode onto the field with a roar,
Straining like a blue tiger,
Girded with blue-gray steel,
Before the hero's menace,
Most men hid themselves,
His splendid bearing became a terror,
His eyes dimmed as strength filled him,
He screamed like a scourge,
Calling for someone to ride out alone:
“Come, your lion favored by fortune!
Come, your stalwart hero, fully armed!
Come, you who level the great spear!
Come, you who deliver into my hands
The punishment of whoever advances!
Come, your lion of surpassing courage!
Come, your ready wrestler and hero!
Come, you who stand beneath the banner!
Come, satisfy the itching place,
And thrust your spear without delay!
Come, your warrior and your lion!
Come, your wrestler and your hero!
Come, you who slash and wage battle!
Come, you who finish all you seize
And tear their ribs apart!
Let the few remaining and the many come!
Let your hero come and leave no regrets!
My wrath has risen; I stand rigid,
I desire only to level my spear,
Whoever meets me shall die,
I shall put your women in black headdresses,
I shall vanquish your great ones,
I shall devour your weak,
Your wrestlers and your heroes—
I shall gouge out their eyes,
I who ruin nations and slaughter peoples,
