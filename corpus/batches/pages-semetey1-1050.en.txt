If we do not accept what he says,
If we do not give Aychürök
To Toltoy as tribute,
He will act with great cruelty.
Chynkozho, son of Shygay,
Kozho, resolute in his deeds,
Will slaughter us and only then be satisfied!
If we do not give Aychürök in tribute,
If we do not go and settle it that way,
He will cut off our heads without ceasing,
Reach down to our very roots,
Plunder all our goods,
And inflict grievous ruin.
For the aging maiden Chürök,
If we do not go to meet him,
He will begin the great tumult
And utterly slaughter
The dense black Afghans!"
When Azhybay said this,
Many people before the others
Began to clamor;
Many people took fright
And quickly agreed.
Their knees bent,
And their young men ran.
From Khan Akun's court,
As tribute for Toltoy,
They drove some forty red camels;
As tribute for Kozho,
They drove eighty-eight gray beasts,
All of them fat and barren.
They took men with the tribute
And many head of livestock,
Prepared their white banner,
And bound it high as the sky.
They now went out to meet
The Zhediger.
Together with their livestock,
Before warrior Kozho,
They now brought the tribute.
Azhybay and Tümönbay
Folded their hands across their bellies
And bowed in submission.
When he saw this tribute,
Chynkozho, son of Shygay,
Kozho, resolute in his deeds—
When his wrath took hold,
His custom was to begin a great tumult;
When he entered battle against an enemy,
His black horse flew into the sky;
His custom was to lay waste
All those he fought.
Chynkozho, son of Shygay,
Born a khan surpassing the Argyn,
When his wrath took hold,
Life fled from the Zhediger.
Raging and roaring,
Mighty Kozho thundered;
Roaring in anger,
He called Toltoy of the Zhediger
And ordered him to come near.
When Toltoy approached,
Mighty Kozho glared in anger
And began to speak:
"Your father's grave, warrior Toltoy!
We became pale stars in the sky,
And friends until Judgment Day.
At this time, oh Toltoy,
While my dear head remains,
If an enemy comes to challenge us,
I shall slaughter them utterly.
While my own head remains,
If you heed my words, oh Toltoy,
Over the whole world
I shall raise you up as khan!
Do you understand, oh Toltoy?
The Afghans have submitted;
Do you see that all have come?
