“Almighty, while I yet live,
You have made my misery boil like this!
While my precious head remains,
How can I surrender my people
To the infidel son of the ogress?
If death has come, let me die;
Until my final hour arrives,
Let me try my strength in combat
With that dog, the ogress’s son.
While life still surges within me,
How can I surrender my homeland,
Its golden pillar and rallying cry?
Sarybai seems to have gone mad;
Until only one of my doomed people remains,
Let me test myself against him!
Grasping true steel and breaking his ranks,
If true death comes, let me fall to a bullet;
Let us take true aim at each other,
Hack one another down and die,
Let blood reveal the depth of our torment,
Let blood pour down in torrents.
Let courage show itself,
Let flowing blood spill like water
Against the infidel who seized the people.
His courage is cold, his venom strong;
If challenged, he lays a man flat.
If you grapple with the infidel,
Abandon all hope for your precious life!
He is skillful indeed, a hero indeed,
The very eye of a blood-drinker,
A black and terrible calamity,
Created as a dire scourge,
The very black disaster itself.
To devastate and tear up the land,
To drain blood without mercy,
Raging and standing proud,
This is the one who came prepared.
He is the black scourge itself,
Ready to mow down the ruined,
And all the settled people.
He skirted the edge of my hills;
I fought, but my strength did not suffice.
He laid low all who faced him,
And bound my heroes;
Not a single soul escaped
His fury without a wound.
Sarybai’s onslaught
Destroyed every living soul.
Short mace and broad shoulders—
Was Sarybai’s onslaught
Truly unmatched in all the world?
The earth cannot swallow all he grinds into it;
When the infidel rages,
The black earth with its grassy breast
Cannot endure this madman!
He will finish the battle at its appointed hour;
Sarybai the swine will consume
The abundant world and countless herds.
He will unleash a joyous slaughter,
Taking maidens and women as plunder—
The maddened swine will seize them!
He will tear down every house,
Fell the plane trees and lay them low,
And bring true disaster.
You made me a lion before all others,
Yet made me lesser than Sarybai;
You made me mightiest of all creation,
Then let a fairy maiden deceive me
And disgrace me before so many women.
Turning my words away from mankind,
Making my eyes unable to see,
Drying up my rivers and springs,
Stopping both my ears from hearing,
I was cast into an iron cage
And remained captive in the horde for many years.
Almighty, who fashioned all in the beginning,
You made me a son to the bloodthirsty lion,
Then made me the plunder and widow
Of Sarybai and Kök Monchok.
You let my living hands be bound,
