His heroism is great as well.
His own eyes saw it clearly,
The foremost sage,
Racing across the face of the earth,
He would emerge alone from the race,
His four racehorses were ready,
From Saykal, whom the prince had taken,
There was a soul who followed behind me.
Settling them on two summer pastures,
Was it the multitude that migrated?
I lived in freedom,
Delighting the people, he went laughing,
He had become the people's enclosure and refuge,
His heroism, his lionhearted courage,
Now it struck the palate like springtime,
