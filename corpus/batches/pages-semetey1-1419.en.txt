Upon a divided pass and open plain,
Beside a black rock like a yurt,
Atop the black rock,
Crouching like a blue tiger,
He faced toward Beijing.
Without taking its strap around his neck,
The immeasurable spyglass
He drew from his breast:
A broad spyglass with an adzelike mouth,
A great spyglass a full fathom long,
Its inside glowing red like fire,
The great spyglass left by the bloodthirsty hero.
When he drew it from its case,
It extended a full fathom.
When bloodthirsty lion Manas
Trampled down
The whole world,
When he alone knew
And held dominion over
Much of the face of the earth,
When he humiliated the khoja in Kokon,
When he destroyed
The afflicted one in Bukhara,
When he brought turmoil
Upon all who challenged him,
When he seized those
Who defied him
And struck them down
Upon the steppe,
When he made broad Talas his fortress
And guarded it,
When he crushed whoever he encountered
And consumed them,
Yesterday, Azhybai and Khan Bakai were there,
And Almambet and Chubak were there too;
All the lions were there.
Yesterday, loading ammunition on an elephant,
Taking the warriors together,
All the heroes gathered.
They found no mountain difficult enough to cross,
And no enemy to fight.
Beyond Andizhan,
On this side of Namangan,
In the shelter of the Kokon khan,
Upon a yellow plain like the moon,
Dragging their wagons,
Leading a bay mare without a saddle,
Sixty Russian surveyors
Came out.
Sword in hand and shouting,
Mighty Alman then
Attacked them with a clamor
And took it as plunder.
The great spyglass taken as booty
Had its handle wound in gold.
Its maker was a friend of David;
When news came from your prince,
The hunchbacked smith Bölökbai
Repaired it, poor man.
Ah, dear smith Bölökbai—
To see it satisfied the heart.
He cut it from ruby stone
And had sixty facets made
Upon its glass.
When its wheel was turned,
It divided with a shrill sound;
A haze three months away
Appeared as near as three days.
He turned its wheel
And brought it to his true eye.
Toward Chin-Machin and Beijing,
He now aimed the spyglass in earnest:
