Their marksmen were at the front;
Their heroes were at the flanks.
Their hardy men were at the front;
Their tigers were at the flanks.
Their spearmen were at the front;
Their lions were at the flanks.
Without twitching the horses' mouths,
Many here wheeled their mounts.
Holding spears and fitting banner tips,
Many here stood ready.
Steeping themselves in bloody blood,
Fighting without fleeing,
Many here were warriors.
Streaming along the riverbank,
Surging across the ground,
They were told, “All advance abreast;
Ride with room and do not collide.”
Spearheads clashed;
Men's heads knocked together.
Commanders of ten swept forward;
Commanders of a hundred towered.
Their ancient fury stirred;
They became an army of many thousands.
They had horns and trumpets sounded,
Loaded troops upon rhinoceroses,
Loaded ammunition on elephants,
And gathered all the Jediger people
To carry out merciless slaughter
And seize Aychürök.
They went to exact, bit by bit,
The vengeance of wounded Toltoy.
The drum's voice thundered;
The brass horn's voice rang.
The gray-mottled pennant became their banner;
A wailing clamor arose.
The white-mottled pennant became their banner;
The air filled with battle cries and clamor.
Toltoy of the Jediger,
His countless army bending the earth—
His spearmen were at the front;
The lion Toltoy was at the flank.
He was driving them forth;
Swarming like ants,
They came seething out.
It was impossible to tell if they were worms,
Impossible to tell if they were people;
They came seething out.
Their heroes were at the front;
Renowned Kozho was at the flank.
Tölök and Tölöbay
Were behind the lower army.
Kalcha and Kadyrbay
Were at the army's qibla-side flank.
Wearing a black supa coat,
Riding a black noble horse,
Dear Kozho was at the flank.
Along the bank road of Kum-Kyya,
They came forth in splendor;
Behind raging Kozho,
He drove them onward.
From beside Tash-Kyya,
Most of them came forth;
Along the bank road of Kum-Kyya,
Half of them came forth.
Their great banner floated;
The brass horn's voice clattered.
Sword points swayed;
Spear tips flashed.
Their drums boomed.
Shouting to the great host, “Advance!”
Hero Toltoy thundered.
Gun barrels appeared,
Men's minds were shaken;
As though gone mad, the Jediger
Came forth clamoring.
Their surging people roared,
Crying Toltoy's battle cry.
The people stirred and shouted,
