Your Chubak, who came wound tight,
Became truly angry.
Riding Kögala, roaring,
Without hearing what was said,
Excited as though struck by wind,
Like a seven-headed creature:
‘Earlier, in broad Talas's hollow,
Along the tamarisk valley,
The teeming Argyn and many Nogoy,
When they were camped there,
Kumiss by day, girls by night,
When that was how things were,
Wearing marten-fur caps in pairs,
When they were swaying,
Together knowing the Kyrgyz,
When they were enjoying themselves,
Where is the letter written then?
Where is the oath sworn before God?
