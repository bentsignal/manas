Long ago he had seen it thoroughly,
Lion Bakai, the mighty one.
He had seen wonders and lands,
And had seen many things alike.
Traveling a winding road,
Crossing land that curved around,
To the foot of Teshik-Tash,
My uncle, elder Bakai,
Now entered at speed
The edge of the Kara-Kum.
He crossed Buurul-Tün,
And now scattered the Kara-Kum.
He came to a ridge like a saddle back,
And had only just bent forward and crossed it;
On the far side, through the haze,
The Chinese call it Tyngsha—
He crossed that Tyngsha.
The tulpars beneath them,
If one spoke of their legs, were like clouds;
The ground beneath their tread burst apart,
And when they merely opened their mouths,
The earth split with a crash.
Their flesh divided into joints like a sheep's,
Their backs straightened like a ram's;
Their flesh divided into joints like a colt's,
Their backs straightened like a greyhound's.
Their mouths opened wide as saws;
White foam mixed with blood
Scattered across their chests.
Their contracted hooves
Spread as wide as bowls;
The places where they trod
Were dug out like furnaces.
At a spur of the ending mountain,
At a sickle-shaped ridge and headland,
On the pass of Kara-Kapchal,
Toward the place where hero Khan Kongurbai's
Sentry lay,
Toward the branching successive ridges,
Facing forward and bracing his waist,
Your valiant Bakai now went,
To the projecting cliff and caravan road,
To the place where that ruined swine's
Fox lay.
Taking the lone path straight,
Your blessed uncle Bakai,
Holding it firmly by its collar,
Led Kumayyk.
Knowing many hidden turns of the land,
Your able saint Bakai—
When unlocking the land's secrets,
The ogre-like old man
Could not be confounded by villains.
When he traveled in darkness,
Once set on the trail, he never lost it.
Was Bakai not practiced
In learning land by reconnaissance?
Scouting and grieving over the land,
Was Bakai not practiced in it?
Onto a black rock large as a house,
Hero Bakai climbed;
He now turned Kökcholok's head.
While his gray wolf Manas lived,
What he had seen and learned
He now told the prince.
