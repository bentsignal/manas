Semetey’s kinsman;
Baitailak, son of Bakai Khan,
Also came as support;
Kalyk, son of Sary Khan,
Skilled against the foe he fought,
His bright aid of use,
Ready for slaughter,
Became a companion then.
All sons of khans,
They raised the pale-gray banner,
Raised the cry “Khan Manas!”
Braced themselves like lions,
And all mounted like lions.
Receiving a letter from the caliph,
Receiving the elders’ blessing,
The leopard’s sons, who wrought slaughter
When challenged,
Now rode into
The Jediger host.
From the Jediger city,
Moving swiftly and gathering themselves,
Turning like lions,
Onto the spur of the broken mountain,
Onto the looming promontory,
Semetey suddenly climbed.
When the young man truly looked,
Spears swayed like reeds,
Weapons glittered like stars;
Dawn broke in clouds,
And, crying “Khojo!” as their battle call,
The Jediger came shouting.
They raised the mottled gray banner;
The Jediger covered the land,
Raising the cry “Khan Toltoi!”
Divided into many kinds,
Each formation looked different;
Their dust swept up like a mountain,
And without stopping they raised their cries.
Toltoi’s host was immense,
And had covered the land with them.
Riding Sur with silver bracelets,
All riding stout, full-grown horses,
Tölök, master of judgment,
With Jediger countless as ants,
Trampled wherever they passed
And covered the Afghan city.
Shouting from below,
Raising the cry “Tölök!”
He too covered the land.
Kalcha and Kadyrbai,
Tölök and Tölöbai,
At the edge of the lower host,
With their princes at the front,
Had invoked Babedin
And loosed the raiding host.
Those on yellow horses streamed out,
Separated as another division,
Appearing wholly yellow.
The gray-horse riders divided off,
Appearing as men of substance.
Their dust rose to the sky;
The people divided into seven,
Appearing like monsters.
The vast, wrathful host roared,
Raising the battle cry “Khojo!”
Saying, “Submit now to what we demand;
Give us the old maid,”
They flowed along the bank,
Making the earth split and heave;
All the Jediger
Had covered the land with skill.
Drawing bows like rain,
Firing bullets like hail,
The spearmen shouted;
The surging, massive host
Raised the battle cry “Toltoi!”
The musketeers stood along one flank,
Their finest men in front,
