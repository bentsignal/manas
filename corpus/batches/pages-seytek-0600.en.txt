His might pierced men to the ribs;
The people saw that he was a giant.
Every mortal who faced him died—
A peerless champion, a great calamity,
A truly ruthless, ominous heart,
A great calamity born in truth.
When the infidel flew into wrath,
The whole world stood waiting;
O broad black earth with your hairy breast,
Tear through the dense ranks
Of the swine descending upon your head.
This lion, son of Manas,
Had matured into a hero
And become the equal of any hero’s son.
He had crossed steel spears,
And warred against men of renown;
He had taken the yellow rolling ridges,
Defeated the khan’s people in battle,
Broken through heights and opened roads,
Shaken the world and carried off plunder.
He had surged forth and become a hero,
The equal of any hero’s son.
He wore a thick, bulletproof coat
And rode bullet-swift Buurul.
He had felled heroes, as the people knew,
Worn his white coat of mail,
Mounted his white-gray steed,
And ruled the people before the nation’s eyes;
Yet he had never seen an infidel like
Sarybay, the monstrous giant’s son.
He loomed like Ala-Too,
And towered like a craggy peak;
Dust rose wherever he moved.
When the immovable infidel stirred,
His mere rumbling drove souls from bodies.
No hero dared advance against him,
And none he faced remained alive.
His aspect was one of dreadful torment,
His brows hung like a yurt’s felt covering,
And warriors who saw him seemed ready to flee.
He seemed ready to trample down whoever faced him,
His fangs jutting fiercely,
The front of his saddle sagging beneath him.
His majesty appeared supreme,
And any man who watched him
Was left utterly bewildered.
His courage burned like fire
As he advanced upon the bloodthirsty lion,
Staring about like a devourer,
With all his weapons ready.
Relentless, he transformed in fury,
Raging in savage wrath,
And addressed the bloodthirsty lion:
“Sarybay, son of the monstrous giant,
If I am the consummate hero of old,
If I am Semetey, son of hero Manas,
And you are the foe I have sought,
I will slaughter all who grapple with me;
I will kill and devour them all.
I am the very image of ferocity,
One of those who take men’s lives.
I am the target that steps into the arena,
And I devour every soul that grapples with me.
I am calamity’s black eye,
The blood-drinker himself.
You vain slave who plays the prince,
Today I shall trample your head,
And scatter your four kinds of livestock.
I shall settle your true account
And utterly sack your people.
I shall seize your mares as plunder
And bind and capture your giants.
If all my rage is roused,
I shall crush your people
And trample your broad Ala-Too land
Flat as a black road.
Stiffened with fury,
I shall slaughter your Kyrgyz people,
Make widows of your women,
And bind your heroes as slaves.”
