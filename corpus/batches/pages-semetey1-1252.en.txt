Let us come out in single combat,
Fight for seven days without pause,
And let those whose death has come die;
When what Allah has written
Descends upon our heads, let us meet it.
Let us exhaust one another,
Test one another's mettle,
And make the contest splendid.
Not only the maiden Chürök—
Let us seize all the beauties
Of the accursed Afghans who lag behind the migration,
Their sable caps turned back!"
Toltoi roused himself thus,
And Chynkojo spoke:
"You have lords, you have a khan,
And all this multitude of people.
Let us wage relentless war
Against the Afghans whose villages are whole.
Do not lose your wits,
O Zhediger, do not flee,"
Chynkojo said, driving them in.
He divided off the bowmen,
Divided off the strong,
Divided off the wise,
And divided off the foolish;
Thus Chynkojo arranged them.
They came wave after wave in multitudes,
An army covering the earth,
Their banners bending to the ground,
Shouting until the earth resounded,
Raising the cry of "Zhediger!"
Those whose swords were keenest,
Whose spears had steel points,
Heroes who struck with battle-axes,
His heroes who struck with battle-axes,
His warriors who drew bows,
He made all surge forward together;
Thus had Chynkojo arranged them.
He ordered the Zhediger
And divided them into seven;
With these he came ringing
And wholly engulfed the land.
When Khan Akun's sentry
Took a lamp and looked,
Along the shore-road of Kum-Kyya
They came spreading into view.
One could not tell them from worms,
So thickly they came seething.
Along the side of Tash-Kyya,
Most of them came into view;
At the embankment of the shore-road,
Half of them came into view.
Along the shore-road, moving softly,
Most of them came into view.
Look at the reeds, look at the flood;
Look at the people who will engulf them,
Raising a cry and unleashing upheaval.
Look at the great host engulfing them,
Raising its customary cry.
Look at the trench being dug;
Look at the army coming engulfing them,
Shrieking like crows.
Seeing them, the sentry
Beat the doolbas.
The tower-mounted doolbas
Was beaten hard,
And Khan Akun's many soldiers
Now rose and gathered.
They readied their coursers
And tethered their swift steeds;
Their unsheathed swords swayed.
The Afghans were formidable,
And drew together in a body.
They had made a trough from pinewood;
How many heroes had earlier
Demanded of Akun,
"Give us your old maid."
