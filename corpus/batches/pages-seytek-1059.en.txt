Sightless in extent was Kara-Shor,
A dreadful road from which none returned.
No living creature or beast
Could pass through it; all were stopped.
Whoever ventured there boasting of courage
Had his borrowed life cut short.
Behold this wonder:
Those inhabiting that place
Yielded their prey to no living soul.
They simply swallowed every creature whole—
Black beasts as large as horses and camels,
Setting one above another,
And making one abase another;
Behold this contest.
At its lower edge
Stands the famed city of Barbar.
Within that city
There are gold and silver of every kind,
And riches beyond measure.
It yields its treasure to no one,
And no living souls enter it.
Seeing it, Akjoltoy said,
“Here is the wonder of the earth,”
And sly Joltoy stood astonished.
If you ask where he stood,
It was upon a black ridge with lofty banks.
Of all the wonders created in the world,
Akjoltoy beheld such a mystery:
To the east lay this tribulation,
A night that blinded the eyes,
And its passes were ridged foothills.
The black dragon Mar
Had made that land its home.
Akjoltoy saw it;
He saw a land
Where no mortal’s foot had trodden.
Upon the bank of the sprawling valley,
His mind racing in many directions,
He pulled the reins
And turned Kulunsur’s head.
From Manas of former times
A great telescope had remained as a gift;
Without loosening its strap from his neck,
He drew it from his breast,
Displaying his valor,
And revealing himself as a true hero.
Any creature that saw him
Fled, despairing of life.
Atop Kulunsur,
He sat leaning to one side,
Filling his mind with resolve.
The noble hero, wolf of battle,
Did not flinch or care,
Considering suffering and doom—
Even certain death in the end—
Not worth the hem of his robe.
He turned the wheel
Of the telescope left by the magnanimous hero,
Set it firmly to his eye,
Turned its focusing screw,
And raised it to his eye.
He gazed across Keng-Özön’s expanse,
Toward the place where peris played,
To the marshland of wondrous delights,
Where moon-beautiful Akbermet,
The ill-fated peri-maiden,
Was swinging on a swing.
He stretched forward and stared.
Smoke drifted away in wisps;
Red tents and blue tents,
And bright, many-colored possessions—
Women’s belongings came into view.
Smoke rose and divided toward the sky,
And marvels of every kind appeared.
Anyone who looked carefully
Was left utterly bewildered.
There were wonders of myriad kinds,
And women’s belongings came into view.
Erected for an outing,
A white tent stood to the north.
