His true courage rose to the full,
He glanced to either side,
Growing fierce with anger,
Licking his lips and nose,
Pouring out his anger on the people,
His courage boiling, his spirit surging,
He summoned fierce resolve.
His precious life to death
He simply bound and cast away.
Tooqyzyl, the horse beneath him,
High-backed, with a low rump,
Would not tire when driven ahead.
Broad of chest and low of mane,
The handsome swift steed,
His black-jawed warrior’s whip
He grasped and swung around,
Without halting Tooqyzyl,
He struck it at the kneecap.
Then Tooqyzyl
Its thighs spread like a sheep’s,
Its back straightened like a hare’s,
Its mouth opened wide,
Its once-stiff hooves
Spread out as broad as plates,
Showing itself a true swift steed,
Leaping and gathering itself like an arrow,
The kestrel-like boar’s muzzle
He turned without tiring.
A blue spear smelling of blood,
What use was it when he charged?
The infidel warrior, brimming with strength,
Khan Sarybai, that warrior,
He broke ice and conquered mountains,
Shattered fortresses and defeated foes.
In earlier days his worth was known,
Who had fought against all the world,
His name heard across the world,
His renown reached far and wide.
Sarybay, fiercely angry,
Now God had struck him in earnest.
A blue spear smelling of blood,
He thrust it out with a roar.
Aiming for the warrior’s brow,
If approached from afar on the other side,
Aiming for the seat of his heart,
Aiming for the black eyes of that wolf-like hero,
The very place where he would die,
Apple-shaped white breast,
At the level of his windpipe,
Let the spear strike without wavering;
Then he will fall.
The moment of death is here.
Even if he had eighty lives,
Even if he swelled like a blue tick,
That foolish woman,
This is my time to turn and spear her,
Now my moment has come.
The moment for him to soil himself,
If I stretch out and strike true with my spear,
The whining infidel,
That wayward woman's fancy,
It's time to fall.
Even if he had ninety lives,
There is no stopping him now;
Unable to restrain himself, Sarybay
Now he poured out his anger.
This will give the world,
Now that he has defiled it,
This Sarybay is mad.
He was furious like a hungry lion.
He has cunning, and his venom is strong;
Who has ever escaped death?
A seasoned hound who has seen much,
A slave who has seen many conflicts,
Scouting and spying on the foe,
Being an expert in everything,
He is a servant long accustomed to it.
Without counting those around him,
Without looking about him,
His very cunning ran wild,
