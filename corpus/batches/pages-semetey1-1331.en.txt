The heroes who enter battle,
The most skillful spearmen,
The finest, complete in stratagems—
Let us summon and gather them,
And against infidels like black worms,
Against hero Khan Kongurbai,
Let us wage war without fleeing!
Understand what I have said;
My auspicious son, heed me.
While gray wolf Manas lived,
The whole world stirred;
Kakan-Chyn is no easy place.
When he campaigned against Beijing,
There were four hundred twenty thousand troops;
Heroes went and khans went,
And to costly Beijing
Men came even from India.
Many a lone hero went;
Those who went to Beijing
Went prepared for death,
Saying, ‘From Kakan-Chyn's Beijing
I shall not turn back.’
Talas filled and the land glittered;
Your formidable lion elder
Drove all those people onward
In a packed multitude.
His stride reached Beijing,
And he humbled it without regret,
Though the Muslims seemed palm-sized.
Whoever said, ‘My horse is unmatched,’
He said, ‘I shall slaughter and eat his horse’;
Your father, the gray wolf lion,
Drove them by force.
Beijing is no cheap place;
To a man who goes alone,
That Beijing is no people
Who would give even a saddlebag as blood-price.
They are no people against whom one goes alone for vengeance;
They are no bare-chested,
Open-shirted Bukharan folk.
They are no people of Kokon Khan,
Whom one humiliates and reduces to misery,
Yet survives among them for a month.
In Chinmachin's Beijing,
That people has a true army;
They are no people who avoid contest,
Nor a place that leaves conflict unanswered.
Your talk of shamelessly going alone
Is no proper word.
Here are the eyes that went and saw;
Here are the words long spoken.
This is the hand that crushed them;
I am the one who fought them.
Wading through black blood,
Your dear uncle, Khan Bakai,
While his bloodthirsty lord lived,
Fought six months without fleeing.
Yielding nothing to the lion hero,
Neither advancing nor retreating,
They stood midway.
Lion Syrgak and Almambet,
Until the soft reeds ripened,
Until the flower of the myyzam season flew,
Fought fiercely for six months.
Spears broke with a crash,
And slaughtered peoples cried out.
Heroes died and lions died;
In that holy war,
Chinese and Kyrgyz died alike.
If you face Beijing, there are wonders;
It is a great fortified city,
With ramparts that have endured much.
Your blessed uncle Bakai
Has many epic tales of it.
The road to Beijing divides five ways;
Do not say, ‘I shall send my army to Beijing,’
And bring peril upon your own head.
The road to Beijing has five branches;
