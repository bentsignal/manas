Külchoro, fairer than any man,
From the pavilion with golden pillars
Toward his sister-in-law, a woman like the moon,
Came walking softly.
Aichürök was his sister-in-law,
Yet more than that, his mother;
She had come when he was tiny,
And he had clung to her in a mother’s stead,
For she had fed him doe’s milk.
To the sister-in-law he regarded as his mother,
Choro approached and said:
“Sister, it is truly the Jediger whom I face,
And valiant Kyyaz with whom I contend.
Your Külchoro is like hardened red iron;
Let no one say he met a true slaughter
And perished there.
I was your renowned brother-in-law,
And you were my heroic sister-in-law.
Have you a coat for me to wear?
Let me proudly don your coat;
The one I must confront is valiant Kyyaz.
I shall face him once and see;
If fate has come, I shall die.
I shall behold the unyielding truth
That the Creator has inscribed for me.
If death comes and my days are done,
Do not grieve for a servant like me.
If I should die,
I was a son worth no more than dogskin;
If I survive,
I am a servant worth no more than sheepskin.
How could I be one who girded his waist firmly,
One born of the same blood as the falcon,
Sprung from the pure loins
Of Manas, radiant as the moon?
How could I have burst forth, tearing
The womb of my poor mother Kanykey?
When was I ever a son of Manas?
If my strength fails and power leaves me,
I shall die at the hands of valiant Kyyaz;
He looks ready to strike.
He rages like a tiger,
And none who clash with him survive.
His movement is like a gathering storm,
His countenance like a snowy mountainside,
His menace like a deep abyss;
Let alone the Jediger, if he is roused,
He seems able to swallow the world.
He rages like a tiger,
And none who fight him survive.
When he opens his cavernous mouth,
The cold blast issuing from it
Is like the wind along a mountain ridge.
The broad expanse of his two cheeks
Is like a vast blue field spread wide.
Your dark-browed Külchoro
Looks ready to devour all who confront him.
His upturned mustache
Is like a saber from Bukhara.
The icy glare of his two eyes
Is like flame burning upon a highland.
The narrow bridge of his nose
Is like a saber’s sheath.
When you look into his eye sockets,
They resemble the bed of a dried-up river.
The fierce, dark-browed warrior
Looks ready to drain whoever meets him.
The heroic Choro’s imposing form
Seems able to turn a mighty river.
If his stubborn fury seizes him,
He will unleash a grievous disaster;
If he comes within reach of Kyyaz,
He will seize him and dash him to the ground.
Not valiant Kyyaz alone—
Though the Jediger swarm in countless hosts,
He will slaughter them without leaving a hair.
When the falcon stirs,
Would the whole world dare confront him?
If all creation attacked in fury,
Could any man defeat Choro?
