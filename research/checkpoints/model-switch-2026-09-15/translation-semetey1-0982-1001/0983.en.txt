My sister-in-law is foolish,
His wife is greatly spoiled,

Going far without sparing their horses,

Hurriedly rousing from sleep,
Hastily putting on his clothes,
Alarmed by the arrivals,
Your elder, hero Bakai,
Saddled his gray noble steed,
Alarmed by his boys:
'Oh, my children, my two colts,
Are there gray hills and mountains?
You are in great haste.
Tormenting your poor souls,
Is an enemy laying siege?
If the enemy has come from Mecca,
Champion boys, tell the truth,
Let me mount Kokcholok,
And drive them all the way to Mecca.
If the enemy has come from China,
Let me saddle and mount my noble steed,
And attack, breaking up their ranks.
Speak without groaning,
You are in such haste;
Boys, tell the truth without groaning.'
Hero Bakai said this
To the boys who had hurried so greatly.
'Ah, my senior wife, get up,
Some yellow, freshly fermented kumys,
Pour it for my boys.'
Springing up, his senior wife,
