Was no beast that could quite catch him.
Skimming over the face of the earth,
Circling Khan Akun's city,
He harried Toltoy.
Around the marsh of Üyrülmö
He chased him three times.
Around the marsh of Chogulma
He chased him in a full circuit.
At the foot of the mountains
Of Keng-Opol he pursued him;
Along the water of broad Ürgönch
He pursued him.
Across boundless Ak-Chiy,
He went after him;
Along the edge of the rolling plain,
He pursued him.
At the far end of the swelling mountain,
He pursued him,
And beside Akun's exhausted city,
He pursued him.
He chased him around six times.
Watching from atop the tower,
Ayçürök
Mocked Toltoy.
“Ayrok, ayrok!” she cried,
God has struck Chürök mad.
“Rather than flee in a panic
From nine-year-old Külchoro,
Mountain-like Toltoy, you should die
And behold Judgment Day!
Can there be a hero like you?
You blustering brute,
Is it right to call you a hero?
Rather than scurry away, you should die;
Can there be a fool like you?
Külchoro is twelve,
Brave Toltoy is thirty-five,
Yet you have fled in terror—
Ayrok, fool, ayrok!” she cried.
“Shame, fool, shame!” she cried.
“You should behold Judgment Day;
Rather than flee about like this,
You should strangle on your trouser cord!
You are no proper man,
Fleeing from a mere youth.
You fool akin to a beast,
You are fleeing from a child.
Instead of going off fleeing from a child,
Your wineskin nose bulging,
Is there meat to set upon the platter?
Royal bridegroom, have you any shame?
You came to fight only in pretense,
And proved to be careless;
Ready to flee at the sight of a figure,
You are a phantom Toltoy.
Unable to face a child,
You are a womanly Toltoy.
Rather than flee about like this,
Accursed Toltoy, you should die
And behold Judgment Day!
Rather than run away flapping,
You should wheel and thrust your spear!
Rather than scurry away,
You should move and drive your spear!
Hurl the child from your spear
And make me your bride!
Oh, bridegroom, shame on me!
Failing as a bridegroom, drop dead;
Sink and die without offspring!
Rather than this, you fool,
Do not remain alive—drop dead!”
After hearing this from Chürök,
He stiffened in rage,
And wheeled around
Surkoyon beneath him.
He brandished and took in hand
His steel spear with a knobbed shaft.
