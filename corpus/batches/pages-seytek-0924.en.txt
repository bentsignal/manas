Say, ‘She died at Sarybai's hand;
She went to the land of no return;
The lioness met her fate,’
And abandon all hope of me.
Say, ‘Our true champion has died;
The valiant maiden met her fate;
Her blood filled the riverbed;
She was extinguished like a fire.’
Scatter those who have assembled;
Flee in every direction.
But if you are men, if you are warriors,
Strike and wage battle;
Stand beneath the banner.
Seek out and witness
The great, bloody conflict;
When you see it, let us not lose heart.
Say that Kuyaly is dead,
And surrender everything you behold!
In the fierce, entangled battle,
What use is grieving?
In the harsh, ruined ravine,
What use is wasting away in sorrow,
Paling from yellow sickness—
What use is pining?
Champions who have girded your waists,
Sarybai is a champion too,
A black-hearted lion.
Do not stand idle—join battle;
Lift up once
This grievous, bitter burden.
When each one's courage is revealed,
When they arrive with battle cries,
If I fight them one by one
And depart forever,
Lower your spears.
If you are heroes, if you are men,
If you bear the cry of the noble gray-maned wolf,
If you are hawk-like warriors,
And fate appoints me death,
Then those of you who remain alive
Stand as my support;
Keep your promise!
He has a tail and a living soul,
And venom in his purpose;
Let Sarybai despair of life,
Saying, ‘Here is a soul with a mighty arm.’
Such is the word you have heard from me.
Be my support and my strength;
Charge forth against the enemy.
If I fight while holding the upper ground,
Be my prop;
If by chance I am driven downhill,
Be my prop.
Do not let his army engulf me;
Do not let them spear me from every side
And close their ranks around me.
Let them not hem in my hands
Or block my path.
Let him not display his prowess.
Sarybai is the one I face;
Such is our notorious foe.
My allied peoples cry aloud,
Shuddering and trembling.
Would God not strike us
If I lost my senses
And turned back in flight,
Showed the enemy my horse's rump,
And surrendered my reason?
Would He not bring calamity
When the great host panics?
Would He not take them even as they flee?
Therefore heed these words:
Stand as my support.
If you do not support me,
Will not seven wolves
Fall upon and tear apart one sheep?
A lone soul faces many perils;
Will they not drag it away after beating it?
Will not a thousand men
Shoot down one person in a single day?
