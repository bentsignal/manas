To Aichürök’s side
To bewitch her and take her child,
They had gone in,
Intent on bringing Chürök
To some dreadful harm.
Once she knew their enmity,
Moonlike Aichürök—
Who could skim above a horse’s mane,
Who could fly through the air,
Who knew well how to fly—
Would she fail to know an enemy?
If she did, could she be Chürök!
The keen-witted maid Karachach,
The noble-born maid,
When the wicked old women entered,
Cast a chilling spell,
And with boundless sorcery
Blinded the eyes
Of all seven old women.
The seven razors they had prepared
Karachach secretly stole,
And into a bottomless black well
She carried and cast them:
The instant the razors
Struck the well,
Unable to withstand their poison,
The black stone beneath the water
Split clean in two.
The old women wandered in a daze,
Neither mad nor sane,
And lost the razors
The warrior had given them.
Yesterday, when they heaped humiliation upon us like sheep,
When they plundered our royal camp,
My valiant mother Kanykey
Left this solemn charge: “Should you fail to guard him,
Then to the enemy who counts us foes,
When my foal is in desperate straits,
Hand him over.”
Yesterday, when havoc was wrought in Künjüt,
Chürök proved a wise soul;
She and Karachach
Had made a pact.
How Kyyaz meant to bring disaster,
And what the seven old women
Had promised Kyyaz,
Karachach had overheard.
Her knees buckling to the ground,
She ran and reached Chürök,
Who labored in birth,
Her heart torn apart,
Tears streaming from her eyes.
Before another word could be said,
Karachach, flustered and frantic,
Came to Aichürök:
“My dearest elder sister,
If only I see you safe, my heart will rest;
I have no refuge but you.
Have you seen what I have seen?
Do you know what I know?
If you do not know what I know:
Kyyaz, son of Bagysh—
The Seven Valleys are no land for you,
The Jediger are no people for you,
And brave Kyyaz is no husband for you.
If you bear a son,
All the old women have plotted
To bewitch you, gouge out your eyes,
Seize your child and slaughter him;
They have sealed their pledge.
You were a lioness among women.
From wandering Almighty God,
You were the one I prayed for.
Find a way to outwit
The seven wicked old women, sister;
Find the right words to answer
The fierce infidel Kyyaz, sister!”
Thus Karachach spoke,
And before she had closed her mouth,
The seven spell-casting old women—
