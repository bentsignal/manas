When Bakai had grown old,
Did you leave him behind?!"
Leaning his golden-cheeked battle-axe
Against his right side,
Bakai stood there wailing!
Mourning Manas then
Remembering the past
He tore at his beard!
As Bakai stood thus,
His heart was overcome;
Seeing Bakai,
The young son of the gray wolf
Tears fell from his eyes:
The gray wolf born after
His father, noble Manas,
Was overcome with grief;
Seeing his uncle clearly,
His tears poured down.
"Uncle Bakai, heroic sultan,
My dear one,
Now that you have grown old,
Let me be the sacrifice
For your eyes that shot the white argali!
Who made the ignorant know,
My learned old Bakai,
Who made the unperceptive perceive,
My falconlike sage Bakai!
My touchstone before the renowned people,
My legacy left as a gift
By my valiant father, the gray wolf!
My saint born among mortals,
My blessed old Bakai!
Set before me, you are my good road;
If my uncle Bakai the brave*
Walks behind me, he is my countless host!
For an uncle such as you,
I would sacrifice my very life!
Without understanding very much,
I pushed you toward the grave, uncle,
And acted out of arrogance!
My dear elder khan,
Let me offer you a courser;
Take my steed Taibuurul;
If your anger is still rising,
Uncle gray wolf, leopard,
Pour my blood into a leather flask!
My old father Bakai,
Whom I held in a father's place!
Who joined what was broken,
Gathered what was scattered,
And found the means to live,
My sage!"—
Thus spoke Semetey,
Calling Bakai "uncle, bloodthirsty one";
He turned his neck toward God,
Pleaded, calling him dear uncle,
And offered Narbuurul as a gift.
Then your uncle Bakai,
Flying like a white hawk,
Praying to God
Now slaughtered a white argali;
The lion, Khan Bakai,
Now ate until his belly was filled.
Resting on his elbow,
He took a brief sleep;
At dawn
Lying on his side, he slept his fill.
He let all the fine coursers
Graze upon the fescue,
Leading them onward,
Filling their chests with grass;
Without tiring, he sat
And filled their lean bellies with grass.
Champion Bakai the leopard,
My saint who knows what to do!
