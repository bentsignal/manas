Tell me the place where we shall exchange gunfire?"
As Kozho stood surging with fury,
Then Azhy said this.
Broad-jawed and flat-tongued,
Unfaltering Azhybay,
Honking like a young goose,
Warrior Azhy began his speech
And let it pour out with a cackle:
"What you have said is also true,
Stallion Kozho, leopard;
All that you have said is true.
It is also true that I did not accept
The words you spoke at first.
It is also true that I withheld Chürök,
Out of fear of the final turmoil,
And did not give her.
It is also true that with eloquence
I began speaking many words,
Deceived you six times,
And abandoned you.
If these words of yours are true,
Warrior Kozho, leopard,
I shall accept what you demand;
I shall give you all
The maidens whose heads are pledged.
You are indeed a hero, warrior Kozho;
Now I know your true nature.
If you are starved of women,
I shall give them all together,
Even the wife in my embrace.
You are indeed a hero, Kozho;
Now I recognize your mastery.
I shall gather and give you
Every maiden among my people!
I have become your in-law, true Kozho;
Now I understand your words.
You will take Chürök, daughter of Akun,
In a few days;
Speak your truth before you leave!
And again, Kozho, turn back
And do not let your fury seize you.
State your grounds openly;
Tell me, Chynkozho,
Which of you two is the husband who will take her!
Toltoy and Chynkozho,
Whichever of you goes, let that one go;
You have strength enough for one man.
Eat meat from one platter,
And fit yourselves into one bed;
Come back without letting your destination grow old,
And return within seven days!
I shall prepare your sleeping place;
If Chürök says she will not marry,
I shall bind her with a carrying sling."
Hearing those words Azhybay spoke,
Kozho went mad,
And gulped like a rabid dog.
Raging and surging,
He spoke with renewed courage:
"If this Toltoy is unworthy,
Accept what I demand;
If you do not favor Toltoy
To take as a husband,
Then give her directly to me!"
Tümönbay, son of Tülkü,
A harsh, blustering hero,
His seizure taking hold as his soul burned,
Unable to remain standing, surged forward.
Tümönbay stood and spoke:
"You frantic Zhediger,
Whispering two by two,
What have you now said?
Do not babble, Zhediger;
Does a man flee from combat?
If you flee, you have eaten filth!
You swagger, Chynkozho;
If you take Aychürök as plunder,
Will she become wife to all of you?
