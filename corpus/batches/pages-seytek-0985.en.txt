Before the judges of horses,
Having the strongmen beat you,
Having your hair braided in fives,
The possessions in your hands
I will let hostile hands destroy,
If I do not humiliate you like a sheep,
If I do not take you as a captive wife,
May I perish as though never born,
May I die instead of living!
What use is my being a hero?
I will leave this world.
Raising a great tumult and uproar,
I will bring you to your end,
Semetey, in whom you put your trust,
If I do not bring your head low,
You ill-fated one struck from above,
If I do not scatter your four kinds of livestock,
What use is my being Sarybay?
Rather than suffer shame at a woman's hands,
I will leave this world.
I will wage war without asking leave,
That swaggering wanton woman,
If I do not take her as my captive,”
So Sarybay spoke,
A great quarrel and mighty uproar
Were flaring higher and higher,
Still more than before
They were growing fiercer.
Sarybay seemed to overwhelm the whole world,
Bearing down upon it.
His old quarrel
Left no opening in the world,
Closing it in on every side.
Sarybay, as before,
Was growing still fiercer.
Crackling like lightning,
With a menacing roar,
Raising a piercing cry,
Raising a shout of alarm,
The swine had set his heart aflame,
You fought on the stony field,
You clashed with me many times,
When I thrust, you turned aside my spear,
You bitter, vengeful bitch,
You left me without a single maned or tailed beast,
You inflicted that punishment on me.
You left me no horn with which to butt,
You left me hornless and defenseless,
Only my wasted frame remains; I have no strength,
You left me clumsy and broken.
If I do not start a boundless quarrel,
If I do not take you as a captive wife,
I am Sarybay the hero,
If I do not take my revenge without relenting,
May I perish as though never born,
May I die instead of living.
Fighting fiercely in earnest,
Grappling chest to chest,
If I do not wage a furious war,
If I do not take you as a captive wife,
May I perish as though never born,
May I die instead of living.
I am Sarybay the hero,
I will aim and thrust my spear,
I will truly avenge myself.
I will bring you to your end,
To that true place from which no soul returns,
If I do not go there myself,
The pass to which every soul must go,
If I do not cross over it.
Moving, alive, still undying,
Huddling and saying, 'I am alive,'
Gulping and saying, 'I will not die,'
I will claim my inheritance,
A woman taken as booty, he said,
I will seize her as spoil.
He burned with unbearable anguish,
Setting someone upon a horse,
Thus his soul burned with pain,
