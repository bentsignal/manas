“Your first forefather was Tügöl Khan,
Blessed from the beginning by Kydyr.
All your earliest forefathers were khans,
And every one was blessed by Kyzыр.
Whenever they rode forth, their road was fortunate,
And Kyzыр guarded them along the way.
God brought their deeds to success,
While those who opposed them never prospered—
O leopard of surpassing fortune!
Smoke rises over the ridge,
And the carpet spread upon it
Looks like a field of poppies.
The motley red saddle with its high pommel
Resembles a maiden in her splendor.
There are masses of fresh green flowers,
And carpets of every pattern lie spread.
Unless those women came from the fairies,
There is something in them beyond mortals;
Their words, if told, would never end,
And their dowry fills the world.
Those lovely girls, when seen,
Appear like poppies.
When a valiant man beholds them,
His heart is torn and he cannot stand still.
Once he has truly clasped their hands,
Unless harm befalls his fragile soul,
How could he ever part from them?
Waist a handful, face like the moon—
Such is this renowned beauty.
Her countenance is the shining moon;
Truly, great fortune is yours.
A young man who lay with her for one night
And died the following morning—
What regret could he bear
Before Almighty God?
Crimson gown and crescent waist—
If you seek a maiden, this is the place.
Standing face-to-face before you,
They all have gazelle eyes,
And speak words sweet as honey and sugar.
Your good fortune has opened,
And you have met the Night of Destiny.
The dream you saw has come true:
The maiden sovereign seeks you—
No such thing has happened in an age.
When you face one whose beauty blooms like a bazaar,
Could anyone confronting you survive?
A fairy maiden has come for your happiness.
While alive, every brave young man
Dreams of taking a beauty as his bride.
A maiden fairer than a fairy has come,
And among mortals none is like you.
The true spirit of your mighty father Manas
Has given you its aid.
May your fortune and happiness unfold;
Before you, mighty warrior,
May the light of God shine forth.
Nothing like this has existed in the world;
Your forefather Manas leads you,
And his spirit gives you aid.
Though you dig from it, it never runs out—
A treasure-house of amassed wealth is there.
There, play will satisfy your longing;
There are games and laughter there,
And the renowned beauty, the skilled maiden, is there.
When you meet her, she will sate your affection;
The renowned maiden Bermet is there.”
So Akjoltoy spoke,
Telling a thousand different tales,
And uttering words of wondrous delight.
When Akjoltoy had spoken,
Kulansur’s head could not be turned,
And the grey wolf could not remain merely watching.
From father and forefather
Came the wings he inherited for his people;
To be drawn toward women
Had been his custom from the beginning.
The noble warrior, upheld by the spirits,
When he heard this news,
Took on a thousand different airs.
