Külchoro walked amid death.
His frail life cried out,
Every bone in his body ached,
His heart was torn into a thousand pieces,
And tears poured from his eyes.
The Jediger’s black banner
And their earth-shaking uproar
Appeared before him.
Ah, your hero Külchoro thought,
“If my destined hour has come, let me die;
But if the falcon of the moon,
The gray-maned hero Semetey,
Is alive to stand over me,
And still walks unharmed,
What inheritance could I hope to receive
Before my lion uncle
If I spared this sly life
And valued it even as a hair?”
Your lion Choro’s cry split stone,
His roar shattered heads.
He charged like a black lion,
And wisdom came to him from the unseen.
Through their chiefs and champions,
Through the warriors who entered the clash,
Külchoro broke his way.
Among the Jediger who covered the earth,
He attacked and cut a path alone;
Külchoro went forth to rout them.
Their heroes and champions
Külchoro bound as he advanced;
The Jediger who boasted of their strength
Külchoro drove before him.
He girded his slender waist,
And against the Jediger covering the earth
He charged alone,
Raising the battle cry, “Manas!”
When he reached the earth-filling Jediger,
He struck and scattered their ranks.
Wherever his spear could reach,
He crushed them with his spear;
When anyone was caught upon its point,
Your mighty lion Choro
Ran him through and flung him away.
Wherever his battle-axe could reach,
He hewed them with the axe;
Lion Choro surged among them.
Wherever his sword could reach,
He sliced them with the blade;
Your Külchoro carved through them.
Roaring as he poured out his wrath,
He scattered the Jediger,
Spearing everyone he overtook,
Searching, “Where is Kyyaz, the cur?”
He speared the common soldiers,
Searching, “Where is that villain Kyyaz?”
He raced in pursuit behind them.
“Let those fated to die, die;
And let those who remain alive
Endure many calamities.
Let them lose their wits in terror;
From this day on, let the Jediger
Say, ‘Dear lord, we submit,’
And crawl into a pit.”
He raced upon his thoroughbred,
And every giant warrior
Who ventured near him
He seized by the shins and hurled away.
At the sight of Külchoro,
The Jediger crossed
The foothills of Üch-Koshoy;
As if a falcon had struck a bustard,
The Jediger fled.
“Alas, prince, we submit!
We shall not attack—we surrender!”
“We yield!” they cried,
“We are defeated!” and then
They called out to Külchoro.
