If he dies, we share one blood,
While alive, we are fellow countrymen,
While safe, we are companions,
Sharing one name, we are comrades,
Sharing our gains, we are neighbors,
While safe, we gather together,
While alive, we are companions,
We have slept in one bed,
We have shared the same delights,
We have hunted game together,
We grew up from childhood,
We lived badly, then prospered,
We lived poor, then grew rich,
We went without, then gained abundance,
Our camps stood side by side,
Our valleys were shared,
If safe, we shall gather together,
If alive, we shall be companions,
Come share counsel, come, my lion,
Every grief and grievous pain—
Come, my hero, resolve it now,
This is my word—behold:
For God's sake and for mine,
I shall gladly meet them in combat,
Though the people wail and heroes die,
Though all are utterly spent,
Stand behind me as my support,
I shall attack Külchoro,
I shall unleash my wrath on Seytek,
I shall bring slaughter upon the Kyrgyz,
I shall summon strength into my arms,
If she falls into my hands, I shall humiliate her,
I shall violate Chürök,
Brave men, old and young,
Say, “Sarybay has given the command,”
Do not grieve; satisfy your desire,
Let one of the seven days bring death,
Why should I live on in vain?
Let me gladly meet them, thrusting spears,
Let us grapple violently,
Let me perish fighting to the end,
Aichürök is the one I desire,
Kuyaly, on whom my enemies have set their sights,
That radiant beauty—
I shall ride straight at her and seize her,
If turmoil and strife erupt on every side,
Drawing bows like pouring rain,
Shooting arrows like hail,
When the lion's eyes blaze,
When his blood-drinking frenzy seizes him,
I shall settle the score in full,
Making the Kyrgyz shriek,
I shall drive them away and plunder them,
If Külchoro struts,
I shall beat the old madness from him,
If the Kyrgyz roar,
I shall divert the water from their riverbed,
If they boast of their young hero,
I shall snuff him out like a fire,
Thus spoke Sarybay,
Making the wide world narrow as a grain,
Such was his furious state,
Unable to withstand his wrath,
The whole vast army marched out,
From the dreadful rage of that swine,
His very flesh bristled,
His eyes sank deep as pits,
When that swine flew into a frenzy,
Unable to withstand his wrath,
Of the scouts standing watch,
Several were slaughtered,
Whatever he faced became flame,
Whatever he attacked ceased to exist,
Nothing remained for him to behold,
Those he routed came to a halt,
Such was Sarybay's state,
Rushing about in frantic haste,
When grief made his eyes blaze,
Whenever the wretch grew angry,
The vast army roared and misery seethed,
