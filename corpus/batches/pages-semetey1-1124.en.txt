Losing their wits,
Abandoned the cauldrons
And fled toward the mountains.
The pale-mottled red banners—
The wailing Chinese
Raised unceasing tumult.
They fell upon the meat,
And all was confusion.
The Muslims who saw this
Lost their wits,
And fled to ask aid
From Koshoy and Töshtük.
They rushed about in panic,
Unable to find their bearings.
The Muslims lost their senses;
Kökötöy's Bokmurun—
They cursed his mother and father.
Fleeing, bolting, and running,
The Muslims reeled in panic,
Despairing of their dear lives.
Ezkara, khan of the Manchus,
Raged on Chongküröng;
The peerless, cunning diviner
Prepared to test the heroes.
He wrapped himself in his armored coat
And girded his unsheathed sword.
If you had seen Ezkara,
He raged like a blue boar.
The hero Manas was not there;
If the chance arose, Ezkara
Prepared to slaughter
All the Muslims.
Ezkara, khan of the infidels,
Had a form as great as half a mountain;
Were there no means from Khan Manas,
He looked able to devour
All the Muslims without remainder.
His fangs protruding,
He slowly gathered
Every swift horse
And placed them in procession.
Leaving no Muslim aside,
Did you see him gather their heroes?
He gathered their heroes,
Placed great Koshoy at their head,
And arranged them in procession.
Khan Koshoy of the Katagan—
He looked upon and assessed him:
"He made Chech-Döbö at At-Bashy
Into a proper homeland,
And made the scattered Kyrgyz
Into a proper people.
Khan Koshoy of the Katagan
Has reached eighty-five;
All his courage
Has fled his body.
His neck has grown long,
His sunken eyes have reddened,
His joints have grown loose,
And his eyes glint off to the side.
Both corners of his eyes fester;
His whole body trembles.
He is a man grown old—
How many stratagems
Can Koshoy of the Burut possess?
If I become angry, I shall not relent;
I would not be satisfied
With eighty old men like this."
Then he saw
Er Töshtük, son of Eleman,
Cast his exceptional gaze upon him,
And began to assess him.
What could dear Töshtük do?
His right shoulder was broad;
He was a hero who could shatter the Oirat.
Thick-wristed and stone-hearted,
His prowess was of another kind.
