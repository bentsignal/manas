Your strength is no match for Beijing;
Those who fought it did not return alive,
Your power is no match for Beijing.
Yesterday, my son, did the news reach you?
They took the road blessed by Khidr,
Your warrior father at their head,
An army of forty-two tumens went,
And in Beijing, untouched by Solomon,
When they were exacting judgment,
Your father Manas was a lion;
Among mortals, one like your father
The Creator who gives life
Will not soon grant again.
Your father Manas was a lionhearted hero;
In Beijing, untouched by a holy man,
There he inflicted suffering.
When it came time to load up, your lion father,
Your sharp-beaked one, could not understand;
Though I sat weeping and speaking,
He would not heed wretched me.
Gathering an army from among heroes,
Taking people and nation in abundance,
What I had said came to pass on that road:
They died in Kakan.
The great mullah who came from Mecca,
Many formidable khans died;
When they clashed in slaughter,
All the mighty ones died.
Your khan-father Manas, lionhearted hero,
Returned after fighting;
Which of them shall I name?
He lost the dear ones
Who had served as his support.
Your hero father, blessed by Khidr,
Entered without looking back and returned;
In the midst of the fierce battle,
He lost the warriors.
When your father began the war,
From the mountain side
Of high-cliffed Beijing they struck him;
The warriors suffered martyrdom,
And your dear father turned back.
Having become governor in Beijing,
He loaded and took much gold;
Then, without realizing it,
He lost the governors.
He grieved as he went on fighting;
Then your lion father
Lost his dear companions.
My lion son, hear this:
The Chinese tribes are a mighty people;
Many who marched to war
Met destruction there!
Imposing Beijing is the chief city;
Those who fought it saw true calamity.
When they went to the city of Chinmachin
To wage a true holy war,
There were formidable and able men,
Many mighty tigers among them.
In the midst of that war
There was old Bakai, blessed by Khidr;
As the slaughter raged,
There were many heroes in all.
Urging on his dun horse,
Your mighty father too was there.
Wise old Koshoy was there,
And all the heroes were there.
There were skillful spearmen,
And mighty wielders of battle-axes.
There were lions born exceptional,
Who did not shrink from death,
Who never turned back before dying;
There were heroes alongside your father,
There were peoples and their khans,
And there too was lion Alman,
Who knew every secret
Of Beijing and Kakan-Chin.
There was your precious khan-father,
