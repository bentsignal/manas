Made the earth thunder and mountains shift,
Tormenting every soul who heard,
As Sarybai sat astride it.
That infidel Sarybai
Charged like a wild boar.
The fierce courage of that swine
Was like a wind that shattered mountains and rocks.
His ferocity was like a quagmire;
All but the hero Külchoro
Seemed to collapse and fall.
Such was the warrior’s state,
The ruthless devourer Sarybai.
The infidel’s fury overflowed;
His roaring shook the valley.
Despairing of life and thinking themselves dead,
The teeming Argyn and Kyrgyz people,
Unable to collect their wits,
Fled amid the thunder.
Sarybai, son of the Ogre,
Seemed ready to reap destruction;
If the scoundrel had his way,
He would stuff the entire world
Into a single saddlebag.
His savage fury seemed all-consuming,
His strength ready to swallow lives.
Each hair upon his face
Was like the haft of a battle-ax.
The fierce courage of that swine
Was like flame burning across the uplands.
Such was Sarybai,
That blazing boar.
The earth split with a crash,
As he roared as though to devour them.
Like a crumbling mountain ledge,
Like a flood pouring down a slope,
Like a deep-dug grave,
The madman came upon them.
Firing his musket and drawing his bow,
Sarybai spared not his life;
He cursed again and showered arrows,
Never sated though he drank blood.
My resting place beneath the earth
Was there beside Zhelpinish.
“To unleash a terrible slaughter,
To avenge my father—
This alone was my desire.
My prowess is unlike any other,
My valor is beyond measure.
I shall make good the words I spoke;
How could I fail to avenge
The grievance famed among the people!
Ever since I became Sarybai,
I have defeated all who confronted me,
And swallowed all who charged me.
I have hewn down everyone I met,
And massacred and despoiled
Many champions and mighty giants who opposed me!
I speak to shatter your courage;
I shall slaughter many of you and take revenge!
I am the black eye of disaster,
Calamity itself.
No winged creature can escape me;
I am an impassable black precipice.
I am a dense forest of snares,
I swallow all who grapple with me.
I shall strike terror like a dragon,
And take my vengeance everywhere!
I am the very eye of calamity,
The black scourge itself.
I am a vast black valley
That cannot be exhausted even when shoveled away.”
So saying, Sarybai,
With fiery flame burning from his mouth,
Took a mountain-like mace in hand,
Struck Zhelmaian on the flank,
Broke through the narrows and opened a path,
And attacked Külchoro,
Standing there in wrath.
With his battle-ax he struck
The shrill-voiced brass spear.
