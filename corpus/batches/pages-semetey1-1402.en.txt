That would-be hero, Lord Kong,
We shall tread upon his brow!
If mighty Allah grants us a path,
We shall scatter all his wealth!
The one you will grapple with is the Kakanchin,
The one you will oppose is Bakburchun;
Lungtu, Kengtu, and Dyngsha—
While Manas was alive,
I encountered all these men!
My valiant morning star, listen well:
The road to Beijing has five branches.
In my old age you make me lead the road,
And have heaped hardship on this wretched head!
When I seek a night’s lodging, you make me a guest;
In your uncle Bakai’s old age,
You would have me begin the tale!”
Over there, hazy and angular in the distance,
Ending in a dim and shadowed point—
For a man riding a powerful horse,
My mighty champion, listen well:
That is a desert forty days across;
The land looming beyond it
Is the country of the Manchus.
That white ridge beneath forked clouds
Is Chong-Shangkai itself.
The sight of it once stirred my heart;
I beat those people black as coal
And took tribute from them!
While the learned Manas lived,
Alas for those woeful days,
I used to do whatever I knew how to do!
Whoever offered resistance,
I would seize and cut down!
I would cast humiliation upon them like a flock of sheep;
From the graceful beauties—
With thimble mouths and camel-calf eyes,
With darting black eyes,
Crimson dresses and moon-slim waists—
I would take tribute in daughters,
And satisfy my desire in this way.
While my lion was alive,
This was a land where I went as governor,
A land where I took its gold and silver
As tax and tribute.
On the face of that slope
Stands a building raised high across it.
When this campaign comes to mind,
My soul cannot be held within a hand!
Thinking often of former times,
My beard has turned white;
When I think of them, I grieve!
My old fury is stirred,
My beloved colt,
And I lie here telling my sorrow!
The sable cap upon one’s head—
My beloved colt,
Who has not worn one at a jaunty tilt?
While pride remained upon our heads,
Who did not live in delight?
When they saw your uncle,
No khan of China failed to panic.
Yesterday, when he assembled the Muslim giants,
When we brought hell down upon
Beijing, beyond the reach of the flood,
And exacted its reckoning,
Why did I not meet the decree
God had ordained at that time?
While my companion was alive,
Why did I not die then!
Black-sooted and bulletproof—
