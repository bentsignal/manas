If they came near the palace,
Aychürök, daughter of Akun,
Was exceedingly prudent and wise;
Yet wit alone could not save
Poor Chürök from the infidels.
Keeping the hero's condition unknown,
She had become absorbed in diversion;
For many days on end
She had lain beside Semetey.
Azhybay and Tümönbay
Had learned of the one between them.
Wondering, "How will those two come?"
She had gone into her bed.
For many days and many nights,
The doomed woman had not slept;
She had sunk into pleasure.
When those two arrived,
She lay sleeping without stirring.
At Aychürök's house,
By her door, the gatekeeper
Began a conversation;
They asked the gatekeeper:
"Aychürök, daughter of Akun—
Is Chürök in the house?
The turmoil that stirred the whole world—
Is that trouble in the house?"
If you ask how Chürök fared,
She woke with a start from sleep,
And rolled onto her right side;
Her excited heart grew calm,
Her eyes opened wide as bowls,
And Aychürök sprang up.
From outside the door came a shout:
Azhybay and Tümönbay
Were calling out for Aychürök.
Aychürök, daughter of Akun,
Rose thoughtfully, drawing out her thoughts,
Stretching tall like a young plane tree,
Arching herself like a lynx,
Reluctant to utter a word,
Maturing in beauty like a doe,
Her hair winding around her head,
Maturing in beauty like a deer,
Her hair winding around her feet.
Radiant Aychürök
Turned and came out through the door.
"I have a grave already dug;
My dear Aychürök,
I have boiling misery.
Toltoy of the Zhediger
And his consummate hero Kozho
Have descended upon our heads;
They have scattered all our livestock.
Unless we give you as a tribute,
They will level the deepest fortress
And slaughter every one of us.
He has seized and plundered the people;
If we do not give you first,
A force as great as the Zhediger will come,
So numerous the earth will sag.
My dear Chürökbay,
If you heed our words,
He too is a father's son;
Go to brave Toltoy.
If you go to Toltoy and die,
We shall see your bones;
Saying, 'She became her people's shield,'
We shall have a tomb of gold built
And bury you exactly so.
Understand well all I have said;
My golden Chürök, heed my words!
If you do not heed our words,
If you do not go to brave Toltoy,
They will water their orchards
And turn this place into gardens;
They will take your aged father Akun
And make him gather firewood;
If the Zhediger take us in their wrath—
