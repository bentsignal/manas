At every sight of you the heart is reassured;
There is no refuge but you.
A challenger has come to dispute with you;
A heroic lion has come to oppose you—
An enemy named Sarybai.
He is the lion who will wrestle you;
God has granted his wish.
Hero, the term you gave Sarybai
Has now expired, he says;
“Hero, my time to wrestle has come,” he says.
The roaring ravager
Has arrived in all his strength.
He has readied his cartridge pouch
And prepared his powder and shot.
His destructive intent is plain;
He has put on armor no bullet can pierce.
He has made ready his ornamented spear;
The six-layered black earth
Seems to smoke from its depths
Beneath the fearsome boar.
Heed the lord’s words:
A warrior has come to wrestle,
An enemy has come to joust,
An enemy has come to fight.
Shouting as he entered the field,
The enemy of old came as your equal.
Before the terrible scourge that has arrived,
Hero, dread has fallen upon my heart.
If the great host now joins battle,
How can its camp remain settled?
Before the torment of that mighty swine,
What will become of the village?
Watching him, I burned with anguish;
I utterly despaired of life.
I looked, understood, and grieved;
Today I have despaired of precious life.
Not Sarybai but a bullet has come,
A rending flood has swept in;
A fierce force, a mighty conflict—
From the infidel’s wrath,
The black earth itself trembles.”
So saying, the diviner
Stood there delivering his news.
The great drum was made of camel hide,
And was three fathoms thick;
Its hide was camel hide,
And when struck once with an axe,
Its booming rolled out upon the road.
Its thunder spread across the earth;
The wrestler struck it when flight threatened.
Yesterday, when the dawn shone clear,
When light broke through the earth,
When the nightingale sang in the forest
And the cuckoo mourned in the mountains,
When the bright dawn broke,
A booming sound was heard.
When the great drum was struck,
Startled to his fly-like soul,
He saddled Kulansur,
And the fated Seytek rose.
The people cried out in tumult,
Raising a clamorous alarm,
Their banners unfurling in splendor:
“The fearsome foe has come to battle!”
All the champions grew excited,
All the lion-maned warriors were stirred;
The army shouted and surged forward.
Wise men surpassing all in judgment
Proclaimed this conflict aloud;
Commanders of ten shouted fiercely,
And the imposing champions,
Broad-chested and wide-breasted,
Declared that their hearts welcomed his coming.
All the champions grew excited,
All the heroes cried out,
All the wrestlers armed themselves.
Sarybai joined the conflict—
Such was his fierce wrath.
When they grappled, no bullet could pierce him;
Their strength was spent and their power gone.
