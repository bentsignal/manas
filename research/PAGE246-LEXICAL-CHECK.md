# Page 246 lexical check and transliteration audit

Checked 2026-09-14 by the same translation worker; no independent specialist review. No prior English Manas translation consulted.

## Supported correction

`manas-2010:p0246:b001:l007`: **Боз керешке булуң чер**.

Recommended draft: **“A gray shelter, a secluded thicket—”**

The initial failure came from looking for *кереш* as if *-ке* were necessarily a dative ending. **Керешке is itself a dictionary headword.** Yudakhin sense 2 means shelter/protection/barrier; its illustrative verse specifically uses forest as shelter from rain. [Yudakhin: керешке](https://el-sozduk.kg/керешке/).

*Чер* II means dense forest or thicket, supported by both Yudakhin and the 1969 explanatory dictionary. [чер](https://el-sozduk.kg/чер/). *Булуң* includes a corner, recess or sheltered nook; Yudakhin's forest compound denotes a separate patch of woodland. [булуң](https://el-sozduk.kg/булуң/). *Боз* can mean pale/earth/ash gray, as well as several contextual senses; gray is the conservative literal epithet here. Its exact poetic attachment remains less certain than the shelter/woodland sense. [боз](https://el-sozduk.kg/боз/).

The following two rows say that God has given warmth untouched by wind. The line is therefore best understood as a protective woodland metaphor for Manas. The English supplies punctuation and an indefinite article, not a recovered missing word. No raw emendation is required. Confidence: strong for shelter/thicket, moderate for the full nominal syntax and gray epithet. Do not upgrade to specialist-verified text.

## Page 183 remains a lexical gap

`manas-2010:p0183:b002:l001` contains **«Үттүгүңө шоодай», - деп,**. The submitted draft had “Üttügüngö shoodai!” followed by the English reporting clause. Although the reporting clause was translated, the entire quoted utterance was not. It must not count as a fully translated English verse row. Root is preserving it separately as an explicit lexical gap; this worker has not altered the 165–184 batch during this audit.

Yudakhin/2015 dictionaries explain шоодай as bare/isolated/jutting, but that does not establish the meaning of the complete utterance. [Dictionary entry](https://el-sozduk.kg/ШОО/). A possible connection with үт, a narrow hole, does not supply a defensible grammatical or idiomatic translation of Үттүгүңө. No invented sexual curse, threat or paraphrase has been substituted.

An additional public Kyrgyz printed witness was acquired: *Түркілік тәрбие / Turkic Upbringing*, Turkic Academy anthology, Almaty: Intellservice, 2013. PDF and printed page 339 reproduce the same Kyrgyz utterance alongside a Latin-script transliteration. The page was rendered and visually checked: `tmp/page183-anthology-witness.png`. This confirms that the spelling also occurs in another printed setting, but supplies no Kyrgyz lexical explanation; independence of the underlying source text is unproved. Only Kyrgyz and its transliteration were consulted for this comparison.
