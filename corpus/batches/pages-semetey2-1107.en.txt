Looked ominous to my eyes.
If the raging Jediger gray wolves
Overflow their bounds and attack,
How could any man withstand them?
The seething Jediger
Are fearsome indeed—
Could even all the peoples of the age endure them?
Shamed at our own hearth,
Are we to die in panic?
Shall we submit to the Jediger,
Lead camels as tribute,
And surrender untold livestock,
Only to die an ignoble death?
Whimpering like young puppies,
Are we to die dishonorably?
Know that Kyyaz comes in might,
Bringing great peril to our precious lives.
The chestnut horse beneath him
Is a steed that flies like a cloud,
And brave Kyyaz upon it
Is worth a thousand men.
Kalcha and Kadyrbai,
And below them Tölöbai,
Have a force of some four thousand men.
In his wrath he seems able
To reduce the whole world to servitude;
If he battles the world,
He seems able to leave slaughter in his wake.
When he stiffens in fury,
He could turn the waters of a river;
Anyone who provokes his vengeance,
The roused Jediger
Could kill and crush to dust.
Sending armies in from all four sides,
Trampling wherever they reach,
Grinding it beneath the earth,
They could spread out their sevenfold host.
Smashing mountains and rocks,
They could march on, leaving a broad black road;
If they close in savage combat,
They could crush anyone they encounter.
Even if all the peoples of the world arose,
Not a few but a multitude,
Damn those Jediger—
They could overrun them in a single campaign.
Anyone who crosses them
Will behold the face of Judgment Day;
If they fight in burning wrath,
Not only the Kyrgyz—
They could divide all the peoples of the age.
Whoever beholds their fury
Would cry, ‘Mercy, my lord, I submit!’
And fall prostrate before them,
Surrendering utterly.
Silver-eyed and sun-faced,
My roaring heroic uncle,
You yourself are here,
And the true hero, ever ready for war,
Your matchless eagle Külchoro,
Your warrior, is here.
Let us halt the Jediger army,
Take fine horses from our pastures,
Load up treasure and gold,
Clothe them in sumptuous robes,
Choose beautiful girls for them,
And mount them on swift horses;
Perhaps we should present all this
To the vast Jediger people.
Let us neither quarrel nor feud,
Nor wage war before a year has passed.
Let us not fight until not one
Solitary soul remains.
Let us accept their demands
And pay all their tribute.
Let us obey what they say,
Turn our stallions’ heads,
Raise our hands amid the tumult,
And declare that we submit.
