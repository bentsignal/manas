He faced giants and ogres,
And none he fought escaped alive.
Breaking away with some forty warriors,
Brave Syrgak of Khan Ulak
Appeared, stunning the mind,
As though alive;
He shouted, “Hero!”
And called out for Külchoro.
No soul could withstand his wrath;
Genial Syrgak, the lion hero,
Again leveled his spear.
To the three in that battle,
He gave his aid as a comrade.
If you looked to this side,
There rode one upon Aisarala.
Though foes were tens of thousands,
He swept them away in every battle.
He wore yellow rawhide trousers,
Bore a spear without a tassel,
And girded a sword without a sheath,
Biting hard upon his lip.
He wound his sash around his waist
And cast the world into tumult.
Tall and broad-shouldered,
Both wrestler and lion,
From the city of the Khakhan
Came this fierce lion from China.
The mighty hero gave a shout;
The earth broke and heaved,
As though seven thousand troops had come.
Straining like a lion,
He readied and leveled his spear,
Shouting from the front,
Calling to the hero Külchoro,
His only son.
Power rang from his battle cry,
And from the fire on either side
Hair enough for socks was singed away.
The hero Külchoro
Was accompanied by your uncle.
Raging and charging,
He prepared to face the foe.
“My broad-shouldered uncle Külchoro,
You are a spring flowing down the valley,
A lamp seen among the people.
You are a legacy left by a hero,
A noble one remaining as his sign.
The fierce enemy Sarybay
Has battled you beyond capture;
Alas, how fares my son?” he cried.
Taking a shortcut along the narrow path,
From before his khanly nephew,
He led Buurul, the horse ridden to battle,
Urging it hurriedly onward,
And shouted encouragement.
The mighty hero whose patron spirit lived,
The lord supported by the holy spirits,
Himself accompanied him.
The Sarala beneath him
Shone as though touched by moonlight;
A red banner with a golden crescent
Fluttered in his hand.
“Charge, my son, charge!” he cried,
“Hold your spear ready!
Let me see what troubles you.
Against Sarybay, whom you must fight,
Clashing and thrusting,
I shall stand beside you.
Let me see the measure
Of that swine Sarybay!
You were a hero, you were a lion,
A warrior who knew the enemy’s ways.
Level your spear and break their ranks;
In fury, drive the enemy away!
Your heroic father is beside you—
Now unleash true slaughter!
Sarybay, who has challenged us,
Has shown his enmity thus.
Do not spare your precious life,
Do not glance to either side.
