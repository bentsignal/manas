My dear sons, listen!
When you face them, fire,
And go on campaign to Beijing;
Remember me then
As your lame ritual father!”
Thus Bölökbay finished
The warrior's speech.
Treasurer Bölökbay,
Ah, your dear ritual father!
Within the treasury itself,
At the side of the exit door,
Were five guest palaces,
Built exceedingly strong.
To the mouth of that treasury
No man not fated to die went;
Whoever entered without care
Never escaped suffering.
At the treasury entrance stood
Poor Khan Bölökbay's
Champion guards.
With saddlebags full of bullets,
Their knees bending to the ground,
Many champions lifted them.
They ran carrying twelve saddlebags
Of bullets and powder,
And entrusted them
To the prince, the butcher's son.
After Bölökbay gave him these,
Brave Semey, son of Manas,
Your lion who had studied the lesson, said:
“My ritual father Bölökbay,
We should devote ourselves to you!
My falcon father was a khan;
Thinking the inheritance left by my father
Was gone when my father died,
I find there is a man like my father!
I have courage and fierce longing,
And all my equipment is complete.
I may entrust myself to God;
I will set right whoever attacks me!
I will gird my waist firmly,
And overthrow whoever I face.
If fate arrives, I will die.
Kongurbay is lord
Of heavenly Beijing city;
I will wage fierce war
And thus avenge my father
Who departed this world!
I will campaign against the khan;
If Almighty Allah supports me,
I will unleash a calamity
Upon the black-hearted Kalmyks.
Without bringing slaughter and taking captives,
Without spilling blood and taking revenge,
I will not cease while any remain alive,
Nor cease fighting for six months.
Without learning the ground by scouting,
Without raiding horses from Champang,
How could I fail to fight
The warrior like a black mountain,
Whose courage is high and venom strong,
Who sets right whoever attacks him—
The wrathful infidel,
Warrior Khan Kongurbay!
If I do not take revenge, let me die;
If I do not pursue my father's blood price,
Let me sink childless!
Against the Chinese, swollen like a bladder,
I will wage fierce struggle.
If my strength suffices against the infidel,
I will subdue the Gün-zhang-zhung people,
And inflict bitter grief.
I will avenge Almambet,
My beloved ritual father!
The one I will fight is Kakan-Chyn;
The one I lost is brave Chubak;
The one I will war against is Kongurbay.
