Smashing his walled fort,
Toppling his earthen fort,
We shall bring utter slaughter!
If Khan Shooruk grows angry,
Mounted on wondrous steeds, breaking the ranks,
We shall sport at spear-fighting.
If Shooruk strains against us,
We shall not spare his fly-like little soul.
