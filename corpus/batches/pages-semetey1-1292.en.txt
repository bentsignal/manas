Like a cloud about to rain again,
His face turned wrathful.
He had no intention of making peace;
If Kojo got him into his hands,
He looked unlikely to leave him alive.
Look at the madman—
Fit to be offered as a sacrifice;
If spared because he was kin,
He looked ready to cut off a head.
Kojo stood seething,
Speaking to Semetey:
"That is a dark road from which none return,
The road you have traveled halfway.
That was the host that deceived
Your broad-chested father Manas six times.
Orphaned of your father,
You are the one who wandered away
To Temir Khan's city.
I am the one who grappled
With your father Manas the lion.
Now my anger has risen and I stand enraged;
I shall seize your migration from Talas,
My orphan, son of Manas,
And take my vengeance by crushing you.
You raided my migration from Keng-Kol;
This year I shall avenge myself
Upon those accursed slaves.
Into my palace like a white gull
You brought Khan Bakai;
The uncatchable Kara At
You had seized, Semetey,
And made slaves ride it bareback.
The line you drew at Keng-Kol,
The spectacle you showed me yesterday—
You have come to behold
The torments of Judgment Day,
Accursed Semetey;
You have come to die
At the hands of mighty me.
When I hear that you have camped at Talas,
Accursed one, I shall seize
Your wife Aichürök.
Upon Buurul, not yielded when asked,
I shall cast a cold lasso;
Taibuurul, not yielded when begged for,
I shall take bareback as booty.
I shall bring splendid slaughter;
You have greatly provoked my rage,
And I shall cut off your head.
I shall drag you along the road
And expose you to the Dangyts!
I hear that you are my kinsman,
Accursed Semetey;
I shall kill and bury you,
And seize your slaves!"
The earth split with a roar;
Kojo raged and thundered.
God now struck Kojo,
And he spoke words of great humiliation.
He stood bald and stark,
Like a post for tethering horses;
This Kojo had abandoned the faith
And become an infidel.
He began a grievous humiliation,
And was capable of carrying it through;
Plainly, Kojo was capable
Of inflicting everlasting humiliation
Upon the valiant hero.
If Kojo got into his hands
The orphaned son of Manas,
He was capable of slaughtering him
As a sacrifice without hesitation.
He left no honest soul in peace;
Kojo the kinsman came to no good.
Dear Kojo the kinsman
Had crookedness in his words
And cloudiness in his eyes;
