“Agysh has thrown the Kalmyk!” they cried.
With bloodthirsty Manas at their head,
There were a thousand black camels,
And a thousand black-blazed mares.
“Take them—here is your prize!”
They drove the herd up to him.
Without accepting that prize,
Brave Agysh hurried about.
Then Agysh spoke,
Uttering outrageous words no man would say:
“I threw your champion; my hands are free.
Release my hand, Manas,
And show before my eyes
The marvel I saw earlier.
I will not turn aside for these beasts;
I will not miss the entertainment.
I will not take this prize;
I will not leave my maidens.
I will not turn aside for this prize;
I will not part from the young brides.
I will not accept this prize.
For the exciting games and revelry,
I will not surrender my girls.”
Brave Agysh rushed away,
Running toward the girls;
Brave Agysh fled off.
Agysh did not take the prize.
The Muslims divided among themselves
The prize belonging to brave Agysh.
They had seen the unruly gray-maned hero
Prove himself a true warrior;
Honoring the forefathers, the Muslim people
Divided up the prize.
After the prize had been taken and settled,
When the Muslim faithful stood assembled,
Akun Khan, khan of Afghanistan,
Spoke at length of his solemn pledge
To the hero, bloodthirsty Manas:
“Hear the end of my words, Khoja;
Yet my words are not exhausted.
At Khan Kökötöy’s funeral feast,
Before the great assembly,
Bloodthirsty Manas and Akun
Once became pledged in betrothal.
Akun rose and said this:
“A star moved in the heavens;
Manas and Akun Khan
Became friends until the next world.
You gave Koshoy, khan of Katagan,
A pair of leather wrestling breeches,
And truly received the blessing
Of Koshoy, father of the people.
Now, snow-leopard Manas,
You have been granted a child.
During the summer just past,
When the people moved toward the highlands,
In the season when horses’ coats turn dark,
At Belendi’s Pass,
Watching argali and chasing gazelles,
At the place where I hunted game,
Upon the ridge of Belesche,
Where the wolf cubs played,
As I rode toward a certain place,
A whirlwind was dancing there.
On the slope of Uluu-Too,
A gathering seemed to be celebrating.
