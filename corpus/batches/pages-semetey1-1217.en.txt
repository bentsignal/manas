Their fortress stands on the slope;
The infidel people are numerous.
Their grass above has grown thick;
The Muslim people are few.
Accomplished Manas, heed us!
Will seven wolves fighting over one foal
Leave even its carrion?
They are a people of seven ages;
If they strike the palm-sized Muslims,
Will they leave even our carrion?
Their root is a black lake;
They are the inexhaustible Chinese people.
Can any man match them?
If these infidels surge like a flood,
Will they spare a soul?
Can one who clashes with the black, monstrous Chinese survive?
Can one who grapples them remain alive?
With the dog whose mother is forsworn,
Let us not enter into enmity.
Let us not invite it upon ourselves;
Let us not bring calamity
Upon our dear heads.
Let us not see slaughter from China
And all die together!”
When the Muslims said this,
Hero Manas surged in wrath.
Raging then,
He confined some,
And beat others.
Leaning toward every valley,
Their migration remained behind—did you see?
Saying, “They have acted thus
Toward noble Prince Manas,”
The palm-sized Muslims
Themselves remained—did you see?
“Will we not all reach the root together?!”
The Muslims murmured;
Before China could destroy them,
Manas himself would slaughter
The palm-sized people of Islam.
The accursed bloodthirsty man's
Command lived in his words,
And stubbornness lived in his eyes.
This accursed bloodthirsty man,
Quarreling with China,
Speaks many such words;
If you live, wait and see—
The palm-sized Muslims,
He will destroy them himself.
Will he heed the words of the weak?
Will a river-flood engulf us,
And many souls die?
It is a surging flood;
The Chinese people are mountain-like.
If a mountain falls, will it spare us?
Can one who attacks
The teeming infidels survive?
If you suddenly grapple them,
If we suddenly attack,
Will they not bring calamity?
Will we not all reach our root together?
If we match strength with the infidels,
Will they not bind alive
Every one of the whole people?
From Jylgynduu, Keng-Kol, and Talas,
We are fated to suffer humiliation
Because of ill-starred Manas.
This is his hardened habit;
Your grim bloodthirsty man
Would drive everyone into China—
Such is his way of bringing slaughter.
May the incorrigible wretch never prosper;
He remains bellowing.
If we merely watch, upon the whole age
He will unleash utter turmoil.
Let us go to the accursed man;
Saying, ‘My horse is thin,’
Let us lodge our complaint.
