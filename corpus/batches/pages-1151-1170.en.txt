The two ate their fill of its meat.
Traveling without pity,
Riding far, sparing no horses,
Over land with no mountain even an ankle-bone high,
With no hollow big enough for a saddlecloth,
Through desert where no dog had passed,
Along a winding road,
He turned his horse's head.
Your poor Almambet
Stood there troubled,
And to Syrgak beside him
Spoke these words:
“On the bluff ahead,
There is a path for a single horse.
The duck has left the lake.
For not arriving two days earlier,
Misfortune falls on Almambet.
Six days ahead,
There is the mighty one called Great Malgun.
He has a club as big as a hut,
And hands with which to wrestle.
