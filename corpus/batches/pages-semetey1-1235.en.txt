These were my elder sister's words:
"Go out and meet
Your valiant brother-in-law Toltoy."
Do not swagger away, poor man;
Stand there where you are!
You have come as a bridegroom—
Is a bridegroom like you?
Does a bridegroom cross
A saddle-backed ridge?
Does one come courting
And then flee again?
Can a bridegroom who runs away
Be called a proper bridegroom?
These were Churok's words:
"Go out and meet
Your mountain-like brother-in-law."
Turn your horse's head;
Noble brother-in-law, wait!
Thirty brides and thirty girls
Have begun their games in a stream;
Among them your wife
Sways like a fairy.
Appearing like a whole world,
They came out separately to meet you,
Saying, "The gray-maned bridegroom comes."
If you play, there are games and laughter;
There are riches for the entertainment!
Do not flee the pursuer;
My dear brother-in-law,
Swollen with pride before his in-laws,
Can a bridegroom flee?
If we flee trembling,
Can we be called a bridegroom?
Hey, brother-in-law, wait;
Turn your horse's head.
Does a man come courting in a swagger
And then flee?
Can a bridegroom who loses his wits
And panics be called a bridegroom?
As though he had seen his captor,
Can a bridegroom bolt toward his village
And flee?
Kulchoro kept deceiving him.
Would hero Toltoy, who had seen spirits,
Stop for deception?
Would he turn his horse's head?
Would God strike Toltoy
By making him stop for a boy?
Unless his head were severed,
Would your hero Toltoy stop?
"His voice has drawn near," he said.
Hero Toltoy truly fled,
Whipping Surkoen;
Hero Toltoy truly fled.
Dear Toltoy floundered,
Both coat skirts flapping;
Hero Toltoy fled clumsily.
After Toltoy fled without stopping,
Kulchoro spoke the truth,
Telling every secret
Within him:
"Turn your horse's head;
Hero Toltoy, wait!
If you ask who I am,
I am God's beloved servant,
A companion of Khan Manas,
The Chinese Almambet's son.
When he was dead,
I was a slave in a dog's hide;
While he lived,
I was a slave in a sheep's hide.
If you ask my lineage,
I was Semetey's servant.
Turn back and stand;
Come unleash a great tumult.
Take my horse and me
Away as plunder!
