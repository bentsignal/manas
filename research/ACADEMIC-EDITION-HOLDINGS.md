# Academic-edition acquisition follow-up, 2026-09-14

The [National Academy's bibliography, published16February2026](https://kruia.gov.kg/news/show/manas-eposu-boyuncha-chykkan-kitepterdin-tizmesi/ky) supplies specific acquisition leads: A. Jainakova's Semetey books1–6 (2015/2017), with listed lengths556,496,612,540,608,576pages; Karalaev Seytek in the2018 Classical Studies and Texts series, two volumes614/578pages; and a2019 Dilazyk Manas edited by Jainakova, Akmataliev and Kolbaeva,1024pages. The list also labels one2013 Semetey entry as book3 while separately repeating books1/2. Treat that as an unresolved bibliographic inconsistency, not proof of an acquired third volume. No full texts are linked in these entries, and no500,553-line reconciliation follows from the list.

The [Jalal-Abad university library's November2023 accession bulletin](https://jasulib.org.kg/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%9D%D0%BE%D1%8F%D0%B1%D1%80%D1%8C-2023.pdf), PDFpages9–10, records one copy each of academic Semetey books2–5,2017, in departmentАХЛ, at Lenin Street25, Jalal-Abad. Catalog markС30; classification821.151 forbook2 and821.51 forbooks3–5. Book2's summary covers Bakai's account and the reunions with Burulcha, Kulchoro, Kardigach and the hostile relatives; it therefore overlaps the current translation passage. Book3 covers return/migration and marriage;4–5 cover the China campaign. This is dated physical-holding evidence, not confirmed present availability or a digital acquisition.

A [15March2017 public-broadcaster report](https://utrk.kg/s/11648) quotes editor Akmataliev describing a manuscript-faithful publication that restores earlier politically cut material. This strengthens the need to compare academic editions with the current witness, but does not establish an exact line count or that our2010/2013 editions contain every archival verse.

Current status: no academic book acquired through these leads; no institutional message sent, no purchase made. Existing traditional-verse translation continues with explicit gaps and a provisional source count. First-complete-English status remains unproven.
