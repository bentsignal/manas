He will slaughter every last person.
Külchoro would reap their midst
As though cutting grain with a sickle.
Not merely the Jediger, but the whole world—
Not merely the Kyrgyz, but all mankind,
Not merely mankind, but Rome itself—
If the gray-maned hero rose in wrath,
He would swallow them all, leaving none.
If angered, he could chew up
The old men and spit them out.
Hero, this panther Semetey
Could devour all the Jediger;
His brow seems broad enough to bar the sky,
As though he could swallow all he sees.
His silhouette is like a cliff with an overhanging crest,
His strength like tempered blue steel.
He seems able to devour whoever strikes at him;
Every limb is like a tiger’s,
And none who grapples with him survives.
I climbed a ridge and braced myself,
And saw the champion’s figure
Towering like a lofty snow-white mountain.
That gray-maned hero’s valor
Is found in no other man.
I searched, but that formidable man’s courage
Is found nowhere among the people.
Ah, the valor of that noble-hearted lord!
Born for these Kyrgyz,
He is a fortress of tempered blue steel.
Against the infidel hordes like black beetles,
It was fitting that he slaughter their people
And opened the road through Beijing.
Through Chinmachin and Kakan
It was fitting that he opened a road;
Slaughtering their frenzied Kalmak champions
And destroying their warriors,
It was fitting that he went there;
It was fitting that he brought
A murderous battle upon the Chinese.
From terrible disaster and calamity
He truly delivered
The Kyrgyz people.
I saw Semetey
And lost my wits and senses;
Unable to find a way, I panicked—
My body trembling and my senses gone,
I fled to you, brave Kyyaz.
I saw Semetey:
High-eared and handsome of face.
If you catch word of that black-fated killer,
Flee far away.
Like a ravenous lion or tiger,
He is a man poised to seize his prey;
Like a true tusked boar,
He is a warrior poised to strike.
Born for the whole wide world,
He appears like the constellation Ülkör;
He seems like the pillar
That props up the sky.
When you see that formidable man,
Your heart is torn apart.
He seems like the unstoppable
Tempest of the whole wide world.
When stirred, he can make the world heave;
To the vast multitudes of Kyrgyz,
To the flourishing Kyrgyz nation,
He seems a father.
He is the dark, watchful eye
Of all courageous champions;
He is the very lion
Who leads the nation and guards the people.
No living soul can face his wrath;
Of those who grapple with the hero
Who protects his people, none remains alive.
With one ear standing high,
As if guarding a valley like a ridge,
He could drain anyone who fought him
More readily than he would crush a fly.
