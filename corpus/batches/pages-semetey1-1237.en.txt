Toltoy fled with a roar;
Dear Choro spared no effort.
The leopard's patterned spear—
Your valiant man aimed and held it,
But could not thrust it flexing.
He had made his spear immense,
Made his spear unwieldy:
"Would I not have ended
Swaggering Toltoy's devices?
He made the spear beyond measure,
Made his spear unbounded;
Had he made it more measured
For Toltoy at the flank,
Would I not have ended his devices?"
He strained, gripping the spear,
But could not drive
The leopard's patterned spear flexing,
And quarreled with his uncle.
His waist bent forward,
His eyes bulged,
Early words became late,
And young Lord Choro's
Two eyes became four.
"If only I could spear
Swaggering Toltoy to the ground,
Offer him as a sacrifice,
Cut off his head and bind it,
And bring good news
To my heroic uncle and lord.
At least I might ask for
The khanship of the Argyn,"
He thought, aiming for the shoulder blade,
For the lower edge of the lung.
He entered measuring his thrust.
The armor upon the shoulder blade,
Iron no spear could pierce—
"If by chance I thrust astray,
I will remain in regret."
He aimed for the backbone,
For the lower edge of the heart,
And drove in the spear.
The armor upon the backbone
Was iron no spear could pierce.
"If I collide by chance
And thrust astray by accident,
God will take me thus;
It will become a mistake.
If by chance it happens so,
God will take me."
Kulchoro lowered his aim
Toward the lower skirt
Of the bulletproof armor.
"If I thrust hard there,
At the edge of the backbone,
If I strike true then,
At the edge of the bladder."
He gripped the spear enormously
And used exceptional cunning.
Seizing and swinging
The leopard's patterned spear,
Without sparing his dear life,
He drove it firmly
Into hero Toltoy's fat rump.
When the spear struck hero Toltoy,
From the edge of the fat rump
It failed to reach the bladder,
Two fingers below its edge,
It struck astray.
Toltoy's thick flesh
Held it back with flesh;
Toltoy's thick coat
Held it back with cloth;
The spirit of his father, hero Bagysh,
Raised and protected him greatly.
Skirting the bone of the lower back,
The valiant spear reached him
And sank four fingers deep
Into his buttock.
