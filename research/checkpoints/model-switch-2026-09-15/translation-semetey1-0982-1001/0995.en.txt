Onto the ridge of Agynek,
Walking on foot for diversion,
To see the city of Oogan,
The leopard lord grew excited.
The dear lion who had kept still,
The boy Semetey yearned
To catch sight of Churok,
Like a man possessed.
The dauntless hero grew restless as it grew late.
The great spyglass left by his father Manas,
To turn it upon the face of the earth,
Aichurok, daughter of Akun,
To learn of her through it,
He climbed the ridge and braced himself,
Swaying like a peri-born being.
When he came onto the ridge,
Do not ask about the champion.
Before him was a black rock,
Around it, meadowsweet and cotoneaster mingled.

The noble dauntless hero turned,
Your surpassingly born dear one
Readied himself like a lion.
To tell each detail would be difficult.
From Manas, gone to the other world,
Many objects remained as gifts.
Among those objects,
The most renowned was the great spyglass.
A broad spyglass with a hoe-sized mouth,
A great spyglass a full fathom long.
When drawn out, it lengthened a fathom,
Its inside reddened like fire.
It had a handle made of gold.
Your noble mother Kanykei
Said, 'Take it on your travels.'
It had a case like a breast amulet,
Its interior dark and dim.
The leopard's great spyglass
Delighted any mortal who looked through it.
Perhaps you have heard:
His khan father, Khan Manas,
The khoja in Kokand,
When he was humiliating him,
The petition officer in Bukhara,
When he had defeated him,
With no one left to answer back,
When he was trampling over them,
The people, Khan Manas,
When he was ruling over them,
Beyond Andijan,
On this side of Namangan,
In the domain of the khan of Kokand,
On a plain yellow as the moon,
He seized it as plunder.
He had ruby stone split,
And placed in its lens.
He had its handle covered with gold.
If he repaired even a little part,
A thousand fillies from the horse herds,
