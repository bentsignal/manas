A pine pole
Was raised high.
At Kokotoy's celebration
They began an immense game.
They spread riches all around
And tied a precious jamby
To the tip of that pole.
Saying, “May we shoot down the jamby,”
Each man had his own desire.
Exactly sixty tanap of cord
Were tied to that jamby.
They proclaimed it widely:
"Your able young marksmen,
Shoot at the jamby," they said.
For shooting the jamby,
Kongurbay, khan of the Kechils,
With cotton sash and broad boots,
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Khan Joloy of the Kalmyks
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Orongu the woman champion
Sent out a thousand champions.
Muzkindik of the Shibees
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Ezkara, khan of the Manchus,
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
The enormous champion Orokkyr
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Jamgyrchy of the Esheks
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Muzburchak of Buudayyk
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Hero Toshtuk of Eleman
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Khan Kokcho of the Kazakhs
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Kozubek of Kokon Khan
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Fork-bearded Synchybek of Andijan
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Alabek of the Six Cities
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Akun Khan, khan of Afghanistan,
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
The pair Janaaly and Karach
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
Kokboru, son of Urum Khan,
Sent out a thousand marksmen.
At the foot of that jamby,
Their guns rattling,
The marksmen came leaping forward.
For the marksmen's spectacle,
They clenched their whips in their teeth
And urged their horses onward.
All the marksmen
Stretched their necks,
Their matchcords glowing red.
He raised a flag by the jamby;
The frantic Bokmurun
Raised a clamor among the people.
They fired in waves of thousands,
But lay unable to hit it.
Stray bullets flew unknowingly,
And many died there.
Many watched
What the Almighty had wrought.
Many tilted their bows
And went about drawing them.
Can a person be sated with wealth?
Many were immersed
In a spectacle no man had seen.
Heroes who deemed themselves heroes
Were torn from beneath their mounts,
As black smoke billowed,
