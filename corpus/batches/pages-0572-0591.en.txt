Toward an overhanging promontory,
Toward the edge of the rugged mountain,
Toward the face of a jutting ridge,
Toward its vast, still desert,
Of the sacred shrine,
Toward the place one crosses to reach,
Riding boldly on Akkula,
When he crossed and reached that place,
God put him to trouble.
In the opening and closing of an eye,
Before one could say this or that,
He met straight before him
A white-bearded wandering dervish.
The dervish stood and said:
“You are my champion, my support,
Created firmly, fashioned well,
