How he lost his wits—
I saw it with my own eyes,
Hero Bagysh of the Zhediger
Fleeing from among the people.
Growing angry with the khans,
The lion, tiger Manas,
Whipped his steed
And rode toward Koshoy.
"My uncle, elder Koshoy,
Saint who has sheltered me!
At the point of death, take one last meal:
Against the black Kalmyk like an ox,
Go down yourself, elder!
They are a host lying in countless ranks;
Among the scattered Muslims,
No one but you
Is suited for it.
With your beard pure white,
You yourself are fit for the contest.
Uncle, when your journey is near,
Wrestle Zholoy for us;
Win us the great honor, uncle!
Of the towering black Kalmyk,
Go see his full strength, uncle.
Let him seize his former fury;
If he falls into your hands, tear him apart, uncle!
Swing and hook him, uncle;
Seize him from the inside, uncle!"
Then Koshoy said:
"When, grown old, I could not
Lie in warmth,
When the cuffs of my trousers
Would not fit my legs,
You met me when I suffered
The humiliation of eighty-five;
You met me when I had seen
The far side of life.
I too, my colt Manas—
When drunk from drinking arak,
When young and sound in mind,
When my body was full,
When hellish old age was absent,
When my patterned spear
Smoked in my hand,
When infidel and Muslim alike
Jostled before me—
Why did I not die on a mountain pass?
The sprawling city of Beijing—
When I besieged it,
Its kalday and duushu—
When I beat them,
Making their ingots clatter,
When I took them away,
Why did I not die there?
Against darkened Beijing,
When we exchanged blows,
Over Bilerik,
Fighting so many days,
Captive Bilerik—
When I pulled him from his place,
Ezkara, khan of the Manchus—
When I pulled him from his hands,
Why did I not die then?
Eighty-five is an old age;
Your extreme aging
Is the wrath of the one God.
Go down against the champion yourself, Manas!
When my whole body was sound,
When old age was not upon my head,
Barefoot and bareheaded,
Twenty-five passed by;
Dear time, when the earth trembled!
If my calves were firm as a child's,
If decrepitude were not upon my head,
Would I not grapple with Zholoy?
However much Koshoy refused,
Tiger Manas would not relent.
