# Additional academic-source search

Checked 2026-09-14 local time (2026-09-15 UTC). No new Karalaev narrative witness acquired; the 500,553-line target remains unreconciled.

- National Academy's indexed [9 March 2017 announcement](https://naskr.kg/ky/2017/03/09/manas-eposunun-akademiyalyk-alyt-toktomu-zhakryk-kordu/) identifies the six-volume Semetey academic edition, prepared by Ainek Jainakova and edited by Abdyldajan Akmataliev. Full page retrieval failed; no downloadable volumes were located. This corroborates the existing bibliography lead, not availability or total verse count.
- [Kabar, 23 May 2025](https://kabar.kg/news/shajyrbek-abdrahman-uulu-manas-eposunun-akademiyalyk-varianttaryn-tkrp-berdi/) reports Shaiyrbek Abdrakhman uulu transferring academic epic materials to the State Archive, National Manas Academy and National Manas Theatre. The article discusses prose retellings and future Karalaev recordings; it supplies neither manuscript files nor a verse inventory. Do not mistake those retellings for complete original verse.
- [Manas Discovery library](https://manas-discovery.kg/китепкана-все/) lists Orozbakov, Jusup Mamai, abridged/combined versions and literary retellings. Its indexed 2023 Semetey/Seytek PDFs explicitly identify Togolok Moldo, not Karalaev. None can replace missing Karalaev passages or count toward his reported total.
- [Kitepkana academic trilogy catalogue](https://kitepkana.kg/манас-ааламы/манас-академиялык.html) is indexed as a four-book Karalaev set. Retrieval returned 502, so edition identity and files remain unverified. Earlier project research separately recorded failure to acquire its 2010 listing.

Searches combining Semetey, Jainakova, 2015/2017 and 556 pages chiefly returned bibliography entries or scholarship. Existing Osh and Jalal-Abad holdings remain leads for obtaining the academic edition, not proof that its complete text is in this repository. No institution or person was contacted.
