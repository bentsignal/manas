If I spear his Manas and lay him low,
I will crush and drive away
The Buruts who came to the feast,
Humiliate Khan Koshoy,
Overpower Khan Bakay,
And bind without killing
Old Koshoy and Bakay.
I will utterly smash and slaughter
All the Buruts.
I will not let them taste their salt;
I will plunder their daughters,
Bind their heroes,
Of the accursed Buruts,
And take their wives as booty."
He angrily spurred
The horse Algara beneath him;
Incorrigible Kongurbay
Made it pace over rise and hollow.
Wrapped in blue armor,
Kongurbay, if you saw him,
Strained like a blue tiger.
His eyes were the size of a lake;
If you saw the Chinese man,
Kongurbay was gray-maned.
He had brows like a lying dog
And a head like a great cauldron.
He had reached twenty-five;
That was his age this year.
His right shoulder was broad;
He was a lion born under favor.
All twelve limbs were perfectly matched.
For Beijing in Kakanchyn,
God Almighty had created him
As a defending lion.
His bearing was extraordinary:
Kongurbay, son of Alooke,
The noble champion who came from Beijing.
Among the Chinese in Beijing
A khan named Alooke arose;
Kongurbay, son of Alooke,
Drove life out with his voice,
And drew embers from the hearth.
For Kokotoy's joust
The mighty Kongurbay came out.
Roaring and swaggering,
Raging and stamping,
Kongurbay and Algara
Came together.
Six thousand Chinese and forty commanders
Mingled as his followers,
Leading Kongurbay,
Preparing his every maneuver,
And placing exactly sixty spears
In the Manchus' hands.
Leather belt and twisted waist,
The raging hero Kalcha;
Golden crest and tasseled belt,
The lordly great Kalcha;
His bristling mustache
Was like nettles grown by an outhouse,
And the filth of his pipe
Like grain mash eaten by sooty children.
He strained upon Algara,
Jerking the golden reins.
The sight made one's heart waver;
If you saw Kongurbay,
He loomed like Ala-Too.
Bulletproof blue armor,
Strong armor upon his chest,
He wore it twelve layers deep.
He rejoiced at the word "enemy."
With noble champion Manas—
He knew he would fight to the death.
The bulletproof mail—
He knew he would wear it.
Destroyer Manas and Kongurbay—
He knew they would clash.
