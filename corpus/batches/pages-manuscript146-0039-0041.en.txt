When he rose against the clan,
Let us all stir together,
When he strikes them down, he casts fire,
Hearing word from the good Kiyoor,
Do not ask ill of Kiyoo,
In this world he himself is discerning,
Even in death his eyes are open,
Bearing his father’s likeness, he became a hero,
He had known surpassing fortune,
Gathering there together,
They went in fours and looked,
Covered all about in divisions,
They went down below and looked,
Wondering how his encampment had settled,
Wondering what his stratagem had become,
They went to test it and see.
His wealth and his abundance,
Lay there too vast for the earth to hold.
To that wife of Kenen,
She bore sons one after another,
She gave her sons rhyming names,
Before the birth, a seer had gone there.
As though a sovereign had remained,
They slaughtered livestock in abundance,
Finding the greatest delight,
“Let us gather the foremost horses,”
As though sharing salt with the bald man,
Meeting together with Onko,
What he had driven was enough to suffice,
Ruining seven great leather vessels,
There came a time beyond all measure,
From the mortal who stood watching.
Driving them on without sparing a soul.
They say forty remained out of seventy,
From a hundred lesser champions,
They say he took what had not been reached.
The khan-hero was left deep in thought,
The Kyrgyz stood ready to attack,
A feast vast as Nadoola remained,
What are ninety-two,
Again from his going,
What are the two who settled.
Leaving none of these cunning schemes unused,
This is the deed called speaking out,
A single fist from the bloodthirsty one’s track,
This is the deed called Kalay.
If the lone youth gathers an army,
If he pours out his rage and roars,
If Karagak comes forth and strikes,
Dust flies up to the sky,
From where does the army mass together,
If there is a din that splits the earth,
If there is an uproar that bursts the ears,
If Karala’s eye has the heart for it,
If he begins again and gathers his warriors,
If the people call it the khan’s preserve,
This deed is the black open steppe,
His musket booming—
If we do not spill their flowing blood and take it,
If the wise champion does his work,
If we do not raise one great thunderous tumult.
The hero was born by Blue Heaven,
We remained to reckon it up.
