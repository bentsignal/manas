And Black Chinese and Manchus died.
Intending to wage a true battle,
The giants Chynkojo and Toltoi died.
Coming to grapple with me,
The Black Chinese host died.
From the beginning, the one God
Made me mighty throughout the world;
But in the end the Creator
Humbled me before Sarybai.
Against this infidel Sarybai,
Shooting and fighting,
No human strength sufficed,
Nor could a lion’s teeth pierce him.
When at last I took stock,
My weakness became plain;
Fighting, I could not match his might.
O mother eagle, I could not bring him down,
I could not defeat that son of a bitch.
My power left me, and I grieved;
Of this borrowed, passing life
I thus despaired.
If an unyielding enemy comes,
I shall turn my charger’s head.
Of the humiliation that came outright,
I am telling you the truth.
This is the truth of the tale begun;
This is your warrior Choro’s secret.
Lioness sister-in-law Kuyaly,
I have sung my song to you,
I have told my secret as faithfully as my creed,
I have spoken all my truth.
If you are a hero, if you are a lion,
If you are the one renowned among the people,
The leopard’s companion,
If you are my champion sister-in-law,
Then wage the battle fiercely.
From the hands of the wrathful one,
From the fatal bullet that has struck us,
From the hands of our true enemy,
From Sarybai’s venom,
From the fury of this burning hell,
From the dragon’s poison,
Spare not your life, heroic sister-in-law;
First of all, for your hero,
For the aid his strength requires,
For your child Seytek and for me,
Wage the battle fiercely.
From the hands of that venomous one,
If your strength suffices, sister-in-law,
Deliver us and save us.
The descendants of the magnanimous lion—
Do not let them vanish or be lost,
Do not lose them and end their line,
Leaving it barren as a salt waste where no grass grows.
Do not let it be said that Sarybai
Destroyed the lion hero’s line;
Wage the battle fiercely.
Fight the clash with all your might;
From death, torment, and calamity,
Preserve them yourself!
If you are a valiant one, if you are a lion,
Kuyaly of Kaiypchal,
The finest among women,
The most graceful in tasseled ornaments,
The mightiest beneath a white headscarf,
If you are that heroic sister-in-law—
Sarybai, whom you shall fight,
Trample him into the black earth.
The descendants of the dear hero—
Protect them as their mother.
Behold our condition;
Sarybai has brought disaster,
So deliver his punishment into our hands.
Wage the struggle fiercely;
From cruel oppression and calamity,
The descendants of the dear hero—
Deliver them and save them.
Take me, Külchoro, and brave Seytek
As companions at your side.
