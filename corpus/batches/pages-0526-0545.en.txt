It was firmer than those he had seen.
What my gray-maned elder Koshoy
Had said was fitting!
When he looked this way,
There was ridge upon ridge, barriers;
It was a land of rolling hills.
From its hills and uplands,
Wild sheep poured down grazing.
Looking where they poured down,
The hillside grass grew sparse.
From its rugged mountains,
Its deer poured down with a ‘Shoo!’
Looking at the rough ground,
The grass on the rough ground grew sparse.
When he looked this way,
There were the foothills of Üch-Koshoy.
Would those who dwelt here
Ever have an unfulfilled wish?
When he looked across,
There were Kara-Buura and Chatkal.
Ah, dear broad Talas,
Was it not the finest of lands,
