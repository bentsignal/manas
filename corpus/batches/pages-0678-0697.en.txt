'We sorcerers have come before you.
Have you learned what we learned?
Have you sensed what we sensed?
A deep-dug pit is coming.
Is it in your knowledge?
The great, renowned Manas is coming.
If Manas comes, he will not leave things well;
A great burden comes upon our precious lives.'
