Seeing him would set the heart at ease,
Saying, “A son born of a khan,”
Looking upon him would satisfy my blood,
We have made him our ward and have no regrets.
If he matures into a hero,
If he becomes the equal of heroic sons,
We shall give him the throne and make him khan,
And make him a worthy hero,
We shall take shelter in him
When this child grows up.
He will reach Afghanistan and lay it waste;
How could Jet-Kait escape unharmed?
They will don armor no arrow can pierce,
And attack a people
No man has heard of or seen.
They will journey to lands
No ear has heard of and no nation has seen.
If your child survives,
Firwood spears will clash,
And wherever he strikes, war will rage.
If your son lives,
The Jediger of Jeti-Özön
Will reach the foe and feast on spoils.
Across the face of the earth
He will be famed as Kyyaz’s son.
Our dream has turned out well:
The captive woman will bear a son,
And our cause will not fare badly.
The child this woman bears you
Will have perfect fortune;
The enemy who challenges him will be humbled.
His gaze will be a surging flood;
Whoever grapples with him will be humbled.
The way he faces will blaze like fire;
The enemy will halt without confronting him.
He will be a flame burning on the mountain pass;
Without confronting him, the enemy
Will halt!
If your child survives,
See what his valor will be:
In the marshes of Chogulma
They had gathered together.
They recorded the days,
Saying, “This is the count of days,”
And vied in calculation.
They recorded the months,
Saying, “This is the count of months,”
And disputed among themselves.
They reckoned the count of days
And entered the days in writing.
They reckoned the count of months
And learned the truth for certain.
Speaking softly but firmly,
Bearded hero Kutu
Began to address brave Kyyaz:
“When we count the months,
A full twelve months have passed;
The child the woman is to bear
Is truly your own child.
When the woman gives birth,
Slaughter ninety beasts for the feast
And give the child a fitting name.
Let your heart know no doubt:
Truly, the child sprung from your loins
Is your own.
If it is a son,
Choose the finest of all names.
Saying, ‘Kyyaz has gained a child,’
Wash away every last sorrow
From your heart.
If a fierce enemy host should come,
We shall set him against them,
And make him our war club.
We shall make him master among the people,
A giant among the Jediger nation,
And take him as our champion ram.
If his daily fortunes prosper
And he grows greater day by day,
Like that towering mountain,
We shall make him our mighty bulwark.
