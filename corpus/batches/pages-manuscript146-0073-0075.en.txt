Unless you cast it before the multitude,
If Tengir himself appears before you,
He has the strength to stand against you.
Though your people are many,
The few Kyrgyz are no falsehood,
If we gauge their strength and tell the secret,
You bellowed as though struck down,
My khan, what you have done is not right.
A khan’s command is an iron snare,
When you rage and stiffen in anger,
Our misery begins to boil.
Going to the truthful one and speaking truth,
Our misery has boiled over.
There is much weeping in the breast,
Let his jester make it grand,
There is a god over your rough shoe,
For solid iron there is an axe,
There is deceit within your rough shoe.
That wise man knew the calamity,
If you are a shallow-witted khan,
Hang it from this neck of mine,
He spoke piercingly and defended it,
Speaking of what he saw and knew,
One who brings them safely through,
The white-kalpak people are not an easy people,
Having Karman’s head cut off,
I am not one who stands in your hand.
When the khan’s age comes, cry out,
I am not one who stands in your hand,
Saying, “You revealed the enemy’s secret,”
This deed of yours is not manly.
You cursed him for speaking the truth,
If you make strife, its blow will reach you,
If you rage and stiffen in anger,
You will see it brought to submission.
Your folly will become your affliction.
Know it clearly and keep it in mind,
The heart that was to be driven,
Will the mind comprehend it?
He spoke out when telling the truth,
Yourself, fine as a thread in the true secret,
Now you have troubled my sight,
The ruling khan suffered,
I have no guilt and no strength,
The people clamored and an uproar arose,
The brave soul asked,
If you see wrongdoing among the people,
This will become cause for outcry,
Remain without inflicting suffering,
You are a twin to the envoy.
Warrior, you are peerless and without equal,
You have burning force and flame,
You have wise men and companions,
You have eloquent men who find wise counsel,
Your champion Akbala,
Suradai spoke; understand this.
Your ancient forefather was a great khan,
Warrior Chyng Tengir, understand this.
The four eshens share one root,
If you cut its nose, there is no milk,
The horned foe is a companion of the soul,
There is no day when the thoroughbred fails,
Jeljetpes who stands in your hand,
If you strike the innocent, you will not prosper,
You will stir up a madness best left alone,
Scattering wrath, you rule the people,
You will not prosper for a long age.
When the storm beats down the reeds,
When the hour grows troubled,
When hardship comes from those close to you,
A tongue that will not obey will arise,
A jaw that will not submit will arise,
The people surge in a great assembly,
A perilous hour will come upon you,
Let your thoughts range widely,
Speak and consider the open field.
A small people with unity,
What wolf could break their enclosure?
A numerous people without unity,
You become a child whom anyone may command.
Destroying the innocent,
Warrior, do not disturb the people’s peace of mind.
