This is the place where we donned it;
Kongurbai, khan of the Chinese—
This is the place where I fought him!
We stood out in single combat,
And your capable uncle Bakai
Fought without rest for seven days,
Toppling their mighty men
And spearing them down from the cliff!
Renowned China-Kakanchin,
My dear foal,
Is a people left behind by wretched me!
Against khans clad in berenji armor,
This is the place where I faced them;
Against those in five-layered waistcoats and blue coats,
Against the Chinese people,
This is the place where I fought apart!
Making my horse dance in single combat,
This is the place where I went;
Many a marvelous wonder—
This is the place where I wrought it.
The road to Beijing divides five ways—
I told you that earlier!
The road to Beijing has five branches.
There, where the mist parts,
Where something massive appears,
There, where the mountain summit protrudes,
Where the eagle's peak rises sheer,
For the numerous Chinese-Kakan people,
That is the mine where they dig gold,
The place where they find their wealth!
The tribes who inhabit that land
Are the Solon and the many Shibe—
They are two peoples.
I took much tribute from them in gold;
Bakai was their overlord!
Into the midst of those people,
My stride carried me;
While my generous lord was alive,
I firmly exacted tribute from that people!
There, where the dark hills divide,
Where their peaks appear arched,
That is rugged Kara-Too.
That is the mine from which tea is taken;
When I had the power,
I was the one who exacted tribute in tea!
That is the very border;
That is where the troops stood!
When its jang-jun issued a decree,
That flood would surge over everything!
It is a land my bloodthirsty hero conquered,
A place where the Muslims
Were sated with abundant wealth!
Fair-faced young wives and beautiful girls—
A place where we could not gather them all as spoils,
A place whose gold and silver
None could finish loading!
That rising ridge ahead
Is the land of No Return;
Anyone who encountered it met his death—
Such are those woeful Chinese people!
While my fortune remained whole,
While my bloodthirsty hero lived,
It was a place where we brought turmoil,
Where we clashed and fought,
Where many lives were lost,
And where we took their khanate!
Then my wings had not yet been scorched;
My forearms were thick and thundering,
And I killed whoever I encountered,
Roaming at will!
Not merely China, but the age itself,
Accursed world,
Was then under my dominion!
Those pale hills and burning dust—
That is the road of No Return!
Where the hills rise jagged,
That is the Chok-Tabylgy Pass!
