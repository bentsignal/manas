Your valiant mother Kanykei
Gave travel rations to all four.
Great Beijing was six months away;
Those who went to Beijing died—
Unless mighty Allah watched over them,
What living soul could return alive?!
Lest their clothes become infested with lice,
There was a separate change of clothing for lice;
Lest their clothes become filthy,
There was a separate change for dirt.
My heart burns, my liver is embers;
In this mother’s hands,
What was there, and what was not!
Your opponent is the Kakanchin;
Should you clash in a great conflict,
For every hero I prepared
Twelve shirts and ten pairs of trousers,
Satin, silk, and fine-cloth shirts—
So that you could pursue the blood-price,
I foresaw it long ago,
Gathered and obtained them all,
And into the bottom of a great saddlebag
Placed them together.
For you four I made four saddlebags,
Green and blue in color,
Of exceedingly great value,
Saddlebags holding abundant goods.
I reinforced their mouths with steel,
Stitched them with hidden seams,
And made those saddlebags ready.
They contain powder and shot,
Enough to last twelve months,
So that they would not run out;
I, disgraced Kanykei,
Measured it truly for that need!
So you can stand on opposite banks,
And when the spear cannot reach,
Strike with the gun,
I strengthened their mouths firmly!
I prepared them for the holy campaign!
Accept them as gifts, saying,
“The fierce valiant lady gave them!”
Set them upon your saddle cushion,
Close beside your own body!
From the iron-gated fortress
Semetey could no longer be restrained.
Loading bullets upon the elephant,
Taking fourteen horses together,
Saying, “I shall gird my waist firmly;
Kongurbai there in Beijing—
I shall pursue my father’s blood-price,”
He made his arms and armor ready.
Zulpukor, left by Manas,
He fastened close against his body;
Akkelte, entrusted to him
By his father, gray-wolf Manas,
Was visible upon his shoulder.
When he entered a shooting battle,
It was the treasure left as a gift
By valiant Lord Manas.
Semetey, son of Manas,
Put on Akolpok
And now buckled the gray kise at his waist;
The Forty Saints now supported
The fortunate, heaven-born prince.
