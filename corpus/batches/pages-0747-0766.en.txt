Of that people of fierce wrath,
Their women and girls are beautiful, they say.
Their women formidable, their men dogs,
In their waters lies dire peril.
Their females are most beautiful,
Their clear eyes shine, they say.
Of the person who beholds them,
Not a sliver of his soul remains, they say.
Khan Shooruk became an immeasurable hero.
His army numbered six multitudes,
Any mortal who went was killed.
Raging from that place,
He waged fierce fighting, they say.
The city of broad Kashkar,
Making its women and girls cry out,
He raided it six times, they say.
Breaking rock and making roads,
Such was Shooruk's fame;
He attacked many lands.
He won abundant wealth, they say.
The Noygut of broad Alay,
Taking their women and girls as spoil,
He raided them thoroughly, they say.
His army rides camels, they say,
