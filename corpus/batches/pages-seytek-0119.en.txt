My elder Külchoro has been disgraced,
His stallion hidden so he could not mount it.
Why did you not tell me, Mother,
Your doomed kinsman was dying?
My falcon, my dearest kin,
My precious uncle, my inheritance!
Where is Talas, where is that land,
And where are the Kyrgyz people of Talas?
Why should the offspring of a lordly lion
Wander as a fugitive?
Where is my heroic uncle now,
And where is the road back to our people?
Where is the mighty foe I can unhorse,
Where is the land that will shelter me?
Where is the enemy I can engage?
My dear uncle, my gray-maned lion,
My true blood has begun to boil!
Our livestock grazes in alien valleys,
And our lives have dwindled to a handful.
Where are the Ala-Too valleys
Where one may gallop freely,
And the people whose villages stood together?
What use is wavering?
Does the cautious man not also die?
Must this wretched head not suffer
Whatever God has ordained?
Will the dug grave not hold
All beneath the sun?
The black earth swallows everyone
When the true decree arrives
And the angel reaches out five fingers.
When the taker of souls
Draws near to take your life,
Does not mortal man die
Even at the sight of his broad encampment?
Must not every living mortal submit
To the fate inscribed by the Creator?
Can the offspring of a noble house prosper
By wandering homeless?
If I do not make the prince’s heir
Cease to live as an outcast;
If I do not make it seem
As though one man set the whole world aflame;
If I have strength, I will battle,
If I have power, I will strike,
And unless my destined hour has come, I will not die.
If driven to desperation, Jediger,
May I divide all your countless riches,
Measuring each share with my whip.
If I do not bind and capture
Your champions alive,
And cast turmoil
Among all your lionhearted heroes;
If I do not test your courage,
And, should the Jediger of Jeti-Özön
Fall wholly into my hands,
Divide your goods by measure of my whip;
If I do not make the slaughter glorious
And drive your lithe young women
Back to the Kyrgyz;
If I do not test your khan’s courage,
And carry off the brutal Jediger
As plunder;
If I do not bring misery upon their bey,
And thus avenge
My father, the lordly lion!
My hapless mother, so this is your homeland;
The Jediger took you as spoil,
And you brought me here
After your life was laid waste.
Why should the offspring of a prince
Wander as a fugitive?
My hapless mother, where are our people?
Will a day come when we return to them?
Why should a prince’s own son
Live on the run?
My ill-fated mother, where are our people?
Why did you hide it and not tell me sooner?
Why did you conceal my forefather,
The formidable Manas, Mother?
