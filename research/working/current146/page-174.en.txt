Taking a bare, round stone,
With a bent rope twisted back,
Taking hide and piling it layer upon layer,
He arranged it broad and smooth as a saddle-cloth.
He bound the hands so eyes would reel,
He barred every visible road.
No living soul could pass through the breastwork,
No living soul could pass the spell-worker.
Thus he blew his incantation,
Leaving no way to turn back.
He set its course straight,
For the harsh doom that pursued him,
He set its right path straight.
For the hardship spread in secret,
He set its snare straight.
When he looked all around,
Hoping for the safety of the people,
He failed to meet those he should meet,
And met those who should never be met.
In the time it took him to close his eyes,
An untimely death overtook him.
He was struck down by a dagger,
And their spears were set against him.
The iron rope tightened,
He fell into the net, alas.
Its kick was beyond a man’s power to cut,
He fell into misery, alas.
Wherever he went, grim death would not relent,
He fell into their hands, alas.
The utterly solitary hero’s
Road to death was made ready.
The wrestlers’ equipment was prepared.
When they fought, none came near his measure.
Examine the superior wrestler;
Set the spears for the contest.
Countless wrestlers gathered together.
