When they sought the forty companions,
Your lion Alma the leopard was absent;
Precious Chubak the wolf was absent;
Syrgak, son of Khan Ulak, was absent.
The support on which they girded themselves was gone.
Among the forty champions,
With mighty Alma at their head,
Twelve wolves were absent!
When they entered this Beijing,
It had been a road cut by Khizr.
At that time, when the Muslims
Took a count of their people,
Their army had been forty-two san.
The people’s khans were absent;
Manas grieved and sobbed.
The reason for his sobbing—
All those precious ones were gone!
In Beijing, where he had waged his campaign,
Your warrior had lost Akkula to a bullet
And sent Almambet to a martyr’s death.
His wing had been broken;
He had lost Khan Chubak, his trusted support.
He had raided Beijing and taken much;
Yet all the champions,
Through neglect, Manas had consumed!
Khans Muzburchak and Kokcho had died;
All who encountered Beijing had died.
All the mighty men—
Your warrior had let China take them as gifts!
Those who came died, souls died;
They were slaughtered in Beijing.
Warrior, all who encountered it died.
“I have the strength,” he had said as he came,
Manas the leopard, thundering.
Demanding Beijing and then turning back,
Whom he had cherished as a gift—
