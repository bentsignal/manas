His very form sets him apart from all others,
He is both a hero and a man of bitter fate,
A true man would not dare lay hands on him;
He is a genuinely born hero.
I marveled when I saw his stature—
No other soul like him will be born.
No one facing him has ever struck,
No man has ever dared grapple with the hero.
This truly born Külchoro—
Can anyone who tangles with him prosper?
Against this sacred warrior,
Look—can one who attacks him survive?
For these blessed Kyrgyz
He was born to be their guardian;
For these perfectly formed Kyrgyz
He was born to be their khan;
When enraged against the enemy,
He is a son whose torrent overwhelms them.
For this small, ancient Kyrgyz nation
He was born to be its backbone.
This dark-fated hero—
Seen from the front, he brings good fortune;
Seen from behind, he seems an army countless strong.
His radiant beauty is an omen of fortune,
His wolf-like valor equals countless troops;
His muscles are thick as a man’s waist,
And when he surges, he seems ready to devour a soul.
His strength is like tempered iron,
And when he charges, he seems ready to devour a soul;
His majesty is like a towering cliff,
And when he grapples, he seems ready to devour a soul.
His heart is steel, his outer frame is bronze—
What man could ever stand against him?
When fury seizes and maddens him,
He darts like ten striking snakes;
His combat blazes like red-hot coals—
What wrestler could withstand him?
Would anyone dare face him in battle?
Would anyone he attacks remain alive?
When enraged, he cannot be restrained;
No man he pursues can escape.
My bosom friend, listen to my words:
The two reins and the single bridle—
I yield them to you, my friend; take command.
Against this hero raging in fury,
You yourself must wage the battle.
This handful of the Jediger—
Would there be sin or merit in destroying them?
Take the duty upon yourself;
Hero, wage the battle yourself.
He is the enemy whose secrets you know;
My friend, deal with him as you know best.
Hero, do not stir up
Any needless quarrel.
My lion, whose cunning is complete,
Devise the means to deal with him yourself.
The Jediger will face a true slaughter—
Hero, bind him with your own hands.
Your hero’s courage is unmatched, his venom fierce;
If you do not act as you see fit,
My dearest friend,
He will level every one of us.
If you mean to kill him, kill him yourself;
Hero, if you mean to bind him, bind him yourself!
Hero, use all your cunning.
Do not think I shrink from combat;
Hero, restrain your anger.
Here stand the Kyrgyz people with their chiefs;
If you would cut off my head, here I stand.
This slave of yours has driven me into a frenzy;
Hero, if you would kill me, here is the ground.
Hero, if you are enraged, here is your chance.
Let me offer before you
This fleet bay racehorse as a gift.
Hero, if you bear a grudge, here are my people;
If you would sever my head,
Here am I, your precious Kyyaz!
Hero, as Kanchoro you have prospered—
