Every throat was filled with dust;
Human heads were covered in blood.
The soil spread as drifting dust;
The sky was clear, but the earth overcast.
Not a blade on earth could be seen.
How many souls lay dying;
Not a single person could be distinguished.
The people seethed and gathered;
Those whose doom had come were slaughtered.
Let us leave that there
And begin the tale
Of hero Semey, son of Manas,
The man of surpassing courage:
Striking Buurul once with his heel,
Scowling in wrath,
Against the khoja's host, a whole division,
Semetey charged.
Born surpassing
His father, gray wolf Manas.
Which of his deeds can one tell?
The seven banners that came from Mecca—
He stationed them in seven places;
It was the time your accomplished one charged,
Slaughtering wherever he reached.
The six banners that came from Mecca—
He stationed them in six places;
The lion entered like the moon,
Slaughtering at the first word.
The brave were cut down;
Cowards stretched out in flight.
Born surpassing
His father, gray wolf Manas,
Your wretched hero pursued them roaring;
The great black Jediger
Fled in a thundering mass.
Wherever his spear reached,
He slaughtered them with the spear;
Thus Semetey took them.
Wherever his sword reached,
He struck once with the sword;
Thus Semetey left them behind.
Turning their turmoil into dust,
He pursued without stopping;
Shattering their stone fortress,
He drove them away entirely.
He split them into groups of five and ten.
Those doomed to die died;
Those who remained alive
Suffered humiliation like dogs.
All the Jediger
Fled in a frenzy.
As though seeking to enter the earth,
They fled in wild panic.
Overflowing with grief,
All the Jediger
Despaired of their entrusted lives.
The survivors fled distraught,
Crying, “Hero, we submit!”
And pleading with Semetey.
Some, bewildered,
Quickly dismounted
And cried, “Ah, hero Semetey,
Was not our khan the fool?
What guilt lies upon the multitude?
We were driven after him
By one fool who fled his village,
Through the affliction of the foolish khoja.
Lion son of Manas,
Here is the commanding city;
If you would take heads, here are ours!
If you would dig deep, here is the pit;
If you would boil us, here is the brine!
Slaughter every one of us;
Here is the army that came prepared!
Do not bring misery upon the meek;
If you would grapple, here is mighty Khoja!
Add us to the khoja in humiliation;
Boil us by force in bitter brine—
