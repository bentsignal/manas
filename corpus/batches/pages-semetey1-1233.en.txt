Be offered as a sacrifice,
Be crushed upon the steppe,
And torn apart by the Dangyts?
Rather than die on the open plain
And endure such suffering,
I will flee into my ranks,
Gather my host,
And begin a mass battle;
Rather than die on the open plain,
I will die without regret.
I told him before—
Did he not heed me then?
Ravined spring and sheer cliff,
Fiery, cunning Churok,
Has men girded for battle
And heroes lying in wait.
The cunning infidel Churok
Has a pit she dug,
Misfortune she has set boiling,
And a great force the wretch
Has long prepared.
When an oppressor comes for you,
She has made ready;
Aychurok, daughter of Akun,
Has prepared
All her warriors.
This infidel's man will not prosper;
If he challenges Manas, he will not survive.
Mighty Manas took tribute
From Beijing untouched by Sulayman;
Hero Manas left his mark
On Beijing untouched by Iskender.
Mighty Manas conquered
Beijing where no prophet had gone;
Mighty Manas raided
Beijing no holy man had raided.
Can one who challenges
Bloodletter lion Manas survive?
Toltoy, knowing his renown,
Lost the strength in his arms
And the courage in his heart.
Gray-maned Khan Toltoy's
Chest began to tremble,
And his beard bristled hair by hair.
Kulchoro was coming,
Calling the battle cry "Manas,"
Like a hungry lion and tiger.
Toltoy stood bewildered;
If they fought, he would not survive.
Khan Toltoy, who came to take a wife,
Stretched the drum in his hand,
Struck the drum once,
And struck Surkoen once.
Hero Toltoy took to the road,
Charging toward the Jediger,
As though he would reach them.
He lashed Surkoen beneath him
From side to side,
Spurred both ways,
He made extreme haste,
And fled far away.
Surkoen flew like an arrow,
The racehorse beneath him.
He would not have taken Churok;
This was the commotion
Chynkojo had pressed him into.
When Toltoy returned to his people,
When he turned and fled,
The ground split in shimmering haze,
And dust billowed behind.
Hero Toltoy's Surkoen
Came flying through the mirage.
When he reached a descent,
Hero Toltoy's Surkoen
Thrust his forelegs like a roe deer's;
When he leaned into an ascent,
Peerless racehorse Surkoen's
Mane and tail streamed out.
