Chynkojo was treacherous;
Because of Chynkojo,
Because of that true villainy,
The noble Kyrgyz clashed,
Facing one another in battle.
The nation was shattered and its finest men died;
Through Chynkojo’s cruelty,
So many heroes died here.
Many lofty plane trees were cut down;
A massacre swept the land,
Silencing the necks of many maidens’ champions.
Many kinds of warriors died,
And countless others also perished.
My uncle Toltoy died,
And that noble man entered
The true abode from which none return.
That wicked Kojo Khan died,
And every soul who followed him died.
Chynkojo, that son of Shygay,
Met a dreadful counterstroke;
Because Kojo caused it,
In that counterstroke my uncle,
Noble Toltoy, died.
A flood ruins the mountain valleys;
Among a people, Kanchoro,
A warrior like you brings ruin.
Wind lays waste the land;
A fickle-hearted, fork-tongued man
Like you destroys a nation.
I traced the tale through its traditions,
And questioned those who knew;
From gray-bearded elders
I heard all this,
From those who had lived long lives.
Semetey took you in,
Calling you a legacy left by the noble dead,
And made you his little brother.
For your feast he slaughtered ninety beasts
And named you the valiant Kanchoro.
When you were an infant,
Though you were merely a waif,
He himself raised you.
So that your heart might grow rich,
He cut fat from the choicest meat and fed you;
So that you might become a special kinsman,
His mother Kanykey let you suck
At both her pure breasts.
When you sucked Kanykey’s left breast,
Black blood came forth.
“This suckling will become an enemy,”
His mother Kanykey cried in anguish.
“He will never be kin—let him go!
At heart he is an enemy by birth;
Slaughter this wretch!” she said.
But the panther was magnanimous,
He would not heed malicious words
And disobeyed his mother Kanykey.
Though your tongue sounds gentle,
You will never mend, Kanchoro;
At heart you are an enemy to the end.
Understand the wisdom of my words;
My precious one, my friend,
Heed what I say.
Why should we provoke the blessed gray-maned hero
And bring harm upon ourselves?
Lay down your anger, Kanchoro;
Come, let us depart safely to the borderlands.
Can a full-grown warrior
Prosper after defiling another’s salt?
Can there ever be another
Who wrecks a nation and corrupts its people?
Understand the wisdom of my words;
Do not leave your people destitute—
My noble friend, heed what I say!
From this hero Semetey
The lands of Jylgyn and Keng-Kol will remain,
And from him the white-capped Kyrgyz nation will remain;
The wretched will join these deeds into songs,
