Faces toward Bek-Jailoo,
Its pass-side toward Kan-Jailoo.
On the ridge of Kan-Jailoo,
The nearer side of the horse range
Is by Üch-Koshoi and Keng-Shor.
Wreak havoc and raise turmoil;
This mighty warrior's countless horses—
Plunder and drive them off.
Jediger of Jeti-Özön,
All of you who are able,
Drive away seven thousand horses each;
You weaklings unable to manage that,
Drive away seven hundred each;
You weaklings unable to manage that,
Drive away seven hundred horses each
And make them your plunder.
Twelve tümön of abundant livestock—
Thus plunder and seize them.
Then all your misfortune will vanish,
And you great black host of Jediger
Will lie at ease with swollen bellies.
Unleash the passion in your hearts;
In this way pass beyond misfortune.
Take all his worldly goods
And settle down wealthy.
In the mountains are nine hosts of horses,
Packhorses mingled with thoroughbreds.
If you go in and take them as spoil,
Let your poor hearts rejoice!
On the hills are piebald horses,
Fleet thoroughbreds mingled among them.
Go and plunder them all;
Let your poor hearts rejoice!
Cast a cold catch-rope
Upon the thoroughbred he would not give when asked.
Strike on every side
The countless black herds he withholds despite pleas.
Cast lassos over the thoroughbreds
And enter into this revel.
At Kuu-Tez of Kuugandy
Loose horses are lying now.
Look upon the splendid livestock;
Divide by the measure of the whip
The countless horses left by the mighty warrior.
The great treasury and myriad possessions—
Plunder them all and leave nothing.
Say, “Kanchoro's support
Has brought us this much benefit.”
Go back to your lands, Jediger,
And boast of it with elation.
This mighty warrior's treasury,
The gate standing before the palace,
The garden planted with plane trees,
The true warrior-khan's throne,
The dark eye of the spring,
The very plane tree itself,
The golden-gated horde—
Great black host of Jediger,
Throw yourselves upon them without sparing life;
Launch a mass assault!
Make arrows rain down like rain,
Shoot bullets like hail;
Slaughter his heroes and lay them low,
Set all his people clamoring,
Slaughter his khans and lay them low,
Destroy all who remain.
Make their women don black headdresses;
Slaughter every last one.
Bring terror upon their beks;
Attack head-on and plunder.
Say, “We overran from above
The mighty warrior who fell upon us;
We scattered his four kinds of livestock,
Slaughtered and destroyed his nobles,
And raised a great upheaval.”
Such is the way of combat.
Say, “We destroyed his remaining men
And carried off his women.”
