His courage was fierce and his venom strong;
Abandon hope before Chynkojo,
Whom the spirits had forgotten!
Let us leave that thus
And begin with Chynkojo:
Dear Kojo, renowned lord,
Had, when he wished, such great worth;
By custom he drank the blood
Of whoever came to challenge him.
Saying, "I shall slaughter Manas's son,
And bring slaughter
Upon the accursed fugitive,"
He grew black with rage;
Thus remained that renowned lord.
His saddlecloth was leather;
No mortal could withstand
This Kojo's courage.
Look at the flood coursing from the slope;
Look at hero Kojo, like a flood.
Look at the water flowing with a roar;
Look at the uproar Chynkojo caused.
Look at the dark-sworded conflagration;
Look at the fire Chynkojo kindled.
Look at the black trench that was dug;
Look at the suffering
Dear Kojo the leopard brought engulfing them.
Was Kojo not one born
And come into this world?
Was Kojo not one who punished
Whoever challenged him?
At a spur of the lower mountain,
At a jutting, narrowing promontory,
What did dear Kojo do?
He unleashed an upheaval
And drove the fleeing mass of Zhediger
Before him under the whip:
"Damn your fathers, Zhediger;
If you flee, you have eaten filth!
Is Zheti-Özön such a feeble land?
Is Semetey a greater hero than I?
Not one of you bore a banner;
You have fled straight away,
Not remembering that your falcon Kojo lives;
Unable to stand, you have run.
Zhediger of Zheti-Suu,
While this accomplished head remains,
I shall catch and gather you.
I shall slaughter you all,
And then find Semetey.
Do you not see, Zhediger,
The heroism I have shown?
Will you not share, Zhediger,
The gain I have won?
My heroism is of another order;
My wrestling strength beyond measure!
There is no division at Kara-Suu;
Babbling Zhediger,
While this dear head remains,
There is no death for you at all!
Do not flee, Zhediger;
There is no one who can resist!
Damn your fathers, Zhediger;
You have white-bearded elders
And all your resourceful old men.
In alarm before Manas's wretched son,
You have taken fright;
Great Black Zhediger,
All of you in panic
Have fled away.
If you flee, Zhediger,
I shall unleash an upheaval;
If my black fury seizes me,
I shall seize every one of you
And turn you into black earth!
Would men who have seen an enemy
Panic and lose their wits?
