To your rash lord
Convey my greetings and prayers;
Do not let it be said, 'He came for nothing
And was killed by Toltoi.'”
At these words, Kulchoro,
Surpassingly eloquent, and a lion,
The clever lad Kulchoro said:
“The support you rely on,
Whom you have firmly found—
Is renowned Kozho your husband?
I shall break down your fortress walls;
I shall smash that swaggering
Fool Toltoi, your husband!
Kara-Kyr, where geese cannot circle—
You have found your support:
Tolok and Tolobai,
Kalcha and Kadyrbai,
The strongest of the Jediger—
You have found your husband!
The support on whom you rely,
If my dear head does not perish,
I shall grind to powder
The husband you mean to marry!
My elder's Akshumkar
Does not fold his wings and stoop;
He lets no goose fly from the lake.
Does not fold his tail and stoop;
He lets no swan fly from the lake.

If you keep putting us off, let us return;
If you put us off, sister-in-law,
The devil of it will fall upon your father, Khan Akun!”
On hearing this, Aichurok,
At Choro, Almambet's son,
Looked sideways as she sat.
[HOLD]
She looked on in distress;
What could your dear sister-in-law do?
She implored Kulchoro:
“This Kulchoro standing here
Has fortune wholly on his side;
Were it not for his boyhood—
His elder Semetey
Must be an even greater lion.
There are boys of twelve
Who cannot go in and out by themselves,
Cannot cover themselves when they piss,
Have no shame before their mother;
Those boys know not many words,
Not even two or three phrases.
For a boy such as this,
I would sacrifice my life!”
Saying this, Aichurok
Put aside her taunting words
And began to speak plainly:
“Oh, my dear son,
What you have said is true.
