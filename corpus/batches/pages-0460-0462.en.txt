I am old Koshoy himself.
Watch, you Muslims:
Over the Black Chinese and Manchus
I shall stand as a proud khan!
Heed my words, you Muslims;
I shall bring them to obedience by my word.
The Black Chinese who heed no language,
While mighty Manas is with us,
I shall collar and subdue!
If an enemy comes out to shoot,
I shall drive him before me.
If that lion-hearted hero comes into my hands,
Not China alone, but the whole world
I shall chew between my fangs.
When my appointed death comes, I shall die.
If my mighty champion crosses my path,
If he leads an army toward China,
I shall carry his banner for him.
Beneath that banner I shall stand;
I shall serve that noble hawk.
At Chech-Töbö, standing high,
I had been dwelling at my summer pasture.
For that Manas's sake,
My adversary is Kakanchyn.
This palm-sized band of Muslims
I have brought here under guard.
My caravan brought from the crossing—
Against the accursed Black Chinese,
While my head is still free,
I have come to take revenge.
For a scattered handful of people
I shall spend my wealth;
For fortunate, heroic Manas
I shall sacrifice my life.
Your leaders and khans are here;
You are here from all the clans.
If the Chinese surge forward to attack,
If a formidable man scouts the road,
Do not lose your wits;
My people, do not flee in panic!
We shall wage a fierce struggle
Against the ill-intentioned Chinese.
Facing them and fighting,
Fierce Manas, the champion,
We shall rescue.
Those seething Chinese
Have a ridge they rely upon;
They have a place where they are entrenched.
Against bloodthirsty Manas the leopard,
Those Chinese swept in;
There they unleashed their wrath.
Sweeping down upon Manas,
To seize and bind him in combat,
To unleash their terrible wrath,
Beginning the onslaught, massing together,
They must have swept upon the bloodthirsty hero.
Swarming from every side,
The Chinese must have brought their force to bear.
After fighting for days without respite,
Manas the leopard, the bloodthirsty lion,
Must have been left under siege.
Manas must be grieving and burning,
Despairing of his precious life;
The fierce hero must have suffered.
“My blessed elder, brave Koshoy—
When will my dear elder come?” he must be saying.
If it comes into his heart, that fierce hero
Must be yearning for me.
Your champions and khans are here;
For renowned Khan Manas,
Let us face them and wage war;
Against their entrenched position
Let us fight a desperate struggle.
This is your reason to fight:
Among these Muslims who have come,
Driven into exile and misery,
There are the exiles Akbaltay and Jakyp.
To rescue them,
There is radiant Manas the lion.
One whom a man could never find by wishing,
Born from among those people,
There is radiant, generous Manas.
Do not ask what Manas is like:
He is the champion and the support;
He is the renowned, generous lion.
Without making the Kyrgyz-Burut ask,
For their scattered, diminished people,
He is both champion and guiding mind.
For the suffering Kyrgyz,
He is the master who will guard them firmly.
All my people, listen:
Against the swarming Chinese and Manchus,
Let us take on the hardship.
That solitary Manas—
Fighting without sparing our lives,
Let us rescue him.
And if God supports us,
That lion, your hero Manas,
Over the small Kyrgyz people
Let us raise him as khan.
If Manas, our support, is with us,
Against unbelievers like black spiders,
Against their heroes full of wrath,
Let us sweep in and wreak slaughter.
Against their rulers and lions,
Against Kakanchyn's people,
For Khan Manas's sake,
Let us go to war.
If we fight these Chinese,
They are a seething multitude.
If we clash with the unbelievers,
With their heroes strong of arm,
Their many rulers have their say;
Fall into their hands and the way is narrow.
They have venom that strikes like a snake.
The Chinese people are in forty divisions;
All forty follow forty different ways.
Distinguished warriors who wreck whole peoples—
They have titans and khans.
Among that people of many kinds,
All their titans and giants are there.
Ulukman is there, and khans;
Among those accursed Chinese
Are men skilled in war.
