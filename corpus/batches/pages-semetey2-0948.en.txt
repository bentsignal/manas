In Beijing, where no prophet has trodden,
When all the warriors stand together,
I believe my renown will reach its height.
The Khagan is the one I grapple with;
Kongurbay is the foe I have marked.
I mean to throw Beijing into turmoil,
And my heart is set
On capturing Kongurbay of Beijing.
We grew wrathful and stood defiant,
Ready to slaughter the Khagan’s people,
The black Kalmyks and the Manchus,
And strike them with a mighty blow.
Kongurbay is the one I grapple with,
To avenge my father.
Countless Chinese confront us;
We have firmly girded our waists
And set our faces toward Beijing
To pursue the blood-price of my fierce father
Manas.
I may well take pride in such
A kinsman as Külchoro,
Who closes with Kongurbay
And breaks his army.
Külchoro, my cherished support,
For such a cherished son
I would sacrifice my very life!
For God’s sake and for mine,
Go among the khans
Of the Khagan’s Chinese and return.
If your strength suffices, cast the Kalcha
Flat upon his back and bring him here.
If you are a warrior and a lion,
If you truly are brave Külchoro,
Strike straight at his blood;
If the Kalcha falls into your hands,
Capture him and bring him back alive!
My brave Külchoro, whom I cherish
As my support, go forth!
Though your fortune is full,
No lion like Koshoi will come from Talas;
My sheltering shadow whom I cherish,
No lion like you will arise among the Kyrgyz.
For a falcon-hearted son like you,
Your precious uncle would give his life!
Choros, for a son like you,
All the Kyrgyz together would give their lives!
You are the strength of my rugged back;
Since fierce Manas passed away,
No kinsman has remained to support me.
Merely seeing you puts my heart at rest;
There is no refuge against death,
Yet whenever I see you my heart is at peace.
I have no other shadow in this world but you.
Cursed be loneliness!
My heart burns, and my liver is embers.
Whenever I think, “May my fierce
Külchoro, my cherished support, live safely,”
My heart is comforted.
There is no steed to mount for battle,
To enter the foe with cunning
And charge them with a shout.
Your lonely uncle grieves,
For he has no kinsman but you alone.
Praying that the Creator sustain you,
He goes alone as envoy.
Upon poor Külchoro
May the Creator turn His gaze;
He has no steed to ride on patrol.
Father Creator, do not leave him desolate:
He has no kinsman but this Choro.
I have no stalwart back to bear my mark,
No people but this Choro.”
So Semetey spoke:
“China is a vast realm and the hero is alone—
God, protect my Choro!
Father, for a son like Külchoro
May the roaring Kyrgyz and the many Argyn
All give themselves in sacrifice!”
