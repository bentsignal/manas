I will tread upon the head
Of the lordly Chinese,
And scatter the four kinds of livestock
Of the swine who struck you from above.
If I do not ride forth facing
Heroic Beijing,
And lay grievous hardship
Upon the Kalmyks at Beijing's gate!
If I do not truly enter
Chynmachyn's Beijing and bring slaughter,
And this year avenge the falcons
Who departed for the true world!”
The fine brave son of Manas,
Speaking many words with flashing eyes,
Was impossible to look upon;
The hero's youthful son
Was truly seized by bloodlust.
Flame poured from his mouth,
And the warrior aspect
Of Manas's blue-maned son appeared.
The dear lion grew agitated,
Girding on his gray purse,
Scowling in fury,
Distracting the minds of all who saw him.
When he came in anger,
The descendant of the blood-drinker
Showed the form of a lion.
Straining like a gray tiger,
He prepared to pursue the blood price
From Kakan-Chyn's Beijing.
The ill-starred heir left as a gift
By blue-maned hero Manas
Would not turn back from doom,
Nor retreat a hair from death;
His brow was high, his venom strong,
And he set right whoever attacked him.
From his fathers and forefathers
There were eighty-four armed men.
From the beginning, wherever these blue-maned wolves
Struck, there was red fire.
From his fathers and forefathers,
He thought only what he himself wished,
Heedless of anyone,
Even if his doom arrived now,
Even if he died at once,
Taking no other person's word.
He acted on everything he saw,
Descendant of the empty-headed line,
Who poured blood into black water,
Own son of Khan Manas.
He greased the grassy earth,
And when provoked,
Laid low even his own kin.
Descendant of the accursed one,
Smoking more as he was exhausted,
Own son of turbulent Manas,
Growing stronger whenever men died.
He took his weapons and made ready,
Preparing to mount and ride
Against clamoring China.
Which of his deeds shall I tell?
This butcher's youthful son
Drives white deer and makes them cattle;
He makes the enemy he fights his servant.
He drives blue deer and makes them cattle;
He makes the enemy he targets his servant.
Sharpening his keen steel,
Preparing his weapons,
Rejoicing at the word Beijing,
The hero's youthful son
Put on his bulletproof coat.
Ah, poor lion, swaggering,
His face transformed by wrath—
(The youth born after
His father, magnanimous Manas:
Which of his deeds can we tell?)
To journey on campaign to Beijing,
