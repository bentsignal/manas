Once that warrior is gone from sight
And your greater purpose prospers,
Then trample the foolish son of Manas
Down beneath your feet
And scatter all his herds!
Raid every one of his horses;
Seize all his wealth!
Pour forth volleys of gunfire;
Avenge our forefather, hero Köksö,
In precisely this manner!
Let a thousand spears thrust together
And deliver his punishment into our hands.
Give this slave who boasts of strength
The punishment he deserves!
Do it heedless of blood;
Slaughter all his people without remainder!
Bring anguish upon his nation;
Let not a single soul remain.
My people, this is my command:
Let there be a massacre!”
So saying, Ümötöy
Roared and poured out his wrath.
Becoming foes worse than the Kalmyks,
He summoned the whole multitude:
“Overwhelm him!”
They closed in, weapons undivided;
Once Ümötöy gave the order,
They seemed determined to spare no life.
All together they surged forward.
The dense host of Kazakhs, in plain sight,
Thrust their spears at the noble warrior,
Showing no intention of sparing him.
Their fierce guns cracked,
And together they overwhelmed him.
Their black muskets boomed;
Firing jezails and drawing bows,
The Kazakhs closed in as one.
Then Külchoro—
A lion-warrior surpassing the world,
A hero unlike all other men—
Could not endure the humiliation,
Nor the mindless violence Ümötöy inflicted
When he had come as an envoy.
When faced with such grievous assault,
How could the warrior refrain from destroying them?
When faced with still greater violence,
How could Külchoro merely watch?
He roared in fury,
Raising the battle cry of “Manas!”
He struck his gray horse on the flank
And took his slender spear in hand.
He wheeled and wheeled among them;
Ten died wherever he wheeled.
Five or six died at his waist,
Crying, “Brother, we submit!”
Six or seven lay stretched out,
Dead where they had confronted him.
Every wise elder
And the whole Kazakh people
Recoiled in terror at the attacking warrior.
“We have come to witness
The dreadful havoc of the jezail;
We have come, every soul together,
To lose our lives and die!
This hero Ümötöy
Has proved to be no protection at all.
That dark-hearted infidel,
Claiming Köksö’s blood-price,
Has come to gather
The tribute of Sary-Arka,” they said.
“He has come to scatter all the people
And slaughter them,” they said.
“He means to gather subjects and build a nation
For himself,” they said.
“He means to find his chance
And bring the people to utter ruin,” they said.
The people cried out and an uproar arose,
Their clamor spreading the tale.
