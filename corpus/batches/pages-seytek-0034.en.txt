I obtained something that kills outright:
I had these razors made,
And upon their edges
Had dragon’s venom,
The vapor of a poison that kills without a sound,
Firmly fixed.
I had them wrapped in finery
And consecrated them
For bringing death to the child;
Half my livestock
I spent on these razors.
My seven mothers, dearer than anyone,
If Aichürök bears a son,
Be sure to cut his umbilical cord.
Secretly destroy the child
And put an end to him!
Show me your skill:
Find a way to touch
His navel with the razor,
Destroy him and scorch his mother’s heart;
Put an end to him.
Make the child vanish,
And if you set Kyyaz’s affairs right,
I will grant whatever you ask.
Seven mothers, I will give you
Red brocade measured by the foot!
I will never send you from my side
But keep you as my precious treasures.
Dear mother, I will take you
As my own mother!
Leave my concubine and take my wife;
Leave them both and take the one you choose.
For riding to feasts and celebrations,
For prancing wherever you go,
It is a fine and well-mannered beast:
Take my fleet bay racehorse.
Take all my abundant wealth,
Take every possession I own;
Know how precious this is to me.
I will not begrudge the least of my wealth.
“My cunning mothers, heed my words.
If her child is a boy,
Consider what manner of child he is:
His forebear set fire to grassy lands,
Planted his banner on tufted plains,
And raised an uproar across the earth.
His distant forefather was brave Manas,
Born after going to Altai,
Who shook the whole world,
A lion who led the nations.
He shot down twelve giants,
And became a torment in the heart
Of the Black Chinese and Manchus.
He took golden tribute from the khagan;
The vast, settled Chinese host,
As countless as black beetles,
Was left weeping by him.
Know well what I have told you.
I will give you many animals as gifts.
My dear seven mothers,
If you serve me well,
I will give you red-tailed camels
And lavish riches without stint.
I will make your yaks number ninety
And fill your homes with wealth;
Mothers, I will set your hearts at ease.
I will dress you in robes of red silk,
And make the hems of those robes
So broad that they sweep the ground.
Among the Jediger of Jeti-Özön,
I will make all my settled people
Know and honor you, my seven mothers!
If you should die unexpectedly,
If chance brings you an early death,
I will have a golden mausoleum built
And upon the crown of that tomb
Have an inscription carved in ruby.
I will make all who see it marvel
And spread your fame among the multitude.
From a spring beneath the bending hills
