From wrathful Kyyaz’s hand
Deliver us.”
So Kadyrbay
Sharpened his eloquent tongue
And poured out his heartfelt speech.
Finding Kadyrbay’s words just,
The army clamored and raised an uproar.
If only they had found a way,
The distressed people, surging in anger,
Would have attacked Kyyaz themselves.
They crossed a ridge
Shaped like the back of a saddle;
Many who could find no other way
Fled and reached that place.
Escaping, they went to Kyyaz
And brought him the news.
After thrusting their spears in fury,
The Jediger, for the time it takes meat to cook,
Were left unable to speak,
Grieving in distress.
Many men led by Tölöbay—
Tölöbay, son of Tölök,
And Kadyrbay, son of Kalcha—
Men skilled upon the field of combat,
Broad-mouthed and high-browed,
Eloquent men who gladdened the soul,
Capable men hurrying in alarm—
Before brave Kyyaz
Those strong men fled in dismay.
The people rushed about in panic and turmoil;
Before brave Kyyaz
They went and began to speak:
“Here, take your spear!” they said.
Amid the people’s clamor, they cast
His spear before brave Kyyaz.
Kadyrbay, son of Kalcha,
Spoke fully and held nothing back:
“Jeti-Suu is the land where you dwell;
From Jeti-Suu you raised an army,
A vast host that shook the earth.
You enlisted grim-hearted champions,
Warriors who never retreat.
It seems we came to endure
Great turmoil and terrible calamity;
It seems we came to die
At the hands of the bloodthirsty lion himself.
Assailing one blessed by Kydyr,
We became needlessly entangled in slaughter;
Assailing a lion-hearted champion,
We became needlessly entangled with death.
It seems we came to witness
Unbounded turmoil and great calamity.
I put my trust in Kanchoro,
But your Kanchoro is a runaway slave;
It seems we came today to die
At the hands of the bloodthirsty lion.
Understand well the worth of peace.
You who rule Jeti-Suu,
Who would surrender your comrade to no one
And never yielded weeping before an enemy—
You were a panther; remember that.
Ill-fated Kyyaz, heed my words.
The army has gone mad and seethes;
The people clamor and the nation is in turmoil.
He has come to have them gathered;
That fawning madman has come
To have every one of these people
Slaughtered.
Külchoro is a bullet already fired;
Your boastful ally Kanchoro
Has no gift for diplomacy.
Let us select countless swift steeds
And offer them to the bloodthirsty hero.
Let the Jediger cease their frenzy,
And return without a massacre.
If Külchoro comes surging in fury,
He will do whatever he pleases.
