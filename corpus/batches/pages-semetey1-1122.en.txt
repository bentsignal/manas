They say bloodthirsty Manas arose.
He crushed a thousand champions,
Killed them and thrust them into the earth.
If you do not cut down his crookedness now,
Destroy his forty households,
And seize and restrain Manas,
He has unleashed havoc
And taken plunder worth my thousand camels!
If you do not finish Manas,
He has inflicted harsh suffering," he said.
Before Esenkan,
He went straight in and spoke these words.
Esenkan grew angry:
"If you cannot overcome Manas,
All of you perish together!" he said.
In wrath, Esenkan
Drove him away.
Shamed, Ezkara
Said, "Damn you, Burut,"
And the ill-starred wretch held a grudge
Against hero Manas.
He came to Kökötöy's feast
To stir up conflict,
Hoping to utterly strike down
The handful of Kyrgyz;
Thus Ezkara came speaking,
For the Chinese bore a grudge.
With Khan Kongurbay at their head,
They were poised to swarm upon
The handful of Muslims.
Kökötöy's many wethers
And countless horses spread like a lake—
Bokmurun drove them all together
And gave them for slaughter.
Shouting angrily,
He summoned Bokmurun;
These were Ezkara's words.
When Bokmurun came and bowed,
He spoke as follows:
"I shall eat my fill at your feast, Burut;
Bringing torment upon your feast,
I shall raid your village, Burut!
I shall bring hell upon your feast, Burut;
I shall strike down your company, Burut!
You have migrated from broad Tashken, Burut;
Your braid woven in six strands, Burut—
Saying, 'Whoever would not come to the feast,
I shall go and strike him down,'
You brandished your strength, Burut.
When I surge, I shall crush your strength, Burut;
I shall darken your very roots, Burut.
I have come with a vast army to strike
Your far-flung fury, Burut.
I shall rain upon your head
My winter in the summertime, Burut.
I shall raid and seize your feast,
And crush all your far-flung deeds,
Burut!
At the feast I shall dig
The pit into which you will fall, Burut.
If I grow angry now,
I shall send my army swarming, Burut,
Upon this very place where you hold the feast!
Is the one in whom you place such trust
Your bloodthirsty hero Manas?
I shall plunge my hand into the cauldron, Burut,
And plunder your meat, Burut!
