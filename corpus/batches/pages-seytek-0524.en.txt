Let me drive your warriors aside,
Slaughter and burn a multitude of you.
Let me wreak a mighty massacre;
If your Kyrgyz fall into my hands,
I shall leave your braided women widows,
Stain the trap’s jaws with blood,
Turn your black road to dust,
Extinguish the fire you kindle,
And drive every one of you away.
If I do not bring ruin and slaughter,
Roaring in fury,
Churning heaven and sky—
Then I have never been Sarybay,
Nor ever mounted the steed named Sarybay.
Since I matured and became a man,
And stood as an equal among heroes,
Keeping villages and possessing lands,
Inspiring dread and ruling peoples,
No land has remained unseen by me,
No nation has remained unconquered by me;
I slaughtered khans and captured lions.
How many tens of thousands have I conquered!
I have faced many spears and guns
Without ever losing my wits.
Yet never upon the earth have I seen
A youth like this Külchoro.
He is a champion and a hero,
A youth at the height of his strength.
One worthy to trade equal blows—
Ah, now I see: this is my true enemy!
He is a warrior and a lion;
My coming here has brought me torment.
I sought suffering and conflict;
This is the foe destined to fight me.
He is renowned; he is a lion.
For slaughtering my father, the Monster,
And leaving a mound above his grave,
He truly is a hero, if I speak no lie.
He is mighty, and of a khan’s blood;
I shall occupy Talas and settle there.
I shall demolish your stone enclosures
And burn and consume multitudes of you.
I shall seize your noble ones;
Beginning with Aiköl, I shall drain
The blood of every one of you.
I shall turn Jelmayan’s head,
And slaughter earth-born Jelpiniş
Down to the very ground.
I shall seize and slaughter their khans;
These have always been my mortal foes,
So I shall consume them all, leaving none.
I shall tear apart whoever confronts me,
Gnaw whoever crosses my path;
If I do not slaughter all I encounter
And sweep them utterly away,
If I do not seize and conquer your people,
And swallow them all without remainder,
Then rather than remain Sarybay,
May I sink unborn into darkness,
May I die instead of living!
If I do not wreak slaughter among your multitudes,
If I do not plunder your daughters,
If I do not spread carnage to your borders,
Bind your gray-wolf giant,
And smash open your skull,
Then let me draw out
All your far-flung insolence!
Your heroes and your khans
I shall cast into turmoil and slaughter.
Leaving not one of you,
Your nobles and your khans—
I, the warrior Sarybay,
Shall trample every one of you.
