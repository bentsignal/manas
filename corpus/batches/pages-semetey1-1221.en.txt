He had Khan Manas plunder them.
They had gone as guests
To Kökötöy's feast and turned back,
Intending to use force,
Only to lose Achbuudan.
Many men fell to bullets;
Many had their hands bound.
Many Black Kalmyks and Manchus
Who had come against Manas
Were captured as booty.
The bloodthirsty lion Manas the leopard
Made a massacre there
Of the teeming Chinese and Manchus.
While the falcon Manas lived,
He brought great turmoil upon the Chinese;
It was plain that he was a hero.
“Bloodthirsty Manas the leopard
Will devour us,” they said,
And the khan of the Black Kalmyks and Manchus
Despaired of his life.
His turmoil became known to the people.
Had that Manas not been there,
At Kökötöy's feast
They would have cropped ears short,
Dug deep pits,
And brewed utter misery.
When they were about to dig a well in the place of honor
And scatter the four kinds of livestock,
Would they not have unleashed
Unsparing turmoil?
Did he not save the palm-sized Muslims
And keep those Chinese
From slaughtering them?
The Chinese did not quell their strife;
When they were about to slaughter everyone,
Manas the leopard protected them.
Their cuirasses hanging loose,
I saw all their khans
In the place where they panicked.
I saw the place where Sultan Manas the bloodthirsty
Suppressed
Their murderous turmoil.
I saw the place where he brought
Turmoil upon the whole world.
I saw the place where he separated
The palm-sized Muslims
From the Chinese and rescued them.
Had that Manas not been there,
Would the Black Manchus and Chinese
Not have drawn the line
And made sport of them?
They would have driven them away into slavery
And brought them to their end.
When they were about to slaughter
The Muslim giants,
Khan Koshoi of the Katagan
Fled out from among the people.
Jamgyrchy of the Eshtek
Despaired of his own life
And panicked helplessly.
I saw then that dear blue-maned Manas
Was a lion.
I saw that he was a hero,
Different and surpassing other mortals.
Blaze-headed thoroughbred among horses—
Amid that massacre,
Agybay, Bakai, and Almambet,
Along with Chubak,
Were heroes born for honor.
You keep saying, Chynkozho,
“I will seize
Famous blue-maned Manas's daughter-in-law.”
“God-stricken words!
You have acted by force, Chynkozho.
Will Semetey, born of Manas,
Submit to your violence?
Let it go, Chynkozho;
Will he give us his wife?
