If you are a hero, if you are a man,
If you are Sarybay the warrior.
Kuyaly called for her turn;
Sarybay came up to her.
Here is how the fight went.
God had cursed Sarybay:
He blustered and loomed,
Refusing to listen to reason.
As if he could reap and gather all,
As far as the world's end
As if he alone could reach it,
As if to shatter the whole world,
And grind it to dust.
Sarybay raged;
Such was the course of the fight.
The eighteen thousand worlds
He seemed able to lay waste,
The whole wide universe
He seemed ready to destroy.
Refusing to heed what she said,
Without granting the warrior's turn,
The black-socketed battleaxe
He seized and swung around.
Before the champion could ready herself,
He brought it down with a crash.
Sarybay's contentious ways
She had known for a long time;
A deep-blue iron helmet
That lion-hearted champion
Wore pulled down over her head;
Kuyaly was well prepared;
Armor along her ribs
Her shoulders and heart
She had covered as she came;
Kuyaly was a mighty champion;
For a spear thrust to enter,
There was no opening anywhere.
In taking on Kuyaly,
Sarybay had no luck.
Refusing to heed those words,
Without yielding the warrior’s turn,
When Sarybay pressed the fight,
The renowned lionhearted wrestler girl
Kuyaly would not stand by and watch.
Sarybay, who had grappled with her,
Would God look on and smite?
God forbid such a sight!
He had begun an ugly fight.
Your hero Kuyaly
Cast her life aside.
They stood face to face,
Fighting with heavy blows,
Charging and thrusting their spears,
Jabbing wherever they could,
Grabbing each other by the hems,
Struggling to exhaustion,
The earth torn into gullies,
And human minds reeling.
The field filled with fluttering banners,
A din that split the sky,
They faced each other head-on,
Firing guns and drawing bows,
Clashing in a fierce fight,
A mighty battle, plain to see,
As its roar went up,
The brave souls clashed with a crack,
As they fought on,
The earth was torn into gullies,
And human minds were reeling,
Heavy guns thundered and shook the mountains,
Men fell, struck by bullets,
As the ranks collapsed,
What a fierce struggle this had become!
They waged a hard battle,
Gripping heavy guns and drawing bows,
And how many sank beneath the sky,
Waging a savage fight,
Both were champions, both were lions—
Hero Sarybay and Kuyaly,
Heroes extraordinary among mortals.
