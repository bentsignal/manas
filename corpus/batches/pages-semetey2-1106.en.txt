Their multitude comes pouring forth;
The front ranks of noble
Hero Kyyaz are appearing.
Along the edge of Suusamyr
Half his host is descending;
Along Bek-Jailoo
His khan’s own force comes forth.
From Kara-Buura and Chatkal,
The Jediger, covering the land like sand,
Are also pouring out.
Through every valley, billowing,
Their dust rises;
As they march in a roaring surge,
Their dust rises.
Anyone who sees or hears of them
Feels his soul leave his body.
They have fitted their hollow-shafted spears
With shafts of hard wood,
Saying, ‘Either we shall utterly destroy them,
And slaughter the gray wolf, the blood-drinker,
Semetey,
Or else we ourselves shall die;
We have marched from Jetisuu
To this place from which there is no retreat,
And now that we have come, we shall see it through.’
Their moonlike tasseled banners fly,
Their clamor splits the sky,
Their army rends the earth;
In Jetisuu the Jediger
Have risen to the last man,
Not a few but a multitude,
And Kyyaz drives them onward.
Swarming like worms, the Jediger,
The fierce khan Kyyaz’s host,
Come streaming forth in splendor.
The swordsmen are at the front,
The Jediger seething and hissing,
With their strongest men along the flanks.
The axemen are at the front,
Their shouts loud enough to split heads;
The Jediger advance
With their champions on the flanks.
The musketeers are at the front;
The Jediger swarm like worms,
With their finest men along the flanks,
Firing muskets and loosing arrows,
Advancing in battle array.
Thrusting spears and striking with axes,
The Jediger, bound by their oath,
Come flooding forward.
They come to settle Zhylygnduu, broad Keng-Kol,
And spacious Talas;
Grappling like dragons,
Thrusting spears in glittering ranks,
Piercing lungs and severing heads,
They come to conquer and occupy it.
The Jediger will surely destroy
The small folk who think themselves mighty,
And bring calamity upon them;
They will surely make the girls whimper
And drag them away as plunder.
Saying, ‘We have avenged Toltoy,’
They will surely crush everything to dust.
The valiant men and khans,
Every son who has been born,
And all the countless living souls—
They will surely slaughter them all.
The ninety thousand horses in the mountains
They will surely raid without pause.
The surging Jediger
Will surely reach our very roots;
All the people they have ruined
They will surely drive away as captives.
The girls will whimper without ceasing,
Becoming precious spoils.
The Jediger are seething;
They will surely drink much blood.
