Khan Akun's treasury—
Within that treasury,
Within the treasury she had sixty folded,
And loaded upon white camels.
Saying, "Let my mighty lord eat,"
She brought countless kinds of cooked food;
Saying, "Let him drink until his thirst is quenched,"
She brought liquor distilled from raisins,
Grain cooked in cream,
And costly tea for drinking.
She took a barren three-year-old horse for slaughter,
Leading the horse loose and untethered.
To render service,
From the renowned city of Afghanistan
She took sixty young men as attendants.
The campsite where they arrived and dismounted
Was in the hollow of Ak-Kyya,
Beside Great Urgench.
Delighting all who looked,
Upon the blue-green meadow grass
She had sixty carpets
Of surpassing quality spread out.
Such was the nature of those carpets:
If they soaked in water for forty years,
Not even their edges would fade;
If cast in water for eighty years,
They would not soften and rot.
Their sight opened the heart.
When she took some sixty carpets
And had them spread,
The likeness of paradise appeared.
At the sight, the poor man faltered,
And the gray wolf's heart opened.
Churok, daughter of Akun,
Appeared like a noble maiden.
Before your lord's eyes
Speckled flies seemed to dance;
Words spoken early seemed to become late.
To join in embrace with Churok,
The daughter of Akun,
To reach her there at once,
The lion lord Semetey
Stared with all four eyes.
Fully satisfying the heart,
Of satin and silk quilts
That Churok had sixty
Laid in ordered rows.
Saying, "If his ardor rises, he may put it on,"
She had bulletproof plate armor
Covered in splendid fashion.
Poor, fierce Aychurok
Had some sixty tents
Raised in a wondrous row.
If a young man did not go there in desire,
It was as though his hearth-fire had died.
When one looked at every contour of her body,
This Churok, daughter of Akun,
Was like a demon moving at dusk.
When your lord looked at an angle,
Thirty daughters-in-law and thirty maidens
Poured out and began their games.
In their midst Aychurok
Appeared in the form of the morning star.
Even milk and fermented cream seemed amazed,
Saying, "Can a mortal be like this?"
And the hero's heart opened.
The poor, serene woman's
Manner appeared wondrous.
She walked with a long, radiant stride;
Sixty buttons and forty clasps
Upon the difficult beauty's
Breast glittered brightly.
A body amulet of yellow gold,
Its cord showed at her throat.
The broad-chested lord's
Mind was deeply distracted.
