# Kyrgyzpatent certificate 470 / Karalaev electronic concordance search — 2026-09-15

## Result

No downloadable, mirrored, archived, or publicly queryable copy of the registered application or its Microsoft Access database was located. The official registration bulletin establishes that certificate **470 is not a 500,553-line national-corpus application**. It is a desktop concordance for the *Manas* text in Sayakbay Karalaev's version, containing **19,656 word forms** and **151,695 word occurrences**. Its stated scope and size rule it out as the sought complete trilogy text.

The strongest concrete acquisition route is the registered rights holder and current university affiliation recorded below, or a request to Kyrgyzpatent for access to deposited registration material. Those are prospective routes only; no message or request was sent.

## Relationship to the 500,553-line target

Certificate 470 cannot itself be evidence of a complete 500,553-line database. Its 151,695 **word occurrences** are a different unit from verse lines and are far too few to encode 500,553 verse lines as running text. The title and bulletin consistently describe *Manas* in the narrow component sense; neither mentions *Semetey*, *Seytek*, or the continuations.

The associated printed work, Tashpolot Sadykov and Bakyt Sharshembaev's *Manas Destanı: Kırgızca-Türkçe Büyük Dizin* (Türk Dil Kurumu, Ankara, 2011), ISBN **978-975-16-2383-6**, is a 1,647-page word index. Bookseller and library descriptions say that it indexes the Sagymbay Orozbak uulu and Sayakbay Karala uulu *Manas* variants, listing word forms, Turkish equivalents, suffixes, and occurrence locations. It is a potential aid for checking the *Manas* component, not a demonstrated source for the full trilogy. Physical catalogue records were found at the Türk Dil Kurumu library and Akdeniz University; a current commercial listing was also found, but no open scan or bundled software/database was exposed.

## National-corpus accounting issue

A separate national-corpus paper says the corpus used published Karalaev editions—1984/1986 *Manas*, 1987/1989 *Semetey*, and 1991 *Seytek*—and gives this three-part table:

| Label | Lines |
|---|---:|
| Manas | 84,830 |
| Semetey | 218,787 |
| Seytek | 196,936 |
| Total | **500,553** |

The commonly cited four-part allocation is 84,513 *Manas* + 316,157 *Semetey* + 84,697 *Seytek* + 15,186 Kenen/Alimsaryk/Kulansaryk = **500,553**. Relative to it, the paper's table changes the categories by **+317 Manas, −97,370 Semetey, and +97,053 Seytek after the continuations are folded into Seytek**. These changes cancel exactly, preserving the grand total.

No public database schema, record fields, query result, or explanatory passage was found that assigns the transferred material to episodes or manuscripts. The arithmetic is consistent with a broader “Seytek” bucket that absorbs continuation material and roughly 97,000 lines otherwise counted under *Semetey*, but that remains an inference. Certificate 470 cannot resolve the issue because its registered source has only 151,695 word occurrences and covers the *Manas* component. The table should therefore remain a secondary aggregate, not an inventory-to-text crosswalk or proof that a 500,553-line electronic text is retrievable.

## Concrete acquisition routes without action taken

1. **Rights holder / university copy.** Bakyt Sharshembaev remains listed by Kyrgyz-Turkish Manas University's Computer Engineering department, with current institutional profile and contact information. Because the certificate records him as sole rights holder, he is the most direct prospective source for the 116 MB package or its `.mdb`/`.accdb` data file.
2. **Kyrgyzpatent deposited material.** Cite certificate **470** and application **20170026.6** when asking whether registration deposits include a reproducible program/database copy and what reading-room, inspection, or rights-holder-consent procedure applies. The public register does not state that deposits are downloadable.
3. **Printed-index consultation.** Locate ISBN **9789751623836** through the Türk Dil Kurumu library or another holding. Occurrence references could help identify the source edition and test coverage, although the 1,647-page book is not a substitute for the running text.

No contact, access request, purchase, or submission was made.

## Conclusion

The certificate-470 lead does not provide the missing 500,553-line corpus. It proves that a 116 MB MS Access-backed concordance existed in 2017, but the registered corpus contains only 151,695 word occurrences from the *Manas* component. No public copy or query service was located. The only concrete retrieval path is rights-holder or registration-deposit access, and even a successful acquisition would most likely help with the *Manas* component rather than supply missing *Semetey*, *Seytek*, or continuation lines.
