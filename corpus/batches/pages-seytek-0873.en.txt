Whether wrestler or warrior,
To the whole wide world Sarybay
Seemed like a towering mountain,
“The whole of that small army
He drove before him and devoured,” they said,
Those with sense pondered this,
Unable to gather their wits,
Burning without smoke,
The people despaired of life,
Thus the people stood,
When Sarybay grew furious—
Such was his valor,
Hearing the wrestlers' report,
Hearing that brave Seytek and valiant Choro
Had slaughtered the army,
He bound his soul to death,
As for remaining alive and not dying,
The guest-soul granted by the Creator—
How could his savage fury spare it?
Sarybay, son of the ogre—
Such was his boldness,
Tugging his coat of mail straight,
Shaking out his cuirass,
“I am a hero against those with skill,
And against a foe whose skill has failed,
I am the dreadful end of time,
I am the walnut ember itself,
Burning like a river and slaughtering nations,
When my frenzy seizes me again,
I am the black eye of the scourge,
I am calamity incarnate,
I am the source of every slayer,
I am savagery itself,
Gnawing whoever I encounter,
Devouring whoever I find,
Mangling whoever crosses me,
I am the black scourge itself,
I shall be a bleak ridge
That weary men cannot cross,
I am the black scourge
That mangles any soul it meets,
While you still have mighty strength,
How could I yield my assembled army?
Külchoro has run mad—
I shall give him his full punishment,
If Khan Seytek crosses my path,
I shall seize him and spill his blood,
If Semetey crosses my path,
I shall tear apart his ribs,
If Bakai confronts me,
I shall beat the old man's fury from him,
If Kuyaly grapples with me,
I shall settle her account.”
Saying this, he thundered,
Every mortal human's
Whole free body shuddered,
Sarybay, son of the ogre,
Pressed the wretched people sorely,
His shout splitting heads,
The doomed man's bellowing,
His roar splitting stones,
Against that dread man's wrath,
No one could stand and look,
When the infidel gathered force,
No person could withstand him,
Such was the true might
Of the infidel, the ogre's son,
Then Sarybay
Was seized by his blood-drinking frenzy,
All who looked on were astonished,
No one remained unhurried,
His wrath became a grievous force,
Then Sarybay,
His eyes reddening like fire,
Swelled with menace and strained for battle,
Taking up his slaughtering steel spear,
The raging madman,
With a steel mace as large as a house,
His bloodthirsty eyes rolling,
Glowering, his hair bristling,
