## Identity and coverage

The cover says **МАНАС 1-том**; PDF metadata attributes it to Sayakbai Karalaev. PDF pages 2–22 contain Musaev's introductory essay, signed **С. МУСАЕВ 5/V 1982.** on page 22. The print publication year remains unestablished: this signature date is not a colophon. Word PDF creation in 2015 and modification in 2016 identify digital processing dates. No printed bibliographic title page or colophon was established from the examined front/end matter. The file is a reflowed digital text with Bizdin watermark, not a photographic scan.

Narrative begins partway through PDF22, continues through PDF434; the glossary starts on434 and ends439. The last narrative line is `Жатпай кетти койнуна.` The final glossary entry is `Эриш бузуу`. This is the first Manas book, not the trilogy. No certified verse count was attempted.

**PDF21 explicitly admits abridgment**: the edition omits literal repetitions and some ideologically unsuitable matter, while claiming to preserve the narrative sequence. It describes a five-book project and says this first book covers events from the beginning to before the Great Campaign. Potential shortening into prose is discussed for later books. Consequently, neither this book nor its completeness language establishes all 500,553 verses.

## Opening comparison

Their narrative begins with the same genealogy but has substantive differences: candidate omits the early additional base verse ending `Кыдыр даарыган`; candidate `Ногой` contrasts with base `Огой`, and `Сомпуктун` with `Сомдуктун`. The specific `пирге` reading supports the already documented spiritual-guide interpretation; candidate's own `Оң` typo cautions against wholesale substitution.

These differences establish a different digital textual form from the base. They do not establish independent manuscript or print ancestry. This is exactly the witness already discussed in `TEXTUAL-CORRECTIONS.md`, so the Jasulib mirror adds no independent corroboration and no new restoration proposal.
