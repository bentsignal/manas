Its stock had broken apart.
Poor Bölökbay,
I repaired its stock again,
So the prince might shoot far,
So his enemy in combat
Might lie utterly ruined.
I twisted it twelve times,
And repaired it to be just like
Butcher King Manas's gun.
So Kongurbay, khan of Beijing,
Might stand and face him,
So he might carefully pull its trigger
And strike the warrior firmly,
So it might wreak havoc
Among the teeming Chinese,
So if my sultan enters battle
He might take the ancient vengeance
Of his royal father Manas,
So when he aims it at the enemy,
Its bullet might roar away,
And tear through the head
Of the enemy who comes against him,
I twisted its barrel ten times.
Brave Manas's Akkelte,
Almambet's Syrbarang,
Khan Bakay's Kökchybyk,
Ajybay's Chortkelte,
Brave Chubak's Koychagyr—
As an unattainable inheritance,
For their far-striking sharpness,
So the sons left behind them
Might choose and take them as gifts,
Grow to manhood from orphanhood,
And bring conflict to Beijing,
Shed blood in close combat,
And from the wrathful warrior,
From infidel Khan Kongurbay,
Fight and exact blood payment,
Live to take revenge,
And through enmity avenge their grievance.
There are twelve sharp, straight guns;
If you wage battle against the khan,
This is what I have prepared for you.
Listen, honored sons!
There will be no regret when you fire them,
If you unleash great conflict upon the khan.
I knew from the beginning
That you would go to teeming China;
I always thought you would go.
How could things be right if you did not?
If you did not go to Beijing,
I thought my departed King Manas
Would be as though buried in salt.
So I kept these prepared.
I prepared twelve identical sharp guns,
So you might fire in encirclement,
And be filled with joy.
Preparing all their powder and shot,
I gathered forty Kalmyks,
Without letting one person notice,
Without letting anyone know,
And had this wonder made.
I had earth dug and saltpeter
Boiled without cease for seven years;
I cast bullets from lead
And had the weapons made ready.
I poured and boxed the powder
And had this wonder fashioned.
For the twelve sharp guns,
I had all the cast bullets
Divided into twelve separate lots.
For all those bullets together,
I had rawhide saddlebags made
And filled them with shot and powder.
Such is the nature of a campaign:
No man goes to teeming Beijing
Without making preparation.
This arduous wonder I have made—
