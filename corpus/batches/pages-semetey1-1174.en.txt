Heading toward Bukhara.
At the nose of the accursed mountain,
At its looming promontory,
Just as I reached it,
When I turned my horse's head,
He was clearing the road,
Standing watch,
Mounted on a red chestnut horse,
Its hair tail tightly knotted,
Finding no mountain to assail,
Finding no enemy to fight,
Consumed by his own thoughts.
Like an eagle of Orolmo-Too,
Perched without a wrist-rest,
I saw hero Agysh
Riding out toward me.
When I saw hero Agysh,
His whole body was sinew;
He was a man useful in need,
A lion kinsman of generous spirit.
Against a champion like Tukubay,
Agysh will serve, O people;
To wrestle and win honor,
Put elbow-sized Agysh,
O lion and leopard lord,
Into the wrestling match.
Peerless Agysh was a hero;
When infidel and Muslim
Clashed together,
He was a man who won honor.
When I saw hero Agysh,
His strength was immense;
The valiant, highborn champion
Was himself a mighty hero.
Fierce champion Tukubay
Was a man fit for him to grapple.
To throw him and win honor,
My joyous leopard lord,
Agysh is the champion you have found."
The wrathful leopard
Heeded the queen's words.
Twelve companions and Kyrgylchal
Received the hero's command
And went to seek Agysh.
Though they searched mountain and hill,
Agysh could not be found there.
Though they searched the gathered people,
Agysh could not be found there.
He had had his tent adorned with gold.
When they migrated from Tashkent,
Saying, "Let them provide adornment,"
Kokotoy's Bokmurun
Summoned exactly sixty palace girls,
And had those girls brought.
At Khan Kokotoy's feast,
The palace girls were playing
Along Jele-Karkyra.
Your unruly ruffian Agysh
Had gone to the palace girls
And was playing splendid games.
A great player, a girl's delight—
Such was your Agysh's way.
In a house with girls, he played the fiddle;
In a house with kumis, he served the cups.
With his red belt trailing—
Such was Agysh's way—
Whenever he saw a girl, he asked for her.
With his black belt trailing—
Such was Agysh's way—
Whenever he saw a woman, he asked for her.
When he saw a girl, he grew animated
And began a splendid song;
When hero Agysh saw a woman,
He nearly died of longing.
Hero Agysh was staying
With the palace girls.
Kumis by day and girls by night—
So he was passing his time.
