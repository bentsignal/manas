Finding no head he could tear off,
His courage like a sheer mountain ridge,
The formidable Külchoro
Seemed able to devour any man he grappled.
At Choro’s terrible bearing,
All the Jediger might flee.
If seven hosts of alien foes came,
He alone could crush them.
The hot breath from his mouth
Seemed able to set the valley aflame.
If he merely stirred,
He seemed able to ravage the world.
Broad-hearted as a valley,
His handsome front teeth
Were like tempered steel.
When you looked at his aquiline nose,
It was like the sheath of a saber.
His upturned mustache
Was like a Bukharan blade.
The heat of his two eyes
Was like flame rising through a valley.
When you looked into their corners,
They were like the hollows of dried-up wells.
When that formidable man moved,
He seemed able to swallow the world.
Recovering from his broken state,
Choro became a bloodthirsty warrior again.
His former vigor
Filled him like a bloated blue tick,
And his wolfish valor and heroism
Were as they had been before.
The awe-inspiring poor falcon’s
Hollows filled with milk,
His gaunt frame filled with flesh,
And the time came for him to fight.
With Kyyaz, son of Bagysh,
The time came for him to meet face-to-face.
His figure was like half a mountain,
Ready to devour any soul he met.
Külchoro, upheld by the divine,
Used Choro as his pretext,
And the poor fellow followed him about.
The blue mastiff left by the hero—
Kyzylsart suddenly seized it
And slaughtered the blue mastiff.
Cutting out its diaphragm,
He laid it upon Külchoro.
Kyzylsart was meticulous,
Skillful in devising strange measures.
Upon Külchoro’s back
He fitted the dog’s diaphragm,
And the powdered red medicine
He rubbed on forty times.
The boiled black medicine
He rubbed on time after time.
Then the poor father Choro’s
Painful places subsided,
His eyes opened wide,
Blood coursed through his body,
And the day came when his courage swelled.
The moon waned and the nights passed,
And many days went by.
Vigor seethed and strength filled him,
Power welled up within him.
The days passed as though he lay in comfort,
And all he saw was like the Night of Glory.
His loosened joints were restored,
And all his sorrow disappeared.
His thoughts became a night of blessed destiny,
And the spirited youth and swift horse
Came to the day their worth was fulfilled.
It was then that Külchoro
Returned to himself and recovered.
Kyzylsart had been his healer,
And all his affliction disappeared.
His courage rose and strength filled him.
Think of Choro now:
He could find no mountain to strike,
No enemy to fight,
No stone to chew.
