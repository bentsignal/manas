To that strong-armed infidel,
Fire rose from my very core,
An enemy arose from within me,
The child truly struck down
I see I am about to behold,
True death has come for my poor soul,
It seems I have come to die.
O trader in this fleeting world,
Enamored of this world,
What did I think I would gain?
Kuyaly, the one I clashed with,
Was one who came from my own land,
We jousted seven times,
And she said, 'I will do this to you.'
That renowned champion girl
Has come into her inheritance,
Whoever she set her sights on died,
What she wished for was granted,
Whoever grappled with her died,
And her challenger has come.
Kayipchal's champion daughter,
You dark-hearted, swaggering girl,
A girl cast off by the people,
At your village's edge stands a white cliff,
You shameless, reckless woman!
No waist for a hand to pass around,
No man who has not bowed to you,
A noble-born champion girl,
Becoming a giant beyond measure,
Is it right for her to go seeking a man?
A lone girl like a princess,
Should she go out to grapple?
Roaming among the people and unsettling the land,
Could there be a troublemaker like you?
The only daughter of a noble soul,
Is it fitting for her to seek a man?
Could there be a troublemaker like you,
Unless you meet me face to face?
I draw my sword and sheath it,
Now roused, I speak in earnest.
You cast poison into my ashes,
Making your secret known to strangers,
Thus you brought me to ruin.
You grappled with me, then turned away,
You scorched my poor soul.
You made all your deeds dark as night,
You darkened my heart.
You did it not once but a thousand times,
Why did you stir up so much strife?
You bound my fortune,
I was a warrior, I was a lion,
Yet you broke my spirit again and again.
You drained so much of my strength,
You so often drove my soul to fury,
Starting trouble every day,
Together you brought me to ruin.
My anger rises and I stiffen,
I will not leave any one of you unchallenged,
In the desert of Batkalash,
On the edge of It-Zhürbös, where no dog goes,
I barely escaped with my life.
I owe no vengeance for your father,
Yet you have fought so many such battles,
Swaggering though your strength fails,
You black-faced wretch, until I take revenge on you,
Until I throw you from your horse,
If I do not take you then,
You paid no heed to my words,
You made me a neglected suitor,
You must have loved making me forlorn,
You tracked my every step,
And chased me until you grew pale,
On the plain of Zhelpinish,
I slew whoever came upon me,
Without saying what I had to say,
I went away without taking myself [unclear],
Here I was made to feel neglected,
You are false to everyone born,
You are a soul burned by grief,
Such grief has come over you.
