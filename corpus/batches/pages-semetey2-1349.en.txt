While my grown son was here,
I raised a quarrel and would not nurse him.
I gathered the people into a nation,
Tirelessly brought them together,
And laid the matter before them,
Saying blood had flowed from my breast.
His face shows another nature;
In the end he bears an enemy's malice.
He will burn your wealth and cast you out;
In his malice he will destroy you.
He will turn your spirits to water,
Ruin your camp and fill it with clamor.
Instead of one wrong, he will do a thousand;
He is full of conflict, corrupt within.
This villain will break your power.
His mottled nature will never disappear;
His corrupt disposition will not mend.
His roots are black with malice;
That accursed enemy is full of venom.
If this companion of yours grows strong,
In the end he will become an enemy.
Gathering the champions, I cried out,
"Put out Kanchoro's eyes!"
For many days I sobbed.
When I spoke, my son would not obey;
When I lamented, he would not heed me.
Semetey, born a mighty beast,
Was perfect in every way;
The gray wolf's heart, if you could see it,
Was broader than a lake or river.
When his mother spoke, he glared;
He would not consent or care.
My words, spoken in lament,
He valued less than his boot-tip.
He refused to hear my words
And put me to shame.
He disregarded my counsel
And caused me bitter grief:
"The boy who is my support—
How can you tell me to seize and kill him?
Alas, Mother, what are you saying?
The companion who is my kinsman—
You tell me to seize and slaughter him outright!
He is only one unruly boy.
When a single boy proves wayward,
Unfortunate Mother, what are you saying?
Will the people panic and perch on rocks?
Will one lone boy become an enemy
And devour the whole world?
Will anyone prosper who sees
One infant child as a foe?
Black-hearted Mother, come to no good—
Can what you say be right?
Is there anyone who would slaughter
The nobleman's only hope?
Is there anyone who would kill
The khan's only son and support?
You are my mother in dark times,
My fortress above my head.
Do not twist a ruined affair;
Do not choke with rage, fierce Mother.
Do you mean to corrupt me?
Ah, my valiant, unfortunate mother,
How many monstrous words you speak!
How can I seize and slaughter
The boy who serves as my support?
How can we put to death
The kinsman who is our protection?
Unfortunate Mother, what are you saying?
Who knows whether the child in the cradle
Will one day become a lord?
Who knows whether the child in the womb
Will one day become a khan?
Saying you knew an unborn child
Would someday become an enemy,
My troubled, suffering mother,
You have spoken without reason.
When I climb, you are my support;
