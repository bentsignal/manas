No soul could endure his judgment;
Life left them at the sound of his voice.
Khan Alöökö was incorrigible;
He had a fortress built
And installed his marvel within it.
He unleashed extraordinary turmoil and strife;
That Alöökö was incorrigible,
And no one could answer back
To Alöökö himself.
Again and again he seized people,
And then cast those
Who fell into his hands
Into his forty-rope-deep pit.
He delivered the Muslims' punishment
Into their own hands;
Many people were caught
In Alöökö's net.
The peoples of the world gathered;
Innocent common folk
Were persecuted and slaughtered.
When Alöökö brought suffering
And seized control of the people,
Hero Manas reached fifteen,
Having been born and raised in Altai.
Everything he undertook prospered;
He grew to maturity as a hero,
The equal of any hero's son.
He struck and opened a road
Through the stone house from which Alöökö ruled,
And brought Alöökö to humiliation.
When he trod upon
The lordly Alöökö's head,
He seized ten thousand treasures;
When he scattered them among the common people,
Alöökö, who was a sorcerer,
Fled toward Beijing.
Manas the leopard from afar
Placed grass upon his yellow trail,
Pursued him until he grew pale,
And greased his inlaid spear;
The infidel Alöökö
He laid low at Kum-Aryk.
When he saw the horses gathered as tribute
Were now booty,
He divided all that treasure,
Leaving nothing undistributed
Among all the Muslims.
The Muslims, clan by clan,
All shared it equally.
His heroism became known from this,
And the common people called him Manas.
At Kökötöi's feast,
Before the great gathering,
When they were all about to meet their end,
When the grim Black Chinese
Were about to engulf them entirely,
Was it not Manas
Who brought slaughter upon the whole world?
Was it not Manas who freed
The handful of Muslims
From the Chinese?
There is no end to the tale.
At Kökötöi's feast,
Before the great assembly,
He loaded gold coins on eighty camels
And formed a marriage alliance there.
With Dubaköi and the others at their head,
Was not the blessing given?
For the land of the Muslims,
Was not lion Manas then
A father?
Did he not bring
Relentless upheaval?
Did not lion Manas then
Win equality
For the Muslims?
