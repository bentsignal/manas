Sowing turmoil and strife.
However often I warned you, you would not change;
For as long as anyone remembers,
You have never ceased your violence.
As I searched for you through turmoil,
You came yourself, Sarybay;
The time has come to clash
And joust with me, Sarybay.
Eager to unleash another battle,
Your eyes straining wide,
You came yourself, Sarybay.
The time has come to strike unfalteringly with the sword
And stand amid the havoc,
The time has come, Sarybay.
Rousing the people and gathering an army,
Swelling your host to myriad ranks,
You came yourself, Sarybay.
Now the time has come for you to fall and die,
To behold the broad realm
From which none return,
The time has come, Sarybay.
Called the son of a demon,
Your fame spread among the people;
You subjugated the whole world,
And your violence overflowed.
I shall answer your violence;
My name is Kuyaly,
And I have come from afar to find you thus.
May your oath strike you—stand your ground!
If you are a hero and a warrior,
If you are brave Sarybay,
Do not lose your wits,
And do not flee from what lies ahead!”
So saying, Kuyaly
Gripped her deadly steel spear,
Twisting it firmly in her hand.
“The flesh of his breast is my mark,
The edge of his lung my measure;
His heart has fallen into my hand,
And this swaggering boar
Shall fall at this very spot,” she said,
And Kuyaly thrust her spear.
Then Sarybay,
Bellowing and roaring,
Bent down the spearhead.
His weapon was a steel battle-axe;
The demon-born infidel’s
Strength was formidable.
He seized the battle-axe
And, not letting the spear approach,
Beat it aside and held his ground.
When the deflected spear passed by
And Sarybay had escaped it,
Sarybay bristled with menace
And grew furious.
He overcame
Every danger before him.
Hissing like a dragon,
He roared threateningly;
His shout seemed to split heads,
And the raging boar’s
Bellow seemed to split stones.
His savage wrath poured forth,
His venom set him apart—
Ask no more of Sarybay’s state.
Raging and straining,
He made ready with leveled spear.
“You infidel who ruins nations!
You woman I shall seize!” he cried,
And raged in a frenzy,
While his host surged without rest.
He braced himself and took aim,
His mouth like a burning ember,
His valor fierce, his skill supreme,
His cunning deep, his power unmatched.
His heroism was extraordinary,
His strength beyond measure,
His menace great, his venom strong.
“At the sight of the infidel I am as good as dead,”
And he gave up hope for his fly-like soul.
