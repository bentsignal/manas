Kulchoro, son of Almambet,
Seemed ready to throw the age into turmoil.
He overturned a helmet of blue iron
And set it upon his head,
Crossing and tying
Its fastening under his throat.
He wore a coat of mail beneath.
If you look at the courage
Of Choro, son of Almambet,
He wore eighty layers
Of Kanykey's bulletproof armor.
Kulchoro had put on
The bulletproof armor,
The iron no bullet pierced.
He spread the armor across his shoulder blades
And donned the iron fully.
His eyes blazed like a blue tiger's;
He girded himself, saying, "I trust in God."
If death had come, he would die;
If he remained alive,
He would test himself
Against Toltoy of the Jediger.
If your gray-maned hero met him,
He was eager to face him and thrust his spear,
Eager to cut short
The suffering soul.
Toltoy of the Jediger
Would die if death had come.
Kulchoro was riding out
To test himself in destruction.
The mount beneath him
Was his uncle's tulpar,
Semetey's falcon, whose loss
Had caused strife.
There was an unhealed quarrel:
Saying he would take a wife,
Toltoy had begun a great tumult.
Such was the strange tale.
Made ready like a goshawk,
This was what he had desired:
An enemy to grapple had been found.
As for young Lord Choro,
The mount beneath him
Was his uncle's tulpar;
What he held in his hand
Was hero Syrgak's steel weapon.
"I submit my neck to the true faith;
Ah Almighty, support me!
I make holy war against Toltoy!"
Kulchoro rode out,
Raising the gray-piebald banner,
Heading toward Toltoy,
Calling the clamor of Khan Manas.
Raising the patterned spear,
Kulchoro rode out with this army.
Kanchoro, son of Chubak,
Would ride straight into
The brooding Jediger and thrust,
Fight to the finish for many days,
And make such a spectacle.
He would enter battle with the Jediger
And split its countless army in two,
Holding a spear and breaking formations;
If death came, he would fall to a bullet
And die at the Jediger's hands.
From the shrieking brass spear,
Across the broad ravine no bird could cross,
From the spear no foe escaped,
He said, "If my fate arrives, I will die
And become support
For my renowned lone uncle."
He readied himself like a goshawk
And strained like a leopard;
Counsel came from the unseen.
A man became a marvel;
Able youth and tulpar horse
Had reached the time to prove useful.
