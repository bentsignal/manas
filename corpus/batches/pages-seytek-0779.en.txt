“Summer days bring scorching heat,” they said,
“And when men grapple and fight,
It might suddenly break,” they said,
So they had it tempered in a shaded place.
“Those who take it will wage battle,” they said;
“When fierce combat and tumult arise,
The sword’s edge will strike,” they said,
So that no steel blade could cut it,
They bound it with white gold
And fashioned its many ridges.
When it came within reach
Of the holy man in your town,
When he cornered the one
Who had defied him,
He stretched and seasoned its shaft.
The patterned spear of your mighty champion—
Bölökbai and forty Kalmyks
Fashioned it with great distinction.
While they were making the spear,
A calf was slaughtered for the viewing,
And a whole forest was felled in the desert.
When the time came to temper it,
The black water boiled away to nothing.
When thrust bottomlessly at the foe,
Upon the head of whoever it struck,
Everything vanished in utter ruin.
They joined and fitted its point;
So that every one it struck
Might have his soul leave through his side,
They tempered it with tears.
Give me the splendid patterned spear,
The blue-flashing one, and let me take it;
Against the infidel son of the ogre
Let me clash and wage battle.
If true death reaches me, let me die;
Let me break the ribs
Of the enemy I truly fight.
Such is the manner of the struggle;
Let me take what I desire.
If hostile death arrives and my days are done,
Let me die without a single regret.”
Kuyaly, daughter of Kaiypchal,
Had turned Jeljetpes’s head,
And, saying, “They are the hero’s inheritance,”
She asked for the patterned spear and Zulfiqar
To be given to her as gifts.
Elder Bakai, revered as a prophet,
And the noble matron Kanykei—
Saints who had witnessed much;
For the ruler’s sake, for the khan’s sake,
Your great forefather Bakai
Spread his palms and sobbed:
“From the bullet of the savage foe,
Save the lineage of the magnanimous lion;
From the infidel’s cold hands,
From the pit dug in the earth,
O Creator who grants victory in holy war,
Save them from this dog Sarybai’s snare.
Let not the fleet courser Jeljetpes
Be tethered to his post.
Almighty sovereign God,
Do not let the ogre set his eyes
Upon the warrior lion’s offspring.
Amen, my child Kuyaly.
I was the hero Bakai,
Auspicious throughout the universe,
A renowned lion.
I was the servant who opened
The value of a market held in bondage.
I was the son who unbound the hands
Of the sorrowful held captive.
I was the hero who opened the road
Through calamity tightly sealed.
I was the lion who undid the curse
Of hardship tightly entangled.
Make Sarybai the one beneath;
God, make my child the one above.
My dear child Kuyaly,
Your strength is extraordinary;
A different calamity has arisen now.
