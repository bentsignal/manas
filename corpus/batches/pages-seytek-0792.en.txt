Bowing his massive head into his hands,
Ajybay of the Argyn khans
Humbled his khanly head
And sent an interpreter between them;
Through Janak I received the news.
Crossing passes and treading over stones,
Thinking deeply and grieving much,
Wearing out the gray racehorse,
Withering and aging my soul,
I journeyed forth utterly alone,
Folding up my coat of chain mail,
Exhausting my precious life
And wearing out every racehorse,
Turning them all and training them,
Until every provision was spent,
I traversed the entire world.
Through the world’s four corners
I traveled four full circuits.
When you wear out horses upon the road
And spend your blood to gain an answer,
You reach at last the appointed place.
I am an envoy who has come between you;
I understand human custom.
Among all mortals born with souls,
We live as mediators;
We travel throughout the world,
Heralds who range among the nations.
The valiant lion is Manas’s son;
Born after his father, gray-maned Manas,
He became a lion of a hero.
In the place where the world reaches its end,
A deep-rooted plane tree was established,
And from that root he came into being.
He is the point of white steel,
The descendant of noble Manas the lion.
He was named Semetey;
Know that he is a lion.
If you desire him, behold him;
Know the messenger
Whom he sent especially to you.
Do we not serve as envoys between you?
We sorrowful mortal creatures
Stand here as mediators.
So that the promise may be honored
And brought to fulfillment—
No human passes through the world alone—
Let her marry the lion Semetey.”
While I was delivering this message,
I looked and saw warrior Kuyaly.
When she was still a playful child,
She went riding for pleasure with eighty maidens.
While they enjoyed the hunt,
Chasing argali over the hills
And pursuing wild asses,
Out on the plain of Jelpиниsh,
Beside the great river,
In the marsh among the dense poplars,
While they were setting up a swing,
Barefoot and bareheaded,
A man named Kydyr appeared.
In the dream that closed her eyes,
He spoke of your gray-maned Semetey,
Describing his beauty thus:
“He wears a blue-lynx hat
And bears a blue-mottled shield;
He wears trousers with golden trim
And rides a steed larger than any beast.
He has a red-roan horse,
And honor surpassing all the ages.
His shoulders are broad and his stature tall,
Both lion and mighty hero—
No such man can arise among humankind.
His nose is like a Bukhara blade,
His beard like a quiver’s case,
And the breath issuing from his mouth
Is like flame blazing in a ravine.
Such shall your husband be,” he said,
