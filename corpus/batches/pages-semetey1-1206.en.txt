The finest of sixty,
Best of the forty companions,
Blade of black steel,
Syrgak of Khan Manas,
Rode up and fired once too,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
Yrchyuul, son of Yraman,
With forty tassels at his waistband,
Rode up and fired once too,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
Hero Shuutu in his tent-shaped kalpak
Came and fired once too,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
Kuldur and Chalybay
Arrived abreast and fired once,
Cutting through one tanap of cord.
Kyrgylchal, head of the forty,
Came and fired once too,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
The red-chested hero,
The valiant man who shook the age,
Bozuul came and fired once,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
Like a stack of white wheat,
He too was Manas's companion:
Merry Tazbaymat
Arrived and fired once too,
Cutting through one tanap of cord.
His jester Majik
Arrived and fired once too,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
Black Tologu the diviner,
Whose divination surpassed the people's,
Rode up and fired once too,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
Carefree Serek,
Useful to Khan Manas,
Arrived and fired once too,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
Manas had many companions;
Strongest of them all was Agayar.
When he grew angry,
He began a great tumult;
When his true wrath came,
He could strike down a camel.
Camel-lifting Agayar
Arrived and fired once too,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
Baychoro of the twin Olon
Rushed forward on Aksur,
Arrived and fired once too,
And cut through one tanap of cord.
The torment of this jamby
Spread throughout the world.
Only one cord of the jamby
Remained dangling.
His renown weighty, his name great,
His spirit lofty, an immense lion,
Who subdued many lands
And was created fortunate—
His name was lion Manas.
Generous Manas arrived;
The bloodletter came and fired once,
Cutting through the final cord.
Your hero brought down the jamby.
The forty companions of Khan Manas
Had staked Kokotoy's treasury
As the prize;
Your precious Manas won
The contest in motion.
When Manas won the jamby,
When all forty had reached
The worldly prize,
They shot down the jamby;
Thus the forty companions of Khan Manas
Came into their inheritance.
