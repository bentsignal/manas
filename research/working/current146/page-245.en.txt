The saint spread out his blessing.
This command gives the father to me.
Separated from the life held in trust,
He set the soles of his feet upon stone.
They burned Kenen beneath the sun.
No living soul remained among the captives.
Not one head of livestock remained at the station.
Our roots had not yet known sainthood.
Delivered into the hands of the faithless,
We reach the end in ruin.
Much humiliation and much lament,
We have endured in life.
When it flourished, we scattered it.
The day passed while we stood weeping.
We hurried away from the entrusted life.
Such was the state of his soul.
We froze in the cold and gave away our coats.
Saying “death,” we yielded our hands.
They brought his lips to food.
They clothed him for saddle and place of honor.
A form arose from what remained true.
Making the hero known,
They set it straight for the one who did not descend.
They sent him standing against the enemy.
They made him understand as though he knew nothing.
Thus they sent him into the enemy.
Remain as an elder brother.
We have seen wrath born of blood.
We are gray-maned lions.
Poor hero Kenen,
Immense one, spirit of God,
Many fists beat his restless wings.
They laid hands upon the khan’s daughter.
Her tongue, sweet as sugar, faltered.
The bosom of the cruel day,
These were brave Kenen’s words,
Before the one seated in the middle.
