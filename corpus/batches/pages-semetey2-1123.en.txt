They dismounted and ran to him.
The reins knotted into loops
They placed around their necks;
The great mass of black-clad Jediger
Begged him to spare their lives.
The people clamored in uproar;
It was a grievous conflict.
As they were destroyed, the people cried out,
Despairing of their wretched lives:
“This terrible calamity, this great conflict,
Will slaughter us all!”
Despairing of their wretched lives,
Some would not remain still
But rushed about in panic;
Some wounded Jediger
Fled toward the mountains.
Dust rose and haze covered the land;
Calamity filled the world.
They scattered in every direction,
And while they milled about in distress,
Külchoro proved a true falcon,
Waging a wondrous battle.
From among all the Jediger,
Kadyrbay and Tölöbay—
Elders risen from the people,
Men of wisdom who knew the proper words,
True sages among them—
Kadyrbay hurried forth,
Dismounted and composed himself,
And there the falcon stood
Pleading with Külchoro:
“When we raid horses, our dust rises together;
Our roots are one, our lives are one.
Across the breadth of Jeti-Özön
The multitude lies dead—
Hero, what guilt does the common nation bear?
The whole mass of black-clad Jediger,
Every one of us, elders and all,
Was beaten and battered
And driven here together.
We encountered Kembay;
Each of us was brought here.
Forced to become an army,
Every one of us was driven here.
Is it not our khan who is a fool?
What guilt does the common nation bear?
Is it not our leader who is a fool?
What guilt do all the people bear?
A witless khan grows rabid;
May no khan swell with such madness.
Sowing discord among his people,
He turns ravenously upon his subjects.
A foolish khan raves,
Gathers the entire nation,
Brings misery upon his people,
And slaughters his subjects.
If you are a hero, if you are a lion,
Slay brave Kyyaz,
But restrain your anger.
Brave Kyyaz’s wild undertaking
And the turmoil that has shaken the nation
Are the villainy of Kanchoro.”
So Kadyrbay raised an uproar
And lamented bitterly:
“We shall not assail your khan;
Our seed and blood are one.
Ah, hero Külchoro,
We all submit to you;
Restrain your anger.
Do not destroy the innocent people.
Here are the cities and settled lands;
If you would spill blood, here we are.
Here is the ground where heroes lie;
If you would cut off heads, here we are.
If you will receive them, here are the people;
Do not kill everyone who comes.
Do not unleash the fury of battle;
Heed the words we have spoken.
