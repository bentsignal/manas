Jediger of Jeti-Özön,
You will have something to boast of.
Since you were born as Jediger,
Dig a well in the place of honor;
Scatter their four kinds of livestock.
Great black host of Jediger,
Move off in a dark mass along the ridge.
My people, thus avenge
Toltoy, who was your uncle and khan!
Slaughter every one
Of their elders who boasted of strength.
Seething Jediger,
Know that I have spoken thus.
My people, every one of you, heed
What your Khan Kanchoro has said.
Of this son-of-a-bitch warrior,
This oath-breaker who ruined the age,
Slaughter many and take your plunder!
Cut down his plane trees and lay them low;
Kick down his walls.
Plunder and take his gold;
Raise merciless turmoil.
Draw bows and make arrows rain;
Shoot other missiles like hail.
Exert your strength without mercy,
Level his capital to the ground,
And sack it completely.
Bring slaughter upon his people.
Come now, Jediger, do not stand idle;
Take your positions on either side.
Fire your guns and draw your bows;
Fight with blades flashing.
Let bowstrings and arrows thrum,
Let the places they strike burst apart,
Let spilled blood run in streams.
Hack through and hollow out
A place as broad as a ship.
If any soul dies in the fighting,
Bury him in his clothes.
Such is the way of battle;
Thus feast upon the plunder.
Break apart their fine goods;
Bring ruin upon them in earnest.
If his guards resist,
Drag them along the road
And hand them over to the Dangyt!
Slaughter every able man who guards the gate;
Leave none alive.
Great black host of Jediger,
Plunder the warrior-khan's treasury;
Do not leave a thing!
Let dust fly to the heavens;
Let troops boil up in dusty masses.
Let spearheads clash,
And human heads collide!”
So Kanchoro spoke
And issued his command
To plunder the treasury.
He was working a monstrous outrage,
A great and merciless calamity.
Crested banners filled the ranks;
The clamor split the sky.
When Kanchoro commanded,
“Break the horde and take plunder!
Attack them with a roar!”
Choro issued his decree.
Ah, this luckless Choro—
The hair on his crown bristled,
His eyes truly blazed with fire;
His broad front teeth, large as a gate,
Teeth unlike those of any other soul,
Ground together with a loud crunch.
Kanchoro raised the battle cry.
Not one soul remained upright;
The great black host of Jediger
Began to claw at the earth.
As the Jediger stood thus,
Choro emerged in a frenzy.
