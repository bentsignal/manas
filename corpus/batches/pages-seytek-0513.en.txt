And came to tell the champions.
Those ill-fated were captured;
Some champions whose death had not come
Barely managed to escape.
Sarybai struck the doomed
With mace and bow;
The fortunate pressed onward,
While the wretched fled back.
Brave hero Seytek was there,
Facing them and speaking.
The vast army grieved,
Despairing of their fly-like lives.
Their bodies went limp with sobbing;
Unable to regain their senses,
The great host stood clamoring.
The war drum thundered firmly;
Within the army Külchoro
Swept forward like a spirit,
Finding counsel where none seemed possible.
The trumpets blared in excitement,
And his trumpeter grew fervent.
Throwing every soul into alarm,
He made the gray horse beneath him
Stride forward at a stately pace,
And leveled his steel weapon.
He swallowed up the countless host
That had panicked helplessly.
His lashes became leaping flame,
His eyes blazed like embers.
He gnashed his teeth with a crack,
His bloodthirsty visage boiling fiercely.
Flaring with wrath,
He addressed the great army:
“Damn you, mighty host!
You are lions and tigers,
Imposing warriors,
Who came here seeking battle!
But when battle begins, you lose your heads
And flee in terror—
If you act so, people, you are dead!
Those who escaped death
Raised a clamor and fled back.
Some champions are wounded,
While some remain wholly unharmed.
Some champions lost their nerve,
Were soundly pierced by spears,
And fled without finding a way out.
May God never show us his face!
Sarybai is a horror,
They say he turned the army from the hills;
They say he slaughtered without a word
All whom he encountered.
None can overcome him in combat,
No lion’s teeth can pierce him;
His form is strange, his venom strong.
If you encounter Sarybai,
Abandon hope of life and count yourselves dead!
He is fire incarnate,
The eye of a devouring fiend,
The very red essence of disaster.
We have seen Sarybai’s form:
He is like a champion who ruins nations,
Driving men like helpless prey,
Rending them like a hungry wolf.
It is as though he gathered the whole world
And stuffed it into a single sack.
Almighty Allah creates
His servants in every fashion.
His two cheeks hang loose,
As though two giants had feasted.
He turns all before him into desert,
As though gulping them down whole.
No vast host can advance against him;
None who fights him can survive.
Black-browed like a recumbent hound,
As though vultures roosted upon him,
He gnaws whoever meets him,
As though swallowing them whole—
Such is Sarybai’s appearance.
