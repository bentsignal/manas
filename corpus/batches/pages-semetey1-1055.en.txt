They went to wage war, they say;
With Zhanaaly and Karach at their head,
All of the great army
Was slaughtered, they say.
Finding the Argyn and Kyrgyz people,
Finding a land like Talas,
Yesterday, when the boy came to Talas,
His blue-lynx and sable cap
Leaped as he dismounted, I heard.
When he was about to settle in Talas,
'Aychürök is my wife,
And Khan Akun is my father-in-law,'
He sang as he dismounted, I heard.
When his anger was provoked,
He slaughtered forty companions, they say;
When they saw his might,
The whole world fell
Into the boy's hands, they say.
Azhybay, who went about lying,
Is known for telling lies.
She has a father-in-law in a formidable place;
Aychürök, daughter of Akun—
The reason I could not seize and give her is this:
Calamitous Chürök
Has one who girded his waist for her,
Has a place where he lies fortified,
And has a husband in such a perilous land.
If we two go out to face him,
The lion Semetey, rather than us,
Has the power to throw
The whole world into turmoil;
She has such a commanding gray-wolf husband.
Aychürök, daughter of Akun—
My Kozho, if you live, you will see:
She will bring great turmoil upon the world.
Sheer cliff descending into the valley,
Fiery and bold Chürök;
When she assumes the form of a swan,
Only Friday night knows where she flies.
Who knows where
Calamitous Chürök may be!
Unruly and raging Chürök,
The turmoil born among mortals,
Born never to fall into a man's snare,
Never to fall into anyone's hands—
Aychürök was born
To be the Afghans' sorrow.
The people gathered in fascination
And longed for Chürök.
Saying, 'I shall conquer her today,'
You brought turmoil upon Afghanistan.
Do not roar, Chynkozho;
Hold your army back for now.
I shall seize Chürök and give her to you;
You yourself must bear
The turmoil that follows behind her.
I have explained it plainly;
Here is how the matter stands.
Listen, Chynkozho:
She has a father-in-law in a turbulent land.
You committed violence many times;
It is true that I did not consent.
You spoke many lying words;
It is true that, fearing the end of it,
I did not give Chürök.
You committed many kinds of violence;
It is true that I did not consent.
Find wisdom and devise a plan;
If you find wisdom, Chynkozho,
Choose among women and take my masterwork.
You have come desiring her expressly;
Take my daughter Aychürök!
Together with the maiden, Chynkozho,
Take my red-tailed knowledge;
I am one whose soul feared turmoil—
Understand what I have said!"
Kozho stood surging with fury,
Lashing the earth with his whip,
