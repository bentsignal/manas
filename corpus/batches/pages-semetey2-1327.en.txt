There are noble brides and beautiful girls
Beyond your power to carry off.
Drive seven thousand red camels,
And braid the camels' tails.
See this fine spectacle
Provided by Chubak's son Choro!
Six hundred thousand horses graze the hills,
Fine piebald steeds,
Thoroughbreds and racers mingled together.
There are countless swift ponies;
In Talas there is no owner
Besides me to receive them.
Leaders of tens and hundreds,
Leaders of fifties and thousands,
Divide them and return home;
Say, ‘My khan gave us aid,’
And offer thanks to me.
Divide among yourselves as booty
The countless scattered gold coins;
Go tell your homeland,
‘Kanchoro performed a heroic deed.’
Without quarreling or struggling,
Find a way to divide
The hero-khan's vast wealth.
Call me your hero and your lion;
O Jediger, understand this!
Observe the hero's leonine courage
And always remember it!
Snorting in excitement,
Suddenly raising a battle cry,
No people can withstand me,
No charging foe can come near,
No enemy can defeat me in battle.
When a hero stands at the front,
Can any man with a thumb attack?
Can any clawed enemy touch you?
Can a cold-blooded foe advance?
While your valiant Choro lives,
My forearm is thick, my heart is stone,
My body broad and powerful.
While your Choro stands watch,
No one is swept away in the flood;
Jediger, death cannot touch you.
There is no boat upon black water,
But while your Khan Kanchoro lives,
Jediger, death cannot touch you!
In Talas, slaughter the livestock
Of the Kyrgyz and Argyn and hold a feast;
Slaughter their heroes and wreak havoc.
Make the gateway run with blood,
And turn the black road to dust.
Make widows of their beauties,
And slaves of their princes;
Their wise old men—
Smash and kill them all!
Break apart their yurt frames and poles,
And make their brides and daughters weep.
Take their old folk as plunder;
Lay waste to them all.
Make their rulers and lions suffer,
And crush the people of the steppe.
Cast cold lassos
Over the steeds they would never surrender;
Inflict open humiliation
Upon flourishing Talas.
Bring misery upon its beks;
Advance upon these black-hearted people
And plunder them.
My great people, consider this:
A khan's threshold is the Day of Judgment,
And one must speak with care;
A hero's threshold is solid iron,
And one needs skill to melt it.
I have given the order—understand it,
You many Black Jediger;
Obey my word and do not anger me!”
Thus Kanchoro spoke,
That retainer faithless to the salt he had eaten,
