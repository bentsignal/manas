When I led the army out this way,
I, your aged uncle Koshoy,
Came chewing and eating rib fat.
If the leopard Manas is found,
Not a governor's, but a khan's
Khanate I came to take.
When the whole people came out this way,
I came slicing and eating horse rump.
If cheerful Manas is found,
I came to destroy the governor-general.
Whether lying or standing, my wish—
If the generous one is found alive,
My onslaught will be worth the world!
The black spear, Zulpukor,
I mean to wield them together.
If the leopard comes into my hands,
Not merely the Chinese, but the whole world,
I mean to grapple with it.
