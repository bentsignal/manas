There is another people equal to the Kyrgyz:
There are the renowned Oygut people,
And their blessed khan;
Dwelling along the Lop River,
There is also one called Karacha.
Tell him to come to my feast
And see this spectacle!
If he does not come to my feast,
If he does not see this spectacle,
Tell him to behold Kökötöy's red banner with its golden crescent
From atop his own house!
Tell him to braid his daughters' hair
Into five forelocks!
When you travel onward from there,
Turning toward the sunset,
There is a city named Khotan.
There is Jügörü of Khotan,
Another great champion.
There is God's wrath in its water;
Do not weaken from drinking its water.
To Jügörü of Khotan,
Tell this news:
For Kökötöy's memorial feast,
Let him tether his swift horses
To bring and race;
Let him prepare his champions
To come and wrestle.
For my father's memorial feast,
I will slaughter a thousand black male camels;
I will slaughter a thousand black blaze-faced mares.
I will stake possessions of solid gold.
Give Jügörü my greetings;
Tell him to come to my feast
And see this spectacle!
If he does not come to my feast,
If he does not see this spectacle,
Tell him to behold Kökötöy's countless host
From atop his own house.
As booty for Bokmurun,
Tell him to braid his daughters' hair
Into five forelocks!
Tell him to come quickly,
Without making strife or quarrel!
On the left-hand side,
At the edge of the endless desert,
Among the rough, broken ridges,
Upon a gaping promontory,
There is the famed land of Yarkand.
Dwelling in that Yarkand
Is the campaigning champion.
Tell Khan Zhortuulchu to gather his people
And come;
Let him not leave his old men behind,
And let him bring his children riding
As usual upon their packsaddles.
Skirting the hot desert,
Let him come properly to my feast,
Having shod his tulpars.
Tell him to come to my feast
And see this spectacle.
If he does not come to my feast,
If he does not see this spectacle,
I will raid and destroy his people;
I will leave his donkey braying in the ravine.
If he fails to come in his turn,
This is how I will settle his account.
Tell every people you see without fail.
When you travel farther,
Covering ground without sparing your horse,
In the city of Wide Urgench,
In the villages of broad Afghanistan,
Tell their Khan Akun,
And tell all the peoples you see there.
Say that Bokmurun, Kökötöy's son,
Has camped at Üch-Karkyra;
Say that Kökötöy's great wealth
Has filled the face of the earth;
