Rejoicing in Kulansur,
The hero among heroes, the noble warrior,
Was well pleased there.
Having received a letter from Akbermet,
And a blessing from the aged lion,
Akjoltoy, whom he sought,
Had ridden out, following the trail.
The heroes raced and journeyed on,
Traveling an immense distance
With boundless, matchless delight.
The restless satisfied their longing,
The pounding heart grew calm,
And every sorrow scattered.
A clamor split the sky,
A roar of shouting rose;
They could not simply ride without racing,
And spared their lives for none of its thrills.
Their cries rang out in tumult;
So they continued on their way,
Covering ground without stint.
The horses’ brows grew bright,
And the heroes’ longing was relieved.
Seytek rode apart in front,
His heroic bearing revealed,
Scouting ahead and surveying the land—
The noble warrior who never flinched,
The noble hero proud as a cock.
Then he happened to meet
His elder kinsman, grey-wolf Joltoy.
At that time Akjoltoy
Was on the slope of a grey hill;
He had stripped the saddle from Kulunsur
And tethered him securely.
The noble hero, calm and unhurried,
Pillowed his head on his white saddle,
Turned himself toward the qibla,
And prayed to the Creator.
Wrapping himself in his bulletproof coat,
He prepared himself for sleep
And slept propped upon his elbow.
Resting on his side, he eased his soul,
And lay there sleeping his fill;
The peerless grey wolf Akjoltoy
Had fallen into a giant’s sleep.
When grey-wolf Seytek arrived,
His outstretched legs drew in,
His snoring ceased,
And the grey wolf stirred;
His eyes opened wide as bowls.
Rising like a white tiger,
He woke from that sleep,
Joyfully crying, “Baabedin!”
His heroic nature became clear;
He put on all his garments,
Donned his blue lynx-fur cap,
And took his blue-mottled shield and spear.
He looked like a towering blue mountain,
And stretched like a hungry lion.
Telling what he had seen, he began to speak:
“Child Seytek, my leopard,
Quickly invoke Baabedin
And call your horse Moon-Hoof.
The reason you must call your horse is this:
Your destined match has come and camped here;
The road you need has opened before you.
A traveler has camped along the road,
And a path has opened for your campaign.
Have you seen what I have seen?
Have you learned what I know?
Leopard blessed with surpassing fortune,
Mark my words carefully.
Leopard, the road you desired
Has brought you fortune from the unseen.
Your wish has been granted:
The radiant Akbermet
Has come seeking you directly.
