“Let us mount stallions chosen from among beasts
And drive them across every region.
Let us cast hell into their ranks
And strike them down without pause.
Let us act for the sake of our people.
Against the swine who fixed his hatred upon us,
Let us wage a fierce campaign.
Let us reach all seven divisions
And engage them in battle.
Let us search the baggage of the infidel
Who came seeking us, the warrior who ravaged the people,
Down to his very last bundle.
Let us uproot his tent poles
And unleash slaughter outright.
Let us fight to the death,
With red blood flowing,
Tearing and hacking one another apart,
Abandoning these lives and dying.
Let us go into the fray;
How can we lie grieving
Now that Sarybay has come?
We shall cast off humiliation like sheep.
If he overruns the encampment,
Warrior, it will seem that we were afraid.
Let us unleash a dreadful conflict.
Against the infidel whose strength is full,
Against the warrior who came desiring
Battle, turmoil, and devastation,
Let us march to holy war!
If hostile death arrives, let us die;
Let us test ourselves in combat.”
So spoke Külchoro,
And when the champion said these words,
They agreed, accepting them as true.
All the weak-hearted grieved,
But all the champions
Rejoiced at the word of battle.
They donned their bulletproof coats,
And girded on their finely tempered steel.
They arranged all their weapons,
Preparing to scatter the four kinds of livestock,
To bring disaster upon the people,
And to put forward without exception
Their cherished men and chiefs.
They meant to raid the ravines and seize possessions,
Burn the narrow valleys and drive off the horses,
To overtake those not yet claimed by death,
And carry away as spoils
The village beauties and widowed brides.
They fixed their hatred and prepared,
Equipping themselves with weapons.
From the desert of El-Zhürbös
And the pass of At-Baspas,
They encountered the Black Giant.
Hostile death overtook the Black Giant;
He clashed with them and was struck down,
And thus the Black Giant died.
The warriors who remained
Came fleeing in terror.
“Sarybay has slaughtered the people,” they said,
“He has brought upon us
Every strife and great conflict that accursed one knows.”
They abandoned all their possessions,
And many fled in panic.
The news had only just arrived,
And the renowned men gathered.
When they beheld Sarybay’s fury,
Some did not engage him in battle,
Nor stand against him face-to-face.
The hearts of some men
Utterly failed, and they died.
These very eyes beheld their deaths.
However fearsome Sarybay may be,
Let us overwhelm him and unleash battle.
Let us go and bar the road
Of that arrogant swine.
If he is a champion, let us fight him,
Strip to the waist and spill blood,
And stand amid the desperate struggle.
