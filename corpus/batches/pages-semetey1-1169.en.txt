"Take the Burut by the legs;
Lay him out full length," they said.
The Black Kalmyks said this,
While the Muslim faithful cried:
"In your uncle's old age,
May God grant his wish;
May He put the Kalmyk beneath,
And your uncle above," they said.
The Muslim people cried out.
At that very moment,
Khan Koshoy of Katagan
Tugged at the broad belt
Of Joloy, khan of the Kalmyks.
Where your uncle made the throw,
He drove his chest
Into Kalmyk Khan Joloy.
Unable to stop the throw,
Joloy stumbled away;
On the yellow hard ground
His feet could find no hold.
The infidel's bare foot
Wedged into a hollow.
Upon that huge brute,
Your uncle Koshoy then
Fell pressing down.
"Koshoy has thrown Joloy!" they cried,
The forty companions of Khan Manas.
All flew like arrows
And converged upon them.
Among the forty companions
Was the champion named Agayar;
When he grew angry,
He began a great tumult,
And when truly enraged,
He could strike down a camel.
Before one could turn about,
In the blink of an eye,
Where infidel and Muslim
Were seething together,
Champion Agayar arrived.
He shouldered your uncle Koshoy
And ran away with him.
The strongest jacket-clad Chinese
Roared,
And the finest stone-bedecked Kalmyks
Thundered.
Among the Kalmyks was Ylama,
An interpreter for the Kalmyks—
Do not ask what that interpreter said:
"The accursed Buruts'
Best man invited us to the feast;
What is this he has done?
He has apparently been left
To put on bulletproof armor;
This accursed Burut
Has apparently been left to attack us.
There is pressing down, they say,
But no stepping over the head.
There is lifting and throwing, they say,
But no kicking in the chest.
There was permission to throw a man,
But none to kick him in the groin.
At the place where belts are girded,
At the place where contests are held,
This accursed Burut—
What has he done?"
Bridling as though to attack,
The Black Chinese and Manchu host
All surged forward together;
The finest stone-bedecked Chinese
Were carried away with rage.
Those deftest with spears,
Those strongest with battle-axes,
Advanced with a rumble,
As though to engulf the Kyrgyz
And leave slaughter behind.
