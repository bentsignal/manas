The elders came from the far bank.
The few who arrived sat down.
The gold fell into the embers.
The many who arrived were separated.
The fire fell into his breast.
Now strike, mighty falcon, amid the tumult.
Upon the hard road of doomsday,
He remained in the Creator’s hand alone,
Within God’s iron net,
Solely in the hand of death.
Turning wherever he could,
Vast-hearted hero Kenen,
Separated together from his people,
Endured much of this world.
His intent was dark and his heart poisonous.
He was trampled by the true enemy.
The gray-maned heroes entered the holy war.
Brave Kuman, companion of the soul,
Spoke his challenge and judged it firmly.
The orphan was beyond the wind’s reach.
No farming soul could pass your horse.
His eye gazed from afar.
Many rode out beyond the wind’s reach.
From delight beyond the eye’s reach,
He took a spear from the gray-maned heroes.
He was bound with no sheath remaining.
Behind you stood a lofty soul.
The worth of the slippery ones arrived.
He became khan over three peoples.
When he spoke world upon world of words,
A coin spun, beyond the wind’s reach.
The pattern stood upright.
What cast many stones outran the wind.
His soul belonged to the distant land.
