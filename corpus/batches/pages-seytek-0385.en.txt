Külchoro will take your life,
Hero Seytek will spill your blood,
Kanchoro, you beast-like fool,
You have courage but no sense,
You care about nothing,
In all the world no fool was born
To equal you.
A stone will strike your temple,
A spear will pierce your side,
A lasso will catch your steed;
Standing thus, he will shoot—
This kinsman of yours, Külchoro,
Not your kinsman, but your foe,
And the regret will be your own.
You act upon a fool’s words;
When trouble rises behind you,
You will say, “Ah, had I not obeyed a woman!”
Braggart warrior Kanchoro,
You will bite your finger in regret!
You will bring torment on yourself,
Your wake will be turmoil and uproar,
Aichürök will suffer much plunder
And face calamity many times.
You will be left shaking your head;
A mighty upheaval, a great conflict,
You yourself will choose.
If you regard this slave as your own,
My blood is not like his;
You will go before your God.
If you make him your kinsman,
He will rush headlong into action,
Your affairs will flare up and collapse.
Until he grows fat he will fawn,
Then rage and prance;
One day he will turn against you,
And blood will stream from your neck.
Your kindness will become his strength,
And bring disaster on your head!
His temper will sour and he will bare his teeth,
He will turn on you in wrath,
His fury will exceed all bounds;
When this wretch reaches full rage,
He will seize you by the throat.
He will study your bearing,
Then, inflamed, provoke your anger;
He will confront you openly,
And your misery will drive you mad.
He will take your people from you,
And cast your affairs into ruin.
He will smash your mouth,
And knock your teeth clean out.
He will make sport of your people,
And bend your back time after time.
He will not heed a word you say,
But strike your eyes from their sockets;
If opposed, he will bind your giant,
And seize your mares as spoil.
If you begin to wheedle him, saying,
“My own blood brother has become lord,”
He will punch your brains out.
Thus he will truly destroy
Whatever wits you have.
When that madman surges forth,
Will he not bring hell upon your host?
Will he not take revenge without stopping?
Will he not overturn my mountain
And raise a riotous uproar?
Will he not rain down volleys of arrows?
Will he not seize and occupy the White Palace?
He will break the strength you girded,
And utterly crush your people.
If you are a lion, if you are a tiger,
If you are truly my wedded lord,
Hear my words, my khan:
In the end Külchoro will gouge out your eyes!
Hero Seytek will pursue you;
If Kanykey and Aichürök still live,
They will bring your sickness to a head.
If your upstart mounts the golden throne,
Bakai will split your crown.
