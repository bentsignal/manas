Cross the ford for me without delay;
These words I speak in counsel—
Bring me news without delay!
Go and engage them in conversation;
Ask about the condition
Of the falcon that went astray.
Ride out and reconnoiter the road;
Quickly bring back an answer
About the falcon that disappeared.
Will she agree to what you say?
Give my greetings to my brothers-in-law,
Ajybay and Tumenbay;
Will they give their sister?
Take six swift horses.
From the great khan, radiant as moonlight,
Who once received a palm-sized letter,
If he honors the spirits of old,
Tell him to abide by the ancient word;
Tell him to give an answer
About the falcon that disappeared.
Bring back a good answer,
Reconnoiter each stretch of the road.
That renowned Afghanistan—
Is Akun Khan a noble soul?
If Akun Khan has unexpectedly grown old,
Are all the numerous Afghans
People who would open fire?
If Akun Khan is a heroic khan,
Does he remember the pledged betrothal?
My matched pair of heroic companions,
You two companions, twinlike and equal as hairs,
Go safely and return unharmed.
Whichever one of you goes, go now;
Return quickly and do not keep me waiting.
If you set out mounted on a thoroughbred,
Keep the thoroughbred's mouth reined.
When she appears upon the open plain,
Give greetings and blessings
To Churok, daughter of Akun.
My Kanchoro, son of Chubak,
Like iron drawn and tempered,
I used to think you would become blue steel,
Burning everything in the direction you faced;
I used to think you would be fierce.
Your high brow was awe-inspiring,
And your radiance surpassed that of men.
To mount Kokborchuk,
Tire a horse and reconnoiter the land,
And travel far upon a journey;
To scout properly
The thirty daughters-in-law and thirty maidens,
And go obtain news
From beautiful Churok at her games—
How well suited are you for this, wrestler?"
Kanchoro stood stubbornly
And now answered in defiance.
Saying plainly, "I will not go,"
God now struck the poor man:
"I will not casually cross and scout the ford;
I have no skill with daughters-in-law.
While claiming to scout the crossing,
I will not fall into disgrace.
I will not scout your road along the cliffs;
I have no skill with maidens.
While claiming to scout the cliff road,
I will not fall into crime this way.
I have no words to find and say to a maiden;
I have no skill with women."
He said plainly, "I will not go."
God had taken hold of the young man;
The gray wolf born surpassing all men
Felt a little disappointment
In a young man like Kanchoro.
Turning away, he said this,
And your thoroughbred lord looked aside.
