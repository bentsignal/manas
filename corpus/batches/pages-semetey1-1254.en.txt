Firing guns and drawing bows,
They fought in clashing combat,
Dying when their death arrived,
Meeting when all recourse was gone,
Tearing at one another's muscles,
Fighting without turning aside.
A great slaughter was about to occur;
The strong who boasted of their might
Were about to consume the powerless.
These two sides remained poised
For a terrible slaughter.
The Afghan champions
Encountered the Zhediger
And now began the battle.
They had driven them behind them
Because of their desire for Aichürök:
The Afghans saying, "We will not give Chürök,"
And Toltoi of the Zhediger saying,
"I shall reach and seize Chürök,
Even if she has gone into the earth."
His countless army made the earth sag.
Chynkojo, son of Shygai,
Kojo, vigorous in his deeds,
Was Kojo who walked in corrupt strife.
Kojo was a mountainlike hero,
One of the damned.
He wickedly incited Toltoi,
Had him drive his great people,
And was the hero who brought them.
He engulfed them by force;
The slaughter of the great people
In the Afghan city
Was Chynkojo's calamity.
Six forefathers before him had been khans;
His strength reached across the world.
Manas and Chynkojo
Were sons of brothers;
Chynkojo's wound
Would not heal in all the world.
Dear old Tölök
Quarreled over what did not exist;
Because of Chynkojo,
All the people were being slaughtered.
He led the Zhediger.
Their lord named Chynkojo,
Who had fled from Talas,
Said he would seize
The daughter-in-law of bloodthirsty Sultan Manas—
Such was this infidel's corruption.
Chynkojo, son of Shygai,
And their elder leader Tölöbai
Had then driven
All the Zhediger.
Kojo, son of Shygai,
Was born a hero from the hearth,
Surpassing many souls.
He was born equal in standing
To oceanlike hero Manas.
Such was Kojo's condition:
They were paternal kinsmen of equal rank.
Look at his savage conduct
Toward lion hero Manas—
Seeds of one wheat,
Villages sharing one threshold,
Their grandfathers closely united,
Descendants from the same tip,
Offspring from the same root,
Sheep sharing one pen,
Companions sharing one cauldron and meal,
One in blood and heart,
One in courage and arm.
Kojo forgot the spirit
Of Manas, gone to the other world,
And attacked the in-laws
Of the princely son of Manas;
He drove forth an upheaval
And slaughtered the great people.
