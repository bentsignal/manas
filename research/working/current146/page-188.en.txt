If the browed maral did not yield,
If his steadfast soul found no rest,
If his livestock did not tire on the rough ground,
If not a handful of my order remained,
If they tested him, saying, “Enter the wrestling,”
No enemy had a fist redder with blood.
What kind of day was this for the Kyrgyz?
Unless the Chinese hero surrendered him to the foe,
The unyielding armored warrior,
The wind could not overtake the Kyrgyz champion.
He attacked an enemy as vast as China.
It was the Kyrgyz hour of action.
Broad-souled hero Kenen,
He split whatever he faced.
The countless host beyond all reckoning,
He ranged through it from end to end.
Without retreating, he thrust his spear.
He was drenched in bloody gore.
Then he remained in his place.
He ranged about, calling for the orphan.
He speared everyone he overtook.
He hammered the glowing iron.
No wind could overtake his passing course.
He made his station known,
And devastated everything in confusion.
Kaanak’s dense host,
He cast into turmoil and destroyed.
Without becoming infirm, they broke through,
And dismounted to contend on foot.
Though orders came by the thousand,
Such was the fist that guarded the pass.
When a dense host of thousands stood,
When the great battle was fought.
