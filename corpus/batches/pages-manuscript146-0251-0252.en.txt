The leopard surged and whispered.
Clouds broke apart in the clear sky.
Driven by the wind, they changed shape.
Day and night the wind drove on.
Tongues were bound as misery rose.
The heroes endured their span of life.
Their livestock remained, and their heads were safe.
God ordained Tagasha’s course straight.
Between the two they came upon a pasture.
When he looked, they waited while driving the livestock.
They made their living and winnowed grain.
The place where they settled was here.
