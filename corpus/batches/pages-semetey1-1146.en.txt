Upon Beijing of Kakanchyn,
God's seed was scattered.
Seed poured upon Beijing;
His Holiness Ali the lion
Turned back from great Beijing.
There is no great faith here.
Truly, God favored us;
We have remained in their midst.
Such filth is this world;
The breast holds many sorrows.
We have come and attacked a numerous people;
Such is this treacherous false world.
Of the sons of Muslims,
Exactly a thousand souls remain.
Taking wives from the Chinese,
There remains Turkan;
If you ask, you will find him,
The true Muslim Turdana.
If we lament, great sorrow comes forth;
If you ask, you will find
True Kyrgyz descendants
In Beijing of Kakanchyn,
A people called Salaalyk.
Calling it Kökötöy's feast,
We have come to the feast;
If quarrels arise, we have come
To meet what falls upon our heads.
We have come to see
Kökötöy's festive feast;
We have come to be plundered
And die at the hands of the Black Chinese.
We have come to give our inheritance
To the Enchi-Kuyun Kalmyks.
Heeding Köpkölöng's words,
We have come here to die.
While my beard is white as a swan,
While my journey's end is near,
God has struck the Burut,
For they stand trembling against us.
When my time has passed,
God has struck the Chinese,
For they stand opposing us.
The swaggering Kalcha, the formidable one—
If his strength suffices, these Chinese
Have the power to fight.
Among the seething infidels,
With heroic Khan Kongurbai,
There are those who know no human tongue,
Whose words resemble beasts' cries.
There is a mountain-like cyclops
Who crushes anyone beside him.
They have heroes of many kinds
And revenge for their fathers.
If they find the chance,
They have reason to fight us.
The bellowing infidels
Have armor no bullet pierces;
Think of their dagger-men.
The infidels came in strength,
Their waists girded with resolve.
They have heroes held in readiness,
Ready to cause disaster
And confront us at this feast.
Without quarreling or disputing,
Let us consent to their demand
And present them Maaniker.
Let us deceive them with the horse;
Let us find this solution.”
Your uncle Koshoy honked loudly
And offered these words.
After him Bokmurun
Joined in and said:
“They gathered and came to my feast,
And struck my head with a club;
They trampled my wishes
And used force at my feast.
