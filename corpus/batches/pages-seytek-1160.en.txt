Sight faded and night fell,
As he crossed the black ridge.
There Joltoy stopped to camp.
From beneath him he took Kulunsur,
Removed the saddle and left him bare-backed,
Hobbled him securely,
Then sank into a giant’s sleep.
Using saddle and saddlecloth as a pillow,
He prayed to the Creator.
His snoring split the rocks,
The hero’s breath seemed to crack his head;
He slept in stillness, set like curds,
And plunged into resounding sleep.
When he woke with a start,
And turned onto his right side,
A striped tiger with a ringed tail
Was measuring Joltoy with its gaze,
Ready at that moment to strike.
The swift Kulunsur was urinating blood,
And from terror of the tiger
Had fallen senseless.
Behind that tiger,
A black dragon with a tiger’s head
Had drawn close beside it.
“It will tear me apart in an instant;
The man it seizes
Will be ground to dust.
My wandering here was in vain;
Will this life, a guest of seven days,
Now perish for nothing?”
His face turned yellow as straw,
And Akjoltoy, the equal of a countless army,
Stood there dumbfounded.
“What will become of my fate?
The creature behind me bears a mark
And may sting me and suck out my life.
If I do not take heed, I will die;
If it comes against me, a life will end.”
Gathering his courage, Akjoltoy
Revealed his dread majesty,
His eyes reddening like a lion’s.
He was a true marksman,
And his matchlock stood ready.
Just as he raised the matchlock to fire,
The night receded and day broke.
The striped tiger with the ringed tail
Revealed itself as a being of destiny.
Crackling like lightning,
It began to speak in many ways,
Crying out in a mighty voice:
“Fire your gun, Akjoltoy!
Press on without tiring, cunning Joltoy!
I have taken the forms of dragon and tiger
And now stand before you.
To learn how strong and how lionhearted
You truly are,
I tested your mettle.
I transformed into the foremost tiger;
You were a leader of honored heroes,
And before all discerning people,
You were the warrior who faced every foe.
I myself have watched you;
Yes, my son Joltoy, I tested you.
If you wish, I am your blessed Night of Power;
I am the patron spirit who has long supported you,
Who has upheld and guided you.
I am the eye of the seer,
The hero who knows what will come
From six months’ journey away;
I am the eye of the saint.
Do not darken your face as before an enemy;
Lower your gun,
Place your hand upon your breast,
And remain here making your prayer.
What you do would be an outrage;
Open your mouth and repent.
What you do would be reckless;
Stand and think with perfect balance.
Understand well what I have told you;
If you are wise, heed my words.
