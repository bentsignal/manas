Accomplished Kyyaz pitched his yurt.
“For the feast at which I take a wife,
Let all my people come without exception;
Let every soul witness
The bridal rites for Aichürök.
Let the great host of the Jediger,
All the Jediger people,
Come for the honor of taking a wife,
For the sake of giving the feast.
Send word to the people
For Khan Kyyaz’s glory:
To the elders who deem themselves great,
To all the tribal peoples,
To khans who are my peers and companions,
To those who will attend the celebration,
And to the children playing in the villages—
Let no old person remain behind;
Let all the teeming people come.
Let them see this spectacle of mine;
Let these people witness
The standing of the noble, unfortunate woman!
From distant Jelpinish at the earth’s end,
I shall summon every hero who can reach us;
From beyond Arman-Ayan, every giant who can cross,
I shall summon all who make the journey.
From lands where steeds grow weary and livestock falters,
I shall leave not a single one behind.
Of all whom ears have heard and eyes have seen,
I shall leave not a single person behind.
In the lowlands of Jeti-Özön,
For the feast of Lady Chürök,
I shall slaughter countless dark mares,
And innumerable camels;
I shall lay out wealth in equal measure.
I shall open all my livestock for the feast;
For the noble, unfortunate woman,
I shall send invitations through many cities.
In Köönö-Turpan and Bombai,
I shall feast all the peoples in sight
And show them wonders untold.
Facing the four directions,
I shall heap meat as high as jagged mountains,
And broth as broad as Ala-Köl;
I shall make the blue heavens roar
And set all who see it clamoring.
If I do not stage a spectacle before their eyes,
May I die unworthy of the name Khan Kyyaz!
If I do not make it a tale among the people,
May I sink without ever having been born!”
The many wrestlers who heard the decree
Slaughtered countless dark camels,
And countless black mares with white blazes;
They set wealth out in equal measure,
And slaughtered equally from all four kinds of livestock.
Kyyaz began the grand display,
Setting all the livestock and provisions
Out in the midst of the gathering.
Among the Jediger of Jeti-Özön,
With gold bound upon the horses,
With a clamor that split the sky,
Through the stratagem devised by Khan Kyyaz,
Word spread that Kyyaz had taken Aichürök.
The people’s uproar filled every conversation:
“Kyyaz slaughtered the bloodthirsty hero
And made spoil
Of his wife and child.”
His khanly fame rang among the people;
The blue heavens split with the noise,
And the people’s clamor filled the air.
It suited the Jediger well:
“This is Khan Kyyaz’s wedding,” they said.
The Jediger who filled Jeti-Özön
Streamed forth in excited crowds.
To see that feast,
Those without stallions rode camels,
And those without means went on foot;
The women hurried in noisy crowds,
