When his turn came,
Rib bones and joints
Were torn layer by layer,
Spears and swords rang,
They strained and listened,
Let me pass through battle.
While my blessed people still stand,
If by chance I fall behind,
If fate leaves me grieving,
If I am struck down from behind,
And my cherished inherited people
Are seized by Sarybai,
My waist broken,
My nation laid waste,
What inheritance could I attain?
While my people remain unharmed,
For my great ancestral nation,
For the strength girded at my waist,
For the people who are my support,
For my precious inherited people,
Sarybai, who has challenged me,
I shall truly level my spear.
For my royal khan’s people,
I shall sacrifice this life.
Let my teeming people raise an outcry,
Saying, “Alas, our elder is dead,”
“He grappled fiercely with the dog
And gained his inheritance,”
“For the sake of the dear Kyrgyz people,
Our lionlike elder, tall as a mountain,
Before the people into whom he was born,
Departed as a sacrifice.”
“Counting his precious life no dearer than a fly,
Before his true people,
Our leopardlike elder passed away.”
Let the nation wail and the people weep,
Let the people’s hearts be torn,
Let their ribs seem rent apart,
Let the tears pour from their eyes.
Let the people lament and weep,
As though grief would crush them to dust.
He is not one to grow weary;
“Wretch, your sin will be upon you,”
He is not one to abandon us.
He seems savage, his venom strong,
He lays low everyone he meets;
In this wicked-looking infidel
Give up hope of any noble soul.
Would he ever cease his violence?
Amid the heavy calamity of his violence,
Would he spare a soul that begged?
Can we shrink away in fear?
Can this wondrous, Khizr-blessed people
Be handed over as tribute
To the infidel who oppresses us?
Would he spare a single soul?
Against the violent one who spares no life,
Against the black-hearted monster born,
Against the blood-spilling butcher,
Black-hearted and corrupt within,
Against this inescapable torment,
Why should I flinch
Before the black eyes of that brute?
Let us level our spears,
Fight without leaving one alive,
Make the whole world tremble,
Spend every resource of our minds,
Break every steadfast back,
Roam throughout the blessed Kyrgyz inheritance,
Leaving not a single soul untouched,
Against the infidel who has smeared the earth with blood,
Against the consummate, cunning warrior,
Who has brought the age to its end,
The warrior who has ruined the times,
I shall gird myself and wage war,
And meet him truly face-to-face.
Let ribs be split apart,
Let us shed blood without fleeing,
Let us wheel and grapple,
And stand facing one another.
