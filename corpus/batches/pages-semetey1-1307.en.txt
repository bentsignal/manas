The sky was clear, the earth shrouded,
Not a blade could be seen on the ground.
Dust flew and haze burned,
Not a single person could be seen.
The sound of the matchlocks rang from afar,
All the Afghans
Fled and shut themselves in the city.
Tülkü's son Tümönbay,
After ranging for seven days,
Fled in helplessness.
Wounded in seven places,
Argyn's son Ajybay,
When Khan Toltoy came,
After ranging for six days,
He too fled,
Wounded in six places.
At that time in Afghanistan,
There was round baked bread,
And houses built in four sections.
After having his native people slaughtered,
Akun still had an army.
Making Akun wail,
He penned him inside his city.
Those fated to die died,
And those who remained alive
Suffered the utmost humiliation.
They lost their wits,
Many of them were slain,
And those who remained alive
Fled toward the city.
Toltoy made a living hell,
Not a soul came out to meet him,
And many of the Afghans
He slaughtered in heaps.
Akun, Kojo, and Tümönbay
Could not stand against Toltoy.
It was a God-stricken affair:
They were no belt one could gird on,
No people a mortal could trust.
They were a feeble, helpless people.
Unable to find their bearings,
Many wandered on the ridge,
All their horses were stallions,
But they had no saddles at all,
And many more went about with pack saddles.
Unable to find their bearings,
Still more rushed about in confusion.
“The Jediger have destroyed us,” they said,
“They have reached our very roots,” they said;
Many fled.
“They have wiped out our line,” they said;
Many grieved,
When they were about to reach the very end together,
When all the Afghans
Were about to be utterly destroyed,
A cry burst from a throat.
When they heard the cry,
Shouting the battle cry “Manas!”
Külchoro came in fury,
Leveling his spear at Toltoy.
Behind Külchoro,
The trumpet deafened the ears,
The brass horn clattered,
And the drum boomed.
Dust rose into the sky,
Cloud striking cloud,
They raised a din crying “Khan Manas!”
All the sons of khans
Raised a clamor as they entered the host.
Shouting, they thrust their spears,
And many men fell in battle.
They grappled and fought,
Leaving ruin all around.
Baitaylak, son of Bakay,
Came shouting from the front,
And Kalyk of Sary Khan
Drove in, scattering the foe.
