They say Kurman’s life came to rest.
The armored warrior whom Kurman clad,
The pure warriors who pierced the stone,
Calling him a brave man dressed in his mail,
The heroes did not know it.
They met him with their spears,
The full host of dark Kalmyks.
The armored warrior came out to wrestle.
Those who asked, “What is even a fly?”
From the heart of the one possessed,
Those who consumed it in place of food.
They say turmoil descended and he died.
Taking the enemy without pause,
They say he kept inflicting torment.
They say he crushed Karmush.
From the Kalmyk who summoned his courage,
They say he scattered his blood like water.
The armored man wearing Kurman’s coat,
They say he chased and subdued his spirit.
The armored man called Kuramal,
They say he made him writhe and spilled his blood.
A prince emerged from the bull-like host.
In this contest of the mighty,
They say he pressed him down with a grip.
Fire flared, burning from his core.
An enemy arose from within him.
Like a bullet striking the chest,
It burned through him like a root.
It shattered him and became embers in the fire.
They say he scattered the assembled force with his fist.
If he went among the Kyrgyz and showed hatred,
He would make China present its back.
He pursued him like a maral.
His battle cry was “Buгу”; the enemy was a dog.
He seized the hem and laid waste around him.
