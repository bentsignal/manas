When she surges against that maiden,
No mortal can stand before her.
Not Sarybai—the whole wide world
Could not confront and defeat her.
With a crest like a fleeing kulan,
A gold-browed, high-arched saddle,
Was fitted upon Zhelzhetpes, swift as the wind,
Like a sovereign ruling a people.
Prancing proudly, Zhelzhetpes
Seemed destined for good fortune.
He champed upon his bit,
And tore the ground wherever he stepped.
The black earth struck by his hooves
Split apart on every side.
His banner-like tail was a piled-up mass,
Revealing the form of a noble steed.
When he strode with bounding steps, the black earth
Scattered like fragments of a great bowl.
A great falcon broad as a house
Shrieked above her head.
She made her charger rear and plunge,
Throwing all the people into an uproar.
“The maiden is a lion!” they said,
Delighting everyone who saw her.
An ancestral spirit descended upon the maiden;
The tasseled banners parted amid the host.
Her majestic presence,
Her bearing upon horseback,
Loomed like a towering mountain.
Every mortal being’s
Wits were utterly confounded.
She looked to either side,
Licking her lips like a hungry lion.
Battle-axe, sword, bow, and arms
Shone all around her.
The enemy who advanced against her
Lost heart and scattered.
Her readiness was plain—
It seemed the fairies themselves escorted her.
And valiant Manas the fierce
Seemed to have become her companion.
Her breast was like a fleeing kulan’s,
Her eyes those of a hunting eagle—
Such was my champion’s aspect.
Over rugged ridges and rocky crags
She had crossed these mountains before.
For many years already,
Against the infidel son of Zhelmoguz,
She had been a seasoned master,
A foe who often fought him.
They were neighbors upon the same land,
Their peoples lived mingled together,
Their herds shared the same pastures,
Their kumis was drunk at common feasts.
They were warriors raised together,
But, growing stubborn, they quarreled,
Struck one another and fought,
And became bitter enemies.
The swaggering champion maiden
Throughout the eighteen thousand worlds
Had stirred up terrible strife.
When she clashed with a giant, she drove him back;
When she grappled with a titan, she overthrew him.
Her name was renowned throughout the world;
The fame of that maiden champion
Had traveled very far.
She was a skillful master of combat,
A champion maiden born but once.
Eight times she had charged.
The maiden attacked Sarybai.
Though she was a woman, the poor soul
Answered him defiantly and hardened herself,
And came to be called the lion maiden.
She was deft and sure with the spear,
Truly knowing all the stratagems of war,
A mighty maiden blessed with fortune.
To the spur where the mountain ended,
To the jutting promontory,
To meet once in eleven years,
At the place where the mountains ended,
