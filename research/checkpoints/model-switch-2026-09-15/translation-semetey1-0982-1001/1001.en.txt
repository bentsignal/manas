The tail feathers of a peacock,
Upon her blue-gray lynx-fur cap,
She had arranged in ninety forms.
Look at Churok's cleverness,
That one born to cause great turmoil.
Thirty young wives and thirty maidens,
With Aichurok in their midst,
Beside the great flowing river,
Gathered and began their games.
Your great lord, born fierce,
When he saw Aichurok,
Nearly died.
Taking his spyglass in hand,
From the ridge of Acha-Tash,
He roared in fury.
Kulchoro and Kanchoro,
He called his boys by name.
The dear lion who had kept still,
The dear hero now meant to stir.
When they heard his roar,
Kulchoro and Kanchoro,
Bending their knees toward the ground,
To learn the news without delay,
To reach their lord,
Put on their weapons.
Flying like arrows, the boys
Readied their separate horses.
One companion rode
Khan Kobosh's Kokburchak.
One companion rode
Abyke's Aktelki.
Springing like sheep, they mounted.
The furious bloodthirsty hero,
They rode toward him.
Racing and drawing level,
The boys conferred:
'My bloodthirsty elder, the leopard,
Has raised a mighty shout.
His shout is terribly heavy;
It tears at ears and nose.
In this elder's voice,
There is a sign of haste.
Is there a deeply dug pit?
Is there an army raising uproar?
Is there a pit dug deep?
Surrounding and overwhelming him,
Is there some formidable enemy?
Our lord's voice rang out loudly.
Have they come down upon his head?
Has our dauntless lord been hurried?
If unceasing tumult has arisen,
If merciless uproar has arisen,

If a clamorous enemy has appeared,
Let us reach the champion,
Let us wreak great havoc,
The champion-born dauntless hero,
Let us rescue him!
If the enemy has come from Mecca,
Let us choose and mount outstanding steeds,
Scatter every one of them,
And drive them toward Mecca.
If the enemy has come from China,
Let us drive them into Kakan.'
Saying this, the boys
Raced toward their elder,
Their two stirrups clashing,
Dashing onward in great haste,
Turning their horses' heads.
When they reached their elder,
Taking their spears in hand,
The companions of mighty presence
Stood bristling.
