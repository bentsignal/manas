Yet you lie there voicing your regrets.
When you took aim
At an enemy who defied you,
Compelling the whole world to submit,
When you held dominion over it,
Breaking through dead ends and opening roads,
Striking everyone you met,
When you held dominion over all,
When you wore armor
No bullet could pierce to your flesh,
When you raided Kara-Shaar, Kambyl, and Beijing,
Striking those cities,
When you wore a sable cap
Trimmed with blue-black otter,
When you made the whole world submit
And fell upon it,
When you kneaded the whole world
As though it were dough,
When you scorched this mortal world
As though it were a fly,
When you broke through countless armies with your spear
And drove them round,
When you laid the world waste
And knew it as your own,
Mounting a charger and taking up your spear,
With a naked sword belted at your side,
Circling the city of the Khakan,
Which even winged flight could not reach,
When you held it under siege,
When you scourged
The Kaldai and the Shibe,
What were your power and strength,
Your prowess when entering combat?
What skill did you possess?
Has all your vigor failed?
While your uncle and companion lived,
What strength was yours?
Test your might;
Learn the measure of your strength.
If your ancient power remains,
Unless fate strikes us down with bullets,
How could we stay alive and do nothing?
Unless we face the black-hearted Kyyaz
And fight him,
Grappling until we spill his blood,
Struggling until we take his life,
May I die rather than live
If I do not raise open war;
May I sink away unborn.
The hero Kyyaz has grown eager—
I shall meet him in mortal combat!
The sly Kyyaz seems to have gone mad;
I shall wage a mighty war,
Attack with all my resolve,
And chase him as far as Beijing.
I shall slaughter every khan,
Rip open their ribs,
And pour their blood into wineskins!
If I do not wage a vast battle,
If this year I do not avenge
My father who passed into the next world,
If in Jeti-Özön I do not make the Jediger
Keep Jeti-Özön as their land,
And form the Jediger into a nation,
If I do not burn their slopes to embers
And destroy their Khan Kyyaz,
If I do not divert the river’s waters,
If I do not make the black-hearted Jediger
Seem like a fire gone cold,
If I do not crush their champions,
Slay all who oppose me,
And soothe my vengeful heart,
May I sink away unborn.
My sworn enemy is Khan Kyyaz;
I shall try my strength against him.
The one I will grapple with is Kanchoro—
Here is my true enemy!
My khan and uncle who cared for me,
You are a father in my father’s place;
