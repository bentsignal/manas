The archers drew together;
Iron-headed wrestlers
Prepared at the front.
Their warriors of countless kinds
Were the shield
Of whoever they faced.
From the screaming brass horn,
From the trumpet, ears went deaf.
Across a wide ravine where no bird flew,
From their spear no foe escaped.
Driving their heroes forward,
They came out in chosen array;
Their valiant men at the front,
They came out glittering.
Hero Toltoi was a valiant man;
From the rear of the Jediger
He drove them out and came on.
Chynkojo, son of Shygai,
Had unleashed havoc,
Beating the great host
And driving it without letting it flee.
When they saw the wrath of these men,
The many Black Jediger
Had no strength to flee.
Putting on their shields,
Committing themselves to fate,
They fitted their oroipo bullets;
The Jediger of the Seven Valleys
Covered the land all together.
The host divided into hundreds,
Looking ready to run forward;
The host divided into thousands,
Looking ready to overwhelm.
They shouted their battle cries,
Dust billowing to the sky;
Their heroes grew excited,
Spurring their sluggish horses.
The Jediger now covered
The many Black Afghans;
If allowed to go unanswered,
They would reach their very roots,
Shatter the stone fortress,
And slaughter them utterly.
They would make the meek ones wail
And offer them as sacrifice.
They would seize their daughters for pleasure;
If the Jediger had their way,
They came to wreak slaughter
And bring devastation upon Afghanistan.
The strong man’s day had dawned;
As though setting out on campaign against China,
He had bound his head awry.
Chynkojo was a corrupter;
His day had dawned.
While matters stood this way,
Blue-gray mist parted in the sky;
With a piercing shout, a myriad host—
Chynkojo’s army appeared.
If no one drove them back,
They would simply overrun the land,
Bring turmoil upon this poor head,
And reach the very root;
They would overwhelm and carry off
Chürök, daughter of Akun.
Men swarmed in rage,
Soldiers teemed everywhere;
A myriad host came
To cover Khan Akun’s fortress.
Their thick-armed wrestlers,
The snarling Jediger,
Advanced and covered the front.
When the many Black Jediger
Were about to overwhelm and carry them off,
Black blood flowing in torrents,
And the people turning back to cross,
A coat of mail with golden collar
Glittered upon a breast;
