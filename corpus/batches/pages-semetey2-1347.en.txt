When climbing, he was his support,
The child of my bloodthirsty lord;
When riding to holy war, he was his swift steed,
The kinsman of the bloodthirsty lion.
Külchoro, left as a gift
By my champion father Albambet,
The wrestler and lion of the world,
Went boldly into single combat.
I heard that he was captured,
Caught in a net from which no bucking,
Startled creature could escape.
They say Kanchoro and brave Kyyaz
Took him alive;
They gouged his right shoulder,
Flayed him without mercy,
And reduced the falcon to this state.
They made Karagul, chief of the herdsmen,
Into a woodcutter;
They slaughtered the Sary-kan people,
And did every injury they knew
To my leopard uncle Bakay.
They say they plundered old uncle Bakay’s livestock
And feasted themselves;
The madmen made him a kökbörü ball
For forty wrestlers.
Mother, your thoughts must be troubled;
The child who drank your breast
Has brought overwhelming affliction.
Mother, did you see what I saw?
Did you learn what I know?
The pit already dug is coming,
The bitter brine already boiled is coming;
An immense, teeming army is coming.
White tasseled standards raise a tumult,
A tumult that splits the sky;
The massed black Jediger
Come as an overwhelming host.
Their champions are at the front,
Driving us into confinement and slaughter;
Mother, behind your son Kanchoro
Your senses have surely failed.
Firing arrows that embitter the soul,
They come flooding out—
Mother, the child who sucked your breast.
Look closely, mother, and see
That your child has become an enemy!
Pull crimson silk from the bias—
It will not part;
That madman struck by your breast milk
Shows no sign of turning toward us!
Pull green-mottled raw silk
From every side—it will not part;
When that good son of yours draws near,
He looks like one who will spare no life.
Breast milk has become mere dry curd;
When he grows fat, he raids your people.
Your footcloth is worthless as felt;
Breast milk has become dry curd no longer—
When he grows fat, he raids your people.
Your spring flowing from the hills,
Your brave son who sucked your breast—
Did your foster foal devour you?
When the time came to mount and ride,
Did he say he would be an equal brother?
Yesterday, on the plain of Jylgyndy,
Along broad Talas,
At Kara-Buura and Chatkal,
When the Kyrgyz lay spread across the land,
When Kanchoro, peerless in the world,
Was sucking your breast,
You did not heed every evil word;
You did not recognize his enmity.
This black-hearted choro
Will now fulfill to the end
The omen appointed for you.
My ash-gray foal, look closely:
He has drawn his bow like pouring rain;
Administer his punishment yourself.
