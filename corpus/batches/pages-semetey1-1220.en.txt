That bastard born of foul urine,
That shrieking Burut—
It seems his way struck me;
It seems the hand
Of screeching Manas struck me.
Those squealing Buruts—
It seems their road reached me;
It seems the hand
Of shrieking Manas reached me.”
Before Joloy could close his mouth,
Hero Manas carved through
Their center.
Almambet cut his way
Around their flanks.
Hero Chubak hemmed in
Their very center.
Khan Bakai raided
One of their wings.
Hero Syrgak crushed
Their outermost edge.
Agybay pierced
One of their flanks.
Hero Kyrgylchal drove through
The side by their throats.
Hero Shuutu laid waste
One of their wings.
Raising the gray-mottled banner,
The Chinese raised a wailing clamor.
Though it was said they would drive them off with blows,
They slaughtered them as they drove them;
Almambet, son of Aziz Khan,
Unleashed extraordinary turmoil.
Khan Manas's forty companions—
Told to take a cap, they took a head;
Told to spill blood, they spilled demons;
Told to drink water, they drank poison.
They unleashed a wondrous massacre;
Every Chinese upon the plain
Lay sprawled dead.
They unleashed the great slaughter.
They captured horses
Worth a thousand times the prize.
Those who advanced to the front
Each took seven thousand captured horses;
The meek who could not reach them
Each took seven hundred horses.
The crust of the earth was torn open;
The Muslims went away
Sated with prizes.
Standing face to face,
Firing guns and drawing bows,
They fought without fleeing.
Seven banners brought from Mecca
They planted in seven places,
Slaughtering wherever they reached.
Among the Black Chinese and Manchus,
Only a few common folk
Escaped by fleeing.
Manas found the booty
And ten thousand Kalmyk households
He raided and devastated.
They took the gold as booty
And drove off the livestock, fully sated.
So many camel colts, so many goods—
They could not load them all;
So many noble young wives and beautiful girls—
They could not divide them all.
The Kalmyks who faced them
Stood on opposite banks;
Between them lay a great river.
Unable to reach with spears,
They fought with guns.
As they exchanged battle cries,
The expert Almambet
Shot Joloy's Achbuudan
On the farther bank,
Trapping many of them,
Some ten thousand Kalmyk households.
