Sary Khan stood and said this:
“My only one left by the bloodthirsty hero!
Those who go to Beijing do not return;
No man has defeated it in conflict,
And no soul who went returned alive.
Toward harsh and perilous Beijing,
Pursuing your royal father's blood vengeance,
You have set out to grapple!
Return alive from Beijing;
Let them call you ‘the hero's son,’
And return safe from facing them!
To Beijing, whence those who go do not return,
My hero's only one,
You have set out in conflict!
With that Beijing, whence those who go do not return,
You have become enemies.
May your journey be through spear battle;
That you take no countless army—
At this I grieve.
Kongurbai in Beijing—
May victory over him be yours!
Toward Beijing that you face,
May your going be in the cool autumn;
My only son of a hero,
May the enemy you oppose be overcome!
May what you strike number six hosts;
May your splendid Bakai
Return as khan from Beijing.
My child, all else is false, but death is true.
Against the teeming infidels,
Charging with spear extended,
Let them call you ‘the hero's son’;
Now is the time to see
The calamity that comes upon your head!
Will a son not die for honor?
Will he not see
What God has inscribed for him!
Is it right to flee about,
Unable to avenge your royal father?
A man born for honor—
Can he remain unconquered without striving?!
Semetey, left by the noble one,
The mountain you lean on is iron.
Semetey, son of Manas—
Do not let them say he dwells in his village,
Unable to avenge his father.
If your doom comes, die and return;
If God's written decree
Comes upon your head, see it and return!
Of Chinmachin's Beijing,
The true lord is Kongurbai;
With the black-hearted Kalcha,
Grapple and spill his blood!
If almighty God the Creator
Created you by his will,
Go and break his ribs!
The enemy you must overcome is Kongurbai;
If your heroic father Manas supports you,
Bring him low and unleash conflict.
The one you contest is Chinmachin;
The enemy you oppose is Bakburchun;
The one you fight is Kakan-Chyn.
Will a young man not die while grappling?
Will he not see the decree
Almighty God has written for his entrusted life!
Does a man not go against the foe
And struggle to take revenge?
The road of holy war is a great road;
Is it right to turn back?
You grapple with the many Chinese;
Can you avoid fighting to the death,
Tearing lungs and taking gall?
Extend your spear, break the host,
And return having avenged your lion father Manas!”
