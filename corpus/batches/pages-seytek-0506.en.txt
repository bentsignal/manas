Surely this is the day he came seeking help.
Our treacherous foe Sarybay—
They say Sarybay is a mighty warrior.
How can we surrender the dear people
Of the Argyn and Kyrgyz
To that rabid, accursed fool?
Let us face him boldly,
And if death has truly come, let us die!
Let us fire our guns and draw our bows,
And meet them in a bloody clash.
Let us stand firm and die,
Seeing their ranks upon the heights,
Their champions and their warriors,
And confronting them face-to-face.
Let us shed our frail lives and spill our blood,
Fighting them in earnest,
And test ourselves in combat.
Against an infidel like Sarybay,
A warrior beyond all reckoning,
Let us clash and spill his blood,
Breaking the bones of one another’s ribs.
Let us stand amid the conflict,
Give up our lives, and fight,
Raising the battle standard and filling the banners,
While grave calamity becomes an uproar.
If hostile death arrives, let us die!
Against the enemy who comes of his own accord,
Let us split lungs and spill gall,
Raise our cries and thrust our spears,
And test ourselves in battle.
If death comes from the infidel,
Let us behold the strength of his courage!
The steep valley and its pale rocks
Are the road I was born to ride.
The furious infidel,
A warrior of that kind,
Is the prey my hand was born to take.
Along the road measured out before us,
With the army we ourselves have seen,
We shall open fire.
Sarybay, son of the monster,
Was left an orphan
When he reached the age of seven.
Those who saw his courage were frightened.
Without ever turning back, Sarybay
Has studied wondrous incantations
From that time until this.
He has grown into a gray-maned wolf,
And his time for vengeance has come.
He has occupied a mountain fit for a war banner
And raised an uproar that splits the sky.
If opposed, he means to wreak slaughter,
To destroy the descendants of Khan Manas
Together, leaving not one alive.
He rages with renewed courage
And busies himself preparing.
He has mustered an entire army
And brought an iron-rope net.
They have all assembled together,
Saying, “I shall unleash all the strife I know;
I shall mow down the bloodthirsty Manas’s descendants
Until none remain.”
He has brought great warriors from among the giant devs
And gathered troops all along the road—
That accursed wretch himself.
Reading the trail and laying grass upon it,
He has pursued us until he has turned pale.
He heard the talk amid the clamor,
And heard that the gray wolf Manas
Still had descendants.
He heard that in broad Talas
They had established a city.
He heard that the towering gray wolf
Still had a son.
So he formed an army and gathered troops,
Mustering an immense host.
It is no ordinary darkness or night,
But the infidel’s furious demon.
He set his enmity upon us deliberately,
That scorched and rabid wretch.
