A dream that made my heart leap!
My dream last night will not leave me well;
When I consider last night's dream,
There were eighty-four armed men,
And wherever they struck was red fire.
His face turned dark with fury:
Semetey, son of Manas,
Had come in the dark hour,
Thick-armed and stone-hearted,
A great elephant-limbed brute.
His broad-socketed patterned spear
Was shod with steel at its base.
Against the mortals who clashed with him,
He showed no fear and gave no opening.
He began a terrible rage;
Stretching all of us out,
He seized and slaughtered us.
He gouged out our eyes,
Slaughtered us in a massacre,
And made our heads roll;
The crows drank their fill of blood.
He gathered and seized the herdsmen,
Led by the great hero,
Destroyed all of us together,
And carried out a massacre.
What can this mean?
Interpret this dream for me!
Looming like a lion,
He strode out from the hut;
Crushing whatever stirred,
He smashed it with a battle-axe.
What can this mean?
Interpret this dream for me!
A flood came against Beijing,
Shattered the stone fortress,
And utterly destroyed it.
All its willows lay fallen;
All the buildings
Of this Beijing collapsed.
What can this mean?
Interpret this dream for me!
Fire came from the west
And burned all the mighty men.
Caught in the blazing fire,
You yourself burned away.
What can this mean?
Interpret this dream for me!
Semetey, son of Manas,
Champion of the Buruts,
Destroyed wherever he reached,
And his conflict struck us!
His menace was fierce, his wrath strong;
He seized and slaughtered, laying all flat.
I fear for your horses;
I despair of my life.
Countless men led by Manas
Surged over us,
And a thousand men led by Bakai
Thronged around us.
I utterly despaired of life.
Before them
Rode a man upon a red-gray horse,
Its hair tail knotted tight:
Tall and broad-shouldered,
With a face like oiled wheat,
Eyes like twilight mist,
Wide-mouthed and cliff-browed,
A man resembling an executioner,
Both champion and hero,
Who would subjugate the world—
That hero was a blood-drinking warrior.
He rode a horse no bullet could catch
And wore armor no bullet could pierce;
His muscles were like a bull's back,
As though he would defeat anyone who grappled him.
No mortal could go against him;
None who struck him remained alive.
His eyes blazed like fire;
He gnashed his teeth and chewed up heroes.
He himself followed the faith of Islam:
