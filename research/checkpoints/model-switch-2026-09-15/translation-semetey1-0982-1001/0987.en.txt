When I was laying siege,
Making the kaldai crash,
When I was beating him,
Why did I not die then?
Alas, when Manas was alive,
[UNRESOLVED]
When they were within my grasp,
Kakan-Chyn and Keng-Tung,
When I was laying siege to them,
The seething Chinese,
[UNRESOLVED]
When I was beating them,
Why did I not die then?
When my accomplished Manas was alive,
Turmoil over the face of the earth,
When I was unleashing it,
Anyone who answered back,
Seizing him for sacrifice,
When I was slaughtering him,
Why did I not die then?
This life of mine brings a bad name.
My courser Manas, take me with you!'
Bakai stood saying this.
Dear Kanykei,
Taking pacers as gifts for the three khans,
Taking a golden staff in her hand,
Roared in fury,
And called the two twins:
'Kulchoro and Kanchoro,
Before the Five Wild Sheep stars set,
I would turn to nurse you five times.
Your fathers' graves, you two wretches!
Has the renowned God struck you?
Better never to have been born!
Die rather than go on living!
When a horse dies, you wretches,
[UNRESOLVED]
When an elder brother dies, you wretches,
His wife is to be cared for.
If the elder brother is gone,
When his wife becomes unruly,
His younger brother must restrain her rage.
Gouge out her eyes in punishment,
The infidel who abused a khan,
Slaughter her as a sacrifice!
Whatever calamity comes of it,
I shall face it myself.
She and I are kin,
Kin of equal standing.
I shall pay the blood-price myself.
Whatever trouble comes of it,
I alone shall bear it!'
Girding her overskirt,
Roaring in fury,
Calling Kulchoro,
Taking her golden staff in hand,
She struck Kulchoro once.
Struck by his mother,
The able-born Kulchoro,
That wretch at twelve years old,
Do not ask about his reckless bravery.
His black-sided heavy whip,
He took in his hand, folded double.
Bending his knee to the ground,
He ran to Chachykei,
To make her tell about the falcon,
That foul-tongued woman,
To settle accounts with her and return.
When he drew near the fortress,
Chachykei's forty young wives
Barred the gate,
And held the door shut.
Roaring and shouting,
Calling, 'Open the door!'
Kulchoro now arrived.
Their tightly barred door,
The champion-born Kulchoro
Kicked apart and entered.
