My grief would scatter and vanish,
My brow would open like the dawn,
And the ancient enemy be defeated.
Will the day ever come when death is gone?
When the people rest calmly, free of turmoil,
Will the day ever come when we may lie at ease?
Will the blue-lynx and sable cap
Ever sway again in celebration?
With the people freely singing laments together,
Oh, my dear one, will a day of joy
Ever come?”
Thus Seytek stood grieving,
While the people cried out, their hearts bleeding,
Lamenting and raising a clamor.
The mighty maiden, daughter of Kayypchal,
The maiden who stirred turmoil throughout the world,
Was extraordinary in bravery,
And unmatched in strength.
Her immeasurable valor,
Throughout the whole wide world,
Had surpassed even that of lions.
Gentle in nature and pure in heart,
She was a healer and a sage,
Whose eyes could see afar.
Her figure was like a radiant sun,
A graceful and beautiful maiden.
She combed her hair like a parrot’s plumage,
And was born as white as the moon;
No other soul like her had arisen among humankind.
The hair down her back billowed softly,
Like the down on an owl’s legs.
Her figure rose tall and stately,
Like the sacred Night of Power.
Your champion had a warrior’s frame,
An immeasurably mighty wrestler.
That peerlessly born hero
Looked solemn without smiling,
And wore a silver-mounted quiver.
The radiant, graceful woman
Had a face illuminated like the sun.
No ill-fated one like her existed in the world,
Her nature unique, her face like the moon.
Her words were ardent and deeply alluring,
Her bearing unique, her eyes like a camel calf’s,
Her luminous face softly glowing,
Her bosom broad, her chest expansive.
Beautiful and dark-eyed,
Such was Kuyaly to behold.
Her gentle nature was plain to see;
It seemed she would grant my every wish.
It seemed she had been born mighty,
With valor, courage, and a radiant face—
The Almighty Lord
Had bestowed these without measure.
Her dignity was rare and pearl-like,
Her form like a maiden made of light.
Without so much as a smile, she stood solemn,
Her calm deliberation evident;
Whenever one saw her judgment,
A mortal soul entrusted to God rejoiced.
The lion-maiden wrestler’s
Character revealed itself thus:
She strode behind them, solemn,
And the poor woman of camel-like stature
Had her moonlike face bathed in light,
Her heroism plain to see.
She donned her battle armor;
The mighty maiden, fitted for war,
Destroyed whoever approached her,
And went forth to win the great prize.
Her arms were exceptional, her fury fierce;
She laid her mortal enemies low.
Her heroism became renowned,
And enemies who faced her recoiled.
Those who coveted her were slain,
And many reins were pulled tight.
