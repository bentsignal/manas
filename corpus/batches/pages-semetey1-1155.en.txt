My blood remains one spoonful.
My joints have failed; I have no strength,
No power to wrestle.
If I jostle with this infidel,
My joints will come apart,
And my body will sway.
The reason I am like this:
I descended beneath the earth,
And seven years were completed;
Since I came above the earth,
Barely seven days have passed.
What I say is true; I have no recourse.
I have no strength to wrestle
The swaggering crow-like Kalmyk.
I have declined from my former days;
You asked whether I was formidable,
But today your Töshtük
Stands in the form of doomsday.
If I unexpectedly wrestled
The swaggering black Kalmyk,
Would my hip not strike the earth,
And would not the handful of Muslims
Lose all their honor together?
Would the black earth not swallow me?
If I fell sprawling,
Would that not be my deathbed?
If I speak plainly,
My tale is unending:
When the giant black bird Zymyryk
Was driven beyond all recourse,
I cut flesh from my thick thigh
And gave it as food.
I bear such a wound.
Will the Kalmyk not seize upon my weakness,
Throw me down stiffly,
Take his unspent vengeance,
And break the back
Of the steadfast Muslims?"
When Töshtük spoke thus,
Lord Manas left him.
He seized Zhamgyrchy
Of the Eshtek people:
"You are a hero of the Eshtek,
The lion of a whole clan;
How are you against the champion,
To throw him and win the prize?"
Losing his wits,
Zhamgyrchy of the Eshtek
Fled from among the people:
"I shall not approach Zholoy,
Nor attack the Kalmyk.
I have given Manas my answer:
I will not go to the Kalmyk
And die for nothing."
"Hero Bagysh of the Zhediger!
How are you against the champion—
To win the prize if you throw him,
And be left with nothing if you fall?"
Then Bagysh said:
"I will not go to the Kalmyk;
I will not die needlessly
At the hands of mighty infidel Zholoy.
I have a land called Zheti-Özön,
And a people called Zhediger.
I made dear Zheti-Özön
Into a homeland by my own skill;
I made the seven Zhediger clans
Into a people by my own skill.
I raised a kite into a hunting bird,
Gathered a mixture into a nation;
I trained a wandering bird into a hunting bird,
Gathered wanderers into a nation.
If Zholoy kills me,
The kite will return to its place,
The gathered mixture to its people;
The wandering bird will return to its place,
And the wanderer to his kin.
I will not go to the Kalmyk;
