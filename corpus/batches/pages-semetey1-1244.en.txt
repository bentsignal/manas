Becoming a stranger to the Argyn people,
Was it wrong that I came to your village?
You seem to have been defeated—
Are all twelve parts of your body whole?
Ill-fated Khan Toltoi,
What shall I do with your lies?
You said countless men led by Manas
Had emerged from Batkal—
Was this not a lie?
Manas blessed by Kydyr had died
And beheld the place of Judgment.
Saying Bakai had appeared,
You spoke meaningless words.
Lord Bakai had been a khan;
When his prince passed away,
Herding camels in Talas,
He was a broken man.
Saying Almambet had appeared,
You spoke in reckless haste;
He went to behold the face of Judgment,
And has long been dead.
Saying Chubak had entered,
You spread an uproar.
A star was eclipsed in the sky;
In cowardice, Chubak fled Beijing
And escaped alive.
He was a coward then;
No one knows where he went.
Speaking so many useless words,
Toltoi, you will never prosper;
If you continue like this, poor man,
There will never be another like you!
“The shameless face
Of that accursed Toltoi,
Who has caused so much trouble”—
As though slaughter had struck him,
He lay twisted aside.
Saying, “Where is his flesh?”
Chynkojo’s wrestler,
Receiving Khojo’s command,
Looked upon
Dear hero Toltoi’s wound.
Below the belt at his waist,
The flesh was torn and flayed,
Gouged out and blackened,
And swollen thick
As though become a dark-red berry.
Among the Jediger host
His healers were more than could be counted;
His careful physicians were even more.
The healers gathered,
Set a cupping horn upon the wound,
And drew out together
Every drop of bad blood.
There was medicine called “Üpü”;
They applied it three times.
There was medicine called “Sebep”;
They applied that as well.
Though they treated Toltoi,
Who had come seeking a wife,
The wound gave off a strange vapor;
The patterned spear that had passed through
Carried such a terrible poison.
Its point had been sharpened and formed—
Semetey’s patterned spear,
Whose sight delighted a man.
So that whoever it struck would fester and die,
It had been tempered for six full days
In a dragon’s venom.
Whenever it made a wound,
The wound turned foul and festered—
Such was the patterned spear’s nature.
Khojo saw it and spoke,
Uttering a secret marvel:
“No hero drove that spear;
A child must have thrust it.
A hero does not punch with a spear—
It was only a shallow thrust.
