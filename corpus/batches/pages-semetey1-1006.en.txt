Both saddle bows were broad iron;
It would not shift when ridden downhill,
So skillfully was Akkangky made.
He shortened the crupper,
Lengthened the girth,
Drew the flank strap tight,
And lengthened the breast strap.
Kulchoro asked his elder
For the noble courser;
As soon as his elder gave it,
Without setting foot in the gold-soled stirrup,
Onto the noble courser
He vaulted in a single leap.
Would that hot-blooded young man stand still?
Once he had put on the fine-cloth robe,
Once he had mounted Buurul,
The sight opened the heart:
Gray-wolf Kulchoro
Looked like Semetey.
When the young man began to ride away,
His elder waved his hand and shouted;
Your poor lord called after him:
"Listen carefully to my words.
Lest your eyes become blind,
I had you made into a mullah.
You have the learning to read a lesson;
Buurul is my own wing.
Keep him reined on stony ground;
If you meet her on the open plain,
Give greetings and blessings
To Churok, daughter of Akun.
Do not behave like a heedless youth, my dear one;
If you see a raven, do not chase it.
The poor beast is in the fullness of condition—
May he not plunge a foot into a pit
And fail to pull his leg free.
Amid thirty daughters-in-law and thirty maidens,
With Aychurok in their midst,
May he not go lumbering awkwardly;
May they not mock you, saying,
'The poor man's horse is lame.'
Be wary of this.
You were always reckless, Kulchoro;
This is a thoroughbred courser.
If you see a crow, do not chase it;
May tall Buurul beneath you,
Sparing nothing of his poor life,
Not break into a headlong run,
Set his foot in a hollow,
And fail to pull it free.
May he not approach the maidens at the ford
Limping with a haughty air;
May the maidens and daughters-in-law there
Not mock you, saying,
'It is the lame horse of that poor fellow.'
My only one whom I have cradled,
I have given you Akolpok; wear it there.
I have given you the tall courser; ride him there.
If six divisions of black enemies appear,
Try striking the foe without flinching;
Understand: this is what I tell you.
Many enemies will lose heart,
And many foes will come over the land;
There are many hills that harbor thieves,
And many enemies lying in wait,
Many places with towering cliffs,
And many heroes ready to fight.
If you are delayed, it will bring disgrace;
Buurul the horse is entrusted to you!
You are only a boy of twelve;
Do not begin an uproar.
I have no horse beneath me,
And no coat upon me;
Do not leave your elder and lord
Stranded in this place!
Go swiftly, my Kulchoro,
Scout the city of Akun,
