“Keep watch over your seven aged mothers.
Let human eyes attend to us.
Gather together all the Jediger
Of Jeti-Özön,
And before my son Kyyaz—
So your seven mothers may be content—
For the sake of the one Creator God,
Know that the desire of poor old women
Like your seven mothers is for husbands.
Fulfill our yearning words:
Choose men of thirty,
Obtain your people’s consent,
Issue your command throughout your lands,
And wed us to them.
Gold is stone and stone is stone,
But our husbands will be more precious.
Let nothing stand between us; may these words
We have spoken come true.
If that wretched woman in labor
Bears a son,
We will contrive to steal him.
To that true realm of no return
We will send the child.
In our frenzy we will take her child;
Whether in jest or earnest,
We will suck his blood while he still lives,
And share in the blood
Of the champion destined to rise above the multitude.”
So spoke the seven old women,
Uttering monstrous words like ogres,
Proposing their scheme to brave Kyyaz
And speaking of terrible trouble
For Aichürök.
Saying, “So it shall be,”
And that everything was settled,
They made their pledge.
Before the seven fiends went
To Chürök in her labor,
Kyyaz said, “In Khan Kyyaz’s house
You shall carry firewood and pour water,
Water your horses tirelessly,
And make your names renowned among the people.
I shall set your hearts at ease among the nation
And make you queens, rulers among women.
My dear seven mothers,
Consent to what I have said
And grant what I desire.”
Thus brave Kyyaz
Spared none of his wealth.
To slaughter the child
Of the woman Aichürök,
He appointed the seven old women.
The seven corrupting crones
Agreed to his demand:
To remove the young child
From this world,
To fill his body with poison,
And receive gold from Kyyaz.
“My dear Kyyaz, we agree;
According to the meaning of your dream,
We shall undertake this deed.
We will serve
A precious man like you, my son.
We shall obey your words,
My son, and grant your wish.
We shall ease the pain that ails you
And open wide your dimmed eyes.
We shall bring back what you have lost
And banish your grief.
If we fail to do your bidding,
Cut off our heads with a sword;
Spare not the least breath of life,
But slaughter us all.
If we make that child disappear
And set Kyyaz’s affairs right,
Consent to whatever
Your seven aged mothers ask,
And grant us our desire.
This is our wish, Khan Kyyaz;
Look kindly upon it and approve.
