Let us camp together as neighboring clans,
And become acknowledged kinsmen.
Let us bind our spears together;
If disaster or conflict arises,
If many treacherous foes appear,
Let us unite our strength in one place
And fight the enemy together.
Let us camp nearby as neighbors
And become kindred clans.
Let us wash our soiled linen together,
And tie the umbilical cord together.
Let us unite as one
And become mighty steeds.
Let us remain neighboring households
And become kinsmen.
Binding our spears together,
Fighting our enemies together,
Pursuing our claims together,
And scouting the land together.
The Chinese stand beyond the ridge,
Firearms confront us ahead,
And many enemies seek to attack.
If we do not die, we shall see joyful days;
If we remain alive, for you
We shall serve as a protecting host.
For you we are beyond counting;
With twelve tails and forty sweeping plumes,
We shall be wings upon your flanks.
If you carefully heed my words
And look into my eyes,
If you rule over me,
When you journey, we shall be your road;
When you enter battle, we shall be your glory.
If you enter the cold, we shall keep you warm,
A coat with silken ties and copper sleeves,
Your robe that shields you from the cold.
If you seek shelter, we shall be your haven;
If you keep and nurture us, my hero,
We shall be sweet provisions in your treasury.
When you climb upward, we shall support you;
If you spare us and make us your people,
We shall be your closest aid.
An inexhaustible store, however much you consume,
A vast treasury and great inheritance,
We are what you need.
If you lay us before you, we shall be a white road;
If we walk beneath you, we shall be a great inheritance.
We are a countless host of mighty presence;
Hero, leave us alive and put us to the test.
An inexhaustible store, however much you consume,
We are a broad treasury and inheritance.
Shaking our heads like wretched men,
We stand within your hands.
If you slaughter us, we last but one day;
If you spare us, we shall last a thousand years.
Hero, we are a great treasury and inheritance.
If we live, we shall repay your salt
And perform your service.
Hero, we are the richest treasure and inheritance;
If you raise us, we shall be your banner.
We are useful captives taken as spoils,
Steel rarely found even by master smiths,
We shall hone ourselves like black steel.
If you discipline this people,
We shall be fledgling gray hawks
That no mountain wolf can withstand.
We are sweet-tongued nightingales
Singing beneath your blessed fortune.
We are keen-eyed falcons
Preening upon your perch.
If we live, we shall gather together;
If we die, we shall share the same faith.
When we camp, we shall share the same land;
While alive, we shall remain together,
Kinsmen brought together by fate.
Hoping you will not quarrel with us,
But take us as your kindred,
And gladly set us free,
To ride across the desert lands,
The precious inheritance you have spared.
