Now I shall avenge that ancient wrong.
Brave Kyyaz’s humiliation of me,
His tyranny, never leaves my mind.
He slaughtered my ruined people
And cut away the sinew
From behind my right shoulder.
I had no remedy and no strength;
I lived long as a slave in this foul world.
My heart burned, my liver was aflame,
But my orphan has come, and I have matured.
My pent-up grief has opened to speech,
The shame exposed behind me is covered,
And my honor has now been restored.
My torn seams have been mended,
My valiant Seytek has grown,
The pain of my sickness has eased,
And my eyes, sealed as with paste, have opened.
Now it is my turn to raise
A towering pavilion on the open plain.
It is my turn to take revenge on my tormentor,
My turn to become a hero.
In the narrow defile of the open country,
It is time to recover my caravan.
In that dragon’s narrow pass,
It is time to take revenge.
My ribs have grown firm,
And compared with former days,
The flesh around my ankles has filled out.
Against Kyyaz of the Jediger,
We shall break each other’s ribs,
And spill blood like hail.
Against the infidel in his full strength,
Against bloodthirsty warrior Kyyaz,
I am going to test myself in combat,
To grapple and die;
I am going forth to my death.
I am setting out to behold
The narrow road from which none return.”
So spoke brave Choro.
Choro, sound in all twelve limbs, said:
“How shall I take my revenge
If I do not turn and strike his flank,
If I do not bring down the villain first,
If I do not unleash turmoil
Over the sixty young wives and widows
He carried away as plunder?
After so much death and humiliation,
If I do not avenge myself anew,
May I sink away as though never born;
May I die rather than live.
I shall confront Kyyaz
And break his ribs.
I shall heap humiliation on him like sheep;
We shall ram and fight,
Enduring disgrace,
Seizing and tearing at each other’s ears,
Wrenching and straining against each other,
Cutting off heads like those of sheep,
Ripping bellies open like melons,
Ramming and battling,
Standing locked in desperate strife,
Fighting with our lives cast aside;
I shall bring a dreadful calamity upon him.
From boastful Kyyaz
I shall take my old revenge.
While my mighty head still lives,
He plundered my people
And broke my waist across,
Extinguishing like a dying fire
The spirit I had cherished,
Leaving me weeping in humiliation and grief,
Driven from my encamped people.
While my round head still lives,
How can I surrender my people?
With frenzied warrior Kyyaz
I shall fight to the full,
And test myself in the struggle.
I shall aim and thrust my spear;
