I faced him and stood my ground,
Disgracing my noble head,
And fought for sixty days.
Spears shattered at their shafts,
No armor remained untorn,
My back bent and bowed,
My clouded eyes bulged.
The accursed swine would not relent,
However much blood he drank, he was never sated;
Gathering courage and raging,
He never wearied or grew tired.
Against this infidel Sarybai,
This infidel of boundless courage,
Embers of burning tamarisk would not pierce him,
Nor bullets when I fired my white musket.
Zulputkor, inherited as a gift
From my heroic father Manas,
In the labor of confronting foes,
In the turmoil of battle,
Shattered all along its length.
Its hilt was steel, its point sharp,
Its steel-wound thong secure;
It had witnessed a great many
Battles and conflicts like this,
Yet the steel shattered and only the sheath remained—
The world has never seen the like.
“He comes sealing every narrow pass
And ridge so no invading foe can cross back,
He comes advancing.
A thousand times stronger than before—
Not merely a thousand, but countless times—
His menace fierce, his venom strong,
Damn you, Sarybai,
He comes advancing ever stronger.
When we met face-to-face, the madman
Seized the tawny rolling ridge,
And attacked the settled nation.
He took the precious bulletproof coat,
He took the mighty black giant warrior,
He besieged Talas and attacked.
Why have you diminished me so?
Having created me in the beginning,
You made all twelve parts of my body whole.
Yet in the end, Almighty,
Unable to overcome the wrathful foe,
You made me equal to a dug grave.
When I leveled my lance and struck,
Sarybai did not even sway.
The reason he did not sway
Was that hostile death had come,
And fortune and wealth that traveled with me
Had deserted my wretched head!
Deceiving and beguiling, treacherous world,
It seized and hurled me aside.
When our mountain-high fortune remained,
We could drain a surging lake,
Subdue whoever faced us,
And mark his resting place in the earth.
We crushed many who confronted us,
And always won the prize in battle.
We ground down all who came,
Cut them apart, and thrust them into the ground.
With courage of steel and hearts of stone,
We were unmatched from birth.
None could come near our rank,
But now the doomsday has dawned:
I cannot prevail against Sarybai,
My guardian fortune has departed,
And my dire enemy has arrived.
The world does not heed my words,
And in the end Sarybai
Is about to double my back,
About to spill my blood,
About to drain my strength,
About to break my spine,
And wrench the life from me.
My mind reels and my heart sinks;
He is about to drive out my breath,
And dark foreboding divides my heart.
Sarybai is the one I face,
