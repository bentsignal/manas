A perilous road went to Kokon;
One road came into Afghanistan,
The safe road of Andijan.
He circled and surveyed them all;
The orphan boy Külchoro
Now marveled at the land.
As Külchoro looked carefully,
There was the crest of a rise;
When he rode up and looked,
There stood Manas's cairn.
Upon the cairn stone
Was Almambet's inscription.
Yesterday, when they were bringing disgrace
Upon the kozho in Kokon,
When they were imposing violent turmoil
Across the face of the earth,
When mighty leopard Manas
Struck the Kyzylbash people
And returned them facing home,
That was when heroic leopard Manas
Had gone down there;
It was the place where Almambet,
Chubak, and brave Manas had lain.
"This is the black stone's inscription;
This is the name of Khan Almambet.
This is a pass left by the forefathers,
A place where heroes lay.
It is a pass brave Toltoy
Will cross without noticing.
Over there is Batkalysh Hill, Besh-Belchir;
Sovereign God, bring it face to face
With boastful Toltoy.
If he suddenly arrives,
If Toltoy unexpectedly appears,
Let us level our spears straight
And fight without drawing back.
Let us be drenched in bloody blood,
And stand fighting without fleeing.
If brave Toltoy suddenly turns and runs,
Buurul will drive him onto the plain;
From the feather-grass-covered rise,
From the broad-backed high pass,
Unless he dies, Buurul will catch him.
God, guide the path
Of the lions gone before;
Lion Manas, with whom twelve joined hands,
Stand as my protector."
Growing bold and bracing himself,
The renowned leopard kinsman
Swayed as though struck from this side.
Humming, without a care:
"Toltoy of the Zhediger
Is said to be approaching."
He did not value him at his trouser cuff.
God Almighty, the Omnipotent,
Had given courage and proud daring
Without measure.
The leopard's Syrnayza—
Ill-fated Külchoro
Now took it firmly in his hand.
The white reins steeped in dye
He wound twice around
The pommel of Akkangky.
Saying, "I take the risk—Allah, support me,"
He now planted Semetey's Syrnayza
Firmly into the earth.
He now set up a stone support.
Without saying Toltoy was coming,
Lion-born Külchoro
Grew weary and fell asleep.
He now began to snore;
Fearless Külchoro
Slept on without stirring.
He lay there propped up,
Sleeping in the saddle.
As for Külchoro's guest,
Now let me tell
The tale of brave Toltoy.
