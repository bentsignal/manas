Tell me the man you girded to your waist!
Azhybay, son of Azhy,
You spoke idly;
Saying, 'I shall give Aychürök,'
You have deceived us for five months.
After seeing your false oath and lies,
I resolved to inflict a spectacle;
Do you see that I drove out
My dense black army?
If you love splendid quarrels and strife,
Do not hold back, my Azhy;
If you will not give Chürök,
Prepare the host with which you will contend.
Fighting with you,
We barely escaped death;
Because of what happened before,
Because of Aychürök,
We narrowly escaped death.
Firing guns and drawing bows,
We fought with cracking volleys.
For the lone maiden Chürök,
A band of heroes died on your side,
And a band of heroes died on mine.
Listen closely to my words, Azhy!
Until the pliant stalks ripen,
Until the law's ashes fly,
If we barely escape death
After fighting hard for six months,
Does one prosper by loving strife?
Will this bring us any benefit?"
As Kozho stood surging with fury,
Tümönbay, son of Tülkü,
Proved a man of stern temperament.
Azhybay, son of Azhy,
Was a hero of surpassing eloquence;
Speaking softly but casting weighty words,
Azhybay, son of Azhy,
Began to address Chynkozho:
"If you advance, here is the steppe road;
If you overrun us, here are the Afghan people;
If you dig deep, here is the pit;
If you would boil misery, here is the bitterness.
Warrior Kozho, hear my words;
God knows, and you decide.
Take notice of poor men like us,
My precious Kozho, rather than slaughtering us
For the sake of a maiden.
Do not begin a wondrous conflict;
Saying, 'He will not give Aychürök,
He will not accept what I say,'
Do not reduce palm-sized Afghanistan
To slaughter!
There is much sorrow in the breast,
Blustering Chynkozho;
God stands witness to these words!
Take care, Chynkozho:
I have no reason to begrudge my daughter.
You did not come as an in-law driving livestock,
Bowing in submission and chewing fat;
You did not depart after speaking kindly,
Nor offer tea before us.
Saying, 'I shall take Aychürök,'
You offered no colt for slaughter.
You gave no kindly words,
Warrior, saying, 'He spoke badly,'
You say,
You have no grounds to bluster.
You made me taste humiliation,
And after scorning my hospitality,
You thought to take through contempt
My daughter Aychürök.
Do not take offense at that maiden
And vent your wrath.
Saying, 'He will not give Aychürök,'
Do not flatten my city!
It is also true that I did not accept
Your pledged words,
After you lied so many times
