Forty companions of Khan Manas,
Choose tulpars and mount.
Against the Black Kalmyks and Manchus,
Enter with true courage.
When they draw near the prize,
Raise the battle cry in earnest.
There is no overcoming tulpar Akkula;
When the stallion comes in,
Take the grand prize!"
Hearing these words from Bakay,
Skirting along Karkyra
And guarding Chong-Kegen,
The Muslim host rushed forward,
Crying down the valley.
Dust billowed to the sky.
To inflict severe torment
And demand the horse,
The Kalmyk host rushed forward.
The army spread across the steppe,
Engulfing it not a little but greatly,
Dividing into many kinds.
The ground cracked and thundered;
The brass horn shrieked.
The drum was beaten loudly;
The surging multitude of lords
Advanced in a closed mass.
Dust flew and haze burned;
Armored men stood apart,
And their heroes appeared.
The multitude covered the Ili like black worms;
When one looked,
Flying earth and dust hid it.
Dust struck against dust
And curled into the sky.
As though making an attack,
The people poured toward the horses
Like black worms.
Heads of beasts collided,
And spearheads struck one another.
When rough hill country came,
They crossed it with force.
Then your lion Sultan Alma
Covered the piebald horse
With his abay cloak.
Your elder Ala flew like an arrow,
Riding steadily along the road,
Making his headdress tower.
Your dear dark hero Almam—
His ancestor was Chylaba.
Wearing Chylaba's coat,
The poor man labored on,
Putting on the Chinese crown.
Upon that crown
Were coral and pearl ornaments.
The great Chinese of Kakanchyn
Did not recognize your elder Ala,
And their poor heads were confounded.
The spectacles of gray-maned Lord Almam
Gleamed;
Flask-sized coral stones
Shone upon his crown.
The Chinese who saw him cried, "My khan,"
And stared in amazement.
Your elder Ala rode on,
Preparing his feigned gait.
Foal of a bay mare,
Tulpar of the black herd—
If the leopard's Akkula
Meets me head-on,
I will charge and seize its reins,
And bring it to my horse's side.
I will raise the battle cry
Of the Black Chinese and Manchus,
Lead it between
Infidel and Muslim,
And thus win honor.
