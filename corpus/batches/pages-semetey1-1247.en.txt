Brave Semey, son of Manas,
A hero vast as river and lake,
Quickly invoking Babedin,
To the path of the one God
He pledged six fat horses;
To the spirit of his father Manas
He also pledged a white-gray mare.
To the spirit of his elder,
He pledged a two-humped camel.
Destroying the kalday was hard work.
Gathering troops from many lands,
When he saw Kulchoro,
The dear prince could not remain still;
His heart softened and he sobbed.
“I have seen your figure—
Am I awake, or is this a dream?
Has God truly granted it?
My Kulchoro, small as a knucklebone,
From Toltoy, huge as a mountain,
Have you truly escaped and returned?
Have my eyes truly seen you?
My figure standing before me,
My support left to me
By the lions who ruled as kings!”
The prince went to him sobbing;
They met safe and sound,
And he welcomed the boy.
“Ah, Almighty, Babedin!
For a boy such as you,
I would sacrifice my dark life!”
When the prince said this,
The boy said:
“My dear elder,
For a heroic elder like you,
I would sacrifice my dark life!
On hard ground there is no cradle;
I let the fleeing enemy escape—
For wretched me there is no blessed portion.
In a level place there is no cradle;
I let the departing enemy escape—
For wretched me there is no blessed portion.
Blue-maned elder, do not grieve.
May I sink away unborn;
Rather than live powerless,
May I die and live no more!
My dear elder,
Had you not thrust your spear without measure,
Had he not made his spear lunge,
Would I not have undone
Swaggering Toltoy's stratagem?
A blue haze gathered in the sky;
Your Toltoy who came to the frontier
Fled openly and escaped.
A black cloud gathered;
Your Toltoy who came to take a wife—
May this wretched world come to ruin—
Fled plainly and escaped.
At swaggering Toltoy
I thrust twelve spears at once.
The bronze lance in my hand
I spared through my inexperience;
Father, alas, what shall I do?
I spared it through childishness.
Toltoy fled; I thrust at him,
And I was left helpless and empty-handed.
When He makes a mortal, He makes a giant;
Rather than make a person powerless,
Why does Almighty God
Not leave him uncreated?
I reached him and thrust my stout spear;
This poor head, born for hardship,
Let the fleeing enemy get away.
Here is sovereign Afghan land;
Who let the fleeing enemy escape!
Here am I, poor Choro!
Here is the Argyn people with cities;
