Would they not all recognize the treachery?
If we let the Chinese slaughter
The mere handful of Muslims,
On the dawn of Judgment Day,
Will they not ride us like black donkeys?
One day we will die in the end;
If the creatures accuse us,
Saying we surrendered it from fear,
What answer will we give?
While I possess robust strength,
How can I give away my racehorse!
Let me contend
With the Chinese gathering strength.
To the Chinese infidels before me,
While my falcon head remains,
How can I give my thoroughbred!
If they commit outright violence,
Let me grapple with them!
If the Kalmyks bring disaster,
I will drive them toward Altai.
Now I will slice the scalp
Of damned Zholoy!
The seething Chinese people
Seem eager for it;
Let me fight them to the death!
I will mount Akkula;
The swine have multiplied,
And I will drive them as far as Kakan.
Fed full at the feast, these Chinese
Seem to have grown ravenous.
They seem to have gone mad,
Becoming men eager to wrestle.
If the Chinese behave like this,
I will pull out their stopper.
If the dogs start tearing at us,
I will slaughter them utterly.
I will take the horse
Of the infidel who acts as tyrant.
If these Chinese act as tyrants,
I will slaughter them to the last.
If they find warfare pleasing,
I will slaughter them until they wither.
If this is truly their intent,
I will remain and trade spears!
I will first offer warm words:
‘You came as guests; stop.’
I will go to the baldheads
Of the stone-carrying Chinese.
If they do not heed my words,
I will fight them to destruction.”
Thus brave Manas spoke,
His eyes like twilight mist,
His face like oiled wheat,
Venom pouring from his face,
His form like a naked blade.
When seen from the front,
Your magnanimous Sultan Manas
Had the dread of a dragon.
When seen from behind,
He had the dread of a thousand men.
Preparing his weapons and gear,
If you saw your gray wolf,
He raged like a blue tiger.
Making his dun horse prance,
He split straight through the center
Of the seething infidels.
Toward Zholoy and Ezkara,
Those calamity-bringers,
He turned his horse's head.
Khan Manas rode through
And stood wrathfully
Beside those two:
“You assembled Chinese people,
This is Kökötöy's feast.
The Burut are not a headless people;
Gray-wolf Manas is their head!
I honored you with an invitation;
Eat your meat with honor.
