The last of the six lions,
You are truly a hero!
Bloodstained warrior, your prowess
I prized and knew long ago.
'To take you by the hand,
To lend a hand and support you,'
This I had been asking.
You are guileless, you are a lion;
You are an openhearted warrior.
Your ancestral spirit was created greater;
You are a lion who shows no mercy.
If you strike with battle-axe and spear,
If you fight amid battle cries,
And in wrath against the enemy
Stand facing them,
I shall keep you from the wrong road
And set you on the right path myself.
If you roar in anger,
If you call, 'Shai Ata!'
I shall ride a gray horse vast as the sky
And raise a piercing battle cry,
