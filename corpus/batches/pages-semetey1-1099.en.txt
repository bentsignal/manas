He put each household in proper order,
The fires of the teeming multitude
He had extinguished,
And moved them all together.
He had the ornate gray yurts'
Wall frames and roof poles folded,
And loaded them on red camels.
They dismantled the yurts without straying,
And crossed the water without delay;
They drew the red camels' tails
Diagonally up the slope.
Calling it Khan Kökötöy's migration,
He made all the people
See its splendor.
Kökötöy's great wealth
He had loaded on countless gelded camels.
He arrayed them all in splendor
And covered them with robes of honor.
He dressed the graceful girls
In red robes,
Letting the hems of their red robes
Trail diagonally upon the ground,
And Kökötöy's son Bokmurun
Made his prowess known.
He dressed the brides in kete cloth,
Letting the hems of their kete coats
Spread wide and touch the ground,
And Kökötöy's son Bokmurun
Made his generosity known to the people.
He had the swift horse Maaniker
Tethered beneath a rich caparison.
He opened up forty rooms of yellow gold
And spread it all on display.
He had all that gold
Folded into great bundles
And loaded on yellow gelded camels.
He extinguished the fires lit
By the Kyrgyz of Tashkent
And moved them all together.
He raised skyward
The red banner with its golden finial;
Infidels and Muslims alike,
Toward the frontier at Karkyra,
He set them heading for Karkyra.
When Kökötöy's son Bokmurun
Moved out from Tashkent,
He had me mounted on a dappled bay pacer,
Dressed me in a robe of silk,
And led me along to the feast.
He had me mounted on a yellow-dappled pacer,
Dressed me in a robe of honor,
And led me among the multitude.
At that time my head
Barely rose above the saddle basket;
At that time my age
Had barely reached six years.
At Kökötöy's memorial feast,
Koshoy went and I went;
Alas, you are Kozho—
When did you ever go with us?
These are the words I have spoken,
Saying you are an extraordinary Kozho.
That was the time I went and saw
Kökötöy's great multitude.
Tens of thousands of massed camels,
And joining those camels,
Many more camels in abundance.
Everyone who saw
Kökötöy's caravan was amazed,
And many people asked one another:
“Good people, whose are these countless camels?
They are the camels of heroic Khan Kökötöy,
The camels that carry rice.
Whose are these countless men on foot?
They are Kökötöy's footmen,
Who render service.”
