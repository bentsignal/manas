My Syrgak was a gift.
For a poor soul fallen into trouble,
My Syrgak was needed!”
Heroes Almambet and Chubak wept.
Leaning on the gray short musket,
Khan Manas, scarcely able to stand,
Also stood wailing and weeping.
When he came close to the three,
These were Syrgak's words:
“I was your hope; give it up!
My cherished ones, three lions,
Sever your hopes of me!
A battle-axe struck my temple.
If I do not die, let me make my way
Into distant Mecca!
