And all the food inside spilled out;
Thus was the sorrow
Of the leopard-hearted gray wolf revealed.
He had discerned the true meaning,
And the cauldron possessed by the enemy
He had cleaved into seven pieces.
The lion who yearned for his worthy kinsmen
Came up and looked;
As he prayed for the people’s safety,
Kanchoro, who had been on guard,
Came running, shouting,
“Prepare yourselves—the enemy is coming!”
He summoned the two heroes.
The camel-hide war drum—
Flee far away when you hear its voice—
At the command,
The champion beats it in haste,
And the eardrums of lesser men
Simply burst.
The signal was firmly sounded,
And the many warriors who had hung the cauldron
All mounted their horses.
If battle brings a mighty clash,
What regret could the people have?
Then Kanchoro shouted,
And at the beginning of his speech
Called out as though bearing glad tidings:
“O my dears, have you seen what I have seen?
Külchoro and Semetey,
You two glorious heroes, make ready!
Have you sensed what I have sensed?
A massed host comes without breaking formation,
And within that host
Comes a fire blazing like a beacon,
An army that will burn all in its path;
When an army comes, it comes in multitudes,
Like a deep-dug trench and rampart.
Raising their tasseled banners,
Enemies of every kind are coming.
When I behold the approaching host
And pray for the people’s safety,
I see the vast army of the Jediger,
So vast the earth seems to bend beneath it.
I heard reports from those who came:
They say it encamped along the Jetisuu;
They say Kyyaz, hero of the Jediger,
Has become a thousand times
Greater than brave Toltoy.
Keeping court and reigning as khan,
Growing overbearing and ruling the people,
With houses spread across the hills,
Among the Jediger of Jetisuu
He has become the sole sovereign khan.
The generous Jediger people
Are now a body without a head,
Subdued by brave Kyyaz;
He comes ravaging lands and driving nations before him,
Leading his lions onward.
He comes leading heroes whose blows
Would scatter carrion to the winds,
Leading lions who spill blood
Whenever they enter combat.
A host of countless thousands comes,
Not a small force but a multitude;
If we rush boastfully into battle,
Our fleeting lives will face great peril.
No man could defeat them;
No stratagem could hold out against them.
No envoy could pass between us,
And none who meet them will survive.
Divided into many formations,
The Jediger warriors
Appear almost numberless.
Along the shore road by Kum-Kyya,
They come pouring forth,
Swarming so thickly they seem like worms,
Streaming out in splendor.
