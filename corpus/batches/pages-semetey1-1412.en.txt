May Kongurbai burn to death—
Kongurbai's watch-post,
With foxes upon its pass,
Argali upon its heights,
And ducks upon its lake;
That fire-doomed swine's
Calamity lies in such a place.
The place is famed as Suuk-Tör;
It has a pass where men take courage,
A place where they lie entrenched.
The most advantageously placed of his guards,
The finest of his sentries,
Is that vile infidel Kuuördök.
If we capture it, my only one,
God will have granted it to us;
If the duck escapes,
May God strike us!

Look at the people who will engulf us!

And multitudes from Kangai.
They cannot match me in a contest,

That duck is a sharp-eyed scout;
If Kuuördök reaches Beijing,
It will spread the news in every direction.
Its wing is fierce and formidable,
And it has the habit
Of cutting down whatever it faces.
It has lords and it has a khan;
In that great city of Beijing
Are marvels beyond all hearing!
If that duck catches sight of us,
It will carry the news to Beijing.
Understand all that I have told you;
My precious son, heed my words!
The honored Kalcha is a great lord;
If that infidel is roused to anger,
He will sweep out from Kakan!
These are no people to trust.
This Beijing before us
Has might and has a city;
In mighty Beijing
Are all the fiercest forces!"
Old Bakai said:
"Scout the upper side well!
If we catch the duck,
We shall set our trouble right;
If we pray to the Creator,
My young champion found late in life,
We shall contrive to take their duck!"
At the pass of It-Kyian,
After Bakai spoke these words,
They went up to the duck's watch-lake,
To the reeds upon the lake's surface,
Which the Chinese call Bangpang.
