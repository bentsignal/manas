They began a storied battle.
Standing with faces transformed,
They leveled and thrust their spears.
Making one place their battlefield,
They fought by turns.
They sent forth their wrestlers,
And brought out their heroes
To fight single combat.
From this side came one champion,
From that side came another.
They leveled spears one against one
And fought in single combat.
An Afghan champion
Reached the Jediger;
A Jediger champion
Crushed him into the ground and passed on.
From behind, the Jediger
Wheeled and surged over them;
From this side, Khan Akun
Unleashed the general battle.
Dust curled into the sky
And poured from the melee.
Commanders of tens swept like sickles;
Commanders of fifty barked.
Their spears jutted upward.
Commanders of hundreds ran;
Commanders of thousands grieved.
Standards flapped,
All the khans cried out,
And fierce slaughter began.
Jediger and Afghan
Met face to face.
It became a grave and perilous affair;
They unleashed a general battle.
Before long,
Kadyrbay, son of Kalcha,
Bearing seven sacred standards,
Raising an earth-splitting clamor,
With dust curling into the sky,
Poured in from the flank
And began battle anew.
Tümönbay, son of Tülkü,
And Ajybay, son of Ajy,
Had gathered an exceptional army;
They too had an answer
To give the Jediger.
Many heroes stood side by side;
Ajybay, son of Ajy,
And broad-throated Kadyrbay
Now made a spectacle.
Girding on his Oroipo cartridge pouch,
Ajybay, son of Ajy,
Charged in without sparing himself.
They stood face to face,
Leveling their spears in silence;
No bungler was allowed to flee,
And heroes emerged after thrusting foes.
It became a clumsy, brutal battle;
Black muskets cracked,
Fierce guns boomed.
Slaughter spread starkly;
Many precious heroes
Lay dead and outstretched.
The earth became hazy with vapor;
The armies seethed in uproar.
For those who knew and saw it,
The tumult never healed.
Many brave men died;
Those who remained alive
Were drenched in black blood.
The host glittered and heaved,
Having entered the clamor.
Many who had not hastened before
Now entered the tumult.
The brave roared,
While cowards trembled at the edge.
The grief-stricken cowards
Fled away one by one.
