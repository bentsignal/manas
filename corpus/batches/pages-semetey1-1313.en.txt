His mind turned elsewhere,
And the treachery of the swaggering butcher
Became apparent.
Their quarrel was over a horse,
And Kanchoro became estranged.
With his eyes clouded,
Kanchoro came to seek a fight.
He grieved over Surkoyon,
And in the end became an enemy.
All the Jediger
Were slaughtered by the blue-maned wolf,
Both Chynkojo and old Toltoy.
They had quarreled precisely
Over Akun's daughter Ayçürök,
And all were slaughtered.
Among the great Afghan people,
The best at taking spoil
Took six hundred horses each;
The lesser men who could not seize as much
Took sixty horses each.
The accomplished blue-maned wolves
Took seven hundred spoils each.
The lesser men who could not reach them
Took seventy spoils each.
They struck the Jediger and took cloth,
Loaded cloth and took gold coins.
As spoil from that people,
They took five girls with braided forelocks.
There was honor among all who knew;
Khan Akun's Afghanistan,
Saying, “Our hostile enemy is dead,”
Set its heart at ease and was fulfilled.
They slaughtered a mare for Babadin,
And in their joy scattered
Yellow gold from the treasury.
Old Akun of Afghanistan
Climbed atop the tower
And made a proclamation.
After God had truly bestowed his favor,
After he had seen Semetey,
“My dear, great people,
Now that my extinguished fire has kindled,
I have resolved in joy:
I will hold Chürök's wedding feast.
“I will slaughter ninety sheep for the feast,
And slaughter all fitting animals as offerings.
I will gather the multitude,
And make merry to gladden the people;
For Ayçürök and Semetey,
For these two I will hold a wedding feast.
My child Ayçürök,
Yesterday, when my beard was already white,
While I was on a journey,
In the desert of Mediyan,
At the shrine of Babadin,
On the pass of the direct road,
I found my Chürök.
There are two fateful sayings;
Some people say
Chürök is a peri's daughter.
Could I fail to give my noble child
To her rightful master?”
Driving seventy red camels,
To bear loads when they migrated,
He fully prepared the pack animals;
Khan Akun made his testament.
Having overcome the great enemy,
Semetey received great honor.
