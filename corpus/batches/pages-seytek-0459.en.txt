Saying, “God has restored the magnanimous lord,”
The Black Giant stood rejoicing.
Külchoro stood there distraught,
From time to time crying aloud
And pleading for protection
As he begged his elder brother.
His elder brother stood unmoved,
And Semetey held all
His many golden, honeyed words
Of no more account than his boot-heel.
Speaking softly, then firmly,
Your wretched lion Külchoro
Let loose and began his speech.
Among the spearmen, this Choro
Possessed great prowess;
He could face six divisions of foes,
And possessed great strength.
The dark ridge with its lofty crest
Was truly a mountain pass.
He was the renowned, fortunate one,
Who, when the whole world made war,
Went forth alone into battle.
When he spoke, his words were lucid and clear,
Eloquent in speech and keen of sight—
Külchoro himself was plain and forthright.
Broad-jawed and fluent-tongued,
He was a great orator who never faltered.
From hellish torment and affliction
He had escaped many times.
Such was Külchoro’s nature:
He sharpened his dark tongue
And declaimed before the people;
Though the contest lasted months and years,
He neither stumbled nor tired;
Though humiliated and forced to plead,
He never let his hands and feet be bound.
His words spread like sugar;
He was created unlike other men.
He had received the people’s warrant
And the saints’ blessing.
Yet his heart would not soften upon the riverbank,
Nor would their blood mingle, though it boiled.
He had not seen that radiant face.
However eloquently Külchoro spoke,
He received no word of well-being.
He gave not the slightest answer,
Nor acknowledged the mighty warrior,
But tormented him with force.
He acted only through gestures,
His ears deaf and his eyes blind.
The poor, bewildered people
Had fallen into Külchoro’s hands,
There at the cave where they stood.
Külchoro kept hold of him,
Refusing to release his lord,
Holding his precious one motionless.
He held fast to his tent-pole,
And held his hero as a gift.
The precious falcon had been found,
And the beloved Choro’s precious
Honor was thus restored.
His grief dispersed and lifted;
Brave Külchoro’s
Once-blinded eyes opened,
And he beamed, crying, “Elder brother!”
The honorable kinsman-warrior’s
Moonlike face shone brightly.
Even now, to cross away like a spirit,
Semetey stood drawing back.
The Black Giant and Külchoro
Seized the bloodthirsty warrior together.
Külchoro stood thinking this:
“Long ago my khan and elder brother
Ruled the people and cared for the nation,
And was a great lord;
But now, today, my lion of an elder brother,
Speaking no words and uttering no speech,
Has become mute.”
Külchoro stood grieving.
“What I said has come true,” he thought.
