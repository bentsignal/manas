Black as a crow,
Ravaging the people and ruining the land,
When the people speak of the wicked,
Of those who fluster and panic,
Their heads shall be struck off with swords;
If anyone dares to mock,
His eyes shall be gouged clean out.
Such is my fierce wrath—
You shall obey my command!
If you do not obey my command,
Your vast host covering the earth
Will be slaughtered!
Not one of you will remain;
You will go to your doom.
When Sarybai gathered his forces,
The people clamored and an uproar arose,
The war drums thundered,
Sword points quivered,
Spearheads swayed.
God forbid the sight:
They roared their battle cry,
Shouting and raising the war cry,
Crushing all the people,
Raging without cease,
The great conflict plain to see,
They came surging forth,
A myriad host swarming like worms,
Came pouring forth.
The rabid madman Sarybai
Followed behind that army,
Driving it onward.
Too numerous to distinguish,
The God-accursed myriad host
Came swarming forth.
Behold the arrows raining down,
Behold the unbroken cannon fire,
Behold the clamoring host.
Behold the pits they dug,
The foes who pitched camp to fight,
Who battled without retreat;
Behold the army driven onward,
And Sarybai commanding it,
The tyrant who destroyed nations.
Their banner rose like the moon,
Their shouting became a tumult,
They surged like a raging flood,
A multitude filling the land;
At the sight, the mind reeled,
And splendid Sarybai
Towered like a green poplar.
That infidel Sarybai—
Throughout the whole wide world,
That shield-wielding warrior—
See his brows like a crouching hound,
See his head as large as a cauldron,
His beard like a quiver case,
The breath issuing from his mouth
Like flame from a bellows.
Ask no more of that wretch;
Behold what he looked like:
His nose like a jagged ice ridge,
As though he would devour the world.
A fierce and venomous man,
Whose menace made men panic,
As though he would slaughter the whole world
And stride on, licking it clean.
Such was the bloodthirsty tyrant.
He thundered before his army;
Unable to endure his wrath,
The great host fled in thunder.
He grew ever more menacing,
Raged and made ready;
A single hair of his mustache
Was like the haft of a weighted whip.
Furious and maddened,
Assuming a hundred fearsome forms—
Such was his appearance.
Allowing no warrior ahead of him,
He loomed like a grain sack.
