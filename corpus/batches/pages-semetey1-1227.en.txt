Has God struck my eyes?
Before these benighted eyes,
Everything appears black.
From fording water in the darkness,
It seems the water has risen into my eyes.
What can this be?" he said,
And quickly dismounted.
Along the single horse-track
He reached down and felt with his hand.
A track met his hand;
The places struck by the hooves
Were hollowed like a ravine,
Carved as deep as an irrigation ditch.
Had a giant walked
Upon this desolate road?
Had those pursuing reached it?
Or had a monster
Passed along this road?
Had those who were coming truly arrived,
And had my appointed death come?
Drinking vodka, he grew excited;
Hero Toltoi, heated with drink,
Spoke without knowing he would die.
Dear Toltoi the leopard
Roared in his anger
And thundered with courage:
"If they call me a hero, a hero I am;
If a teeming great enemy appears,
I shall enter alone and test them.
I gird my accomplished waist;
If an army of seven myriads comes,
I shall enter alone and attack.
While this head remains,
If Akun has an enemy,
I shall do all I know;
His army too great for the earth to bear
I alone shall slaughter.
If it is an enemy from Beijing,
If it brings grievous peril,
I shall drive it as far as Beijing;
If it is a Muslim army,
I shall drive it as far as Mecca.
While this fortunate head remains,
I shall smash its fortress walls
And drive it away completely.
If Akun proves honest,
I shall give gold by the foot
And try meeting him in friendship.
If Akun proves treacherous,
I shall drive and crush him
And divide his village in two.
If he does not submit to my words,
If he does not give his old maid,
I shall smash his lattice and roof-poles,
Make his daughters-in-law and girls weep,
And crush his city-dwelling people.
If these Afghans distress me,
I shall send the Zhediger engulfing them
And tread upon their heads.
Unless God strikes me,
If they call me a hero, a hero I am;
If an army of one myriad appears,
I alone shall test it.
Whoever resists me,
I shall take together with the sword;
If an eager enemy comes,
I shall put it to slaughter.
If an army of one myriad meets me,
It is my turn to bring it ruin.
Now is the time to put on
My smooth bulletproof coat;
Now is the time to assail
The whole world;
Now is the time to mount
Cloud-flying Surkoyon.
If enemies of six ages appear,
If a mingled challenge arises,
Today, without wavering,
