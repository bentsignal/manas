He burned forests into desert.
When he advanced, the enemy grieved;
His heroic, lionhearted prowess
Became known throughout the world.
The enemies who encountered him
Fell from their horses like severed sheaves.
When my uncle grew wrathful,
The people of the khagan fell.
He threw all who faced him into uproar,
And brought strife upon all he met.
He leveled even the soaring Ala-Too
With consummate skill;
He saved the Kyrgyz of Ala-Too from suffering
And made them a united people.
Building as he went, he made them homes;
Planting as he went, he made them orchards,
And guarded Talas as their homeland.
Among the peoples of the world, that noble soul
Made the renowned Choro a lion.
My uncle Semetey
Was perfect in every way;
If you looked within his heart,
It was broader than the face of the earth.
Such was my uncle,
Whose homeland was broad Talas,
Whose father was mighty, magnanimous Manas.
When I gathered together
The four corners of the earth,
When I subdued the whole wide world
And ruled it,
When I honed
The black-steel Zulpukor,
When I drove onward
All the peoples of the world,
Breaking ice to make a road
And bringing evildoers low,
Breaking rock to make a road
And bringing challengers low,
When I pursued all who fled
And let none escape,
When an enemy truly attacked,
If he grappled, I sent him on his final road
And dealt out punishment with my own hand.
No mortal dared face me;
No attacking enemy defeated me.
When I leveled my spear, it became flame;
When an enemy attacked, he ceased to exist.
He crossed the Urgench and opened a road;
He slaughtered heroic Kojo and brave Toltoy
And laid the two of them low.
He unleashed his fury fiercely
And slaughtered every warrior
Who boasted of his valor.
He broke through ravines and opened roads,
And laid hands upon the whole world.
A panther like a tiger,
My spearman uncle’s prowess
Was indeed beyond compare.
It was well known throughout the world
That his might was unsurpassed.
Plainly renowned and noble-browed,
His gray-maned valor stood apart.
Many called my tiger uncle Külchoro
A tiger among men,
Who devoured all he encountered.
When he tore men apart, he never had his fill of blood;
My uncle was a ravenous wolf.
My uncle, sustained by the sacred spirit,
Was called the people’s protector.
While strength remained across his broad back,
He made his gray-brown stallion dance;
Though countless myriads of foes came,
He hacked them down with his sword and subdued them,
Soaking his blade in blood.
Whoever provoked his wrath,
He killed and cast upon the ground.
Thick-armed and stone-hearted,
A full-limbed, bold young warrior,
He brought great calamity upon all he overtook.
He broke mountains and leveled the land.
