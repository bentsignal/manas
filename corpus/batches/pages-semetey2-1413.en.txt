He long ago surpassed them all.
He buried every sworn enemy
In the earth without digging a grave;
He is the mightiest among the people.”
Against enemies in bitter combat
He had caused many a conflict.
Of your elder Külchoro the slave,
Küzhürmön had cut open
Both rows of his shoulder blades,
Breaking through the forest to make a road;
He humiliated my elder,
Külchoro, lion among men,
Opening those wounds,
Inflicting calamity,
Dealing him humiliation,
And beginning his violence.
If one keeps telling it, it never ends;
There is still much in the unfinished tale.
But the young hero Karadöö
Was there to joust with him.
Beyond lay Keng-Sai and Alai;
The Karadöö you see
Held much within his heart:
If the spear cast as an omen
Found its proper chance, he would
Offer Küzhürmön as a sacrifice,
Drive his spear in to the shaft,
And plunge it wherever he faced.
His insides had long burned with misery
To avenge Külchoro;
Such was the poor man's state.
Taking the contest as his omen,
Karadöö's heroic heart was troubled;
Tears flowed down both cheeks
And poured over his collarbones.
He looked at nothing around him,
Gave no thought to his own death;
Taking up the spear without concern,
He counted Kyyaz's massed host
As less than the hem of his garment.
He wore a gray brocade coat
And rode a gray Badam horse;
He adorned and tightly knotted
The tail of his swift mount.
Like a molting vulture he glowered,
Like a crouching tiger he advanced.
When Küzhürmön entered the contest,
The sight dismayed the heart;
His build and great size
Looked like a gray hill.
He sprang like a tiger;
None who fought him would survive.
He charged like a wild boar,
Tore like a wolf,
And lumbered like a bear.
Küzhürmön came to strike with his spear
Before one could even cry, “Speak!”
He could sling anyone he met
Across his saddle and descend the ridge;
If anyone turned aside,
He could catch him even at a furious gallop.
Enraged, he seemed to pursue the fleeing;
Küzhürmön would seize and crush
Anyone who opposed him.
His face was hard as iron,
His exertion solid as a cliff;
His surging seemed to shake the world,
As though he would swallow anyone who turned.
He seemed able to win
The offered prize without a word.
Küzhürmön had come;
Huge and well matched to a hero,
He entered the spear contest that time,
And possessed immense strength.
He had jostled the whole world;
In contests with the spear,
The madman had won every prize
Without letting anyone utter a sound.
