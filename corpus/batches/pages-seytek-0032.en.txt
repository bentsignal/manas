Saying, ‘Now he has come of age,’
Heed these words.
If a son is born, Jediger,
Slaughter countless beasts for his feast,
And put aside all such
Slurred and foolish talk.
Let us strike down whoever goes astray;
So none may babble slander,
My people, let us lick
The edge of the sword!”
They kissed the bullets and swore an oath.
When the Jediger heard this, they said:
“Enough, warrior Kutu,
What you say is sound counsel.
May the seven forefathers of anyone
Who disobeys you be infidels.
Do not babble, Jediger;
Our father Kutu’s words
Are wisdom.”
All manner of men live among a people;
If someone should suddenly babble,
Or rave without warning,
Let us beat him to pieces
And punish him cruelly.
Let us ignore his pleas
And gouge out his eyes in punishment.
Let us raid his mares from the pasture;
One who spreads such idle talk
May well have his wrath aroused.”
The Jediger shouted and the people stirred,
And they sealed their pledge.
After Kutu had spoken these words,
The Jediger dispersed.
The people departed one by one,
And the Jediger rode away.
While matters stood thus among the Jediger,
The Jediger’s brave Kyyaz
Was an extraordinary champion,
Yet Kyyaz’s treachery
Was as vast as Ala-Too.
He would weary those who fought him
And make those who grappled with him crawl.
He would bewilder whoever met him
And straighten whoever opposed him.
No man could face him.
If this son of yours survives,
He will ride along the Sombuk River,
And the man who contests him will crawl.
He will meet anyone who challenges him;
If your son matures into a champion,
Whoever clashes with him will crawl.
If he turns, strife will begin;
Many who see him
Will break ranks and flee.
When the child is old enough to play,
Let us hang a sword from his neck.
Let no winged creature strike him,
No beaked creature peck him;
Let him drink from no dirty vessel
And wear no soiled clothing.
Saying, “He is heroic Kyyaz’s son,
Himself a manifest hero,”
Let us cherish him as our protector.
If by chance she bears a son,
Whoever says this or that,
Whoever feeds on filthy gossip,
Let us cut off his head with a sword
And utterly destroy
Every scrap of his property!
If someone suddenly babbles,
If the people should rave without warning,
Brand him a corrupter of the nation
And give him that name.
If there is some prattling wretch,
Seize him and gouge out his eyes.
Let us plunder much from him
And levy a heavy fine;
If some fool raves,
Drag him along the road
And hand him over to the executioners.
