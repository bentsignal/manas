# Manuscript digitization lead, checked 2026-09-14

[Azattyk, 15 March 2019](https://www.azattyk.org/a/kyrgyzstan_manuscript_literature/29821335.html) reports that the Academy manuscript collection was 80% digitized. Its interview with collection head Asel Isaeva says the material included Manas variants and old books; another interview distinguishes original manuscripts, Cyrillic transcriptions, and computer transcriptions. The article also records disagreement about selective publication and acknowledges that some material was not included in printed books.

The inspected article supplies no item-level download for Karalaev inventories 925–953 or 960–966. Its percentage is a historical collection-wide claim, not evidence that this project possesses every verse or that all scans are currently public. No new source PDF was acquired from this lead. Existing institutional request drafts remain unsent.

The old Academy 2018 Bagysh Sazanov publication announcement now redirects to the Academy homepage. It cannot establish the contents of a Karalaev edition.
