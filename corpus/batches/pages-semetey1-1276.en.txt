On seeing their dead,
Many went about in anguish.
Saying, “That fucking dog Kozho
Has done this to us,”
Many went weeping.
They dismounted and crowded forward,
Hanging their belts around their necks;
Before Semetey then,
Many begged for their lives in supplication
And witnessed
The Creator's command.
Many merely swooned and fell,
Lying as though dead.
Many wandered about
With their saddles around their necks.
Their girths had broken;
Their eyes darted about,
Their legs hung limp,
And many ran in panic.
With no tebetei on their heads,
Barefoot and bareheaded,
Many fled.
Wandering in groups of five and ten,
Many scattered apart.
Many appeared
With swift horses beneath them.
Many sought out their own torment
And became entangled.
Unable to gather their senses,
They abandoned and cast off their horses;
Many became trapped
In pits and hollows.
The Jediger people shrieked.
When the lion prince Semetey
Charged through the army,
They lost their wits
And fled shrieking.
Wherever his spear reached,
He slaughtered them with the spear.
It was a time when he did
Wonders no person had seen.
Wherever his sword reached,
He hacked them with the sword;
It was a time when he slaughtered them.
It was a time when he did to them
What had never been done.
The surging Jediger
Had fallen into panic.
Unable to gather their senses
Before the leopard prince and sultan,
They had fled.
Let us leave that there
And begin the tale
Of Kulchoro, son of Almambet:
Raising high
The red banner left by Manas,
Shouting, “Manas! Manas!”
And crying Manas's battle cry,
Facing Khan Toltoy's army,
The leopard boy Kulchoro
Drove in and scattered them.
Looking all around,
Licking his lips and nose,
His two eyes darting,
His two legs dangling,
He darted in from the side.
When they came before him,
Kulchoro thrust again and again
And flung them with his spear.
Did you see the consummate skill
Of Choro, Almambet's son?!
Did you see the earth-filling Jediger
Reduced to nothing?
Choro appeared like a wolf;
When he plunged among them,
All the Jediger
Split into separate groups.
Wounded, they went away,
