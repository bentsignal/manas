Thirty young wives and thirty girls
Had begun their games.
Your sister-in-law in their midst
Played and laughed,
Renowned Churok at their head.
Choro, who stood watching,
Felt his heart drawn toward them.
Kulchoro, who had never seen girls,
Was fascinated at the sight,
And began a great commotion.
Upon the horse Red Buurul,
Kulchoro turned aside,
Into the terribly swollen Urgench
He plunged headlong.
Seeing the girls, Kulchoro,
That wretch who had never seen girls,
Did not look all about him.
Against the terribly swollen Urgench,
He did not spare his life in the least.

No dog dared bark at it.
A short-tailed, gray-maned lion

Saying, ‘I shall confront him,’
The enemies who challenged him perished.
‘This is that black-spotted leopard,’
The Afghan people said, and honored him.
