Chynkozho, son of Shygay,
Kozho, costly and forceful in his deeds,
Of Argyn-Kyrgyz stock,
Kozho whose fortune was complete,
A renowned hero among
Round-headed mortals.
Taking instruction from Manas,
Lion Kozho had seen many things.
Dear Kozho, the brave servant,
Had a black hat large as a cauldron;
The brave-born Karkybar
Had stature worth an army.
His appearance was more fearsome than a man's;
He had the will to rule
The eighteen thousand worlds.
There was authority upon his brow;
He had the power to shout commands
To all the Zhediger.
For fear of Chynkozho's wrath,
No person came repeatedly before him.
The vast black Zhediger,
Led by Khan Toltoy,
Gave him no answer back.
At that time no one
Paid Kozho any heed;
When Kozho grew impetuous,
No one remained to turn back his lasso.
His menace was fierce, his venom strong;
When Chynkozho grew angry,
He flattened the Zhediger.
When true frenzy seized him,
Chynkozho's Black Horse
Had wings
Along its flanks.
When Chynkozho grew angry,
He habitually struck all the Zhediger
And crushed them like grain.
He had a people who raged;
Have you heard the news?
When his courage flared,
He customarily drank the blood
Of all the Zhediger.
Brave Kozho, son of Shygay,
Had he not brought upon them
Turmoil no person had seen?
Promising to obtain Aychürök,
Had he not driven and brought
The vast black Zhediger,
All of them rank by rank?
To bring disorder upon Afghanistan,
To seize Aychürök,
Had he not led here
All the Zhediger?
Had the Zhediger not helplessly left
Their own wealth behind
In Zheti-Özön?
When the sun sank shimmering,
Chynkozho, son of Shygay,
Wrapped himself in his mottled blue coat.
If you had seen Chynkozho,
He strained like a blue bear;
In anger he began
To speak harsh words to Toltoy.
Chynkozho overwhelmed
And all but devoured
Every one of the Zhediger.
All the Zhediger,
Including Tölöbay and old Kalcha, said:
"When we said Kozho had come,
It was disaster that had come!
When we thought a hero had come,
It was calamity that had come!
We were a people living at ease;
Will God strike us for his frenzy?"
