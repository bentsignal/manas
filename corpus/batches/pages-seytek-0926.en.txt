Against enemies they encountered,
Against exceedingly wrathful warriors,
Against overbearing champions.
Into the monster's black eyes,
Ready to die if death arrived,
If the Almighty desired her life,
Ready to surrender her entrusted soul,
Ready to unleash a mighty battle—
She, offspring of lionhearted heroes,
Sought from the fierce one's hands,
From death's opposing snare,
From that great menace and mighty venom,
From Sarybai's hands,
To wrest her people free.
This was the mighty tumult,
To be unleashed upon Sarybai:
To meet him face-to-face,
And clash in crushing combat.
Her eager eyes burned fiercely;
Alas, the champion Kuyaly
Watched with both eyes doubled,
And wrathfully made ready.
She put on all her battle gear,
Strapped on her naked sword,
And leveled a gleaming spear,
Ready once more.
She seized the black-shafted weighted whip
And swung it around;
Zhelzhetpes, swift as a boat,
She gripped tightly with her knees.
Crying “Allah!” she raised her spear
And charged straight at Sarybai;
Now she made the struggle fierce.
The war drum was the signal of battle;
Such was that war drum:
It was made of elephant hide,
And when struck once with a battle-axe,
The very heavens seemed to shift,
And the breadth of the earth resounded.
In battle, champions fled before it;
It was the army's foremost shield,
The herald of Kuyaly's battle.
When she struck the war drum,
Her stalwart champions followed behind.
After giving this signal,
She drove her deep-throated, secret spear
Forward with a thunderous blow;
Even that failed to bring him down,
So she struck again with a roar.
The earth broke into clouds of dust,
And all who saw it were bewildered.
Every warrior grew excited,
Every hero was stirred;
The courageous and lionhearted
Showed the valor with which they entered holy war.
It became known to all the people,
And the people despaired of life
As they crowded around to watch.
Then Kuyaly
Buttoned her armor over her whole body
And covered her full face.
Her lionhearted nature became clear;
She donned arrow-proof steel.
She rejoiced at the word “enemy”;
The warrior maiden by name
Unleashed a great battle.
What regret could such a noble soul have?
Her valor was renowned,
Her wolf-like courage plain to see.
At the sight of the foe, her eyes blazed;
Bloodlust gripped her firmly,
And she ground her teeth with a crack.
Her apple-round neck stretched,
Her eyes reddened like a lion's;
She girded her slender waist,
Cantered forth and left the army.
Her bearing and majesty,
Her proud and fearsome countenance,
Made her seem like a ravening beast.
She took up her weapons, eager for battle.
