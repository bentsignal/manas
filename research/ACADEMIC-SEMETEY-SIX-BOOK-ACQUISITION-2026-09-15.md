# Academic *Semetey* six-book acquisition search

Search date: 2026-09-15. Scope: the academic Sayakbay Karalaev *Semetey* edition prepared by Ainek/A. Zhainakova, edited by Abdylzhan Akmataliev, and published by Turar: book 1 (2015, 556 pp.) and books 2–6 (2017, 496/612/540/608/576 pp.).

## Result

No downloadable scan of this six-book edition was found in public indexing. No candidate was downloaded, so there is no new file hash. The strongest concrete access route is the Scientific Library of B. Osmonov Jalal-Abad State University (JASU/JAGU), whose November 2023 new-acquisitions catalog records one physical copy in the АХЛ department for each of books 2–6. The catalog gives the exact edition statement, preparer, editor, publisher, year, and pagination.

Primary catalog PDF: <https://jasulib.org.kg/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%9D%D0%BE%D1%8F%D0%B1%D1%80%D1%8C-2023.pdf>

The catalog records:

- Book 2: Bishkek, Turar, 2017, 496 pages; one copy, АХЛ.
- Book 3: Bishkek, Turar, 2017, 612 pages; one copy, АХЛ.
- Book 4: Bishkek, Turar, 2017, 540 pages; one copy, АХЛ.
- Book 5: Bishkek, Turar, 2017, 608 pages; one copy, АХЛ.
- Book 6: Bishkek, Turar, 2017, 576 pages; one copy, АХЛ.

The National Academy bibliography independently confirms the six-volume set and page counts: <https://kruia.gov.kg/news/show/manas-eposu-boyuncha-chykkan-kitepterdin-tizmesi/ky>. The Academy’s 2017 announcement confirms publication of all six volumes, more than 200,000 lines, preparation by Ainek Zhainakova, scientific editing by Abdylzhan Akmataliev, and a print run of 1,000: <https://naskr.kg/ky/2017/03/09/manas-eposunun-akademiyalyk-alyt-toktomu-zhakryk-kordu/>.

## Search coverage and evidence

- General exact-phrase searches used Cyrillic and transliterated variants of the title, Каралаев/Karalaev, Жайнакова/Zhainakova/Jainakova, Турар/Turar, each book number, 2015/2017, and exact page counts.
- JASU WordPress archive and upload paths were searched. Its public post for “Семетей. 1-китеп” links a 255-page Jusup Mamai edition, not this academic set: <https://jasulib.org.kg/?p=3686>.
- Internet Archive Advanced Search was queried across title, creator, description, and identifier fields. No record for the 2015/2017 six-book edition appeared. The sole title hit was a 2026 derivative “Манас — Роман-Эпопея,” which is not the target.
- Telegram public web indexes were searched with exact title/editor/year combinations. No indexed document or public post for the target set appeared.
- CiNii NCID BB18896798, ISBNs 9789967152397 and 9789967152328, describes the older 2013 two-volume edition and a physical holding at Chuo University; it is not the 2015/2017 six-book set: <https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB18896798>.
- Existing downloadable 2013 PDFs are 1,432 and 1,424 pages and therefore are not candidates for the 556/496/612/540/608/576-page edition.
- The JASU WordPress item for *Seytek* (2013, 1172 pp.), older 1987/1989 *Semetey* editions, Jusup Mamai editions, encyclopedia references, and academic articles were rejected as bibliographic neighbors rather than scans of the target.

## Strongest next route

The best non-speculative route is library-mediated access to the five catalogued JASU copies, using the exact call-number block around classification 821.51 / С 30 and the АХЛ holding noted in the November 2023 catalog. A second route is a scan-on-demand or interlibrary request based on the National Academy bibliography and the confirmed print run. No institution was contacted during this search.
