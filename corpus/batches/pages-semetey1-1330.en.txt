Let me test myself against them!
One who finds words in sudden danger,
Who finds sight in darkness;
Auspicious when placed before me,
Like a countless host when behind me;
My companion gathered in life,
My fellow believer if death comes—
My uncle Bakai, my inheritance,
I shall take as companion to Beijing.
Do not grieve, mother;
Thus shall I go to Beijing!
My support when I climb,
My prop when I descend,
Külchoro and Kanchoro—
My wings for circling flight,
My tail for coming to land;
Külchoro and Kanchoro,
The two are my younger brothers.
I shall take both nest-mates,
And against Chinese as numerous as cow hairs
Unleash such conflict!”
The fine hero, son of Manas,
The lion supported by the spirits,
Had scarcely closed his mouth
When his uncle, elder Bakai,
A saint who had seen much,
To the young son of Manas
Began to speak in a ringing voice.
These were Bakai's words:
“My foal, my son Semetey!
God granted my wish;
This very year your age
Has reached twenty-five.
Yesterday, while my companion Manas lived,
While his fortune remained whole,
He used to heed my counsel,
And listen to my admonition;
He knew what I told him.
I have spoken precious words—understand;
My blessed son, heed me!
This year you went to Khan Akun,
And newly took Aychürök;
If you wish, my son, heed me—
You have become a bloodied foot.
Remain here this year;
Let us grow fat and refreshed,
Hunt game and make sport,
Tell tales and raise a clamor,
And chase and shoot wild asses!
Let this year pass,
And go in the following year!
By the road I myself have seen,
Along valleys, springs, and streams,
Let me lead the way myself,
And against the Manchu khan of the Kechil
Let us wage war at full breadth!
My son, your strength is tired; gird your waist;
This year let us grow fat and refreshed!
If you heed me, my only one,
Let us go to Beijing next year!
My young prince's son,
Let us wage war next year!
Upon the soil of the earth
There are Muslim giants;
If you summon them, will your strength not suffice?
Gather a select army completely!
Gather a great army!
The twelve Muslim tribes—
Let us assemble them together,
Their proud warriors,
