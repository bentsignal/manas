You have wept for nothing,
You accursed Toltoi.
If Semetey had speared you, he would not spare you;
He would never leave you alive.
After Semetey comes Kanchoro—
What would you do if he speared you?
You would not remain alive, Toltoi;
You would depart this world.
Almambet was a Chinese-born man;
As for this wound that pierced you,
There is an orphan named Külchoro,
The son of Almambet.
He was the one who speared you.
Hero Toltoi of the Jediger,
You do not recognize your own folly;
Having taken a spear wound from a child,
You curse everything in sight.
Jediger—damn your father’s grave—
If you flee, you have eaten filth;
Consulting in pairs,
What more did you say?”
The many Black Jediger,
With dear Khojo at their head,
Grieved for Toltoi
And lay crowded around him.
Seething Khan Toltoi—
Let us leave him thus,
Külchoro, who failed to spear him—
Let us begin the tale from there.
Turning back and sobbing,
At the end of his recourse, Külchoro
Screamed and wept in shame:
“Oh, my deeply dug grave!
Damn your father’s grave, false world;
Oh, my sorrow boiling over!”
Saying these words then,
He heaved in fury
And trembled in grief.
“I am a champion of twelve years;
My death came, yet I did not die.
I was not said to have died by Toltoi
And did not behold the next world.
Before reaching my full strength,
I did not die belatedly.
My beloved uncle,
Without caring for his horse’s worth,
Gave me his horse unstintingly.
What shall I do with this hellish world?
My mountain-like uncle,
Without caring for his armor’s worth,
Gave me his armor unstintingly.
Mountain-like great Toltoi
Fled bobbing away; I speared at him—
How can I return, unworthy?
My heart sinks; death is just.
If I serve faithfully, my heart is pure;
After turning back, unable to spear
That doomed Toltoi,
Death is better than this disgrace!
My beloved khan and uncle,
Saying, ‘May his heart be rich with fat,’
Fed him thick slices from the ribs;
When did he ever let
Argyn or Kyrgyz call me a mere child?
We kicked together as foals and yearlings;
My leopard uncle and I
Fought to suck the same breast.
The stallion that could carry Altai—
Unable to take that fool Toltoi,
I stand wailing and weeping.
Toltoi of the Jediger—
I thought him a hero, but he was lesser;
The accursed, pale Toltoi
Was level with the black earth.
When I shouted and leveled my spear,
Though I am twelve years old,
I lacked the strength to take Toltoi.
