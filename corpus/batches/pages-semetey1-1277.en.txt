All fleeing in a stream.
As though they could rescue them,
They all together
Took refuge with Khan Toltoy.
Kulchoro raged,
Darting in from the side;
As though skewering tebetei hats,
With the tip of his spear
He whirled and flung them.
Crushing his golden face,
He made ready like a lion,
Hounding the foul Toltoy,
Crushing seven hundred faces.
Kulchoro, fierce as an executioner,
Hounded the raging Toltoy.
Toltoy, who killed those who grappled him—
Kulchoro hounded that infidel.
Toltoy, who came to seize a wife—
Kulchoro hounded that hero.
Toltoy, who drove the Jediger army—
Kulchoro hounded that skillful man.
Toltoy, who took maidens as booty—
Kulchoro hounded that mighty man.
Toltoy, who bore the Alengir bow and bronze arrow—
Kulchoro hounded him.
Toltoy would not come to fight;
He took refuge and hid.
Scattering the Jediger,
Kulchoro raged
And drove Khan Toltoy away.
His wits gone in confusion,
Kulchoro hounded the panicked Toltoy.
Taking refuge worse than a woman,
Kulchoro hounded the fleeing Toltoy.
Girding the jekene belt at his waist,
He charged alone through
The countless black Jediger.
Girding a white kise belt at his waist,
The lion boy Kulchoro
Attacked the countless black Jediger
And threw them into turmoil.
His bulletproof mail shirt
Showing upon his chest,
The twelve-year-old Kulchoro—
The Jediger host saw
That he would destroy them.
Dust billowed into the sky;
The great black Jediger
All fled shrieking.
Noble Kulchoro
Was an unmoving lion.
He was a hero who could slaughter
Not merely the Jediger, but the whole age alone.
His father, blue-maned Almambet,
Was a lion born surpassing all.
The horse he rode was gray;
His fortunes were complete.
He was a lion born surpassing
The lion prince Alman.
His turmoil reached the whole world.
When he went on campaign to Kakan,
He had left in Aruuke's womb
A fetus of three months.
Your blue-maned lord Alman
Joined their hands
And entrusted him to the lions,
Saying, “Name him Choro.”
Spring of the mountain pass,
Driving the Jediger,
He was Almambet's lamp.
He began the great slaughter;
Fierce-born Kulchoro
Slaughtered half
The great black Jediger.
Raging and shouting,
Khan Toltoy's great army—

In a single-file stream of horses,
