Was born to dark-hearted Zhakyp,
A dark-piebald lion.
When he reached twelve,
He broke citadels and took booty.
No mortal like him had ever been;
The enemies he faced slunk away.
When enraged, he seized them,
And chewed those he seized.
Among the black Chinese and Manchus,
No enemy remained to fight him.
When they heard the force of his courage,
Khan Esenkan wept.
If you hold a feast for Kökötöy,
Send a scout to Talas;
Send word, my colt,
To black-maned Manas!
Hey, Bokmurun, think carefully:
He is the support on whom to rely.
If you hold a feast for Kökötöy,
He is the hero who will be your aid
And the girding at your waist.
If you do not send word to Manas,
May he not dig a well in your place,
May he not surely trample you underfoot,
May he not scatter your four kinds of livestock;
May the Chinese day not dawn
And slaughter every last person
Who went to the feast.
Hairy-soled Tükübay,
The remnant champion of the Chinese,
Yearns to finish off
The dwindling Burut;
Yes, Kongurbay and Tükübay
Carry this longing within them.
At their flank is Börüköz,
A great Chinese champion;
The lean diviner Ezkara
Keeps watching us,
Seeking to unleash havoc.
"Anger is the devil, reason a friend"—
Oh, my colt Bokmurun!
Add wisdom to your thought.
Do not gather the handful of Kyrgyz
By saying you will hold a feast;
Without renowned Manas,
Do not bring ruin upon other peoples.
Oh, my colt Bokmurun!
Do not rely upon your wealth,
And do not hand the handful of Muslims
Over to the Chinese
And fall into calamity.
You are a son intoxicated by livestock;
If you do not heed these words of mine,
By summoning all the peoples,
You will bring them into calamity.
The black Chinese and many Manchus
Are no people who understand a feast.
When he hears the news and gathers his host,
Might Khan Kongurbay not arrive?
Without the tiger Manas,
Might he not utterly slaughter
Your vast black people?"
When Koshoy had spoken thus,
Bokmurun shook with agitation
And sprang up:
"Uncle Koshoy, hear my words.
It is known among the people
That Khan Kökötöy lived,
And that Kökötöy's vast riches
Filled the face of the earth.
Now I shall open their lock
And scatter them among all the people,
Leaving none of them out.
To all the people,
To carry word,
There is the swift horse Maaniker;
For infidel and Muslim alike,
