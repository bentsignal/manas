They raised the battle cry of "Kojo!"
The Zhediger striking from above
Made the lower side roar,
Raising the cry of "Tölök!"
Their flanks shouted,
Raising the cry of "Kalcha!"
An agitated army was coming,
Coming in multitudes to act.
A dug trench was coming,
A great, seething affliction was coming.
An engulfing army was coming;
Upon those who watched,
A mighty upheaval descended.
Many made ready without pause,
Spurring forward, saying,
"We entrust ourselves to the fight."
Many lamented, saying,
"If we bring this upheaval and war
And die in it,
What will become of our poor wives
And young children?"
Many lost their wits in panic
And rushed about.
Many fled, saying,
"If we die there,
What will become of a lone wife
And a single child?"
Many, trapped in the midst
As though God would take them,
Were subjected to plunder.
Many, saying they would turn back,
Met the guards
And were beaten.
The spearmen arrived;
When their khans drove them forward,
The great Black Zhediger
Set out in a rabble.
Chynkojo, son of Shygai,
Kojo, vigorous in his deeds,
Shouted in anger
And summoned the Zhediger.
He grew excited like a cuckoo;
At his flank hero Toltoi
Spoke to the Zhediger:
"Great Black Zhediger,
Do not turn back and flee;
Move steadily in your ranks,
My Zhediger, do not hurry.
Let us surge in shouting
And unleash a general battle;
Let us bind the champions
Of the Afghans who refused the girl.
If the lordly Afghans
Will not give the old maid,
Let us bind their prince.
Let us dig a well in their place of honor
And scatter their four kinds of livestock.
These Afghans have gone mad,
As though they had raised a champion;
Let us plunder their goods
And wreak destruction together.
Let us seize and slaughter their khans
And gouge out their people's eyes.
If they will not give the old maid,
Let us cover their plain with smoke,
Raid everything without remainder,
Turn water into their gardens
And make them into vegetable plots.
Turn water onto their poplars
And leave them staggering.
Let us bind old Akun
And make him a slave.
Let us make Azhibai and Tümönbai
Our herdsmen;
Let us level their straight-built city
And raid it without leaving a thing.
They have chosen war;
Let us engage in strife.
