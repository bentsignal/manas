Dust flew and haze burned;
A long cloud of dust billowed.
Clan after clan covered the ground;
The great host come to the feast
Surged forward, crying out.
The drum was beaten firmly;
All who wished to race horses
Closed in without exception.
They dressed the forelocks
Of the horses meant to win prizes,
Knotted their tails,
Took the shortcut along the narrow road,
And hurried, leading their horses.
They formed nation after nation,
Their numbers like a flooding river,
Murmuring like a vast people,
The multitude swarming thickly.
Turning as they led their horses,
They separated from one another.
The earth split and shifted;
Peoples of many ages filled it.
Because that host was so immense,
The center of the crowd hollowed out.
Kokotoy's countless black livestock
Were slaughtered and spent lavishly.
At Khan Kokotoy's feast,
Before the great gathering,
The people of the khagan ate their fill.
Trenches were dug in great numbers,
And above the trenches
Cauldrons were hung by the ten-thousand.
Broth spilled like a lake,
And meat loomed like a mountain.
When they heard news of the prize,
All the people, every one,
Swarmed like ants
And now came out in groups.
Among the Chinese was Orongu;
This was Orongu's desire:
"I will win the accursed Burut's
Prize as well," he said.
He prepared a rough-gaited bay mare,
That bay mare,
Dressing and setting its tail.
Behind the bay mare
He rolled up and stuffed
A saddle-sized piece of black felt.
Khan Joloy of the Kalmyks
Tied Achbuudan's forelock
This way and that,
And entered Achbuudan,
Saying, "I will win the grand prize."
Manas had Akkula;
Joloy had Surkula.
Akkula and Achbuudan
Were exceptional tulpar steeds;
When one looked at the two,
They resembled hares.
Manas entered Akkula;
Joloy entered Surkula.
With cotton sash and broad boots,
Kongurbay, khan of the Kechils,
Was a swine who knew
The enemy's devices perfectly:
"Let broad-chested Manas
Race Akkula and I shall see him;
I will joust with a Burut like him
And spill his blood.
At the feast for my dead father Alooke,
I will take my revenge;
I will offer the accursed Manas
As a sacrifice," he said.
