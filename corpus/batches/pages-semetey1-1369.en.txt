May your father Akun Khan die.
If you weep in grief,
May all those you love die!
You have gone mad, poor woman;
Should a raider not take the road,
And should a woman not remain at home?
Look at the deeds of this shameless woman:
Should the sorrowing warrior not scout,
And should the wife not remain at home!
If she sits weeping over a patrol,
Will dark misfortune not overwhelm me?!
Release my hand, fierce woman;
Do not bar my road!
Is it right for the son of a true noble
To live without pursuing the blood-price?
The road of a campaign is precious and great;
When a wife sits weeping,
Can there be anything so splendid?!”
His face darkened and grew grim,
His violent rage rose fiercely;
His eyes blazed like a lion’s,
And bloodlust truly seized him.
The black-cheeked rawhide whip—
Its blow would deafen the ears—
He seized and swung,
And with a resounding crack struck Chürök,
Sending the blow across her flank!
Her fine silk shirt
Split along its folds;
From the poor woman, whose flesh was white as snow,
Blood welled and poured.
Her silk-brocade shirt
Split along its folds;
From the wretched daughter of the peri,
Blood welled and poured.
Screaming and crying aloud,
She let go of the reins,
And, sobbing, cursed the prince:
“You would not heed me when I said, ‘Do not go’—
May you never return from where you go!
Then, my sovereign,
May you never see your market-home again!
May you never return once you leave;
May you never see your caravan-home again!
Did I burn what was precious to you,
That you should stretch out and lash me?
Did I provoke your anger,
That you should beat me so brutally?!
Did I burn your treasured possession,
That you should strike me screaming,
And do this wondrous deed?
Had I stirred your rage before?!
You dug my grave deep;
Despising me as a mere woman, you beat me
And brought this marvel upon my head!
My sorrowful prince, be damned;
You were the one who stretched out and struck,
And I became the hide you stretched!”
Turning away and walking, she sobbed;
Semetey’s Aichürök—
The peerless servant woman, rich in wisdom—
Entered the palace screaming.
