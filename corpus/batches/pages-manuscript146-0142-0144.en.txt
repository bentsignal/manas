The day the treasure was gathered and stored.
Do not ask about his bloody words,
Dark-fated was the thought I conceived,
The very heart of harsh misfortune,
Of the punishment that struck your soul,
He has a head as large as a cauldron,
He has a khan who sets things in order,
When he attacks, he makes all sway,
When he surrounds them, he gives no respite,
He takes away the approaching host,
A khan remained who knew no settled place,
The lineage of the wild one,
Living while souls fought beside him,
One who drank and was never sated with blood,
One who spared no life in battle,
Who crushed their steel warriors,
Who crushed even the unmatched wise men,
Raised in the desert breathing poison,
Raised on the crag spilling blood,
Riding abroad and cutting off heads,
Breaking the people like a stallion,
Destroying the nation and throwing it into turmoil,
Making the earth quake with blood,
He himself was a bloodthirsty tyrant.
The great Soz who entered the earth,
Soz Khan, elder among the Kalmyks,
That Soz Khan was among the wrestlers,
Many kaldays stood around him,
My khagan came from the raiders,
Breaking fortune through his courage,
Concerning the third world,
The valor of his wrestlers,
Became known here upon the earth,
One who especially spared no life.
Chyng Tengir, his chosen commander,
Had ordered him to go.
The poor, scant Kyrgyz people,
He thundered, bringing them torment,
Taking up a spear of black steel,
His fury would not cease,
You assemble it with your own hands,
What is this precise tongue of yours?
You tear away what you cannot take by right.
For the unruly son and young warrior,
O Kyrgyz people of the Kyrgyz,
Seeing you safe and alive,
Circling and seizing your land,
You will not act this way now,
Your irredeemable evil,
The appearance of the battlefield,
Let me drive you from the battle,
A steadfast one who will not leave when followed,
Let me strike and shatter him,
What is it that you desire?
Let me set the midst in order.
He prevailed and stood upon the battlefield,
With an exceptionally cutting tongue,
With teeth and tongue like a bear,
With a tongue of black and poisonous venom,
He began to humiliate the Kyrgyz.
You are a warrior who entered the field,
They cast aside the place of safety,
Calling them lesser men like fishermen,
Laying your wicked one in the grave,
Even the broad-hearted hero grew weary of you,
In your tale are herds that migrated,
One day you will see what your words bring,
There will be a struggle with the rebel.
Speaking thus, he thundered,
His formidable bearing surpassed all others,
As he stood enraged,
The people understood in their hearts,
He roared like gathering mist.
