She was the bravest of her sex.
When she reached eleven years of age,
Her father the sultan died.
When she reached thirteen,
She crossed through rain, hunted game,
And amused herself by drawing the bow.
When she reached fourteen,
Everything beneath the moon and sky
She comprehended through her wisdom.
When she reached fifteen,
She became the fairies’ sovereign khan,
And then Bermet ascended
Her father’s golden throne.
When she reached eighteen,
She reigned in royal splendor.
Her vast fairy host and numerous people
She ruled without letting them stir.
Her father, the sultan and padishah,
Had it built of precious stone:
A white palace fashioned of gold,
Fitted throughout with mirrors,
Stunning the hearts of all who saw it.
He had jewels piled there like kneeling camels,
And burning upon its braziers.
She entered it to sleep,
And when the hour of dawn approached,
All manner of things entered her dream;
While she lay troubled in sleep,
There appeared within her dream
A barefoot dervish.
The dervish stood and spoke,
Telling marvels no mortal had heard:
“Listen to my words:
You are the daughter of the radiant fairy,
The most skillful among women.
Understand what I tell you;
My child Akbermet, heed my counsel.
This is the destiny written by Allah;
Look closely at me,
And listen carefully to my words.
He is the rightful heir to truth,
The offspring of the true Kyrgyz;
His first forefather was Tümön Khan,
Blessed from the beginning by Khidr.
His earliest ancestors were all khans,
All of them blessed by Khidr.
Their fortune surged into a lake,
And from the first they were gray-maned lions.
When enemy warriors rose to fight them,
They were leveled with the black earth.
In the hearts of the Black Chinese and Manchus,
They became an incurable,
Deeply cutting pain.
His fathers and forefathers
Shattered royal camps and took their spoils,
And marched against the peoples of the world.
When they seized foes, they spilled their blood,
And enemies who dared attack
Were delivered into their hands for punishment.
They struck down mountains and made them plains,
And acted with the cry, ‘We trust in God!’
They freed the young stallions,
And reduced attacking enemies’
Tumultuous hordes to nothing.
He led the way on expeditions,
And the descendants of the valiant,
With the saint Khidr himself
As their companion and guide.
His forefather was magnanimous Manas the lion,
The youngest of six heroic lions.
No man like him shall arise in all the world.
Exalted by his valor,
He became a mighty, sacred man
And received a visitation on the Night of Power.
Khidr came and blessed him,
All the great champions swiftly rallied,
And those who grappled with him were disgraced,
Struck down upon the black earth.
Sacred Manas
Received wisdom from the unseen.
