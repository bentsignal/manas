Many noble men pondered this.
"Saying we would take a wife and live in ease,
How many people have we killed?"
Foolish men pondered this.
Across the plain of Jeti-Kyr,
At accomplished Kiyaz's wedding,
Plane trees ringed the place,
With a golden throne at the center.
From the tower, the people's landmark,
A herald shouted.
Hero Kiyaz, son of Bagysh,
Had a decree proclaimed to the people.
Ready as a hawk,
Having devised a cunning plan,
The multitude of Zhediger
Heard the khan's decree was coming:
He would give the masses
A great wedding feast for taking a wife;
Now that the prince had taken a wife,
He would scatter all four kinds of livestock.
Without binding ropes or blindfolding eyes,
He would open a spectacle
To astound the multitude;
Wrestlers would seize each other's cuffs,
Come forth and grapple,
And stage a spectacle.
The poles would bend;
In this extraordinary affair,
Champion warriors would joust.
Then hero Kiyaz
Made a proclamation to the multitude.
Dear Kiyaz shouted,
Calling the Zhediger:
"Come, every one of you, leave none behind!
Damn your fathers' graves, Zhediger!
Call it Khan Kiyaz's design;
Call it the wedding feast
Of the noble, unfortunate beauty Aichurok.
Do you know, Zhediger?
From your broad, level Kyzan,
I have brought my migration.
For the hero consumed by longing for Churok,
I sit here taking vengeance.
The banks of Zhylgyndy, Keng-Kol, and Talas
I have taken and now possess.
For the two khans Chynkojo and Toltoy,
I sit here taking vengeance.
Making the earth marvel
And call me Kiyaz the hero,
I have won her and sit here.
The noble peri Aichurok,
That radiant, unfortunate woman,
I have taken as my wife.
The gold and silver
Of gray-maned Semetey, Manas's son,
I have loaded and brought home.
The thimble mouth and dark eyes,
The great shining camel-calf eyes,
The crimson dress and moon-slender waist,
The graceful beauty—
I have seized and brought her here.
Rejoicing in the strength that seized her,
I shall hold a feast for many peoples.
If I rise against the whole world,
I intend to overthrow it.
I am your lord and your lion,
Your gray-maned hero Kiyaz!
At the gray wolf's words,
Multitudes of Zhediger,
Your champions and your khans,
Lose yourselves in the spectacle;
Let every one of you plunge into the fray!
Now that I have taken the noble peri,
I have openly held a feast for my people;
I have conceived a design beyond mankind.
I shall begin many wonders;
For moonlike Lady Churok,
Not only my livestock, but my very life—
