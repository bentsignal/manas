If he does not heed my words,
If he regards me with hostility,
I will seize the black banner,
And cast his people into turmoil!
Say that Bokmurun, Kökötöy's son,
Has camped at a suitable place;
Say that Kökötöy's countless riches
Sprouted like grass in spring.
The four corners of the world,
After his noble father died,
Have strength to wait four years.
Let every khan who hears the news come;
From the seven ages of the earth,
Let every living soul covering the land come!"
After Bokmurun had said this,
Baymyrza rose and spoke:
"Summon the four corners
All around the world.
If you do not act with princely dignity,
Summon all the peoples with elders,
Every one of them,
And do not behave like a child.
I know the people
Of the Kara Khitai and Manchus.
There are the people of Kakan-Chyn;
When all their tribes gather,
They have a custom of shedding blood.
There is ill-minded Zholoy,
And the formidable, vengeful Kongurbay;
They are enemies who will clash with us.
May infidels as numerous as cow hairs
Bring no trouble upon us.
After gorging on your feast,
Bokmurun, think of this:
May they not cast hellish torment
Upon your head, small as a button.
The man who gives the feast bears blame;
The man who gives the feast bears sin.
If your descent is low,
If the grass grows upward,
If the infidel peoples are many,
If what you rely on is like iron,
May none say that wretched Bokmurun,
Kökötöy's son, let infidels
Plunder Kökötöy's great wealth
When he offered the memorial feast.
This conduct of yours is arrogance;
Be firm and gather the people.
Do not offer a feast and have
This little band of Muslims beaten—
That is the mark of conceit.
Do not have this handful of Muslims
Plundered by the Kara Kalmyks and Manchus
Till only red tent-poles remain;
Mind their drunkenness and act!
The hero Manas is in Talas;
Bokmurun, you are twelve;
Find proper measure and act!"
When Baymyrza had said this,
Bokmurun rose and said:
"Baymyrza, rich man's son, bald fool,
Your camp is a difficult ravine, princely bald fool.
Your mount is a lone colt, princely bald fool;
What you drink is black tea, princely bald fool!
Mind your own feast, bald fool;
Clench your backside and take the road, bald fool!
I will not let you near my feast, bald fool;
I will not make you a burden on my head, bald fool!
You appeared as an enemy to my feast, bald fool;
Go away, do not come near me, bald fool!
Do not camp as though you were a great host, bald fool;
Do not swell the crowd here, bald fool!"
Thus Bokmurun,
Stamped upon the ground he walked,
Scolded Baymyrza,
Pounded the earth and raised dust,
And had his seven thousand attendants
Drive the swift horse Maaniker
Out from among the herd.
