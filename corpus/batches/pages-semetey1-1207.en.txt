Let us leave this matter here.
There are racehorses sent to run,
And infidel and Muslim
May raise a commotion.
Each man's head bears a burden;
There is the duty of cheering a horse.
Infidel and Muslim
Have chosen racehorses they entered.
There are tulpars born among horses,
And a plain no winged creature can cross.
If a quarrel starts from a small thing,
There is a danger that infidel and Muslim
Will slaughter one another.
The swift Maaniker is among
The horses driven away.
If an opportunity comes, the Chinese
Have deeds they may perform.
There is the source of much strife,
And there is Kokotoy's feast.
Gray-maned Bakay began to speak,
Letting his words run obliquely.
This is what Bakay said:
"O people of the Muslim faith,
Your khan stands here;
All you clashing kinsmen
Stand here.
The wisest of the kinsmen,
Your deftest man stands here.
Skilled with the spear, a blood-drinker,
Your strongest man stands here.
Great host of common Muslims,
Let one in a thousand come;
Let your able reasoner come.
Your courage remains, O Muslims;
Let your strongest manlike fighter come,
Let your hero of complete cunning come,
And witness this device of mine.
Kongurbay was humiliated,
Khan Joloy was disgraced,
And Tukubay was utterly killed.
There is the sorrow of the dark hour;
Hear, O Muslims,
Whose turn it is to grapple.
You have dealt terrible wrath
To the black-worm infidel,
To hero Khan Kongurbay.
Joloy has become vengeful;
Tukubay died without a word.
No man has defeated by battle
The seething Chinese host;
These surging Chinese
Are a flood no barrier can hold.
They are the proud Chinese people;
They have won many prizes,
And you have disgraced them.
Many regrets remain after a man;
Such is this treacherous world.
We received it from learned tradition
And asked those who know.
No man could match them;
Whoever did did not survive.
Their lowlands were grassy,
And their people were numerous.
Their spirit was great, their name renowned;
Whoever fought them did not survive.
The Black Chinese creatures
Have come to the last man
From the people of Chynchy-Mynchy,
From the land of the true Khagan.
The Chinese host is a flood;
At one crooked provocation,
It is ready to engulf us.
