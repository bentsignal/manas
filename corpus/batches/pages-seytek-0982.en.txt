Heedless of one another,
Sparing not their hair-thin lives,
They began a calamitous fight,
Both of them, their dear lives
Staked on a single hair,
Their fierce battle roared,
Swallowing blood to the full,
Tearing all their coats to shreds.
When they struck with clubs, neither flinched;
When they hacked with axes, neither stirred;
When they lashed with whips, neither cried out;
When they crossed swords, neither uttered a groan;
They fought a desperate battle,
A strange life, a soul amid a throng,
They staked it on a single hair;
A precious life, a dear soul,
They staked it on a single hair.
A bloody slaughter, a relentless pursuit,
They led their champions into it.
Seizing each other by the collars,
Whoever came near
They struck with an axe;
For two days and nights
They never ceased their thunderous clash.
They gathered force and charged,
And crashed against each other,
The standards swarmed, banners everywhere,
Battle cries rose in a tumult;
Look, the uproar swelled,
And a vast battle raged.
They tore at one another without regret,
Swallowing blood as if it were water,
They thrust their spears at one another,
Sparing not their precious lives,
They battered one another.
There was no way out of it,
They were enveloped in pitch-black dust,
A strange hardship rose like a ridge,
A fierce tumult and strife arose,
A clamor seemed to split the sky,
The cries rose in a roar,
Each faced a grievous struggle,
Neither could prevail in combat.
Neither could discover
The other's true measure.
Each was a formidable trial to the other,
And neither could defeat the other.
They battered one another,
Yet neither could bring the other down.
The earth broke and shook,
Their strength matched,
They struck without gaining the upper hand,
They grappled and fought,
Yet neither could prevail against the other.
A mighty clash, a great din,
Such was the uproar,
All the men were spent,
Their spears shattered and were used up.
An extraordinary battle broke out,
The strong drove the weak before them,
And slaughtered them as they went,
A great clash and mighty roar,
A throng of cries and clamor.
Now the ordeal grew harsh,
Renowned khans fell dead,
And warriors meeting face to face died,
They fought a grievous battle.
As if these lives were not living,
They simply cast them away.
Khans died in the fighting,
As the fierce battle raged,
The champions held nothing back,
Clashing with a crash,
Into death, doom and calamity
They went against the foe.
Their strength failed from weariness,
Hero Sarybay, the mighty champion,
A man the people had never known the like of,
Now kept the battle raging.
This clash rang with cries,
