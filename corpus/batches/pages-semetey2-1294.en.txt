I broke through the gorge and opened a road.
My domineering frenzy seized me;
The man who shared my mother’s breast and grew beside me,
My twin hero whose name rhymed with mine—
I bound Külchoro.
When someone provokes my vengeance,
I never retreat even before my own kin;
When murderous rage seizes me,
I will not give an inch to anyone nearby.
Close in, Jediger!
If you are sons capable of taking revenge,
Make ready, Jediger!
With a cast-iron socket
Fitted wide and deep,
Yesterday, when he came out to fight
The teeming black-clad Jediger,
When he faced them head-on
And thrust his spear with a roar,
The spears were drenched in blood
And lions fell dead upon the ground.
Külchoro charged in with a cry
And unleashed terrible wrath;
Your forefather, the gray wolf Toltoy,
Külchoro slaughtered and left for dead.
Having lost their men, the Jediger
Led home their ownerless horses.
‘Alas, my dear kinsman
Died in such-and-such a place!’
Thus the Jediger wailed.
He destroyed their young men in their prime,
And felled their mightiest;
He drove his wrathful spear deep,
And slaughtered mighty Toltoy and Chynkojo,
Leaving not one alive.
This Talas is no land for you;
Külchoro’s people are not yours.
This blustering false slave—
Gouge out his right shoulder,
Flay his right flank,
Gouge out his left shoulder.
Neither dead nor alive,
Leave this evil-minded slave
Stranded between the two states.
Hack him smooth and make him helpless;
Make him a slave to the herdsmen.
Inflict your fiercest wrath;
Upon this villain who played the hero,
This boar who caused such turmoil,
Take revenge from every quarter.
Though he was once a man apart,
At heart he is our enemy.
When both his arms are gone,
And only the hero’s shadow remains,
Whip him, beat him, and flay him.
Gouge out his eyes and blind him;
If he defies you again,
Hack into the nape of his neck.
For beasts, ravens, and crows
Make of him a proper feast.
Upon the enemy fallen into your hands,
Inflict torment without restraint;
Take the vengeance you have long harbored.
Drive this crippled wretch away.
Make him the slave of slaves;
Torment him and take his life.
Beat him sick and stretch him on the rack;
Deliver his punishment into our hands.
Devise exquisite tortures;
Make him labor, beat him, club him,
And subject him to a dog’s humiliation.
Break his spirit and fill him with pain;
Dig into his sides until they become sores.
Beat him flat and stretch him like a hide;
Thus deliver his punishment.
Take your boundless revenge.
Make him serve forty herdsmen and eighty slaves,
Fetching their firewood and water;
Make his soul smolder, and take his life.
