Let me stand before them here
And see how able they are.”
As though a lance had pierced him,
Hero Syrgak sprang up:
“Perish with your horses!
My joy, my Almam, I shall not drive them!
By driving the horses away,
What desire would I fulfill?
My precious Almam, my lion,
Leaving him alone among the Chinese—
How could I abandon him and go?
If my doom has come, I shall die.
Kongurbay, son of Alöökö—
Ah, world, I have been left yearning!
I shall face him once and see!
Leave me here, dear Almambet;
Look after your horses yourself.
Me, your younger brother Syrgak,
Expect me within seven days!”
Clasping hands and saying farewell,
The two lions, not moving away,
Held each other's hands.
Without saying many other things,
Entrusting their final charges,
