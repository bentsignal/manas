He loaded the white musket.
From the hero’s fury,
The black earth, whose breast is hairy,
Stopped the shot only in its deepest layers.
When he raged in wrath,
The whole wide world stood watch;
The thick black earth below
Smoldered through its deepest layers
From my khan uncle’s fury.
When the providential hero lived,
Crimson steel took heads;
Against whoever provoked his wrath,
He ravaged the world and set it aflame.
He kindled the grassy lands
And set the meadows afire.
My exceedingly noble sultan uncle
Was truly a hero;
Among all humankind,
That peerless, keen-eyed eagle
Was truly a lion.
He broke through dead ends and opened roads;
My falcon uncle, my eagle,
Broke through on every side and opened roads,
And struck down all who opposed him.
He swept aside everyone he met,
And burned everyone he encountered.
Along the great road no host could overtake,
He made his bay horse dance;
Subduing everyone he encountered,
He made his assailants crawl.
He broke fortresses and took khans;
Against innumerable foes,
He raised dust such as no man had seen.
At the hero’s fury,
The enemies who grappled with him stood amazed.
When his fit of rage seized him,
He fiercely launched the turmoil.
He cut off the heads
Of the enemies who attacked him and subdued them;
He was truly a mighty mainstay.
Yesterday, on the plain of Jylgyndy,
Along broad Talas,
When my formidable lord lived,
Whenever restless, I would shoot mountain sheep,
Then turn back and lie at ease.
When the mighty hero lived,
I, poor wretch, would test my strength:
Setting them in rows, with my bow
I would shoot eighty in succession.
Such was my former nature,”
Said Külchoro, and thus
He spoke in verse of his former prowess
And the great conflicts,
And told of the mighty turmoil
That could never be healed.
Heroic Seytek heard his secrets
And readied himself like a hawk.
From the panther hero’s words,
Wisdom came to him from far away;
A sheathless sword was buckled on,
And your wretch blessed by the holy wanderer
Stepped forth, prepared to speak:
“My uncle Külchoro
Is the guardian above my head,
My fortune in heroic deeds.
From the traditions handed down,
And from questioning the wise,
I learned that assembled hosts stood together,
That he was an ember blazing like a minaret,
And throughout the whole wide world
There was none like my uncle.
He was a hero and a mainstay,
A lion surpassing all mankind.
He was one of those heroes
Who brought torment upon their foes in battle,
One of those souls who stirred
Great turmoil across the world.
His lashes were flame and his eyes embers;
No man was ever so fearsome!
