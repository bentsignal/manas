The Black Chinese as big as camels
Fell sprawling.
Looking at his front,
The bloodthirsty one of surpassing fortune
Had the form of six thousand warriors.
When the hero turned his back,
He had the terror of a dragon.
When he faced them, the Chinese parted.
The bloodthirsty one of surpassing fortune

At one glance, a thousand men;
At another glance, my hero
Was a san of men teeming over the earth.
Such was Manas's appearance:
The youngest of six lions,
Magnanimous Manas, lion and hero,
God's beloved servant.
His fortune was complete.
Right there in Altay,
When he reached twelve,
Intrepid Manas the leopard,
This was where he wrought slaughter.
“He is a bloodthirsty Burut!” they said.
The kalday saw and grieved.
That Manas was a lion,
In the seething Chinese people's
Record of renown it was inscribed.
Their strong men wearing short coats,
When the lion entered among them,
Tumbled from their horses, one by one.
Their sturdier wrestlers
Truly despaired of their lives.

The fucking bloodthirsty one,

The seething Chinese,
The gray-maned hero drove into submission.

Their strong men piled up like mountains.
