Let us leave that matter so,
She had known before the true resting place
From which there is no return,
Wailing and crying aloud,
Sobbing that she would die with him
And lie beside him by the river,
There was no hope left by the hero.
There was no heir left by the dear one,
No token left by the gray wolf,
Such is this wretched, shadowy world.
Thinking he would rest in a rivered land,
There was neither shade nor shelter,
No distant sign remained behind.
Thinking all would be as a fire gone out,
Let mighty Kyyaz take
This head as his spoil,
She of silver eyes and sun-bright face,
The pillar of heroes among the brave,
The leader of the people among champions,
Wished that he might leave descendants behind.
Letting the goose be devoured by birds of prey,
Because of a single camel calf
She let herself be called a captive wife;
For this she had come as spoil,
Making the Kalmyk’s head a mountain,
And raising a woman’s head as a hero,
She had given her heart to Kyyaz.
The dear woman Aichürök,
When twilight fell and the sun went down,
When all the people lay fast asleep,
Would spread out the hair upon her back;
Into brave Kyyaz’s embrace—
Let us begin again from here:
The moon waned and the days passed,
And so many years went by.
When Kyyaz took Chürök that day,
When he laid waste to Talas,
Akun’s unfortunate daughter
Had intended to die,
But remained alive instead—
For the hero had left within her
A child of four months.
She meant to depart in her grief,
Yet remained alive and did not go;
Semetey had left within her
A child three months in the craving.
She meant to die from shame,
Yet your sister-in-law remained alive,
Because the ill-fated one in her womb
Was a token left by her beloved.
Parted from her hero,
The wretched, distraught Aichürök
Lamented and burned with grief.
Saying, “A token of the hero remains,”
This tormented, ill-fated woman
Made the three-month child in her womb
Her hope and consolation.
Sobbing and crying aloud,
She meant to die from shame,
Yet remained alive,
Because hope lay within her womb.
“My bloodthirsty lord is dead,” she said,
