It was the hour of his lament.
Submitting himself to God’s command,
It was the hour of his sorrow.
Today the world is hardship,
An hour that could never be satisfied.
Only the Kyrgyz people remained.
Among the eighteen thousand worlds,
It was the hour when he stood again.
Do not ask it of the true God.
His head appeared as vast as a mountain.
The gray-maned hero came onward.
Unable to endure Kadan,
A host came from his own people.
He went on, descending and sitting.
The dreadful Kalmyk stood arrayed.
The armored warrior wore his coat.
Sand and dust rose in turmoil.
Do not ask it of the true God.
Sparks flew from the glowing furnace.
When he bit, his teeth crunched.
Blue flame poured from his mouth.
He made the lungs whistle and shriek.
Dark-browed like a dog lying in wait,
His eyelids filled with tears.
Do not raise the shrill cry.
His dread visage was poisonous.
Now his soul was being drawn out.
The mark of calamity appeared.
Of the many kinds of Kyrgyz,
Of the master of a thousand and one troubles,
From among courageous mortals,
He asked after the one behind him.
They went scorching the earth.
Unable to endure Kadan,
How many lives returned to death?
To Kenen, who mounted the steeds of vision,
His terror raised clouds of dust.
Now he reached the place of slaughter.
