What else do you say now?
While you were in the Seven Valleys,
You boasted, ‘I shall seize and slaughter him,’
And began your speech with bravado;
But when you saw Külchoro,
You cried, ‘We have suffered grievously,
We are slaughtered and doomed!’
And cast away your spears.
Rushing about in frantic panic—
Damn you, Jediger—
This is the example you now set!
While you were in the Seven Valleys,
You vowed, ‘I shall catch him and exact blood-price;
I shall fight fiercely for six full months
And grapple with him in carnage!’
You blustered in your haste,
But when you saw Külchoro,
Like a flock struck by a falcon,
You fled wherever your eyes could see!
While you were in the Seven Valleys,
You stood there raging;
But when the time came to face him,
Unable to find your bearings,
You panicked, straining for a plan,
Crying, ‘Now we are slaughtered!’
With what face can you flee?
Understand now what I have said!
Stallion-like hero Kyyaz,
If you are no man, sink into the earth!
Damn you, Jediger,
Understand what I have told you;
My foolish people, heed my words!
Of the many mares brought for provisions,
Catch and slaughter a white mare;
Let us feast together to our fill
On choice meat and rich horse-ribs.
Rather than flee in scattered bands—
Damn you, Jediger—
Let us turn fierce as wolves!
Let us unite our strength in one place—
Damn you, Jediger—
Fire our guns and draw our bows,
And advance to face the enemy!
Let us bring down the accursed bloodthirsty man’s
Banner from Keng-Kol;
Come, wretched people, let us fight
And bury the bloodthirsty man in the earth!
Let Kanchoro and Kyyaz, the two khans,
Set this undertaking right;
Let us destroy this magnanimous khan
Whose forefather was a noble khan,
And take both reins and bridle
Into our own hands!
Let us become khans over his realm,
Men of immeasurably precious lives;
Let us impose our will and rule the people,
And take affairs into our own hands!”
Then Kanchoro,
Seething and enraged,
Was visited, valiant man that he was,
By sudden inspiration from the unseen.
Roaring furiously,
He summoned the vast host of Jediger,
Calling for every last man to come.
They took the many mares kept for provisions
And slaughtered a white mare;
They chose men from among the people,
Saying, “This shall be the fate of anyone who flees,”
And dipped their hands in its blood.
They cut switches and swore by their strength,
Making a solemn covenant;
If any people refused to submit,
They punished them and forced matters straight—
Such was the nature of their cruelty.
Driven to desperation, Kanchoro declared,
“This is what shall befall whoever flees;
And this is how those who do not flee shall stand.”
