When he saw him charge,
What could dear Agysh do?
He stood and awaited his turn.
When Tükübai reached him,
Brave Agysh held out his forearm.
They seized each other’s arms;
Before either could call for a blow,
The heroes wrenched their arms free.
They heaved and strained,
Tugging and jerking,
Grappling at the appointed ground,
Facing and tripping each other.
They opened their mouths like caverns,
Shouting as they pressed toward the earth.
The strong men twisted each other’s necks
And tore at every point of leverage.
Infidel and Muslim alike
Crowded round and watched,
As the champions strained.
Everyone who saw it marveled.
They grappled day and night,
Until two days were complete.
The assembled people watched their struggle
And knew what was happening—do you see?
As though he were watching a brother-in-law,
Agysh burst out laughing then;
He went on laughing—do you see?
The youngest of the twelve lions,
A servant beloved by God,
The dark gray-maned snow leopard Manas,
At Agysh’s laughter
Was seized by anger.
His bloodlust gripped him hard;
His eyes truly blazed like fire.
His broad front teeth were like a gate,
Teeth unlike those of ordinary folk,
And they flashed white as he bared them.
Still swollen with rage, toward Agysh
The snow leopard rushed.
“Lift him, Agysh—take him!
This Kalmyk fit for burial,
Lay him upon the black earth!”
Snow-leopard Manas roared.
The black-jawed rawhide whip
He seized and swung.
Charging in then,
At elbow-sized Agysh
He lashed across the ankle.
His ankle flared with pain,
And now his anger reached its height.
Tükübai heaved like a mountain,
But his maneuver went badly.
By both his lower legs,
Brave Agysh, a true hero,
Bent down and seized him.
Giving the infidel no time to breathe,
He tore him from the ground.
He made the infidel stagger,
And whirled the champion around.
Hairy-Sole Tükübai—
Hair sprouted from both his cheeks,
And a groan came from beneath his hem.
Tükübai’s arms were pinned;
Above brave Agysh’s head
He spun like a blue-backed colt.
His plight had turned dire.
A black rock as large as a yurt—
Skillful Agysh reached it.
A black rock as large as a house:
The mountain-sized champion
He dashed upon that stone.
The prowess of brave Agysh,
The gray-maned wolf, was revealed.
The great champion Tükübai
Was split into two pieces.
The prowess of elbow-sized Agysh
Was revealed to all.
