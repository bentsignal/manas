Plundered the poor man's possessions,
And took them as booty,
That magician said:
‘An astonishing son will be born among the Burut,
A poison surpassing men will be born,
For Kakan-Chin's Beijing,
An uproar bringing upheaval will be born!
This Beijing standing here,
A son will be born who will burn it!’
A sheath without a crookedness,
What Chong Ayar said
Was true, not a lie.
That Burut, may he burn,
Our eyes blinded, failing to see,
He had been born in Altay!
Your father's grave, wretched Burut!
If you live, you will see:
The Burut will raise an uproar in Beijing!
Braid your thoroughbred's mane,
The valiant blood-spiller Manas,
Within five or six years,
You will see him in your Beijing!
He will not let the plow turn.
If that Burut makes trouble,
He will not let us remain in this Beijing.
That bastard blood-spiller,
That deceitful one who ruins the world,
This Beijing the four companions never trod,
He is the soul who will enter proudly.
This Beijing Sulayman never touched,
He is the slave who will call it to account.
Manas is no ordinary son.
If he clashes with Kakan,
He is not one who will fail to crush it!
Magicians, when I sent you,
You proved a wretched lot!
Your eyes blinded, failing to see,
Your fathers' graves, Manchus,
You had become blind!
Across Altay, Manchus,
You had failed to travel.
The child in your hands,
You had failed to recognize him!”
Thundering in wrath,
He began a fierce onslaught.
The sixty or so magicians who went to judge,
Without leaving one alive,
He slaughtered them all.
Hearing news from his governor,
Hearing, “Khan Manas has arisen,”
He began issuing stern commands.
He threw on blue armor.
If you saw Esenkan,
He raged like a blue tiger.
He began a fierce onslaught.
The many Torgut and Manchus,
He slaughtered them.
The signal bell was rung,
The army lost its senses.
When their khan gave his decree,
Could the people fail to assemble?
When Esenkan poured out his wrath,
The Chinese as numerous as ants,
Could they stand watching without assembling?
Esenkan on his golden throne
Began a terrible torment.
When they saw Esenkan's wrath,
The mighty men who wore jackets
Gave up hope of life!
The stone-badged men ran.
The wrestlers in bonds
Said, “We are dead,” and despaired of life.
Khan Esenkan shouted,
Calling his many soldiers:
“Come, your mighty men who wear jackets,
Come, your finest officials with rank buttons on their caps,
Come, your deft and skillful spearmen,
Come, your mighty men who spare none!
Come, your heroes, wolves of war,
Who strike the enemy without drawing near,
Come, your broad-chested wolves!
Leave your cowards, let your heroes come,
Who do not flinch when a spear strikes,
Come, you who will go as heroes!
When you go to face the enemy,
Facing them and thrusting spears,
Come, you who will bring great suffering!
When a black gun crashes,
Who enter without drawing back,
Who strike the enemy without tiring,
Come, you who claim to be great,
All of you like this, come!
Come, your heroes of surpassing courage,
Shouting at that blood-spiller,
Come, you who will thrust spears without holding back!
If the people are slaughtered amid shouting,
Without hurrying into panic,
Come, you who will stand beneath the standard!
All truly courageous men, come,
Trick riders, swift runners on foot,
Great marksmen in the treasury's service,
All your skillful men, come!”
When Esenkan unleashed his wrath,
There were white banners and standards,
There was shouting and uproar,
There were blue banners and standards,
There was uproar echoing in the sky.
Leaders of ten wheeled about,
Spearheads jutted upward,
The brass pipe's voice shrilled.
Mottled blue flags appeared,
All who saw them divided,
Broke into groups of many kinds.
When their khan gave orders,
His soldiers separated into divisions.
Those who would not heed were slaughtered.
His Torgut gathered to the last man.
There were Manchu and Chinese people;
He gathered all he had.
When their khan gave orders,
No one remained unassembled.
“Ezkara, khan of the Manchus,
Khan Zholoy, khan of the Kalmyks,
All of you go, none stay behind,” he said.
“My great magician Karazhoy,
Against the bloodthirsty Burut brat,
Please bring upheaval,
You too must go, do not stay behind!
Your marksmanship was unsurpassed,
You had valor,
Hiding when you saw a dry stalk.
