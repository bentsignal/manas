A cloud of majesty arose,
The rear-guard champion Jooshuk,
His strength was famed among the host,
Then the warrior crouched low,
A champion emerged among a thousand,
Known among every people,
All the Kyrgyz fell into uproar,
They leveled their spears firmly,
The earth split in that hour,
Every tethered horse stood ready,
Dust rose so thick the eye could not see,
If you saw it, smoke and flame filled the conflict,
The Kyrgyz stood alone, a gathered host,
The measured ground could not contain them,
Their spears could not reach their limit,
The dear Kyrgyz, a mighty army,
The day boiled with the massed foe,
A thousand Chinese against one Kyrgyz,
When he struck, he dragged them aside,
He scattered blood across the great host,
A single soul remained alive,
They swarmed like ants,
The earth turned slick beneath the trampling,
Chinese troops attacked from every side,
They came back from ages past,
The foe died and the feud remained,
Wrath stretched long,
The enemy remained upon the dear earth,
When the Chinese engulfed them,
In a brief stormy moment,
When distress descended upon their heads,
Calling it grief that came from China,
At the place where they clashed,
The warriors stood firm-kneed,
They never took their eyes from the hero,
Upon the head of the one who emerged,
They honored his glory.
