And spoke to Azhybay:
"Accursed Azhybay!
If you say you will give the maiden, tell the truth;
Do not babble of grievous afflictions.
When I demand the maiden, you squirm,
And provoke my anger
By babbling empty words.
Is Talas where he dwells
Greater than the waters of Zheti-Özön?
Azhybay, sunk into the earth,
Look closely: is the orphan boy Semetey
More of a hero than I?
Among the Argyn and Ogoy peoples,
I dwelt in Talas;
This army of mine deceived six times
His proud father Manas.
Your Talas is no such land;
The orphan you praise,
Look closely, is no greater hero than I!
When my wrath takes hold,
My black horse flies soaring;
While my dear head remains,
It is my custom to strike
The eighteen thousand worlds to powder.
Accursed Azhybay,
Do not babble and provoke my rage.
If you will give the maiden, say so;
If not, state your answer.
Tell me the fortress for which you girded your waist;
If you will not give her, tell your truth!
Over Chürök, whom you refuse when asked,
I shall cast a cold lasso;
Chürök, whom you refuse though I desire her,
I shall seize and drag away.
If I stiffen and wrath takes hold,
If you do not accept what I say,
If you do not give Aychürök,
I shall dig a well in your place of honor
And hang a cauldron upon your breast;
I shall scatter all your four kinds of livestock.
If you resist, Azhybay,
I shall open your eyes wide as flour dumplings!
When I came besieging, you said, 'I shall give her';
Your oath has expired, Azhybay.
Speaking much falsehood and truth,
Your affliction has gone too far, Azhybay.
You will accept what I say,
And you will give Aychürök.
If I leave you freely in your place,
You say there is a khan greater than me,
You say there is a son of Manas.
Semetey and I are kinsmen,
Born equal at the same window;
Our villages were together in Talas.
To tell the truth, Azhybay,
Aychürök is the wife I shall take.
Speaking evasive words,
You wretch, where have you wandered?
When I grow angry, Aychürök
Is the widow I myself shall take.
If he rages and fights,
I myself shall slaughter
That fugitive son of Manas,
And I myself shall meet
The hell that comes from it!
If you do not abandon your treachery,
Hear my words now, my Azhy:
If you truly provoke my wrath,
I shall trample your hearth,
Overrun you without ceasing,
And leave none of your stumps;
I shall finish and wipe them all away.
If you provoke my rage, Azhybay,
I shall overrun your Afghanistan!
If you will give her, tell the truth;
If you will not, say so.
Without scheming, you Azhy,
Name the ridge where we shall cross spears,
