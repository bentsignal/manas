The accursed woman Aichurok
Had flown over and surveyed
Every inhabited land.
Her eyes saw into the distance;
She was a wise woman who had seen many lands.
Among all women in flowing dresses,
She alone had won the race.
When the luckless one was a girl,
Her father Akun wrapped and veiled her in gold
And had her raised in splendor.
Knowing she was a noble peri,
A great judge surpassing the people,
One with ears to hear and eyes to see,
A discerning judge of sorrow approved her.
After accepting Akun's hospitality
And eating the meal he offered,
He departed saying, "This daughter of yours
Will bring upheaval upon the earth."
With a tail trailing like a cuckoo's,
The great sage father Kuroshon
Saw her and judged her.
Looking upon her outward form,
He found her perfect in every measure.
My father Kuroshon uttered a marvel;
He said this to Khan Akun:
"From the ridge of the Mediyan desert,
From the shore of Bel-Soorun,
While pursuing game and surveying the land,
You found Churok in the open plain
When she was still an infant.
As though finding the Night of Power,
You rejoiced at that blessing,
Your heart surging,
And named her Churok.
When I observe her,
Many a soul will lose his senses,
Thinking, 'If only she were mine.'
Infidel and Muslim alike
Will lose their wits and contend;
The peoples who see her will quarrel.
Your Churok will prove truly beautiful,
And strife will arise before her.
She is a peri herself,
The dark eye at the heart of conflict.
She is one of those firebrands
Who divide peoples and slaughter nations.
Her face is a burning ember;
No servant of God is her equal.
Heed these words:
Give her to a lionhearted hero
Who will yield to no one in the world.
She is a maiden famed as Churok;
If your daughter lives,
Her conflicts will shake the age.
She is famed as a noble peri;
If this child of yours lives,
Her troubles will touch all humankind.
Will those who desire to take her
Ever lie still without quarreling?"
That discerning father who saw her
Sharpened his precious tongue
And before Khan Akun
Spoke for many days.
He began every kind of discourse;
Each of his words was excellent.
If you attend to your discerning father,
And consider every word,
Each strikes like a spear.
If your name is Khan Akun,
If you are one who clearly knows
What comes and what departs,
Joy and suffering,
Violence and humiliation,
Then heed these words
And find her rightful master.
Her remote ancestor was Tugol Khan,
Khydyr's blessing entered her lineage;
Her foremost ancestor was Babyr Khan,
