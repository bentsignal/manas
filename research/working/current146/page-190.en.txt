A false vision appeared in the distance.
A shovelful of night fell upon repentance.
A merciless day descended,
Entangled with the fall of night.
Hero Kenen ranged among the enemy.
Such was the wrestler’s courage.
It was the hour when he set his words in order.
He laid hands upon every hardship.
It was the hour when he stood and opposed them.
My strength belongs to a life held in trust.
It was the hour when his fire stood blazing.
The reason they gathered was this:
No coin was left to anyone.
The face of day would not grow calm.
The poor hero entered the wrestling.
They fought without circling away,
And grappled without counting the host.
The sons of the roaring stump,
Once more they drew Tabylby’s soul.
Without handing this man to China,
China bound him and branded him.
He walked upright toward your side.
Following the right course, he did not close his eyes.
A radiant power moved among the people.
To give the enemy a trial,
Let me return toward the living world,
Having suffered the torment of the grave.
While he stood upon the battlefield,
Mighty God, grant him faith.
Of the forked one who walked before the khan,
He proved a hero against China.
For the khan’s daughter—for what cause?
For the white-capped Kyrgyz people,
I shall not withhold my life as a sacrifice.
They loaded him as though facing the bullets,
And exposed his bundle to gunfire.
He seized the gathered treasury.
The great Kyrgyz are an inherited people.
Before he could take it, they made him surrender it.
