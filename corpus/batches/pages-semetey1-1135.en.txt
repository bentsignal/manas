The black Kalmyks plundered
Kökötöy's fatty meat.
Unable to restrain the many infidels,
The Muslims grew weary.
From Ezkara came a challenge;
From Zholoy the black Kalmyk
Came fierce turmoil and strife.
If you truly are a hero,
Go now, Manas;
The Muslims are about to be destroyed—
Go and separate them!
You are a khan, a tiger!
Here is the city with many towers;
Here am I, if you would cut off my head!
Külaiym, seated in mourning for Kökötöy—
The infidel stretched out his hand,
Laid hands upon the bereaved woman,
And God struck the Kalmyk.
With hero Kongurbay at their head,
The Chinese girded their waists firmly,
Ready, if they found the means,
To pursue Alööke's blood-price,
To raid the villages of the Muslims
Who had gone to the feast;
Zholoy of the Kalmyks
Was ready to bring utter destruction.
Bloodthirsty Manas, the tiger,
If you truly are a khan,
You will rise in courage.
When you aim at the Manchu whose turn has come,
You will reach him, Manas.
At Khan Kökötöy's feast,
You, tiger Manas, were absent.
We struck the Koroson firestone
Infidel and Muslim alike,
We summoned every one of them.
The people gathered in a mass;
Dust flew into the sky.
Swarming like black worms,
They escaped the eye when one looked,
And none could tell which was which;
Infidel and Muslim alike
Seethed and filled the place in agitation.
Kökötöy's great riches
Were squandered and scattered;
All the abundant gold
Was trampled underfoot.
Among all those possessions,
Ezkara attacked
The swift horse Maaniker.
Bokmurun, giver of the feast,
Bolted out from among the people;
I saw the place where he fled.
My uncle Koshoy lost his bearings;
I saw the place where he panicked.
There were Muzburchak and Khan Kökchö,
And all the Muslim giants.
There was Akun Khan of Afghanistan,
And all the mighty men.
There was Kökbörü, son of Urumkan,
The lion among heroes.
Unable to withstand anyone,
Before infidels as numerous as cow hairs,
They had a time of reeling in panic.
Sour ayran, blue kurut—
He wrought an end-of-time deed,
Bokmurun, Kökötöy's foul urine,
Trusting in the abundance of gold,
Against the very roots of the Muslim people."
After the boy had spoken thus,
Manas then said:
"Damn your father's grave, young Aydar;
You and Bokmurun are drunk, Aydar!
You have no loyalty, hostile Aydar!
After Khan Kökötöy died
And saw the true world,
Why did you not send word?
