The Argyn and Kyrgyz subjects
Were called the people of Khan Kanchoro;
Kapka and Kan-Jailak
Were called the mountains of Khan Kanchoro;
Kara-Buura and Great Chatkal
Were called the gateway of Khan Kanchoro.
Kanchoro, now seated on the throne,
And brave Kyyaz—those two—
Divided the plunder between them.
They brought a white-gray mare for sacrifice,
And without felling the mare,
Slaughtered her while she stood.
When black blood flowed from the mare,
They dipped their hands in it for their oath.
Those wretches, swaggering,
Made their covenant,
Binding the promise firmly
With a pledge punishable by death:
To pursue claims together,
Kanchoro and brave Kyyaz,
And to fight their enemies together.
“If you encamp throughout Kyrka-Tash
From the Seven Valleys,
And I encamp along the slopes
Of Kapka-Tash in broad Talas,
No mortal man will break through.
If we unite as one,
No cold-hearted foe can invade,
And no enemy can suddenly strike us,”
They said.
The two giants clasped hands,
Saying, “Agreed, agreed.”
They declared the seven forefathers
Of whoever broke these words accursed.
They chewed grains of white wheat;
The two babbled their formulas
Holding the sacred Quran,
They cut a switch and gave their word,
Making a covenant no man could escape.
“The Jediger in the Seven Valleys,
All the people who cover the earth—
My kinsman, I shall withhold none from you!”
Thus spoke brave Kyyaz:
“May I become lower than a beast;
Take my bay racehorse,
And if you desire my servitude,
Do not spare me—take my life!
My dear Kanchoro,
Spare one of us and take the other;
Leave my sibling, but take my household!
Take my low-lying land of the Seven Valleys!
Take my people, who are like the Jediger!
Take my bright red-tailed elephants;
Spare me torment—take my life and tongue!
I have spoken weighty words; understand them!
But beware of your Argyn
And Kyrgyz on this side!
There is deceit in their words,
My friend, and corruption
Can be seen in your people’s eyes.
What if we drive this nation out
In a clamorous procession?
What if, my friend, we destroy this people,
Slaughtering every man and leaving none?”
When brave Kyyaz spoke these words,
The hulking Kanchoro
Started in alarm and said:
“What manner of words are these?
Jylgyn, Keng-Kol, and Talas—
If I wish to reap them, I shall reap them;
If I wish to swallow them, I shall raise an uproar,
Gulping them down like a dragon,
And devour every last person.
If my terrible rage seizes me,
I shall leave not even a hair
And massacre this people;
If I roar in fury—”
