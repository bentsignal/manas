Let us give camels as tribute,
And give them many people as tribute;
Let us accept their demands
And give them whatever they ask.
Let us neither quarrel nor feud,
But preserve our people.
From this tumultuous conflict
Let us escape safe and sound.
Let us say, ‘Our roots and souls are one;
We have been kin from the beginning,’
And offer them reasoned words.
Let us stand against this flood
And survive safe and sound.
Let us gather the daughters
Of the Argyn and the Kyrgyz
And lead them forth in procession.
Offering girls, let us say, ‘We submit;
Brave Kyyaz, halt!’
Let us give him skilled young women,
Girls in red dresses with moon-slender waists,
Graceful, playful girls,
And ask him to restrain his army.
May our cherished people survive unharmed
And return safely to their villages.
Let us become in-laws, exchanging livestock,
Clasp hands, and share rich butter,
Eat meat from a single platter,
Meet face-to-face as kinsmen,
Declare the blood-price for Toltoy,
And offer apologies and gifts.
Let us make Talas our shared land
And the Jediger our people;
Let us all settle together
And become newfound kin.
Let us accept what he says;
Let us place both reins and the single bridle
In his hands now,
Telling him to put the people in order.
Let us proclaim to the nation
That here in Talas we shall
Raise Kyyaz as khan!”
When Kanchoro had spoken thus,
As though pierced in a tender swelling,
Külchoro sprang to his feet:
“You were Chubak’s handsome son,
Three years older than I;
We kicked and tussled like foals,
And suckled at one another’s mothers’ breasts.
Among all the friends and kinsmen
Whose ears have heard and eyes have seen,
When has this bloodthirsty lion
Ever been called a child?
You wander and rave in your speech,
Rehearsing words of treachery;
You praise the Jediger’s Kyyaz
As though he were a monster!
Kanchoro, may a curse strike you!
May the spirits of our forefathers—
Almambet and the eagle Chubak,
The lions Syrgak and Serek,
And gray wolf Manas’s warriors—judge us!
If I should flinch and speak crookedly,
Make my words double-tongued,
Make my speech dishonorable,
And blacken my own face—
If, like you, Kanchoro,
I should sit here committing treachery,
May God strike me down at once!
You have spoken too many treacherous words,
And ended by saying
You would raise Kyyaz as khan;
With those words you have ruined everything.
Do you think Talas is an easy land to take?
The Argyn and Kyrgyz of Talas
Who inhabit the Seven Valleys—
Are they inferior to the Jediger?
May your Kyyaz sink into the earth!
Is he more heroic than our Semetey?
