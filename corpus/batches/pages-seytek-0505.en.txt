The substance of the tale to be told:
Manas’s younger sister was Karzhygach,
And this was the husband of Karzhygach.
He humiliated many warriors,
Broke their formations and opened a path,
When he plundered their people,
And spread slaughter through their lands.
A vile people swept down like a gale,
Destroying their homes and spilling blood,
Laying low many brave men,
And ravaging all the borderlands.
Six swift horses came,
Bringing word to lion-hearted, noble Manas:
“The monster has brought us misery,”
“He has burned our country.”
A letter small as a palm arrived,
And when the tidings reached them,
Azhybay and old Bakai were there,
And all the valiant warriors were there.
Almambet, Syrgak, and Chubak were there,
And many wise old men were there,
And all the valiant warriors were there.
They mounted in fierce excitement,
And the heroes rode out together.
They joined Zharmanas,
And camped beyond seven ridges,
Coming to their brother-in-law’s aid.
After seven days of slaughter,
The fortress walls collapsed.
The immeasurably mighty monster
Fell dead at Khan Manas’s hand,
And his people scattered like frightened birds.
Many of his warriors died,
And those who remained alive
Were divided among them as spoils.
“They slaughtered the monster,”
“They put him to death,” they said.
His fame remained throughout the world,
As did the renown of our father.
“God has taken his father;
Let us fight and avenge him.
If that warrior is still alive,
Let us unite with him.
From grievous torment and affliction
Let us deliver him!”
After he had spoken thus,
They prepared themselves in fury,
And to those bloodthirsty warriors
Inspiration came from the unseen.
They readied all their weapons,
And girded on naked swords—
Heroic Seytek and Külchoro,
Those brave wolves of war.
Unable to stand still when they heard,
Brave Semetey and his champions joined them.
Their eighty-four kinds of armor were donned,
And their sweeping onset blazed like fire.
Stones split and mountains crumbled
As they thundered forth in menace.
From the Argyn and the Kyrgyz
They formed a host of ten thousand,
Mustering an army in a tumult
As dust soared into the sky.
They sharpened their true-steel spears,
And in a single day their valor
Seemed ready to overturn
The frost-hardened black earth.
Then Külchoro
Put on his bulletproof coat
And prepared himself in fury:
“While our mighty heads remain,
Let us exact a fearful price in slaughter.
If Sarybay is truly formidable,
Let us go and fight him to the death!
I have learned this from the chroniclers,
And questioned a thousand wise men.
Yesterday, when he crossed Zhan-Zhürbös,
And the vast hazy desert closed around him,
Zharmanas of that wicked people
Proved to be our brother-in-law.
