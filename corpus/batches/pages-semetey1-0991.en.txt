Dear elder, come and ride with us;
Let us cast turmoil across the world!
If a seething host of enemies comes out,
Let us charge and level our spears;
Let us turn and fight without fleeing,
And rest only when we have won Aychurok.
Your encampment stands settled in Talas;
We would be less than men if we did not take
Your wife named Aychurok.
While your fortune remains unharmed,
Elder, your fortune is like
The shoulder of a mountain.
Do not let people say, 'Semetey, son of Manas,
Had his falcon carried away,
And Semetey, out of fear,
Let his falcon be taken
And failed even to notice.'
Driving fourteen horses from the Kyrgyz,
Let us follow the winding road.
My blessed elder, heed my words;
Let us overtake your maiden bride!
If we prove unworthy, let us die;
Rather than walk the earth alive,
Let us sink away as though never born!
Only now have I heard your story,
And of the wife your father,
Tiger Manas, appointed for you.
You have girded your waist for action;
Now tighten that girdle more firmly.
My heroic elder, stand and hear
The words your young men have brought you!
Your wife proved black-hearted;
She did not know how to honor guests.
She reviled tiger Bakay and exposed
The private affairs of the three khans one by one.
With hero Bakay at their head,
Your three khans sat there abashed,
Their heads hanging like howling dogs.
In fury she hemmed her in;
My dear mother Kanykey
And beat her with a staff.
The beating brought her to submission;
Your two companions exhausted themselves,
For formidable indeed, heroic one,
Was the trouble caused by your wife.
Your noble elder is a khan;
We began the uproar,
And beat your broad-rumped wife
More than a little!
We brought calamity down upon her head,
And, making her cry out beneath our blows,
Forced her to tell of Akshunkar!
If you would have us struck down, Opol lies here;
If you say, 'They touched my wife,'
And have a hero cut us down, here we stand!
Here stands the governed Argyn people;
If you say, 'They caused this disaster,'
And cut off our heads, here we stand!
Young sultan, you now know our news:
Though we pleaded, she ranted on,
And your wife hurled many insults.
For beating your wife and leaving her,
The blame belongs to us, your two companions!
Judge fully which side behaved wantonly;
Poor lord, listen to us—
If you would destroy us, here we stand!
If you send us, the road of the journey is here;
If you pour out your fury, lord, here we stand!"
The young lord was a tiger,
A saint born greater still
Than his father, gray-maned Manas.
He hung close around his neck
The gray purse left by Manas.
That poor lion, speaking timidly,
Appealed to his young men;
Showing that he was their lord,
He placed Kulchoro on his right,
And Kanchoro on his left,
