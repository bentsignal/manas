All the people gathered;
There is much grief that must be told.
For Kökötöy's memorial feast,
Many champions rendered service.
Bokmurun of Kökötöy
Stirred great commotion among the people;
Every great pit
He had dug and prepared properly.
Kökötöy's countless livestock
He had slaughtered without counting.
Everyone who came
He filled with fat from the rump.
Bokmurun of Kökötöy
Was himself a peerless youth;
Parted from his father,
He had reached thirteen years of age.
A treasury filling forty rooms—
He opened the treasury.
Kökötöy's vast wealth
He scattered like charcoal.
Kökötöy's many strongmen
Dug and prepared the pit;
Kökötöy's black male camel
They tethered in that pit.
Surpassing all people in his custom,
Upon the black camel's head
He had a silver bridle fastened
And a costly carpet spread.
Kökötöy's green-hued
Blue saddlebag,
No ordinary camel bag but one for a great bull camel—
Kökötöy's many treasures
Filled many saddlebags there.
Inside that saddlebag,
What was there, and what was not?
The head grew dizzy at the sight.
Inside that saddlebag,
Rubies and gold were mingled.
For his father's memorial feast,
The wretched youth spared not his life:
“Black male camel left by the prince,
Masterless beast fallen upon your head—
How could I begrudge it for my father?
How could I heed the gaze
Of this wretched false world?
I have a spectacle for the people;
Upon the black male camel
I have wealth for the multitude to plunder.
I have a secret gathered here;
For infidel and Muslim,
There is a wonder that will make them laugh.
I have a song drawn from the camp;
For Kalmak and Kyrgyz,
I have a rite to perform.
If you do not submit to that rite,
I have a quarrel to raise.
To openly strip away
Kökötöy's many treasures,
Let naked women come forth;
Walk up and strip them away.
Remain there having grown rich.
For infidel and Muslim,
For all the great multitude,
Put on a spectacle.”
Infidel and Muslim
Gathered together,
Counseling one another and asking,
“Where is there wealth like this?”
Until their council was satisfied and complete,
With a face like an old gourd,
