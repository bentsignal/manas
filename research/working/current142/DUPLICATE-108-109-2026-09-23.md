# Inventory 142: duplicate photography of folio 2424

Source: `sources/raw/manuscript-full-91-146/142.pdf`, SHA-256
`2b1fd33d35b9fec662724180743e44a26bc44d8b347d5f99c373acf98cb6c73f`.
PDF pages **108 and 109** are two photographs of the **same manuscript leaf**,
folio **2424**, taken with different framing. I rendered both at 60 dpi and
compared the visible folio number, opening (`Атар колдо далай бар` followed
by `Муну менен жүз атып`), distinctive middle lines, handwritten margin
**51**, and the closing lines. The verse sequence is the same. The PNG files
have different hashes because the framing differs; bytewise identity is not
the criterion for duplicate photography. PDF page **110** is the next leaf,
folio **2425**, with different text.

This matters for the inventory's physical extent: count folio 2424 **once**,
not twice. Do not infer an exact 51-row reduction in the manuscript total
from its margin number alone; count and transcribe its physical baselines
before revising canonical totals. The saved `page-108.reviewed.jsonl` and
`page-109.reviewed.jsonl` are from the misassigned draft range: they label
the leaves folios 2422 and 2423 and do not represent these two photographs.
Neither draft may be released or used as evidence of different content.

The duplicate must be included in the complete photo-extent audit and
excluded once from any source-line accounting. No manuscript row was
released by this finding.
