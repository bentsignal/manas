Sate yourself with this one spoil!
I am a boy of twelve,
A Kalcha lacking wisdom.
If you are a man, Toltoy,
Turn your stallion's head;
If you are a hero, hero Toltoy,
Turn and stand nearby!
Give us one spectacle;
Take this twelve-year-old boy
Away as plunder!
Does a heroic man flee?
Does a hero panic and hurry?
If you are a hero, Toltoy,
Does a hero flee from a boy?
Arbuurul beneath me
Was Semetey's horse.
Do not flee sprawling, hero Toltoy;
If you take it as plunder, you will be enriched.
You were a hero, hero Toltoy;
Are you a mare, that you flee?
If your courage is true,
Sate yourself with bloody blood;
Stand without fleeing and fight.
Hold straight and thrust spears,
Fight until one is finished;
Joust with spear in hand,
And if you have the means, take me.
If you are mighty, hero Toltoy,
Bring calamity upon my head!
Does a hero spare a boy?
Is it proper to flee from a boy?"
When the boy spoke thus,
Fleeing hero Toltoy
Stretched the drum,
Struck Surkoen,
And leopard Toltoy then
Whipped from side to side,
Spurred both ways,
And fled enormously.
Dear gray-maned Toltoy's
Eyes darted wildly,
And his legs dangled loose.
Without turning aside, hero Toltoy
Fled with a roar.
Without looking around
Or sparing his own life,
Kulchoro pursued him.
Black-bay thoroughbred,
Sieve-lunged and copper-winged,
Spirit-like Roan horse
Lowered his head to the earth
And took an argali's stride.
He lowered his neck to the earth
And ran beyond all measure.
His eyes reddened like a wild ram's;
His stride lengthened with every step.
His hooves thundered on the earth;
The black-steel bit
Clattered in his throat.
Throwing his forelock to the sky,
Beautiful racehorse Arbuurul
Drove his hooves into the earth.
From the feather-grass ridge,
From the high-shouldered rise,
When the Roan came alongside,
Shouting, "Manas! Manas!"
Calling the battle cry "Manas,"
Young Lord Kulchoro
Raised the patterned spear,
Turned the Roan's head,
And leveled the patterned spear
At Toltoy whom you have seen.
Kulchoro was an ocean;
Without looking around,
Without sparing his life at all,
As the Roan passed alongside,
He cried, "Ah God, support me!"
And then raised his spear.
