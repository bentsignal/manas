All means of defense have been exhausted;
This infidel son of the ogre,
With wrath pouring from his brow,
Has become another missile of death.
A bullet flew from the cartridge pouch,
And many bold warriors’ sons fell.
Courage fled and scattered;
The infidel son of the ogre
Appeared ready to devour them.
Only a peerless warrior
Will not flinch when shots are fired.
Unless Allah decrees it,
She does not appear destined for capture.
Fierce is the wrath of these relentless ones;
They laid flat whoever faced them.
Make him turn his back in this hour;
Keep Kuyaly, Kaiypchal’s
Champion daughter, safe!
It would bring shameful renown
If she fell to that fearsome swine.
He pledged himself to many saints,
And seven holy men showed him the way;
His father created order upon the earth
And subjugated the whole universe.
Mighty Manas, wondrous as a peri,
Please watch over
Kaiypchal’s champion daughter;
Great Manas of the mighty spirit,
Please remain her companion!
Seize the cold-faced villain
By the very hem of his robe.
Stretch the infidel who would wage war
Full-length upon the ground!
He began the campaign against Beijing,
And left it desolate.
A vast multitude, a mighty host,
He led all by himself.
He gnawed down whoever he met,
And swept away whoever crossed him.
His skill was extraordinary,
And his heroism was manifold.
Best of the noble khan’s descendants,
My dear brother, watch over her!
Do not let grievous calamity befall;
Kaiypchal, remain her companion.
His lashes were flame, his eyes embers;
No horror was absent from his wrath,
And every word he spoke was a fired bullet.
His stature differed from all men,
And his valor was manifold.
When the enemy charged with a cry,
A scarlet sword was strapped to him.
Like a cloud returning with rain,
His wrath spread across his face.
When the foe came from the flank,
He never turned aside for fear of death.
When a swarming enemy host came,
He could never stand and merely watch.
He girded his seasoned waist,
And charged through seventy ranks.
Light enough for a horse, fit for a colt,
Sleepless and vigilant upon the road—
Brave Syrgak of the young khan,
Your fortune was perfect.
For the sons of the white-capped Kyrgyz,
You were a fortress of tempered steel.
You were a young lion and hero,
The one on whom I relied.
God, set this matter right;
Valiant lion Syrgak,
Block Sarybai’s path!
God, turn the back
Of the ravenous bloodthirsty one.
The truest champion of the Oigut,
Son of Akbalta,
Whose might was known across the world.
With an unsheathed sword at his side,
Bakburchun of the forty Chinese tribes
Submitted in terror at your appearance.
Hungry lion, mighty champion,
