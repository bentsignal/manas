Will be an inheritance for you.
Fighting and exchanging shots,
You made a spectacle in Altay.
The forty companions you had gathered,
By Esenkan's champion,
Great Zholoy, you had them slaughtered.
‘I am utterly alone,’ you said,
And wandered in sorrow.
Champions your equal,
Throwing the world into turmoil,
Merciless mighty men,
Upon your head visited by Kydyr,
