My brow bone must be ill-shaped;
I let a fleeing soul escape.
May this vile, false world be desolate—
My fortune must be crooked.
As I ask the gray wolf about the Argyn,
Just as I was about to become a sultan,
Ah, my deep-dug pit;
Ah, my stain no washing removes!
On snowy ground there is no cradle;
I let the fleeing foe escape—
There is no blessed portion for wretched me!
My brow bone must be ill-shaped;
My fortune was meager from the start!
No good name remains to me;
Calamity pressed down upon my head:
Looming before me,
Mountain-like Toltoy fled.
I let the fleeing foe escape;
Can there be a man like me?
Is it right to call
A wretch like me a man?
Can there be a man like me?
A mortal man may be made a giant;
Rather than make a person powerless,
Why does Almighty God
Not leave his mortal
Uncreated instead?
Rather than be born feeble,
Why did this wretched soul
Not see through
The decree God laid down?
Why did this wretched soul
Not die in youth?
Without caring for his horse's longing,
My noble elder Semetey
Gave me his horse without begrudging it.
Saying, “I failed to catch Toltoy,”
How can I return in disgrace?
Without caring for his coat's worth,
My mountain-like elder Semetey
Gave me his coat without begrudging it.
To Toltoy who fled bobbing away,
Saying, “I myself failed to catch him,”
How can I return in disgrace?
The humiliation of the Jediger—
Would that I had seen it,
Charged alone among their army,
Fought seven days without pause,
And died if my doom came.
Against hero Toltoy and Khoja,
Girding the jekene at my waist,
Would that I had attacked alone,
And before reaching my maturity,
Died without delay!
Against accursed Toltoy,
Would that I had waged a wondrous war
And split his realm in two!
I long to camp on rugged ground;
Against accursed Toltoy,
Against the Khoja gone astray,
I long to wage a wondrous war
And be drenched red with blood.
Would that I split his head and cut it off,
And before Semetey
Carried it deliberately as a burden.
Against mountain-like gray wolf Toltoy,
There is no bruise upon my body,
No wound anywhere upon me.
Trembling worse than a woman,
Is it right that fugitive Toltoy escaped?
Can there be a man like me,
Who let the fleeing foe escape?!
Is it right to call
A wretch like me a man?
Curse your father's grave, world;
Accursed childishness—
When will this cursed immaturity
Leave my head!
