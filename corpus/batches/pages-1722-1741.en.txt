I have let a bad name fall upon me;
Almambet, Chubak, take me with you!
Chasing the white deer,
I did not die when I made them cattle.
Beating the whole world,
I did not die when I threw it into confusion.
Almambet and Chubak,
I did not die when I made them giants.
The whole world, let alone China,
Striking and destroying them all,
While the mighty ones were still here,
Then, when I was left on foot, I did not die.
Black Beijing and Kambyl,
If only I had died while besieging them!
The governors and the Soloon,
If only I had died while beating them!
Their walls built forty cubits high,
I did not die when I made a road through them.
Their red gold coins,
I did not die when I made a feast of them.
Beijing of Kakan-Chin,
I did not die in the year I besieged it.
Its abundant gold coins,
I did not die in the year I plundered them.
Did I only go on grieving instead of dying?
Those no scrutiny could bring into one’s hands,
All the champions,
Good heavens,
Did I hand them over to Beijing?
I returned with a bad name;
Oh Almambet, Chubak, take me with you!
Yesterday, in the hollow of Zhylgynduu,
I would slice and eat horse rump.
If Almambet were beside me,
I would say, ‘I shall overthrow the governor-general.’
When I was terribly hungry,
I would slice and eat horse-belly fat.
With my companion Chubak standing there,
The whole world, let alone China,
I would say, ‘I shall destroy them.’
A bulletproof fine-cloth shirt,
I would say, ‘I shall put it on.’
While my devastating Almam was here,
Against the eighteen thousand worlds,
I would say, ‘I shall advance.’
Black-steel Zulpuqor,
I would say, ‘I shall fight with it.’
My Khan Chubak, one soul with me,
If he stood beside me as support,
Against the eighteen thousand worlds,
I would say, ‘I shall shoulder my way through.’
Has the banner I held been broken?
Man, have my enemies found peace?
Has the boundless lake turned to mud?
My Almambet and Chubak,
Alas, my Syrgak,
Has the cruel black earth swallowed them?
Yesterday, I dwelt in Talas, settled in its land.
Twelve learned men of Islam—
Which of them would obey my words?
Making them all cry aloud,
I drove them off bound fast.
Beijing that no prophet could tread,
Saying, ‘I shall tread its royal place of honor,’
Of my blue-maned sovereign Almam,
Saying, ‘I shall release the grief within him,’
Kongurbay, khan of the monks,
