They adorned him with a black plume,
And tended Choro like a young child,
Wrapping him in layers of rich fat.
With every kind of healing medicine,
They dressed his wounds again and again,
Until his whole body began to move
And strength entered him.
A hundred times more than before,
His strength returned;
Fivefold beyond his former state,
Külchoro, upheld by the sacred spirit,
Came back to life.
His strength was restored anew;
There was no enemy left to fight.
Rampaging and slashing in his frenzy,
Your lion Choro surged with ardor
And regained his might.
Broad-backed and bracing himself,
He swayed as though struck from this side;
His bulk was like a ridge’s crest,
His surge like a raging flood.
If you looked upon his courage,
It was like the flame above a mountain valley;
His bullet-proof fists were like great knobs.
Such was the appearance
Of spirit-favored Külchoro.
The sable cap worn upon the head—
Who has not donned it with a flourish?
When fortune stood beside him,
Who did not stride about like thunder?
At the sight of the panther hero,
What enemy did not flee in panic?
He wore armor of blue steel;
Your gray-maned hero struck the foe once,
Then fitted his sword into its sheath.
Any enemy who met this wretch
Blessed by the holy wanderer fell.
When he rode, dust arose;
When he entered battle, blood flowed.
For twelve years, this ill-fated Choro
Endured his fate:
Carrying firewood upon his back,
Beaten by honorable men,
And abused by godless dogs,
I burned and hardened in the flames.
Thinking, “If he lives, he will be my shelter;
He will devour the many foes,
Make splendid havoc in the slaughter,
And take vengeance on the enemy,”
I burned with longing for you.
My broken wing has mended,
My only happiness has found me,
My grieving thoughts have faded,
And my tail has grown an armspan long.
Now that the hour of vengeance has come,
Every calamity has been cut away;
My sorrow has fled and I have recovered,
And once again grown mighty.
I received a letter from Kyzylsart,
And together we received Kyzыр’s blessing;
From my precious sister-in-law Chürök
I received a horse no bullet could catch.
After quietly washing away my filth,
From my fierce, tempestuous sister-in-law
I donned a coat no bullet could pierce.
My wasted strength grew full again,
My hungry belly was filled;
For me to eat, a barren mare
Was slaughtered when my orphan arrived.
My foster father Kyzylsart
Appeared with forty caravans,
And now the honor of sorrowing,
Afflicted Choro has been restored.
If sent ahead, his path is white;
If following behind, his army is countless.
The orphan named in the testament has been found,
And thus the honor of Choro,
Born for grief, has been restored.
Its shaft is sound and its tip is sharp—
The hollow-shafted ornamented spear.
