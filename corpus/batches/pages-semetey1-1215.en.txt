Orongu’s dun mare.
That was the last of the nine;
Khan Manas’s Akkula
Now moved out in front.
Kökötöi’s countless prizes
The Black Chinese and Manchu people
Overwhelmed and plundered.
Without gaining possession of the prizes,
The Muslims were left empty-handed.
From the Chinese came Kongurbai,
Black-maned Boroonchu,
From the Kalmaks Khan Zholoi,
And Ylama of the Solon;
Do not ask about the violence
Of those snarling infidels.
Dust flew and haze burned;
The teeming Chinese covered the land.
This was the Kakanchin pass;
The Chinese had come as guests
To Kökötöi’s memorial feast,
Yet it was time for them to clash.
Zholoi came out using force;
Kongurbai came out then,
Humiliating the Muslims.
Great wrestler Ylama,
A Kalmak like great Zholoi,
Wielding axes of many kinds—
The doomed wrestlers,
The massed Chinese and many Kalmaks,
Plundered Kökötöi’s
Many prizes.
It became a deeply dug pit;
Kakan-Beijing stirred,
For this was Kökötöi’s feast,
The beginning of much conflict.
It was a deep, excavated pit,
Misfortune set boiling.
The heroes guarding the livestock
Were beaten down.
The many prizes, those Chinese
Plundered and carried away.
They dug a well in the place of honor;
The prize won by Akkula,
By force then,
The Black Kalmaks scattered.
Kökötöi’s abundant wealth—
They plundered its silks,
And wrought terrible disorder.
They now spoke their claim,
And took the prizes of the horses
That had placed, then returned.
They tormented the people;
Against the Black Kalmak and Manchus
No one remained to cast a lasso.
Leaving no moving livestock,
They drove off the finest.
Whoever resisted,
They put to death.
Those whose death had come died;
At Kökötöi’s feast,
From the Black Chinese and Manchus,
The Muslims suffered humiliation.
The seething Chinese ran about;
When they saw the Chinese people,
The handful of Muslims
Despaired of their dear lives.
They rushed about in alarm;
Some who had lost all recourse
Fled toward the ravine.
The Muslims had giants,
And mortals who called themselves heroes,
Many kinds in all.
The Muslim khans
Lost their wits;
Never mind the prizes—
All of them, even old hero Koshoy,
Fled to save their lives.
Full in courage and strong in venom,
