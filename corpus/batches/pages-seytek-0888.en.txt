Let me toy with him awhile,
Then clash with him and cut him down.
All the vast store of vengeance
I shall exact from this boy.
Father, I shall slaughter every one
Of the people of the boy who defied me,
And glut myself fully upon their blood.
If I do not unleash true havoc,
If I do not plunge my hand into his entrails,
If I do not destroy his whole village,
If I do not take their horses and robes as plunder,
May I die rather than live.
Upon Kulansur, whom none can gain by asking,
I shall cast a cruel lasso;
Kulansur, whom none can gain by pleading,
I shall lead away riderless.
If I do not break the tyrannical rage
Of that son of a bitch,
If I do not curb his far-flung fury,
If I do not cut short his young life—
He struck his spear with menace,
Attacked me with a battle cry,
And drove his spear deep into this warrior.
May you never reach maturity!
May you never see the coming autumn!
He bloodied his spear,
Piercing me without mercy
And nearly hurling me from my saddle!
My very soul screamed,
And the place he struck was shattered.
Through carelessness and contempt,
I nearly met my death;
From this ill-fated boy
I nearly beheld
The land of no return.
He caught me along my flank;
Through my carelessness I let him spear me.
My strength deserted me and I grew weak,
Stunned and senseless,
I nearly witnessed a horror
No human should ever see.
Such is the state of my soul,” he said,
Grinding his teeth like a boar.
Gathering his courage and thinking deeply,
He gripped the hilt in silence.
He did not refrain from combat.
If you ask what manner of man Sarybay was:
He had traveled roads and seen deep mountains,
Roamed far and encountered foes,
A master of the spear, potent in venom;
His dreadful presence leveled all before him.
No human could accomplish such deeds.
A creature well suited to the enemy’s ranks,
He enslaved all who met him,
Gnawed apart all he encountered,
And hacked down all who crossed him.
Strange of face and narrow of heart,
Mighty in presence and venomous in visage,
He shouted and raged,
Girded on his keen steel,
And prepared to confront the boy.
When they came face-to-face, he bellowed,
Raising his battle cry.
He invoked his departed father, Jelmoguz,
Calling upon his giant spirit;
He called upon the lord
Of Jelmoguz beneath the earth.
Breaking through the dead end and opening a path,
The swine attacked at once.
He turned Tookyzyl’s head,
And without pause struck
The hellish blow squarely at the ankle.
Then he leveled
His steel-tipped spear with a thunderous roar.
Furiously preparing himself,
Roaring in menace,
Calling upon all his distant rage,
The hair on his crown stood on end,
Each strand of his beard bristled apart,
And Sarybay’s fatal hour seized him.
