## Boundary evidence and totals

The known line counts for 954–966 sum to **76,568**. Counts are absent online for 961–963, leaving **8,129** lines to reach the edition's 84,697-line core target. This is plausible in scale for the 440 encyclopedia page units assigned to those three inventories, but it is not a crosswalk: catalogue counts may be rounded and the bibliography contains overlaps and discontinuities.

The immediately following old inventory 967/current 146 is also titled *Seytek*, recorded in 1947, and has 9,000 lines: <https://manuscript.bizdin.kg/рукопись/СейтекE/>. Including it raises the known sum to **85,568**, which is **871 above** 84,697 even before the three blank counts are supplied. Thus neither 954–966 nor 954–967 can yet be equated arithmetically to the edited core: an editorial cut, overlap, continuation boundary, or a different catalogue counting method is required.

The catalogue also contains later mixed or continuation records, including current 149/old 4066, titled “Manas (Semetey, Seytek, Almsaryk, Kunansaryk),” recorded in 1968, but it has no public line count: <https://manuscript.bizdin.kg/рукопись/Манас-Семетей-Сейтек-Алмсарык-Кунансарык/>. A 1968 sound recording also includes “Seytek and his descendants”: <https://manuscript.bizdin.kg/рукопись/Отрывок-из-эпоса-Манас-Сейтек-анын-тукуму-Устный-рассказ-9-00-Посвящение-своей-жене-3-00-Төкмө/>.

The **196,936** KazNU figure is best explained as reclassification, not an additional core manuscript target. The coherent 500,553-line accounting is 84,513 Manas + 316,157 Semetey + 84,697 Seytek + 15,186 continuations. KazNU gives 84,830 + 218,787 + 196,936, also 500,553. Relative to the coherent accounting, KazNU moves Manas by +317, Semetey by −97,370, and Seytek (compared with Seytek plus continuations, 99,883) by +97,053. Those deltas sum to zero. The near-exact transfer strongly indicates changed part boundaries, with continuations absorbed into “Seytek,” rather than 112,239 missing core Seytek lines. No public source found assigns 196,936 inventory by inventory.

## Official acquisition route

No public full scans or transcriptions were located. The actionable route is a reproduction/digitization request to the National Academy's Manuscript Fund, specifying both shelfmark systems **old 954–966 (and boundary-check 967), current 133–146**, and requesting complete color TIFF/PDF plus any inventory foliation and line-count sheets. The catalogue footer identifies the Manuscript Fund at 265a Chuy Avenue, Bishkek 720071, +996 (312) 64-63-58. The current institute page lists the same address, the Manuscript Fund head A. Isaeva, institute phones +996 700 000 235 and +996 (312) 39-20-23, and `melis.a.-50@mail.ru`: <https://kruia.gov.kg/content_list/show/62/ru>. These details are recorded for a later authorized request; no institution was contacted during this audit.
