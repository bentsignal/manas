Hero, though I implored you, you ignored my words
And brought me to this wretched state.
My words sweeter than sugar—
You sought to break my back.
You passed through me like consuming fire;
From time to time,
You sought to blind my eyes.
Alas, my lord, you have not prospered;
You brought disgrace and humiliated me.
My heart sinks and I am torn apart:
If I call you alive, you are not among the living;
If I call you dead, you are not in the grave—
Why did you appear before my eyes?
You burned me until I was seared,
Blackened me into a coal,
And destroyed my precious life.
You revealed your mighty valor;
Without smoke and without flame,
You set me smoldering and burned me.
You began this grievous affliction.
On the uplands, the horses are piebald,
Thoroughbreds mingled with stallions;
You abandoned your livestock.
Fashioned with a shaft of gold,
You abandoned your throne.
Your speech is sweet and your words are honey;
Bloodthirsty lord, hear me without causing grief.
Do not loosen my shovel-like teeth,
Do not burn my heart like this.
Do not boil and burn into embers
The man who looked to you for protection.
I mourned and searched for many years;
Now that what I lost has been found,
How can I keep from dissolving,
When I have encountered my tiger, my elder brother?
My precious elder brother, begin to speak;
Lay aside your lordly pride.
Now I shall die at your hands—
The true realm from which none returns,
By the power of almighty Khidr-Allah.
Through twelve rearguards and forty scouts,
I offered my breast to bullets.
You made me suffer boundless affliction
And endure dreadful humiliation.
Famed Keng-Shor and Kan-Jailak,
Ülgülüü-Maral and Kümüsh-Tak,
The banks of Besh-Tash and Kapka-Tash—
You abandoned these lands.
The dependent Kyrgyz and the many Argyn—
You abandoned your people.
O great plane tree with lofty branches,
You abandoned your fruit.
Until the five-argali constellation had set,
She widened her womb five times,
She opened her constricted belly
And let down milk from her stony breast—
You abandoned your mother.
Your father’s confidant,
The noble Bakai, his companion,
Blessed in his old age—
You abandoned your forefather.
Six months in the womb, the ill-fated one—
Hero, you abandoned your child.
Reedy Keng-Kol and broad Talas,
The many Kyrgyz encamped in rows,
Kumiss by day and maidens by night—
You abandoned your homeland.
The moonlike woman Aichürök—
You abandoned your wife.
Argali on your hills, wild asses on your plains—
You abandoned your land.
Placed before you, I was a blessed path;
Sent into battle, I was like a countless host.
You abandoned me.
Though I implored you, you ignored my words;
You bent my wretched back,
Spread your unbreakable net,
And showed me great suffering.
You had Kanchoro strip from you
Your flowered robe that no bullet could pierce.
