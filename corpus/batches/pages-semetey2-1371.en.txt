Stretch her out and beat her like a hide;
Keep striking her as punishment.
Put a felt cloak upon her,
Mount her on a barren mare,
Slash her soles and rub in salt,
Slash her fair face and fill the wounds with worms;
Then give her a writ of divorce
To take to her father Akun’s city,
And drive her off with her head hanging!
From her father Akun’s city
Did this shrew not come
Swaying in a fine fur coat,
Radiant upon an ambling bay horse?
Now let the disgraced wretch return in misery,
Flopping about on a barren mare.
If the hapless woman will not obey,
Seat her upon a bare wooden saddle,
Mount her on a gaunt mare,
Dress her in a filthy old robe,
And drive her off with her head bowed!
From her father Akun’s city
Did this shrew not come
Radiant in a silken robe,
Riding proudly on a gray pacer?
Now let the black-faced wretch return ruined
To the city of her father the khan,
Flopping about on a gaunt mare,
And drooping in a filthy old robe.
Let Akun’s people gossip about her
And together decide her fate.
With no brother-in-law to escort her,
No sister-in-law to receive a bridal gift,
They will say she flew away to marry,
That heaven-bound wonder amid grievous talk,
But lost that husband
And returned wandering and forlorn—
So shall Khan Akun’s people murmur.
Now let her do this instead;
Fulfill the word you gave.
Let us take moon-beautiful Aichürök
And make her our lady.
Fulfill your promise;
Stand by these words!
Your bloodthirsty husband is dead;
That gives a brave man grounds
To take his woman as wife—
Know that this is what has been said.
Before blood is spilled and a brave man dies,
Marry brave Kyyaz.
If you do not marry brave Kyyaz,
Your black hair, long as meadow grass,
Will be torn out strand by strand,
And today your head will be cut off.
Your mighty gray-maned hero is dead,
Your hands are bound,
And you have no answer left.
If you wish to live on without dying,
Heed what you are told
And go as senior wife
To Kyyaz, khan of the Jediger.
If you heed what is said
And go to brave Kyyaz,
Each day will be revelry, each day a game;
Know the weight of these words.
Your dwelling will be made of jewels;
Take both reins and the single bridle—
Full authority—from brave Kyyaz.
Let them call you chief among women;
Become the sovereign
Of the Jediger in the Seven Rivers.
Take off your widow’s garb, sister Aichürök;
Go with honor, without suffering humiliation,
Without letting others consume your gold,
And without being called a captive wife.
Go with your dignity intact.
You were a lionhearted tigress;
Heed what I tell you.
If you do not heed my words,
