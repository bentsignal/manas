The hour has come for him to slaughter us.
Chynkojo, son of Shygai,
The khoja whose deeds were bold,
When summer came,
Would not let us mount even a maned filly;
He brought suffering upon my village.
When autumn came,
He would not let us eat even a tailed lamb;
He brought humiliation upon my land.
He let no tent trappings be prepared,
No crops of any kind be sown,
No furrow be opened in the earth,
No handful of grain be scattered.
Chynkojo's humiliation
Never leaves my innermost heart;
Such is the accursed man's oppression.
All the great people
He broke apart and dispersed;
He imposed hell by force,
And gathered his army complete.
Saying, ‘Give your eldest daughter,’
He used force against Khan Akun.
If we do not obey what he says,
If we do not give Aychürök,
He will level the straight fortress
And raid us entirely;
The accursed mighty Khoja has come.
Leading an army like black worms,
Infidel Khoja is coming;
To bring slaughter upon the people,
Hero Khoja is coming.
Toltoy of the Jediger
Leads an army that bends the earth,
Driving all the Jediger together,
Prepared to ride out in single combat.
Like an enraged tiger,
Leaving no attacker alive,
The deeply dug pit comes;
Misfortune comes to whoever angers him.
An army comes that will engulf
The few Afghans.
A gray-piebald banner comes;
Unless you are our own defender,
A wailing uproar comes.
A white-mottled banner comes;
Hungry shouts and uproar come.
My courageous hero Toltoy—
Where was he born,
That while he has Chynkojo
He unleashes turmoil upon us?
The Jediger have spurred forward
And engulfed us evenly;
Half bear guns and half spears,
Bringing slaughter upon you.
The Jediger are surging upon us;
What remedy can we devise?
Only son of the king,
Here is sovereign Afghan land;
If you do not rescue it,
Slaughter it with your own hand—
If you would take heads, here are ours.
The time has come to receive judgment;
Unless you yourself are sharp,
The hour has come for us to die.
The time for a spectacle has come;
Unless you yourself are our precious one,
The hour has come for us to be slaughtered.
Wrathful Toltoy has come;
Blood ready to flow has come.
If his Khoja raises a shout,
Lives ready to be cut down have come.
If we match strength with Toltoy,
He seems able to uproot the pillars;
If we struggle with Chynkojo,
He seems able to slaughter us without remainder.
From the flank, his Tölök
Seems able to mow down the great people.
If they engulf Afghanistan,
