Dear elder, set out,
Let us bring turmoil upon the world!
If a seething hostile army appears,
Let us charge with levelled spears,
Fight back without fleeing,
And rest only when we have taken Aichurok.
Your settlement in Talas,
We would not be men if we failed to take
Your wife named Aichurok.
While your good fortune remains,
Elder, your good fortune is like a mountain ridge.
"Semetey, son of Manas,
When I carried off his falcon,
Out of fear, Semetey
The falcon he had lost
Failed to seek," let her not say.
Driving fourteen horses from the Kyrgyz,
Let us explore the sloping road.
My elder blessed by Kydyr, hear me;
Let us reach your bride's kin!
If we prove unworthy, let us die.
Rather than walk about alive,
Better never to have been born!
I have now heard your story,
Your father, leopard Manas,
The wife he promised you.
Your waist that you have girded,

My champion elder, listen
To the words your boys have told you!

She did not know how to show respect.
Your mighty Bakai and the three khans,
[UNRESOLVED]
With hero Bakai at their head,
Your three khans sat dejected,
Hunched like howling dogs.
She raged and cornered us;
My dear mother Kanykei
Beat us with a staff.
[UNRESOLVED]
Your two companions were exhausted.
Oh, hero, it was terrible,
The trouble with your wife.
Our lord and elder is a khan;
We began an uproar.
Your wife with an armful of buttocks,
We gave her a thorough beating!
We brought trouble upon her head.
With resounding blows,
We made her speak of the white falcon!
If you would search, there lies Opol.
"They attacked my wife," you may say;
If you would slaughter us, hero, here we are!
Here are the Argyn people with their ruler.
"They caused calamity," you may say;
If you would cut off heads, here we are!
[UNRESOLVED]
Though we pleaded, she would not yield, blustering,
Your wife poured abuse upon us.
For beating your wife,
We two companions are at fault!
Judge well who was unruly.
Poor lord, do heed us;
If you would destroy us, here we are!
If you would send us onward, here is the road.
If you would vent your rage, lord, here we are!'
The young lord, the leopard,
Beyond his father, dauntless Manas,
Was born your greater saint.
The gray belt left by Manas
He fastened closely round his neck.
That dear, stocky lion,
Pleading with his boys,
Showing himself to be their lord,
