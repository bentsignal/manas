Those who took a spear in hand,
Who entered battle without sparing themselves,
He separated those lions into one group.
“My wife and children are left behind,” they said,
Growing sorrowful and grieving,
He separated those deserters into one group.
With shields in their hands,
He separated the resolute into one group.
On a battlefield flowing with blood,
He separated the fearsome into one group.
Those who acted saying, “I trust in God,”
He separated those champions into one group.
Those who piled corpses like mountains,
He separated those champions into one group.
Selecting the capable,
The mighty, wolf-maned ones like wolves,
Gladdening those who watched,
The great host of the twelve khans,
Stretching it out rank upon rank,
Almambet led it onward.
Filling heaven and earth with dust,
Giving each group a flag to carry,
He secured his entire host.
“Those of you who turn back and flee,
You die before your appointed death,”
Champion Almambet threatened.
“Of anyone who turns back and flees,
I shall plunder his goods;
I shall bring calamity upon his head.
This great people who have come,
The whole army of twelve vast hosts,
I shall check their presence along the way.
From whoever loses a man,
I shall exact punishment upon his khan.
Those who turn away and disobey,
Show me those unruly men.
Before the great gathering,
