The forty companions of Khan Manas,
Biting their lips and narrowing their eyes,
Spoke a wondrous word.
Hero Syrgak and servant Shuutu
Flew away like arrows.
Among the Afghan spoil
Were thirty red elephants.
The two heroes Shuutu and Syrgak
Went among the elephants.
Before one could say this or that,
In the blink of an eye,
Khan Manas's red elephants
Had been driven there in a group.
His true thirst for blood seized him;
God struck the bloodthirsty man.
Kanykey and Bakay—
As though he would slaughter them,
He stood avidly.
Bakay sheltered by the khan;
God struck
Young Aydar with the spreading forelock.
When they went among the elephants,
Gray-wolf Manas, the tiger,
Raised Zulpukor in his hand
And struck;
The red elephants before him
Were cut into separate pieces.
Sultan Manas, the tiger,
Watered Zulpukor with blood
And returned it to its sheath.
Making the laugher laugh,
He struck the great war drum,
Announcing that the enemy was coming;
They took the war drum in hand,
Raised the brass mace,
And its champion struck the center.
Ears deafened at its booming,
As though the enemy had come.
Khan Manas's forty nobles—
Each of the forty had a different name,
Famed men who could shatter the age.
The forty wolves entered together
The bloodthirsty hero's treasury.
Your formidable sister-in-law Kanykey
Had prepared
Mighty arrow-proof coats.
All forty companions wrapped themselves in them;
Each of the forty coats was different.
Among those coats
Was the sarinji Almambet would wear;
It bore a letter on its collar
And Almambet's name.
Your sister-in-law Kanykey, the stalwart,
Had made this coat.
Considering her labor,
Its worth equaled an age.
She had its inside tightly lined
And its outside covered at the corners.
She had precious iron riveted to its back;
When spears met, they did not reach flesh,
And it exhausted whoever grappled with its wearer.
She had that coat fitted perfectly
And made to the measure of all forty.
If there were an enemy before them,
If he struck hard,
Untimely death might come;
Leaving them unprepared would be futile.
The forty armors she made for your forty
Revealed their costliness.
She brought forty armors from the treasury
And dressed the forty companions,
Your formidable sister-in-law Kanykey
Making their costliness known.
She had nine clasps apiece
Fastened across the chest;
Your mighty sister-in-law Kanykey
Then dressed the forty companions
In armor no arrow could pierce.
