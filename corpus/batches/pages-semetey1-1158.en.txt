Koshoy's white beard gleamed;
Your uncle Koshoy spoke loudly
And stopped as he turned.
Your mighty uncle Koshoy
Lamented his former days:
"Hey, Khan Manas, my colt!
When drunk from drinking arak,
When young and light-minded,
Barefoot and bareheaded,
At twenty-five, when the earth trembled,
Dear youth passed away.
Eighty-five is a mighty burden,
The humiliation of a living man.
Rather than live excessively long,
It is better that the Most High God
Should take what he created.
Once we besieged
His sprawling city;
Once we beat
Its kalday and ambal.
When we beat them—
Counting now, that time has passed;
Staggering as with yellow sickness,
Old age has arrived.
My colt, tiger Manas,
My joints have loosened,
My eyes glint with pus,
My waist has stooped downward;
Bastard old age
Has mounted me with jutting laziness.
All my joints creak;
In the whiteness of my beard,
You have laid a burden upon me, Manas,
Saying, 'Wrestle this infidel.'
When my journey is near,
My eyes stare dimly,
And my joints tremble.
When I speak, Manas, I have grief:
I have kyldyrama trousers
Made by joining
The hides of forty wild asses.
If I wrestle great Zholoy
Of the Kalmyks, they will split,
And before the people
Your uncle's nakedness will show.
The honor of your elder Koshoy
Will spill like ashes.
His cry will reach God;
Your poor uncle will fall.
Will he not see the onlookers?
If my shame appears,
Will your uncle not die before his time?
Will the Kalmyk not split my trousers?
Driven beyond all means,
Will your uncle not die before his time?!
Zholoy of the Kalmyks—
Looking at him, he is a huge giant;
Among the black Kalmyk people,
That bastard is their master.
Thick-wristed and stone-hearted,
Such is Zholoy's nature.
The broad-limbed brute
Will seize my worthless trousers
By their cuffs,
Tear all my trousers,
And before the people
Leave your uncle like carrion.
Will he not see the infidel's strength?
If they grapple, will he not split the trousers?
Your uncle, the people's lament,
Will he not die before his time?
The black Kalmyk and Manchu people
Have surely gathered.
