Turning wisdom over and pondering,
They withdrew into the hills.
The people wailed and tumult broke out;
They plunged into the uproar,
And entered an ordeal
No common people could face.
If the Chinese swarm over the ridge,
You have no khan to answer them,
No strength with which to fight.
Unable to cross and escape,
Will women and men together
Be taken into captivity?
Without dying or disappearing,
Will we survive the humiliation,
Living on as slaves,
And be brought to misery?
Called the people of that bloodstained warrior,
Will we be cast into the grave?
Will firearms strike us from the front,
And will we be torn apart?”
For the hero Semetey,
All the Kyrgyz people wept;
The wise and skillful wept,
Slender-waisted girls
In crimson dresses wept;
White-bearded elders wept,
Every kind of living soul wept,
Old and young alike wept.
The people lamented in tumult,
Despairing of their tiny lives;
Wearing black and swallowing blood,
The many Argyn and Kyrgyz,
The whole great nation, stood in uproar.
As the people wailed,
Kanchoro shouted in frenzy,
Calling for warrior Kyiaz,
And began his reign of fury.
When they saw Kanchoro's rage,
The white-bearded elders
And all the weeping people
Fled and scattered.
Taking a spear with a steel socket
And a keen, gaping point,
Girding on an unsheathed sword
As though ready to strike,
He arrayed all his weapons:
The jezail, the club, the iron-headed mace—
He made all of them ready.
At Semetey's treasury,
He seized the keeper
Who guarded the gate,
And forced open the treasury door.
Beginning with Bolokbai, the forty Kalmyks—
He slaughtered all forty Kalmyks.
In that treasury Kanchoro,
The accursed traitor, gorged himself.
He plundered the fine cloth,
And unleashed wickedness like a dog.
He ripped an entire sable skin
Lengthwise,
And wrapped it around his leggings.
Swaggering like a hero,
Strutting as though mighty,
He climbed a hill and shouted,
Calling to Kyiaz of the Jediger:
“Come, every one of you!”
Standing upon the hill, Kanchoro—
That pale retainer struck by ancestral wrath—
Cried, “Know a khan's might!
Make booty of the Argyn and Nogai,
You damned Jediger!
Go in and take them as spoil.
See which among them has courage.
Their remaining livestock, girls, and women
Are the khan's spoils;
Measure and divide them by the whip.
There is so much costly gold and silver
You will fail to load it all;
