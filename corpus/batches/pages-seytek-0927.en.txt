Glancing to either side,
The fierce warrior of old
Licked her lips,
Writhing like a dragon,
Crouching like a lion,
Gathering her wrath and making ready,
She prayed to the Creator:
“Let me spill the madman’s blood;
Do not let me be utterly disgraced,
God, grant me honor.
If at the madman’s hands
I fall, I shall die without renown;
‘The wretched champion died,’ they will say,
And I have no people to bury me with honor.
My heart is heavy, my breast aflame;
No grief is worse than sorrow.
Almighty God, Creator,
There is no recourse but You.
Sarybay is the one I shall confront;
I have begun a savage fight.
I have no support to rely on,
No trusted comrades at my side,
No people in whom to place my hope.
Against this infidel in his full strength
I have entered combat—God, aid me!
We are but women by name;
God, aid this sorrowing one!”
So saying, Kuyaly
Did not glance around,
Never thinking she might die,
Did not look from side to side,
And spared not her thread-thin life.
Beneath her was Zhelzhetpes;
She drove him right and left,
Lashing him on either flank.
At her cry the charger thundered,
And wherever his body struck,
The earth burst into ridges.
The warrior maiden upon him,
Whose opponent was Sarybay,
Faced a notorious foe such as this.
Her charger gathered beneath her,
While the warrior maiden above
Frowned without blinking,
Laughed aloud in delight,
And rode on blithely, ringing like steel.
Though they called Sarybay a champion,
She did not count him worth her boot-tip.
Courage and lionheartedness,
Cunning and dexterity,
A mind well cultivated and eyes keen,
And unstinting inspiration—
All these the Almighty
Had bestowed upon this girl beyond measure.
She looked neither up nor down,
Never thinking she might die.
The peerless warrior maiden
Rejoiced at the thought of death,
Showing herself a true hero.
When she turned her charger’s head,
Her well-shafted spear was steel;
When she leveled it with a thunderous roar,
The one she would fight was Sarybay,
The one she would confront was such a foe.
A fierce one with stony courage,
A hero at heart with a warrior’s arm,
To that infidel Sarybay
She became a crushing menace.
With no regret even in death,
Kuyaly unleashed a mighty battle.
The renowned warrior maiden,
Through countless contests and countless feuds,
Had met foes and spread havoc.
She had won acclaim in many lands
And was called the lioness.
God made straight her path;
The spirit of the road and Shayaba
Both upheld her together,
Guiding her wherever she advanced,
And strengthening her whenever foes confronted her.
