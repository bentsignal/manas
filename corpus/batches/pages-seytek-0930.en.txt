Sarybay was calamity itself;
Such was the battle he unleashed.
Ask no more of Sarybay’s state:
When angered, fury choked him,
And any mortal marked for death
Who met that infidel’s menace was doomed.
He thundered in a frenzy,
Strained and hurled vile words.
As he came in wrath,
His savage courage stood revealed;
One glance at the dreadful swine
Showed the look of one ready to kill.
He could not refrain from wreaking havoc;
The troops fled in every direction,
Scattering in panic and ruin.
That infidel Sarybay’s
True ferocity appeared,
And his mind fixed wholly upon battle.
Raging and working himself up,
He spoke in many ways:
“You frenzied champion,
A girl grown old while still calling herself a maid,
A girl who has lived too long,
A girl who mouths deceitful words—
Warrior girl, you have set my soul aflame!
No caravan of yours ever came over the pass;
You have no feud so grievous
As to avenge forty generations of fathers.
No caravan of yours ever journeyed here before;
You have no feud of a hundred forefathers
For which to track my yellow trail
And pursue me until you turn pale.
There was no quarrel between us,
No cause for us to feud,
No word from your protector
Calling us to fight to the death.
No caravan of yours came from Talas,
And your protector has no vengeance to seek.
I shall cast a chill lasso
Over Zhelzhetpes, whom none can take by asking;
Zhelzhetpes, who yields to none he sees,
I shall openly drag away.
I shall make you plead before me in vain,
And force you to become my wife.
I shall wreak boundless ruin;
My wrath has risen and stiffened me,
And I shall make your father my plunder.
If fate decrees it, let me die;
This wretched Kalmak has gone mad—
I shall deliver punishment into her hands.
Striking hard at her spirit,
I shall now finish her,
Spew forth my poisonous venom,
And dig out her roots.
I shall show my savagery,
And die fighting if I must.
I shall crush the life beneath me
And tear apart her rib bones.”
“Here is havoc, if that is what you crave;
Here is the fine spectacle of slaughter!
Here is my she-dog of a girl;
Here is the one who challenged me!”
He struck Tor-Kyzyl on the flank,
And took up his blue-steel spear.
His eyes bulged as if they would leap out,
His jaws seemed ready to split when he bit down;
His face was like a burning ember
As he gathered courage and took aim.
Raging and bellowing,
He had hunted down many khans;
The solid black earth itself
Had smoked beneath his vast weight.
He had crushed multitudes—
The madman, brave Sarybay,
Had come seeking combat,
And many thousands of giants had died,
Struck down by the calamity he unleashed.
In many lands he slaughtered peoples;
Sarybay had amassed blood,
And journeyed for many years,
