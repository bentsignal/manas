The khan's people held a feast
And stayed there rejoicing.
Your guest, brave Semetey—
Now let me tell
The tale of brave Toltoy.
Chynkozho, son of Shygay,
Was like tempered iron;
Brave Toltoy was the courageous one,
His strength equal to half a mountain.
Would Kalcha and Kadyrbay
Stand merely watching,
Without making arrows rain again?
Tölök and Tölöbay
Were the finest of the lower army,
The most skillful at thrusting spears.
Wounded Toltoy's
Pain subsided;
His eyes, huge as felt sacks, opened.
After putting on his bulletproof coat,
The swaggering leopard—
After Toltoy mounted his horse,
The great host of black Jediger
Divided into troop after troop;
Their wrestlers appeared,
Holding clubs as large as houses.
They put bulletproof coats on them
And mounted their heroes on horses.
They set apart those riding gray horses,
The fattest, swaying ponderously;
They set apart those riding duns,
Long-maned and mare-bellied;
They set apart those riding bays,
All thoroughbreds, the very fattest;
They set apart those riding chestnuts,
Which looked as small as falcon hoods;
They set apart those riding kula horses,
Who would enter together and encircle;
They set apart those riding piebalds,
Yellow-mottled like starlings;
They set apart those riding black piebalds,
With smooth, full tails.
Their numbers filled every count;
The time had come
To engulf Afghanistan and fight.
Dust whirled into the sky;
At the call to battle, their heroes
Spurred forward and poured out.
They set apart the heroes
Riding red bay horses,
Their horsehair tails tied tight.
They set apart their finest men,
Who would not stir when a gun struck.
They set apart the capable ones,
Who would not bend when a club struck.
They set apart the hardy ones,
Who would not sway when a whip struck.
They set apart the courageous ones,
Who would steep themselves in black blood
And fight without fleeing.
They set apart the peerless,
The finest among spearmen.
They set apart their best men,
Who knew the enemy's stratagems well.
They set apart those who would not flee
When the black gun cracked.
They set apart those who would not start
When the fierce gun snapped.
They set apart the peerless men ready with spears,
Those who said,
“Our venture rests with Almighty God.”
