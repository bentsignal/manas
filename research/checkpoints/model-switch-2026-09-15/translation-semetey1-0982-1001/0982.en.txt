The foolish woman Chachykei,
Speaking without measure,
To slaughter Kulchoro,
To put him to death,
To offer him as a sacrifice,
To finish him off—
The wretched woman grew frenzied.
That foolish slave, spoiled beyond measure,
God now struck the fool;
May He spare His servants such a fate,
She uttered countless filthy words.
If you looked at Chachykei,
Refusing reason, raging,
Your stubborn sister-in-law spoke,
Blustering and raging,
Your fierce sister-in-law spoke.
Beside where Semetey lay,
Hanging there,
Upon sixty yearlings and four sheep,
Your noble mother Kanykei
[UNRESOLVED]
Amid thirty young wives and thirty maidens,
Your sister-in-law
The knife for slaughtering people—
Seizing it by the handle,
Drawing it from its sheath,
To slaughter Kulchoro,
To put him to death,
Lunging and charging,
Holding the knife in her hand,
She sprang at Kulchoro.
From that foolish-born infidel,
Kulchoro fled,
From the woman he regarded as a mother,
And had taken as a companion.
'Ah, cruel sister-in-law Chachykei!
I know your cruelty;
My recklessly brave lord Semetey—
[UNRESOLVED]
The boy said this and fled.
Kulchoro flew like an arrow,
The wretch would not let her near.
The great woman raised a shout,
And struck out in her anger.
It went wide of the boy,
And struck the door.
The door shattered,
Thirty young wives and thirty maidens
Scattered in flight, crying out.
Kulchoro ran again,
[UNRESOLVED]
Onto Akborchuk's back,
Springing nimbly like a sheep,
As though an enemy had risen in his settlement,
He went fleeing
From Semetey's wife.
Taking the road, he dashed away,
Keeping clear, he urged his horse on.
Hurriedly coming abreast,
The two warrior companions
Conferred in a deserted place:
'Who could know this side of her?
Elder Kanchoro, hear my words!
His wife is distraught;
She nearly killed me,
That croaking infidel.
On the banks of Uch-Koshoi,
My dauntless elder, mighty Bakai,
The deeply wise old man,
From the dauntless lion Manas,
A saint left to us as a gift—
Bakai's senior wife Korpojan,
Let us send her as intermediary,
And learn the news for certain.
