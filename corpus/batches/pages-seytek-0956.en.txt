Perhaps I should somehow outwit that slave
And make my escape.
But rather than flee until I die,
I will die in battle.
Alas, Seytek is a true hero,
A lion surpassing all other youths.
This formidable Seytek
Is the head of the mighty Kyrgyz.
His courage is abundant and his wrath is fierce;
He is the black foundation stone of a dungeon.
When his ribs are fully hardened,
When the flesh fills out upon his shanks,
When the time of his maturity comes,
Will any mortal dare face him?
Will any mortal advance against him?
When his marrow is filled with milk,
When his bones mature and his strength is full,
Will any mortal dare face him?
Will any man who confronts him survive?
Will one who comes to grapple with him
Not die before his destined hour?
His massive strength is like a mountain.
This formidable youth’s
People are a forest and fortress around him.
His conflict is a terrible wrath;
When he charges, he is a lion,
A red-glowing ember—
There is none like him among mankind.
His bearing and bodily strength
Are like a taut bow of dark hardwood.
When his savage fury falls upon a man,
He smashes even stone to powder
And sweeps everything utterly away.
How exceedingly sharp his sword is!
Such is its cutting edge.
Once his spear strikes, it does not release;
Such is the hero’s courage.
It could tear and stretch the corners of a mouth.
The youth’s keen force
Is like the steel bullet of a mighty gun,
His wrath like flame—
Such is his boundless courage.
When the youth thrusts with his spear,
The ribs crack apart,
The hero’s heart gives a start,
And he shatters the spine in ten places.
The devastating youth
Seems utterly beyond measure.
Ever since I became Sarybai,
Ever since I mounted a steed bearing that name,
Ever since I put on the golden crown,
Ever since I ascended the golden throne
And ruled the people and governed the realm,
Living under the name of Sarybai,
How many times
Have I witnessed ruinous, mighty battles
While roaming the earth?
Yet fate never overtook me, and I did not die.
Never have I seen so great an ordeal in battle,
Never have I suffered such humiliation.
Clashing with this wrathful foe,
This blood-spilling raven of a boy,
Contending with this relentless youth,
The face of my right shoulder aches,
The edge of my spine aches,
The breastbone beneath my white armor aches,
And my precious heart itself aches.
Has the spear wounded it,
So that my heart rolls over?
Has it leapt out of its sheath?
Can it be that through this raven-like boy
My appointed death has somehow come?
My hunched back itself aches,
And death’s black eye brings anguish.
The relentless youth who faced me
Has launched a furious campaign;
He has shattered everything upon me
And left it falling to pieces.”
