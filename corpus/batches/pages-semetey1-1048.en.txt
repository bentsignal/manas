These two had given their answer.
"Since the appointed day,
Seven days have passed," they said.
When the Zhediger grow angry,
They have an army that will overrun the land;
They have pits already dug,
And bitter misery boiling.
Raging and roaring,
They have mighty Kozho among them.
Khan Akun's great fortress
Had a high tower;
When one climbed and looked from above,
The sight unsettled the heart,
And the face of the world could be seen.
Azhybay and Tümönbay
Hurried and ran there.
"How lie the Zhediger?" they wondered,
For danger lay on that side.
Azhybay and Tümönbay,
Bending their knees to the ground,
Both ran up
To the top of the tower.
When they climbed to the tower's top
And sat looking out,
A blue-gray haze parted from the sky;
With hungry cries and uproar,
The Zhediger host appeared.
Streaming along the bank,
Their spears swayed like reeds;
Along the bank road of Kum-Kyya,
They came forth in splendor.
Indistinguishable as separate worms,
They came boiling forth;
Poor Toltoy drove one flank
As they came onward.
See the reeds, see the flood;
See the seething mass of people.
At the spur of Tash-Kyya,
Most of them came forth;
Through the narrow bank road,
Half of them came forth.
They came with sharp iron
Fitted to their spears;
They came having prepared
Their lions who would overrun the land.
At the very rear, Chynkozho
Drove them as they came.
Like grains of sand, the Zhediger
Came seething forth.
Toltoy of the Zhediger,
Whose countless troops bent the earth,
Drove peoples of every kind,
Drove a multitude that bent the earth.
The world filled and the land shone
With the army advancing under him.
To seize Chürök,
He drove so many people;
Seven divisions of Zhediger
He had gathered and driven there.
Beneath the multitude of that host,
The earth had sagged.
Tölök and Tölöbay
Were upon the lower ridge face;
Kalcha and Kadyrbay
Were at the edge of the upper host.
All the Zhediger,
Streaming along the bank,
Surged toward the land.
Then Tümönbay's
Heart was sorely troubled;
To Azhybay's eyes,
They appeared like ravenous devourers.
The dense black Zhediger
Separated into division after division,
And split into distinct groups.
