Galloping unseen,
Hero Syrgak drew back.
Kökchebich beneath him,
At the first “Go!” as he set off,
Shattered the stones he trod;
Clods as large as great cauldrons
Whistled above Syrgak's head.
His bit clattered;
Kökchebich's flesh grew hot;
His tail like strands of silk
Lashed between his hind legs,
