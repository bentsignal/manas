I shall agree to what you say;
I shall weigh out the gold by the pood
And give it as the matchmaker's gift.
By wedding Toltoy,
I shall see your sincerity.
If it is truly certain
That you will give the peri's daughter Chürök,
I shall clothe you in a coat of mail
With a golden collar and copper sleeves,
And have you mount
The Karat horse beneath me.
Brave Toltoy's Surkoen
I shall give as a ceremonial horse;
I shall agree to what you say.
To carry your caravan's loads,
I shall choose and give a stud camel.
If you will give Chürök,
I shall firmly give a great bride-price.
If it lies within my reach, unstintingly
I shall show you honor and shelter;
As customary bride-price,
I shall give seventy women as servants!"
When Kozho spoke thus,
To Kozho, who spoke empty words,
Your Azhy bowed low;
Rising before Kozho,
He made obeisance to him.
Azhybay stood before Kozho:
"Agreed, heroic Kozho," he said.
"If I do not give Aychürök,
May my seven forefathers be infidels;
Let him accept what I have said,
And let your hero, bridegroom Toltoy,
Come in seven days."
He pledged it and made ready;
Azhybay rode toward the city.
Tümönbay, son of Tülkü,
Burst from the flank in fury,
And spoke angrily to Azhybay:
"You son of a bitch, Azhybay,
Why could you not keep your mouth shut?
Without babbling, 'I shall give the girl,'
Will you be the one made rich
By taking eight hundred matching horses?!
Am I to be left forlorn,
Eating no meat from a knucklebone,
Riding no dappled pacing horse?!
Will you be the one, dear Azhy,
To take a thousand black camels,
Load gold upon their packs,
Put it into your treasury,
And lie there with your belly swollen?!
Am I to be left forlorn,
Eating no knucklebone's meat,
Riding no dappled pacing horse?!
God surely exists, Azhybay,
For your speech that boasts of eloquence,
For this boundless deed of yours.
Who will hear your words
After this deed you have done?
I have seen what you have done."
Azhybay quarreled with Tümönbay,
And Tümönbay quarreled with Azhybay;
They stopped there together,
And traded angry abuse.
They now reached
The city of Khan Akun;
Toward Khan Akun's fortress,
Toward the palace where the khan dwelt,
Aychürök now kept watch.
To seize Chürök,
To take her as a gift
To Toltoy of the Zhediger,
And to preserve alive
The vast multitude of Afghans,
Azhybay and Tümönbay
Stalked Aychürök,
Skirting the place where she slept.
