As large as an elephant’s head,
Which sucks human beings dry;
And there is a scorpion called Salagün.
There are many such accursed things.
May no living mortal see
The torment I have seen;
May it never befall another soul.
I endured such cruel affliction
That only a spoonful of blood remained,
And my flesh became a blue pulp.
My Karager grew thin as a twig,
And I myself became small as a lark.
When I surveyed that calamity
And cast my eyes beyond,
Something inhaled from six ravines away:
The dragon-serpent Karamar,
Which swallows whatever it encounters,
And bathes in blood when challenged—
How could any person face it?
With costly venom and a merciless nature,
Could I gain any news
Of the frenzied bride who treads the hours?
If one challenges this ill-starred outcast,
Could anyone remain alive?
Would any man survive being caught by it?
Would one who met it ever return alive?
Who could encounter it and say,
‘Let this bride be ours;
Come and accept our hospitality’?
Truth and falsehood became plain,
And I despaired of my fly-sized soul;
The hero himself despaired of life.
Her bearing and her majesty
Surpassed everything in the world—
Such was that infidel’s ordeal.
When I looked to this nearer side,
My heart leapt from its place;
When I counted all her deeds,
Before the khan stood a tower—
Ask me nothing of that tower.
Its hellish chamber was vast as a mountain;
To look upon it was calamity itself.
The tower had a doorway,
But its opening was misshapen.
It had gates at six places,
And at each gate an old keeper.
Her violence surpassed all bounds;
In countless forms appeared
The warriors of that infidel.
The gate doors were made of steel,
And keepers stood at every gate.
Frightened into submission by her appearance,
They wore human noses and ears
Strung about them as amulets.
Whenever they heard the maiden’s command,
They attacked at once.
When they grappled with those who sought her,
The seekers’ blood was spilled.
Such was the valor displayed
By Kayypchal’s warrior daughter.
That perfectly formed, ill-starred woman—
Could even a skillful man subdue her?
If many warriors attacked,
Could any man force her to bow?
Would anyone dare journey there?
After challenging that wrathful figure,
Could anyone survive?
Would any person confront
That dark, ill-starred fiend,
That sinister and accursed one?
Could renowned heroes subdue her?
Her waist is strong and her venom fierce;
Could anyone face and defeat her?
Could anyone who faced
That vicious-tempered warrior return alive?
From the hands of that merciless woman,
Could a mortal return alive to his people?
