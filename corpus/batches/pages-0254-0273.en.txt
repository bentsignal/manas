“I saw a cloud of dust surge up,
Elder Koshoy, hear my words:
I saw a man of fearsome countenance.
His size was like a mountain itself,
His eyes like the beds of lakes,
As though to suck in all he saw.
His brow was like a deep ravine,
As though to swallow all who clashed with him.
His brow was lofty, his wrath was mighty;
He levels flat those who challenge him.
I saw a man of surpassing majesty;
Give up all hope against that man!
Looming with a fearsome look,
He could bring us all to our end.
If he is an enemy, a foe,
He could slaughter us, leaving none!
Since I have been Kutunay,
Brave elder Koshoy,
Since I have been your lookout,
I have never seen such a hero.
My collarbones shook,
My shit-filled guts quaked,
And I came fleeing to you!
His right shoulder was broad;
That man of fearsome countenance
Was a lion who could break the Oirats!
His chest was like a hungry lion's.
Elder Koshoy, make ready quickly!
I have never seen such a man,
With eyes like a hunting eagle's.
God, do not show us his face!
He had girded on an unsheathed sword;
Like a cloud about to rain again,
Wrath had settled on his face.
His aspect shows his might;
He has stirred tumult among the peoples.
A hollow-socketed, painted spear
He held jutting high above him.
If an army came that split the earth,
He would not open his arms in fear.
The men who traded blows with him,
He battered to the ground like sheep.
Drinking blood, he could not have his fill;
He spared none who faced him.
He had the bearing of a champion!
Unable to withstand his wrath,
I fell into turmoil;
From the back of my pale filly
I all but fell off and died!
Since I have been Kutunay,
Since I have borne the name Kutunay,
I have never seen such a hero.
I died where I saw him;
What else was it but dying,
When I fled here without going near!
My collarbones shake,
My shit-filled guts quake.
My fly-small soul cries out;
My heart bucks like a horse
And pounds from dawn till dusk.
At one glance he is one man,
A figure appearing on the ridge;
At another glance he is a thousand men.
As if about to seize someone,
A black-spotted leopard
Surges at his side.
A short-tailed, blue-maned lion
Makes ready before his face.
The giant black bird, the Zymyryk,
As if about to carry someone off,
Has stretched its talons from the sky.
The Forty Chiltens and Nyyaz,
Let me tell it briefly,
Raising cries behind him,
Have become his cheering support.
Ahead of him, my Father Shay
Opens his path, the two together—
And the dervish named Kydyr,
Making the wolf-maned one's horse plunge,
Leads his Toruchaar by the rein.
Seeing the deeds of that being,
My dear elder Koshoy,
I fled toward you;
‘Is he Chinese?’ I thought in alarm.
If you are mighty, go out and see,
My dear Koshoy, my lion!
If he is an enemy, a foe,
Before one can cry this or that,
In the opening and shutting of an eye,
He will reach us with a roar.
Smashing your stone fortress,
Toppling your sand fortress,
He will slaughter every one.
If he is an enemy, a foe,
He will bring us all to our end.
He will roar and launch an attack;
Slaughtering you all,
He will cut off heads with his sword,
He will bring disaster upon us.
He is a hero of fearsome appearance!”
The hearing ear, the seeing eye,
Hurrying and flurrying, stumbling in haste,
Stood speaking these words.
In the high-built fortress,
In Khan Koshoy's court,
My elder, old Koshoy,
The white-bearded saint,
Khan Koshoy of the Katagan,
That Koshoy of sacred power,
Who dwelt at Chech-Töbö,
Whom no contender had defeated,
Whom no man had made to bow,
Whose opponents did not survive,
Whom no one had troubled,
Whom no man dared to face,
Whose blessing enriched the nation,
Who brought light to those he regarded—
At that time hero Koshoy
Kept guards and gathered warriors,
Gathered all his champions together.
Chech-Töbö in At-Bashy,
Making Chech-Töbö his home,
Your worthy elder Koshoy
Made a hero of his aged self.
Kara-Too, stretching across the way,
He had lookouts watch from there.
Your brave elder Koshoy
Had gathered the heroes.
Making all his troops ready,
Of the men who traded blows with him,
Every one he had taken—
Your elder Koshoy had brought them under his rule.
Your worthy elder Koshoy,
Making Zheti-Özön his home,
The lost and scattered people—
Your elder Koshoy had taken the lead.
Hero Koshoy had many troops;
Among those troops,
Choosing a star in the sky together,
Kudaynazar the bare club
And old Koshoy had sworn friendship.
Koshoy Khan's many companions,
Hearing the messenger's words,
Hearing, ‘A formidable man is coming,’
And their great elder Koshoy,
All fell into turmoil,
All the troops who had gathered.
Among those troops,
What was there, what was not!
There was the giant, old Koshoy;
The swordsmen gathered,
Wrathfully making ready,
With your elder Khan Koshoy at their head,
A thousand giants mounted.
Taking up their weapons,
Your champion elder Koshoy was there;
They barred the ridge.
When renowned Koshoy looked,
He was riding the tulpar Toruchaar,
He was wearing silk no bullet could pierce,
He was riding a horse no bullet could catch.
His bulk was equal to a mountain,
As though to devour whoever appeared.
His protectors were one group of nine;
It was their time to protect him.
His supporters were one group of nine;
It was their time to uphold him.
His world was in perfect balance;
He was a lion protected by Shay.
If one looked into his inner being,
It was broader than the face of the earth.
“Oh, my child, the one whose name we heard so often,
The one whose coming we awaited,
The servant of the Almighty,
Old Jakyp's only son,
The fortress of the Kyrgyz people,
The hero of many peoples,
Who will go straight into darkly looming Beijing
And thrust his spear—
It is lion Manas himself!
Not merely Manas himself,
It is his own dark, gazing eyes!
Seeking kin, support and a shore,
The man, the white goshawk, has come!
Mighty Manas has been found,
Your lurking enemy will be struck down.
Kyrgyz people, small as a palm,
Through him your shame will be covered,
Your exposed places will be covered.
The one you would toil to seek,
Your wolf-maned Manas has been found;
God has plainly granted him,
The one your people longed and prayed for
