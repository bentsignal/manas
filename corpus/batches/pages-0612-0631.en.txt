Unable to find you, my lion,
My horse became thin as a cotoneaster twig;
My lice grew as large as larks.
My hooves had become chipped.
My falcon, now that I have found you,
My beak has now grown whole!
You are my support, my strength;
You are my lion who will brighten my brow.
Who will save my life,
You are a ravine thick with meadowsweet.
If enemies come against me and surround me,
To draw from the sheath with a shout,
You are steel fitted and ready!”
Mighty Bakay said these words:
“Turn toward the words I speak;
I will become your faithful companion.
Making you know what you do not know,
With knowledge I will set you on the path.
Making you perceive what you do not perceive,
I will bring you to understanding.
