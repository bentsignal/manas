You have grown puffed up.
Saying, “I bow to no one in the world,”
You have grown full of swagger.
Take full measure of who I am:
I am the mighty, renowned Zholoy.
When your forefather Karakan died,
These Burut standing here—
This very hand of mine set them straight!
