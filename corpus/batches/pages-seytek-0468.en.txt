“It is indeed true that you were parted
And left without the one dear to you.
You were a man whose glory loomed like a mountain,
And it is also true that, parted from your noble one,
You were left humiliated.
The sable cap upon one's head—
Who would not wear it at a jaunty tilt?
While fortune rests upon one's head,
Who would not stride about thundering?
You were parted and separated,
Enduring shame and grieving.
What you have said is indeed true;
I was the one who escaped alive
And became separated from you.
Whenever even a faint cry is heard,
My troubled heart is torn.
Its angry force
Seems like Külchoro's.
That hero Külchoro of yours
Was a man apart from all others.
Crackling like lightning,
Such was the power of his voice.
Whenever he shouted, ‘Uncle!’
His cry carried far.
A doe with her fawn,
At the sound of the hero's voice,
Unable to withstand its venom,
Would miscarry her young.
Some would collapse and die,
While those that survived
Would waste their days in sickness.
My surging heart is torn;
That furious shouted cry
Sounds like Külchoro's voice.
If you seize me, you may hold me,
You may offer me as a sacrifice.
You may simply kill this soul
And utterly destroy it.
Your courage is lofty and your wrath is strong,
You lay low whoever challenges you.”
The noble lord spoke these words,
Saying as follows:
“O my child, what you say is also true.
You have a black and unyielding nature,
And fearful words of wrath;
You strike down whoever crosses you,
And gnaw whoever encounters you.
You set dry grass ablaze upon the ground
And burn the meadows;
Such was your day as the hero Külchoro.
When I look upon your radiance,
It bears the terror of a dragon.
When your cry is heard,
It has the voice of a lion.
If you kill me, you will surely kill me,
If you grow fierce, you will swallow me.
And if your rage seizes you again,
You will utterly finish me.
You are indeed a hero and a lion,
I recognize you by your voice:
You are Külchoro himself.
You are the falcon's dark eye.
When you grapple and lay hands upon a foe,
No man's strength can overcome you,
Nor can a lion's teeth pierce you.
Champion among the valley's heroes,
Mightiest of the falcons,
Favored among the blessed.
All in all,
You are the Morning Star of heroic lions.
My heart trembles and is torn;
That fearsome battle cry
Seems just like Külchoro's.
If you are Külchoro,
Then it is also true that you are a khan.
That my honor once bore a great duty,
And that I still exist, are also true.
That fire has swept through my core
And consumed it is also true.
