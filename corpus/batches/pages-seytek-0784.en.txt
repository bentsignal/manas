Its country is blissful, its people well-fed,
A people of unmatched boldness in the world,
Open-handed and generous with all they have—
Such are the fortunate Kyrgyz people.
If you find its strength, this is your nation;
Yet sorrow lies within your breast.
Dark-hearted and blue-clad,
Immeasurable Semetey—
Such is the son you are to bear.
When you give birth to the child,
Forty envoys from China
Will come, feast at his celebration, and depart.
If this son survives,
They will say he shall overturn the age.
Ten envoys from Russia
Will also feast and depart.
If this son survives,
They will say he shall overthrow the Oirats.
As a famed gray-wolf hero,
His renown will spread throughout the world.
Akun Khan, khan of Afghanistan—
His daughter Aichürök
Will become the wife he takes.
In my dream I caught a charger:
Its shoes were fine and its tether golden,
And I tied it fast and secure.
This one became a star in the heavens,
The eternal companion
Of my son in the next world,
The finest of women.
The luckless peri-born maiden,
The daughter of wealthy Kaiypchal,
Is the strength of your noble hero,
The lioness among women.
If your son meets hardship,
She is the one who will deliver him
From his cruel and grievous enemy.’
So spoke the dervish,
Giving his blessing to my undertaking
And revealing a sign in my dream.
Kuyaly, daughter of Kaiypchal,
Is the finest of women,
A daughter born of light.
She bears the name of woman,
But for her womanhood
She would be a renowned champion of the world.
Her courage is extraordinary,
Her strength beyond measure;
Her nature is unlike any other’s,
A peerless, noble-browed champion.
That maiden is a lionhearted hero,
Shielded by the unseen spirits.
In the end she will be found
As a bride for your son.
By destiny she will come to you
And become your son’s inheritance.
She is the blessing of the Night of Destiny;
She will rekindle your extinguished fire
And save your soul from death.
Joining much that has been severed,
Firmly gathering what was scattered,
She is the one who will gladden your heart.
She will increase your laughter;
She is the black fortress-city,
The priceless treasure beyond finding.
From the hidden arrow of death,
From the accursed missile of hell,
From the grip of terrible calamity,
From the iron snare that entangles,
From the mighty arm and potent venom
Of the savage dog,
From the maw of the devourer of nations,
From the black gaze of the monster—
She is the hero who will deliver him.
Listen well, Kanykei,
To the words I spoke long ago:
The peri-born Kuyaly
Is herself another noble hero.
She will become your daughter-in-law,
And her word will be honored by the people.
