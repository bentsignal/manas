He hewed through to the bone.
Before the hero could recover,
Before he could wreak destruction upon them,
The teeming black-clad Jediger
And bloodthirsty lion, brave Kyyaz,
Fell upon the hero.
Their toughest veteran wrestlers,
Every one of their champions,
Raised shouts from every side;
From all directions
They cried their battle cry, “Kıyır!”
They loosed their bows like pouring rain,
Shooting volleys of many kinds.
The arrows from bows hissed,
The musket balls shrieked,
The cannon shot thundered;
Now they waged a boundless battle.
The ever-ready hero
Spent all his strength and lost his power;
Dark blood poured from his body,
And Külchoro’s strength ebbed away.
The peerless warrior’s two arms
Could no longer rise and hung paralyzed;
His breast filled with black blood.
He had no swift steed to bear him away,
No kinsman in that land to lead him home.
Alone amid the Jediger host—
Such, indeed, is this worthless world.
The great pit dug for him
Now began to boil with misery.
Thrusting spears and striking axes,
They engulfed him there.
Those armed with ropes and armored coats,
All the men with iron gauntlets,
The champions who thrust spears,
The heroes who struck with battle-axes,
Those most skilled with swords,
The strongest of the mighty,
Surrounded him.
On that occasion, the hero
Passed through desperate torment;
The Jediger ringed him round
And cast him into a net of iron.
Over poor Külchoro,
The heroes Kanchoro and Kyyaz
Stiffened in wrath,
Roared in harsh fury,
Thundered in anger,
And growled like lions.
At the sight of their terrible bearing,
Men’s bodies trembled;
Their courage was like a wild boar’s rage,
Like creatures that would leave nothing alive.
Shouting in frenzy,
They summoned their warriors.
Enraged, Kanchoro
Now issued his command to the multitude.
Jerking with every violent movement,
This warrior Kanchoro,
This black-hearted wretch,
Drove the teeming black-clad Jediger
Forward without leaving one behind.
As they surged upon the hero,
The earth split with a roar.
Kanchoro swelled in menace,
Roaring in a hundred ways;
He raged like a ravenous stallion,
Ready to seize
An extravagant prize.
He stamped about in frenzy,
Roaring like a demon,
Wearing an unsheathed sword,
Strutting and keeping watch.
The savage stood there,
Boasting before the Jediger:

If you flee, you have eaten filth!
