If I slaughter one of them, let me
Suck down and swallow his blood.
May I die if I take no vengeance;
If I do not fight to the death for revenge,
May I sink away as though never born.
These two have grown powerful—
Let me test them in furious combat.
Let me grapple and bring destruction,
And take vengeance far and wide.
If death is due, let us die;
Let us meet in combat.
If I do not make this year’s slaughter glorious,
Now that they have fallen within my reach
And the snarling Jediger
Have revealed their enmity;
If I do not thresh them like grain
And bring black disaster
Upon Kyyaz, son of Bagysh;
May I not live, but die.
Mighty Kyyaz is my chosen foe;
Let me try my strength against him.
Standing face-to-face across the field,
Swinging battle-axes and thrusting spears,
Let us fight in a bloody clash.
Gripping the lunging spear,
Let us brace and drive it home,
Lean toward the saddle-girth,
And soak our spears in blood.
If I do not split his head without mercy,
Driving the spear firmly into him—
Its shaft is stout and its point sharp,
The great, deep-grooved war spear—
If we do not thus steep it in blood,
Until the people cry out in turmoil,
Saying, “The champions are at war,”
Until every witness is shaken,
And made to flee;
Until every person who sees it
Despairs of life itself.
A hero should take up his spear,
But my two hands have no vigor.
My strength is spent; I have no power.
Though they still seem to move,
I have scarcely a spoonful of blood.
My heart is flame, my liver embers,
Yet against fiery hero Kyyaz
I have no strength to fight.
Now that I have seen you in this world,
My heart is content with the Creator.”
When Külchoro spoke these words,
Seytek’s heart was overwhelmed.
As he watched,
Tears poured from his eyes.
His mind reeled in anguish;
Such was that grievous day.
Then mighty Seytek’s
Eyes blazed like fire.
The enraged boy
Was seized by bloodthirsty fury.
His broad front teeth were like a gate,
His other teeth fearsome,
Sharp as a sword
And broad as a wrist;
He ground them again and again.
The extraordinary young wretch
Was seized by bloodthirsty fury.
“Jeti-Özön is no land of mine,
The Jediger are no people of mine.
I have been wandering as an exile,
Parted from my fierce lion of a father,
My formidable warrior father!
I have been looking upon Kyyaz
As though he stood in my father’s place.
Though still alive, my precious soul
Has been living as one dead!
Against Kanchoro, who felled the banner,
And Kyyaz, who burned it,
Let me rise and wreak slaughter;
For my father gone to the other world,
Let me take vengeance now!
