And his honor had been great.
Leading his poor soul away,
He set out toward Kaiypchy-Too,
Leaving sixty women and mourning sisters-in-law,
Leaving the camp desolate.
They had encircled the palace,
And when the bright dawn advanced,
When stars fled across the sky
And moved toward the qibla,
As the first light whitened,
When the cold wind before sunrise
Struck and made the bones ache,
As the dawn's light appeared,
The stars fled across the sky
And withdrew toward the qibla.
The haze that blinded the eye
Lifted upward into the heavens.
The earth seemed to split as dawn broke;
The separate peaks of Orkoi Mountain
Became clearly visible.
When the earth opened to the day,
Girding their waists securely,
They raised a roar that split the earth.
If you wish to hear that roar:
They shouted, "Kanchoro!"
And the courageous lions
Drove the Zhediger in.
Upon the sixty sisters-in-law and mourning women,
They attacked all together;
The Zhediger flooded in.
That multitude of Zhediger
Cut down the plane trees,
Pushed over the walls,
Cut down the willows,
And demolished every building.
They turned the gateway bloody,
Made the brave renowned,
Made Kanchoro khan
Over the corrupted multitude,
And the Zhediger flooded in.
They slaughtered and destroyed the heroes,
Smashed the doors,
And made the women scream;
The Zhediger trampled their way inside.
The treasury that sustained the world—
They opened its doors.
Refined gold and white silver,
All the great treasures,
The Zhediger took as spoil
And scattered breast-deep.
They widowed those with husbands,
Beat silk into raw wool,
And seized the noble brides and Aksakal's women,
Plundering and enslaving them.
They raided and seized the palace,
Pulled out the fortress posts,
Destroyed the stone city,
And plundered it completely.
They scorched the earthen fort
And sacked it utterly.
They wrought every conflict they knew,
Plundered the treasury and settled in ease.
They drove red-tailed riding camels,
Braiding their flowing tails;
Gold in pitchers and coins like sand,
They plundered and divided with approval.
Crimson cloth with golden weave—
They divided all of it.
All the Kyrgyz were surrounded;
Girls were seized and plundered as spoil.
The Zhediger, glutted with booty,
Shouted in wild confusion,
Saying, "We have won great spoil!"
And raising the cry, "Kiyaz!"
They pulled out the palace posts,
Plundered everything together,
And wrought horror without hardship.
