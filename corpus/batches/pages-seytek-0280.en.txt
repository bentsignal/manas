The radiant weapon, when raised,
The exiled warrior’s bright treasure—
Akkelte, which fires afar,
The solace of this mortal life.
Such is the nature of Akkelte.
Almabash is a matchless steed,
Heeds neither distance nor nearness,
And carries you far whenever you mount.
If he sweeps past the hostile foe,
They lose their wits and scatter.
Its sights were mounted in gold,
And those who heard its report fell dead.
Whoever came within its path crawled away;
No other short gun ever matched it,
And none who challenged it prospered.
When the holy man in Kokand
Was crying out in distress,
While suffering humiliation,
When crooked rebels raised strife,
Your grandfather, lion of the world,
Was setting matters right,
And cherished and tended
The black-steel Zulpukor,
Keeping it always ready,
In those days when he spread
Great alarm throughout the world—
Your gray-maned grandfather went
To a settlement beyond Almak,
And from a Russian hauling a cart
He seized the gun after coveting it.
It is the heirloom left by your forefather,
Tested before all the people,
A cherished delight and faithful companion.
If one possessed no weapon such as this,
How could he strike a distant mark?
It is the companion of mortal life;
That is the weapon for charging and firing,
That is the sharp arm your forefather left.
When they trampled and scattered our wealth,
And broke open my treasury,
Whoever its bullet struck lost blood,
And with the blood, life itself departed.
Its cartridge pouch bristled with bullets;
The enemies who came to confront it
Suffered for their folly and fell.
When it was aimed toward the foe,
The earth was torn and blood flowed.
When fired it cracked like a beast’s roar,
Smoke curled from its barrel,
And the opposing enemy collapsed.
The fearsome musket Akkelte—
Its fiery bullets are large as fists;
Such is the nature of Akkelte.
They faced the foe and raised it,
And when it fired, it ended lives.
Its bullet struck whatever it marked,
And the enemy caught in its path
Was hurled into utter darkness.
If its bullet struck squarely,
The enemy could find no escape,
Nor gather bones scattered like flies,
If ever caught by its shot.
They grieved, unable to find the remains.
Akkelte strikes from afar;
When foes heard Akkelte’s voice,
And its bullet struck with a crash,
Even the bravest enemy lost courage
And fled away in panic.
None could resist or hold firm;
They were shattered and torn apart.
Its match is red silk and glowing ember;
No gun on earth is like it.
Steel within and gold without,
Its barrel is twisted with twelve grooves.
The foe it was aimed at fell,
No enemy could stand against it,
At every winding ridge it found its mark.
Any slight flaw was corrected
By the smith Döötü and poor Bölökbai;
No other sharp weapon was like it.
