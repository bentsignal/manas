# Oluyat lexical follow-up

On2026-09-14 the coordinator individually inspected PDF373 of the1960 Semetey lexical aid already registered in research/SEMETEY-1960-LEXICAL-SOURCE.json. Its Олуят entry defines a person able to foresee events, equivalent to олуя. This is a primary lexical basis for a visionary/holy-sage reading, separately from the named foster father Oluyat documented in the Manas Encyclopedia Bakay entry.

Revisit the held source clauses on Semetey2013 PDF511 and517 in their full context. Do not replace every occurrence mechanically:511:3:5 refers to the named father, whereas the epithet and blessing clauses may use the common noun. No additional verses were restored merely by recording this lead.
