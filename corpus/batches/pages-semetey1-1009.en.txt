Drawing near the place he must reach,
The companion upon his back,
From running so hard,
Felt his senses reel and scatter.
His tail, fine as willow silk,
Separated into strand after strand
And streamed behind him.
When visible, Buurul shuddered and flashed;
When unseen, he billowed like blue smoke.
His mouth gaped wide as a cavern;
As one watched the courser,
White foam mingled with blood
Scattered across his chest.
When he ran down a slope,
That creature unlike other beasts
Raced with flying birds.
His four basin-wide hooves,
Once his flesh grew hot,
Struck against one another.
Kulchoro upon his back
Wrestled with the mouth
Of Buurul standing in the line.
His hands cramped upon the white reins knotted in loops;
He could not pull them back.
This poor beast was truly born of the unseen
And had broken into an open run:
"Will he go racing headlong?
If broken, hollow ground appears,
Will my death come needlessly?
Is this how my fate will end?
I have never seen," said Kulchoro,
"A kinsman like Semetey!"
He traveled on unable to rein the mouth
Of the courser restored to fighting condition.
His eyes rolled wildly,
And his legs dangled loose;
Struggling against the horse's mouth,
Kulchoro traveled on
Clinging to Akkangky.
Struggling for his life,
Kulchoro grappled fiercely
With renowned Buurul.
Tall Buurul carried him away
Into the hollow of Ak-Kyya,
Then wheeled, descended, and stopped
Beside Great Urgench.
Looking, the eye could seize nothing;
The mist-shrouded companion
Could not tell what lay before him.
For as long as it takes tea to boil,
Like a person overcome with exhaustion,
He could not open his streaming eyes,
Nor properly settle his heart.
As he stood there dumbfounded,
The high mountains thundered;
When Choro stopped and looked,
The birches and poplars
Roared as though a wind were blowing.
It pounded until the ear went deaf,
Gathering and rushing with a roar.
Its name was the River Urgench,
And its surface was foam and vapor.
Overturning and pouring onward,
When he looked downstream,
It appeared like four rivers.
It thundered in humps like a camel's back,
