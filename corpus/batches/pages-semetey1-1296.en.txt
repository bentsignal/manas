He was about to trace the line,
Upon Semetey's head
He meant to work a marvel.
He meant to aggravate the wound,
Upon Semetey's head
He meant to bring calamity.
He meant to burn his heart,
Upon Semetey's head
Your Kojo meant to wreak
Havoc no man could wreak.
He meant to begin the mischief,
Fighting as he flew;
May the wretched Kojo never prosper,
He meant to work a marvel.
Low mist shimmered over the earth,
And mingling with the low mist,
Kojo drew near.
Supremely ready for combat,
From the beginning, in combat,
He was a plainly proven great master.
Drawing near within the mist,
He came within half a target's range,
And saw upon the orphan son of Manas
The place at which to aim.
Seeing it, he took delight,
And now began his mischief.
With a gaping steel-tipped arrow,
At Semetey's head
Hero Kojo began his assault.
Snatching up his arrows,
He turned and drew the bow,
And from where he stood, at Semey,
He loosed six thousand shots.
With steel-tipped arrows at his shoulder,
Chynkojo snatched them up,
And shot a thousand with those.
The fiery Oroybo matchlock,
Chynkojo snatched it up,
And fired a hundred with it.
Poor Kojo then
Drew his bow like pouring rain,
And fired bullets like hail.
Semetey was hard beset;
Had his helmet not been iron,
His fur cap would have been shattered.
The sable cap upon his head
Had been turned into a sieve,
But his coat of mail and helmet,
Those two had stopped the shots.
Had those two not been there,
Chynkojo, son of Shygai,
Upon Semetey's head
Would have brought the end of days.
Made of blue-green cloth,
The great saddlebag Kanykei had made,
A green-blue saddlebag in hue,
No camel's load, but a bull-camel's saddlebag,
Upon the noble Buurul
Was always carried.
Your bloodthirsty hero's ammunition
Was powder unmatched among the people.
That saddlebag held a marvel,
With powder filling its inside.
Powder on one side, bullets on the other—
There was no such wonder in the world.
Among those who fought while flying,
There was no villain like Chynkojo.
Hero Semey, the son of Manas,
Your hero born wise.
His bullets flew apart through the sky and reached him,
Yet his entire body
Went on without stirring,
With ringing impacts, he paid no heed,
And the bullets raining down
He valued no more than his trouser cuff.
Heroic courage and lionheartedness
Allah Most High, withholding nothing,
Had bestowed on him beyond measure.
