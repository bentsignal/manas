The mad woman Chachykey
Spoke without restraint,
And became intent upon
Slaughtering Kulchoro,
Putting him to death,
Cutting him down as a sacrifice,
And ending his life.
The wayward, spoiled mad slave-woman—
Now God struck the madwoman;
May He never give such a person
Such filthy, obscene speech.
When you looked at Chachykey,
She raged beyond the reach of words;
Your stubborn sister-in-law spoke,
Swelled and seethed,
And your fierce sister-in-law spoke.
Where Semetey lay,
Here where he remained still,
For sixty colts and four sheep
Your noble mother Kanykey
Had refined white silver.
Amid thirty daughters-in-law and thirty girls,
Your sister-in-law
Seized by its handle
A knife made to slaughter a man,
Drew it from its sheath,
And rushed to slaughter Kulchoro,
To put him to death.
She charged forward in a lunge,
Knife gripped in her hand,
And threw herself upon Kulchoro.
From that witless wretch
Kulchoro fled away,
Clung to the place of a mother,
And escaped the woman who had seized a weapon.
“Father! Wild sister-in-law Chachykey!
I know your unruly nature;
My swaggering prince Semetey—
I go on honoring his spirit!”
The boy said this and fled.
Kulchoro flew like an arrow;
The wretch could not draw near him.
The great woman raised a shout
And struck out in anger.
From far behind the boy,
The knife struck the gate.
The gate shattered apart,
And thirty daughters-in-law and thirty girls
Fled in every direction, crying out.
Kulchoro ran on,
Bending low for a spear’s length,
He reached the top of Akborchuk
And rushed onward in haste.
He fled as though enemies had entered the village,
Running away
From Semetey’s wife.
He took the road at speed,
And charged onward without drawing near.
Hurrying, the two matched their pace,
And the two equal warrior choros
Took counsel in an empty place:
“Who can understand a secret like this?
Elder Kanchoro, hear my words!
His wife is out of her senses;
That croaking wretch
Nearly killed me.
Along the Üch-Koshoy,
Our blue-maned elder, great Bakay,
Is an old man of immense wisdom,
A sage left to us
By the blue-maned lion Manas.
Bakay’s senior wife Körpöjan—
Let us bring her between us,
And learn the news for certain.
On the face of the willow country,
On this side of Keng-Köl,
Sarykan of the bowl-shaped eyes
Was a person of sacred power,
