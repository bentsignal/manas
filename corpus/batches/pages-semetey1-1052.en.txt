Let us overrun them utterly
And stage our spectacle.
Driving an army that makes the land shine,
Driving all the host in abundance,
Let them give Chürök if they will;
If they will not give Chürök,
We shall begin the turmoil!"
Then Kozho said this:
"Hero Toltoy, whose soul has fled,
Does one prosper by doing evil
To people who have sought friendship?
Are the Afghan people fit for coercion?
Let us not commit this violence.
If the Afghans come to equal terms,
Let us become in-laws, drive livestock,
Bow to one another and chew the fat,
And return this way!
Let us take the maiden with mutual honor
And depart without resentment.
Let us go to their envoys;
If they will give Chürök,
Let us take their promise;
If they will not give Chürök,
Then let us begin the battle.
Look carefully, warrior Toltoy:
Livestock has come as tribute,
The khan has come leading them,
Grievous force has come upon Afghanistan,
All their nobles have come.
Let us hear the envoys' answer,
And then ride out to war!"
When mighty Kozho waved
The red banner mottled gray and white,
It was the signal to dismount;
All the Zhediger
Boiled down from their horses.
Swarming like ants,
They spread out and dismounted.
Dividing into groups of ten and five,
They gathered around the field cauldrons.
Chynkozho and warrior Tölök
Had the Zhediger dismount.
Hurrying between the sides came
Azhybay, son of Azhy,
And Tümönbay, son of Tülkü.
Azhybay, son of Azhy,
Was a surpassingly eloquent servant,
Swift-tongued and skilled in argument,
A man of exceptional eloquence.
Tümönbay, son of Tülkü,
Proud-hearted and blustering,
Very courageous and stern,
Was a ruthless hero
Who never turned back from death.
After seeing Kozho and Tölök
Coming toward them,
They had their thoroughbreds prepared
And the horses tethered far away.
They spread foods of every sweet and fine kind
In abundance before Kozho,
And beneath him had a thick layer
Of splendid carpets of every kind laid.
Tölök and Chynkozho
Halted the Zhediger host
And the two now came between the sides.
When he saw the appearance
Of Tümönbay and Azhybay,
Could Kozho stand without surging forward?
"Azhybay and Tümönbay,
Tell the lawful and unlawful truth.
If you will not give her, name your danger;
If you say you will, state your answer!
If you say you will not give Chürök,
Let us begin the turmoil;
Let us send the armies over you,
And set your bitter sorrows boiling.
Tell me the purpose for which you girded your waist;
