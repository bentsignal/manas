My elder's patterned spear—
When I gripped it and turned its neck,
Facing toward the foe,
When I threw myself against Toltoy,
And thrust the spear straight,
When we fought to exhaustion,
I had no support on whom to rely,
No fortified place to hold.
Standing in peril by myself,
When we faced one another.
I had no support on whom to rely,
No fortified place to hold.
When we faced one another,
My renowned elder lion was absent.
I had no cliff geese could not circle,
No rugged black mountain;
Lion Bakai, who sat as my support,
Was not here.
Fire from one's own core is terrible;
An enemy from one's own kin is terrible.
Chynkojo, with whom I shared secrets,
Will he encircle me from behind,
Turn back his great army,
Take Buurul from beneath me,
Leave Semetey on foot,
And seize him as plunder!”
Walking heavily and exhausted,
Leaning upon the patterned spear,
Külchoro stood there.
Let us leave his ruined state
And now tell
The tale of Khan Toltoy.
Dear Toltoy flailed about,
The two fronts of his coat flapping,
Covered in swirling dust,
And blood by the red bucketful.
Surkoen ambled;
The blood flowing from his rump
Ran in streams
Across Surkoen's fat haunches.
At times he regained consciousness;
At times he could not.
His time had broken apart;
Blood flowing from his wound
Spilled upon the black earth.
Hero Toltoy appeared
About to depart for the true world.
His eyes darted away;
Gray wolf Toltoy then
Let his legs hang loose.
His spirit was sorely torn;
He bore the look of one dying.
Surkoen ambled;
Blood flowing from his neck
Ran down his collar.
Raging and shouting,
Bending down the willow tops,
When bright dawn broke,
When light fell upon the earth,
Toltoy went on screaming.
His face distorted then;
Hero Toltoy retched,
Thudding from time to time;
Hero Toltoy vomited.
He did not recognize his own fault
In fleeing before the boy.
Then he cursed Chynkojo,
Making sport of
His father and mother:
“Accursed Chynkojo,
Did I not tell you before?
Did you not hear me before!
The deeply dug pit has come;
Manas at their head, surging,
The engulfing black army has come.
A dark day has pressed hard upon our lives;
Bakai at their head, a thousand men,
Have surged out from Batkal.
