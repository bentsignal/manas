Such was the wonder they beheld.
His forefather, mighty Manas,
Cried, “Stand firm beside my child!”
Shouting in a ringing voice,
He reached out and laid hold,
Supporting him beneath the arm.
He set right what had been broken,
Rejoined what had been severed,
And gathered what had been scattered.
“May the ancestral spirits be my companions,”
Sarybai had once prayed.
But now, “Spirit of my true forefather,
Drive back the bloodthirsty son!
Drive all five claws of the spirit
Into the flank
Of that vaunting boar!”
Then a dazzling vision appeared:
Gray-maned Manas, the leopard,
Seemed like a battle standard
To young warrior Seytek.
“Drive on, my son, drive on!
Seize that slave before you!
Terrify the life from him!
Do not spare him from death—
Steep your spear in blood!”
With Maral at their head, forty men
Shouted before him,
And when he looked, they had become tigers.
A lion opened its jaws,
And the spirit of my heroic father Manas
Grasped Seytek by the hem,
Holding and supporting him without release.
“Do not fear when battle comes!”
Thus brave Seytek
Launched a furious struggle with him.
May God never show us its like!
Clashing their pinewood spears,
Seytek and Sarybai
Stood their ground and thrust at each other.
The earth broke and split,
And all who saw it stood aghast;
Their bosoms filled with clotted blood.
They struck ever harder,
Thrusting face-to-face,
Pressing knee against knee,
Seizing each other’s collars and tugging,
And whenever the gray-maned champions found an opening,
They struck with their fists.
As the fierce conflict mounted,
Those who drew close and saw them
Spared not their lives,
But grappled collar-to-collar.
Their coats were torn to tatters,
Their bodies were gashed all over,
And at every place they struck,
Their clenched hands filled with flesh.
They butted like rams,
Seizing each other’s collars and tugging.
The savage struggle mounted,
They planted their feet and wrestled,
And again the champions came to grips,
Spitting at one another.
Spears, swords, and battle-axes
Were utterly shattered between them.
If you would see a marvel, behold this:
The immense battle thundered,
The wondrous tumult rose,
As Sarybai and brave Seytek
Thrust at one another face-to-face.
Behold the strength of those wrestlers!
Tookyzyl and Kulansur
Were steeds surpassing all other beasts.
In that vast and ruinous battle,
As they met and thrust,
Their coats split and fell in strips,
Their fragile lives were hard-pressed,
And blood streamed and was swallowed.
Their precious, honored heads
Were sorely pressed by spears;
It was a savage battle.
