Let us place all four between us
And learn the news in that way.
On this side of Talas,
Beyond Keng-Köl,
There is Jamgyrchy of the Esheks;
Let us seek him among the men,
And make his senior wife Jaynake
Our envoy.
Let us somehow obtain news
From that witless, wicked woman.
Let us appoint the envoy firmly.
My elder is a true man—
How could he have called her ‘wife’?
My sister-in-law is witless;
He has pampered his wife too much,
And the wretch has grown loud and wild.”
They traveled through half the night,
Rode far without sparing their horses,
And when the pale dawn drove onward,
When light touched the earth,
When daybreak opened dimly
And light scattered over the land,
The two went directly
Before your brave elder Bakay.
When great Bakay in the encampment
Saw the boys’ appearance,
He sprang from his place:
“Kanchoro and Kulchoro,
My twins who keep me company,
My sea left from the twin pair,
My morning stars beside the moon,
My cherished sultans,
Your backs I set upon watch—
My dear stars,
You have come in a terrible hurry.
Are your Kyrgyz people safe?
Is he before you safe—
Poor, solitary Semetey?
Is your fortune, braided like bargek,
Standing secure and well,
And your numerous Argyn people safe?”
Your elder Bakay said these words.
Startled awake, he sprang up,
Hurriedly put on his clothes,
And, alarmed by those who had arrived,
Your heroic elder Bakay
Saddled the blue tulpar.
Alarmed by his boys, he said:
“Oh, my two equal colts,
Is there a gray, rolling mountain before you?
You have come in desperate haste,
Darkening your very lives—
Is there an enemy surrounding you?
If an enemy has come from Mecca,
Brave boys, tell me the truth;
I shall mount Kökcholok
And drive them back as far as Mecca.
If an enemy has come from China,
I shall saddle and mount the tulpar,
Break their ranks and strike them.
Tell me your words without stumbling;
You have come in reckless haste.
Boys, tell the truth without faltering.”
Hero Bakay said this
To the boys who had hurried so greatly.
“Ah, senior wife, rise,” he said;
“Pour my boys
Some fresh yellow mare’s milk.”
The senior wife sprang up
And poured Bakay Khan’s great cup
For the boys.
