Scatter his blood like water.
Mighty Kyrgyz, you are our wings;
To strike down a charging foe
Is the custom left by your forefathers.
From time beyond remembering,
When has this strife ever ceased?
Since the earth was made and peoples arose,
Since our tribe became Kyrgyz,
There has been no reckoning of the dead.
We suffered humiliation and gathered again;
Harried and scattered by the enemy,
We were oppressed for seven generations.
First we were in Altai,
Many were scattered to Kanghai,
Many of us went away to Eren,
For our persecuting enemies were many;
Many Kyrgyz descended
Into depths from which none returned.
Because of cruel enemies,
At times we scattered,
Beyond the land of El-Körbös;
At times we Kyrgyz went
Among the people of Hindustan.
Blood was shed in combat;
From age to age,
From time to time,
We Kyrgyz suffered mighty oppression
And were divided by the measure of the lash.
Despite enemies who dealt us wrath,
Our precious lives still appeared alive.
When hostile enemies came,
We clashed and battled;
Blood was shed and warriors died,
But the formidable, blessed Kyrgyz people
Never failed to take revenge,
For honor and for the people.
Their heroes died.”
So spoke the brave gray wolf,
The princely son of Manas,
Stirring a great commotion throughout the world.
He makes us slaves lower than beasts.
He lets us sow no crops,
He lets us camp nowhere freely,
He lets us tether not even two mares.
He will unleash his wrath upon the multitude,
Reduce this land to less than a hair,
And mercilessly slaughter these people.
With brave Seytek and Külchoro
Proclaimed the leaders of the people,
What inheritance shall we gain?
Let us spare not a single life
And perish fighting;
O wretched Kyrgyz people,
Let us pass away as sacrifices.
When the khan dies, let the people die;
Let the whole multitude die!
Once the head that leads is gone,
Once you die and your days are done,
Let the eyes of this fallen head close in peace,
So that the multitude left behind
May see days of happiness!
The wrathful Sarybai
Is a flood that will engulf us,
A black and blazing fire,
A deadly bullet from a matchlock;
His breath burns the soul,
He is an ember huge as Mount Kap.
O lion-born descendants of the Kyrgyz,
Turn aside in every direction
The raging flood he drives upon us!
Stand tall and bare your breasts
Before death’s bullets;
O dear people, vast as lake and river,
Summon the strength to quench
His red embers, huge as Mount Kap.
Advance and extinguish the fire;
That swine who seeks to rule,
Bind him by a hair
And cast him down.
Do not make Sarybai a giant,
