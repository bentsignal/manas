Did we not live in joy?
His courage and heroism
Grew ever greater as he lived.
Against the teeming Khagan’s realm,
Against the seething Chinese,
He allowed not the slightest calamity
To reach us, but stood guard like a hero.
He was an iron fortress and stronghold;
God will surely never again give
The Kyrgyz another like him.
Why, bristling and pouring out your wrath,
Did you Jediger of the Seven Rivers
Bring calamity upon yourselves?
Among this surging multitude,
Whom of yours had he ever harmed?
Since becoming Semetey,
Since bearing the name Semetey,
Whom among the Jediger of the Seven Rivers
Had he ever injured?
When had he ever invaded your people?
Kyyaz said, ‘March!’
And you wretched, accursed people
Simply obeyed and marched;
You seething Jediger,
Boasting of your strength, took up spears
And rose in tumult.
If the warrior-hero, son of Manas,
Had not carried off your fields,
Had not scattered your treasury,
Had not driven off your wild horses,
Had not strewn your goods from your saddlebags,
Then by obeying Kyyaz’s wicked command,
Attacking the lion hero
And needlessly entangling yourselves in misery,
We have, my people,
Trodden upon
The dragon’s tail.
Can anyone who challenges
This grim hero Semetey prosper?
Can anyone who fights him survive?
Did he not hear of it yesterday—
The late warrior Toltoy?
He was wise and heroic,
A discerning kinsman;
Becoming an outsider among his own kin,
Chynkojo, son of Shygay,
Whose ambition reached across the world,
And Semetey
Were sons of two brothers.
Look at the treachery
Of that swine who died in flames.
Seeking to wage a bitter fight—
Khan Akun was Semetey’s father-in-law,
And Aichürök was his wife—
Chynkojo, son of Shygay,
Truly incited the people
And started a conflict.
Because warrior Toltoy committed that deed,
Many gray-bearded elders
Gathered troops and raised an army,
And all were caught in slaughter.
Terrified before our lord,
We offered our horses in submission
And barely escaped the magnanimous hero.
Chynkojo and Toltoy went mad,
And personally went to attack him;
But all the multitude
Became entangled in unforgiving wrath.
The Jediger, as though eager to die,
Against the grim hero, son of Manas,
Rashly launched the attack themselves.
When had Semetey ever brought hell
Upon your host, Jediger of the Seven Valleys?
When had the grim hero come against us,
Threatening, ‘I will raid your village,’
And brought violence upon us?
By attacking the lion hero,
Today we have entangled ourselves in misery.
