That lively Akylay of before
Spoke of an auspicious sign:
"My dear elder sisters,
My honored sisters-in-law,
Last night as I slept, I dreamed;
In my dream I saw a fortunate sign.
A black-spotted tiger
Ranged through the city,
And the dogs would not stop their sharp barking.
A scarred gray-wolf lion
Came raging into Afghanistan;
Such good fortune appeared.
Saying, 'I shall go out to meet him,'
The enemies who opposed him were destroyed;
Saying, 'This is that black-spotted tiger,'
The people of Afghanistan honored him.
What can this mean?
Younger sister and elder sister,
Interpret this dream of mine!
A giant black bird, the Zymyruk,
Stretched its great feet down from the sky.
Unable to withstand its wrath,
The besieging Jediger
Fled away like crows.
Forty men, each worth a thousand,
Within this city of Afghanistan
Established their royal camp firmly;
They took everything in the direction they faced.
What can this mean?
Interpret this dream of mine!"
While Akylay stood speaking thus,
Bold Kynymjan began to speak:
"That black-spotted tiger
Was my khanly brother-in-law Semetey himself.
He will grasp his blood-drinking weapon
Because of my honored elder sister,
And cast havoc
Among the enemies who oppose him.
The scarred gray-wolf lion,
Who burns everything he faces,
Was my heroic brother-in-law himself;
From Kojo, who brought disaster,
He will deliver us.
The giant black bird, the Zymyruk,
Stretching its feet down from the sky,
From his father, gray-wolf Manas,
My elder sister's husband
Was born to surpass him, we have heard.
My tiger brother-in-law, that gray wolf,
Will come here himself;
If an enemy comes out to fight,
He will cast havoc among them.
A husband has come
For my renowned, unfortunate sister!"
Before she finished telling her dream,
The dark-haired maiden posted as lookout,
And the fair-haired maiden set as guard,
Bent their knees,
Bowed before Aychurok,
And shouted out their good news:
"Thirty daughters-in-law and thirty maidens,
Women playing your games,
Come here!" they called.
"My dear elder sisters!
Did you learn what I learned?
Did you perceive what I perceived?
Did you see what I saw?
A man has come to the farther bank,
His enormity equal to half a mountain,
As though he would devour all he sees.
He is broad across the right shoulder;
When I look at his bearing,
He is a hero who could rout the Oyrat.
He rides the red horse Buurul,
Its horsehair tail tied in a firm knot;
He is girded with a silver belt,
And turns a face radiant as the sun;
He wears a bulletproof coat,
