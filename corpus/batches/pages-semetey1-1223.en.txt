You buried-in-earth Toltoi,
I came thinking you were a lion.
If an enemy faced us,
I came thinking you would be our support;
But you think of impossible things—
You are infidel Toltoi.
Fleeing from a woman in a stagger,
You are womanly Toltoi.
Paying no heed to salt and bread,
You are a Toltoi who has prospered.
Thinking of every kind of thing,
You are fine Toltoi;
Frightened today by yesterday's dream,
You are shaman Toltoi.
At Kökötöi's feast,
A great sage who saw much—
You are old Toltoi;
Knowing well that you will die,
You are saint Toltoi.
While standing at Zheti-Özön,
Saying you would take a wife,
You are industrious Toltoi.
Knowing that you yourself will die,
You are dreamer Toltoi.
Toltoi has indeed gone about
As khan of the Zhediger.
Toltoi has indeed known
Seven years of suffering.
As a saint, Toltoi has indeed
Known his appointed time!
Hero Toltoi, my friend,
If you will go, give me your answer;
If you will not, tell me the truth.
I had thought you were a khan;
Why should I go on relying upon you?
I shall turn back to the people
And now enter among
The Argyn and Kyrgyz.
After that, accursed Toltoi,
Stay where you are;
Then I shall reach your very root.
If Semetey proves too numerous,
I shall offer my Kara At as a gift.
I shall make peace and enter my people;
Then, Toltoi, watch closely—
Afterward I shall reach your root.
When I go and make peace,
I shall take Khan Bakai;
Then I shall bring slaughter
Upon the Zhediger of Zheti-Özön.
I shall make Kanchoro my companion,
Engulf you from above,
And scatter your four kinds of livestock.
If you will go, tell me the truth;
If you will not, tell me that!"
Kojo stood seething,
Speaking as though he would devour
Toltoi of the Zhediger.
At that moment hero Toltoi,
Grumbling helplessly,
Was about to go
To Khan Akun's city.
Through the calamity
Of wrathful Kojo,
Hero Toltoi was about to remain there.
Toltoi of the Zhediger,
With his great army that bent the earth,
Could, when angered,
Utterly engulf the Afghans.
Dear Toltoi could reach
The very root of whoever challenged him.
Was hero Toltoi not
The accomplished khan of the Zhediger?
Did hero Toltoi not possess
Great courage within him?
Surkoyon, touched by angels,
Whom he had saddled five times over,
Could not be caught at a blink,
