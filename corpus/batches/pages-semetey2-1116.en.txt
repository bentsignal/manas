The courageous guarded the flanks.
Nearly ten thousand spread like a lake,
Not a few, but a multitude,
Firing heavy guns and drawing bows,
Again engulfing all in clamor,
In a din that split the sky.
They brandished blue-steel spears,
Raising their deep-socketed lances
High and crossing them,
And began the great clash.
The renowned rode at the front,
Spurring their bay horses,
While valiant Kyyaz guarded the flank.
Never one to stand idle,
Kadyrbay and Tölöbay
Were the men who bore the standards.
A hero comes, a strong man comes,
The lion named Tölöbay comes;
Yet Kadyrbay’s vast host
Recoils and scatters in terror.
The great host loomed like mist—
The brave hero Semetey
Saw this road filled from every side.
“We marched out against the Khagan’s realm,
O dark, fierce, tireless warriors,
And brought a mighty turmoil upon ourselves.
Having escaped death among the Chinese,
Are we now to die among the Kyrgyz?
Having escaped death at an enemy’s hands,
Are we now to die at our own people’s hands?
Having escaped death through ordinary horses,
Are we now to die through noble steeds?
Having escaped death at an enemy’s hands,
Are we now to die at our kinsmen’s hands?”
Semetey stood there towering.
The mighty Külchoro
Laughed beside him, swelling with courage.
Besides Külchoro and Semetey,
There was not another man with them:
The Jediger were myriad, the hero alone—
Such a wretched thing is this world.
There was no noble steed ready to mount;
When a dear life was hard-pressed,
There was no kinsman to shield it,
No forest into which to flee for cover,
No Nogoy people to give protection.
Alone as a solitary pine,
The grim hero stood alone against many foes—
Such is the loneliness of one without a lord.
Neither Sarykan nor Khan Bakay was there,
Nor any of the dear Kyrgyz
Who once stood as his support.
The calamitous campaign and great turmoil
Had now come upon him;
Surrounding the grim hero from every side,
The vast dark host of Jediger
Was coming to seize him,
Climbing over the forked ridge
And appearing above the pass.
The troops below the grim hero
Raised a clamor and commotion,
Thrusting their spears with menace,
Stirring strife for all to see;
They were climbing across Keng-Kol,
The ground of the great conflict,
And coming to crush
The great and fearsome hero himself.
They swarmed like ants,
Seething and writhing like worms,
Raising the cry of “Jediger!”
Carrying banners enough to bend the earth,
The army seethed and filled the plain,
Not a few, but a multitude.
The renowned rode at the front,
Galloping upon their bay horses,
While dear Kyyaz guarded the flank.
He wore armor no bullet could pierce,
And rode a horse no bullet could overtake.
