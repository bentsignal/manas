Far-off misery came to an end.
When Khan Koshoi of the Katagan,
Your elder, father of the people,
Put on the kandagai,
Your elder, old Koshoi,
Cuff upon cuff,
It was as though fitted in place;
Fat rump upon fat rump,
It was as though patched in place.
Old hero Koshoi
Then tested Kanykei,
Daughter of Kara Khan,
The wretched daughter of a saint.
Seeing this, he approved.
Kanykei, the prince's wife,
Bent her knees to the ground
And stood bowing
Before Koshoi, father of the people.
Her prayer to God
Had reached Him long before.
At Khan Kökötöy's feast,
While infidels and Muslims
Seethed there before him,
When she bowed to her elder khan,
He became as heavy as lead.
Koshoi tested the fierce woman:
“Ah, my poor Kanykei,
Her fortunes are complete;
Her twelve limbs are ample.
Her courage is great, her wrath is strong;
She is a woman who will bear a lion.
She is a chosen one among spear-wielders,
A woman who will bear a hero.
She is a seamstress with golden fingers;
Among women there can be none like her,
For she herself is a saint's daughter.
When trouble assails, she will undo it;
She is one of the finest of people.
For the sacred bloodthirsty man,
She is the Night of Power itself.
She was born with excellent wisdom;
Her pelvis is narrow.
Put another way, there is something to say:
There is a reason she might not bear a child.
Her forehead bone curves back;
After the hero's eyes close,
Her chance of surviving is poor.
The hair at her nape is like wool;
When the hero's eyes close,
She was born for sorrow.
The hair on her brow is like grass;
She has little happiness
And much misfortune.”
Koshoi spoke these words.
When Koshoi looked again,
Unable to contain her distress,
Dear Kanykei
Was asking God for a child.
From those dear quail-like eyes,
Tears streamed apart;
From those dear currant-like eyes,
Tears streamed a whip's length.
Wailing in repentance,
Sobbing and weeping without cease,
Turning over countless cares,
Asking my Lord for a child,
Her knees bent to the ground,
Your sister-in-law bowed
Before Koshoi of the Katagan.
As soon as he saw your sister-in-law,
Khan Koshoi gave his blessing.
Infidels and Muslims
Joined in the blessing:
“May Kydyr be a fresh young willow for him;
Until he climbs the mountain ridge,
May God, who will act for Semetey,
Be his protector!
May he grease his white steel;
