What else did you say?
With your hero Kyyaz at your head,
You flustered Jediger
Stand there trembling,
Thinking foolish thoughts.
All of you clamor together,
Looking ready to flee;
Your khan and beks assembled
And said they would run away.
Damn you, Jediger!
You have taken Kanchoro as your protector.
Jediger, for you this valiant hero
Is one of the true gray wolves,
The bright night granted by Providence,
A hero not found even in a month’s search.
You boast and bluster, Jediger—
Here is your champion who will devise a plan.
If Kanchoro finds the way,
What complaint can you Jediger have?
Keep it in mind, Jediger:
Kanchoro is your champion.
While Kanchoro remains alive,
Six mighty rivers cannot divide you.
Let all the world attack in force—
For you Jediger there will be no death.
Seven mighty rivers cannot divide you;
Let armies cover the earth,
Let their multitudes flatten the land—
There is no fortress where I will turn back.
Kanchoro is your swift stallion;
For you Jediger there will be no calamity.
We broke down the gate
Of that fool, the son of Manas, and drenched it in blood;
We turned his black road to dust.
We made the livestock on his pastures fat spoils,
And struck down his imperious lions,
His rash and sullen-minded,
Thunderously boasting warriors.
Across Jylgyn, Keң-Kol, and Talas
We made our long spears dance;
We cut down their great champions.
We made our warhorses prance on the passes
And destroyed every one of their beks.
When Kanchoro grows enraged,
He will make snow fall upon your passes;
No—if you befriend him, Jediger,
He will make bounty rain upon your people.
When my murderous fury seizes me,
Not one of you will survive, Jediger!
I am a deep-blue, rugged mountain pass
No multitude can cross;
I am a black wolf-lion
No enemy can approach head-on.
I broke through the pass and opened a road;
Laboring against the gray wolf,
I attacked the whole earth.
When my heart turned toward one deed,
I laid hands upon my own elder brother.
Damn you, Jediger—
Look at the prize I have taken!
If this is not a prize, what is?
Formidable in weight and fierce in venom,
I have spilled his yellow blood:
He who would yield to no one in the world,
Who would not yield even to the Flood,
The most skillful of spearmen,
The mightiest of brave men,
The most precious of many lions,
The champion of all gray wolves,
A man excelling sixty others,
The most skillful of spearmen—
Külchoro, now taken captive,
The mightiest among the multitude.
My relentless fury seized me;
I greased my white-steel blade with blood.
My anger struck just now,
And I brought down the heedless hero
Whom they had raised with such care;
