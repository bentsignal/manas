There is wealth in abundance.
I will send word to the people;
While my dear head is still alive,
I will gather everything in full.
Today I offered many prizes;
Tomorrow I will offer great prizes.
Uncle Koshoy, wise man,
Wisdom comes from an elder,
From a hero like you.
If I cannot hold my father's memorial feast,
My dear khan and uncle,
Where can I go and hide?
Between the Manchu and the Kyrgyz,
I will dig hearths into the ground.
Infidel and Muslim alike,
Everyone who comes to the feast,
I will fill with pure white fat.
For my dear father's memorial feast,
I will have autumn rice planted.
Great wealth and countless livestock,
For my dear father's feast,
I will scatter and display it all.
If I do not gather the four corners
Of the whole world four times over,
If I do not make such merriment
At my princely father's memorial feast,
May I die before I become Bokmurun.
Rather than walk about alive,
May I sink away as though never born!
Whoever crosses my stubborn will,
I will strike down and kill.
For my dear father's memorial feast,
I will make meat as high as Ala-Too,
And broth as vast as Ala-Köl.
I will slaughter livestock beyond counting
And feed everyone gathered around.
I will display my riches;
I will distribute gold like stones.
I will cover the face of the earth in dust;
Calling it “Khan Kökötöy's memorial feast,”
I will make it renowned among the people.
Uncle Koshoy, wise man,
I stand asking counsel
From a hero like you.
Since my valiant father died,
After Khan Kökötöy saw
The world that never looks back,
How should the village make camp?
How should our encampment be arranged?
Uncle Koshoy, find us wisdom;
What course should we take?
How shall we hold his memorial feast?
How shall we race the horses?
How shall we find the right counsel?
We will bend our necks to skill;
How shall we arrange its splendor?
Uncle Koshoy, elder!
Among the sons of men today,
You were born a saint above the rest.
Bereft of my father,
I am the one left an orphan.
Your help has often reached the people;
You are a true saint,
And I am truly an orphan.
Koshoy, hero who opened the market
For warriors fallen into bondage,
Koshoy, hero who restored the fortune
Of a market held in bonds,
Khan Koshoy whom all desire,
Our dear elder and uncle—
You are the mainstay of the multitude,
A saint who has stood among us.
In a waterless place where the sun boils,
You are a poplar, brave Koshoy;
For the many people of the forty Kyrgyz tribes,
You are their yurt wall, brave Koshoy.
Dear land like Chech-Döbö,
With your skill you made it a homeland;
