Driving an army from Khan Esen,
We have come to no good.
Letting the bloodthirsty one slaughter all together,
We have come to nothing.
Great havoc, vast slaughter,
From that daughter-damned Burut,
We had come to witness.
All slaughtered together, leaving none,
We had come to die.
From before Esenkan,
We set out, unable to wait.
The dense army that filled the earth,
Utterly slaughtered, not one remaining,
Our misfortune has hardened upon us.
Alas, Manas was a lion indeed,
One who would bring calamity.
When our khan gave the command,
We used to advance with countless troops,
