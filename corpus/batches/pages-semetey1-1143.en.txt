Finding no place to cast their weight,
Finding no hero to fight,
They rode about churning with impatience.
Like lions, gray wolves, and tigers,
They turned toward the multitude.
Hoping to reach the place of the feast,
All forty companions
Entered the tumult.
Like an unconquered mountain,
They entered the contest.
If the magnanimous one gave the order,
They would show the seething Chinese
An astonishing sight.
They had courage.
There were companions who drew courage
From calm words of substance.
There were companions who made ready
Their guns and swords in hand,
As though about to strike the enemy now.
There were companions who trampled trackless ground
And made a road.
There were companions who attacked,
Regarding death as less than a hair.
There were companions who, striking countless armies,
Scattered and split them.
There were companions who spilled
The infidels' blood bright red.
There were companions who entered battle unawares
And died.
There were companions called spearmen,
Skilled and cunning.
There were strong companions
Who knew the enemy's stratagems well.
When they became mixed together,
There were clumsy companions
Who struck men wide of the mark.
There were cowardly companions
Who would not part from their loaded baggage.
Who could match the man who speared heroes?
His fame remained with Manas.
Khan Manas's forty companions—
Forty lions who came from forty places,
Each was a lion,
Heroes born for slaughter.
From the Kyzyl-Kyya pass,
Dear Manas appeared in his grandeur;
That was when he came into view.
The Muslims, witnessing the spectacle,
Were under great pressure
And had split apart.
The tyranny of Kakanchyn
Had become plain;
It had trapped and besieged
The mere handful of Muslims.
With Khan Koshoy at their head,
The valiant men could not take command;
They had allowed Kökötöy's cooking pits
To be plundered
By the Black Chinese and Manchus.
They hastened toward their horses
And broke into a run.
The mere handful of Muslims
Had then despaired
Of their mortal lives.
Shouting, “Khan Manas!”
When the forty companions entered,
I saw your uncle Koshoy
Fail to rise from his place.
