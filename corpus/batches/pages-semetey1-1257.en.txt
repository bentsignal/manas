“Silver-eyed and sun-faced,
My thundering sultan,
Golden-eyed and moon-faced,
My dear sultan!
My hoof left by the tulpar,
My young hero left to me
By falcon Manas, the charger!
My banner fixed upon the spear,
My gift left by the gray wolf:
Your Kyrgyz village in Talas,
Your Afghan in-laws in Orol—
Chynkojo, son of Shygai,
Has gathered his army and exchanged fire,
And is about to wipe away your name.
My village is in Orol;
Aychürök is your wife.
Because of Aychürök,
Toltoy surged upon us then
And was about to wipe away my name.
A true black army glittered;
Chynkojo has engulfed us.
Turn your eyes toward us;
If your gray-wolf nature is true,
Come and rescue us!
The deeply dug pit has come;
My support, child Semetey,
It presses hard upon our mortal lives.
Saying, ‘I shall level the inner fortress
And slaughter them all,’
Saying, ‘I shall smash the stone fortress
And slaughter them utterly,’
The accursed mighty Khoja has come;
Upon your weak Afghan in-laws
He has brought merciless force.
Driving them after him,
Through his desire for Aychürök,
Though we said she had a husband, he attacked.
Before this, Chynkojo,
Saying, ‘Give us your eldest daughter,’
Exchanged fire six times.
Riding out with shouts to single combat,
Gray argamaks died;
In the mounted single combats,
Brave heroes died.
Those whose doom had come perished;
Crying, ‘Ah, Aychürök, may you not prosper!’
The Afghan people wept.
Many heroes from their side died;
Many heroes from ours died;
The dead became the black earth.
Saying, ‘Give your daughter as tribute,’
Accursed Khoja raged;
Saying, ‘Give your daughter quickly,’
Accursed Toltoy strained greedily.
They took all the livestock,
And destroyed many cities;
They shattered yurt frames and roof poles,
And made wives and maidens weep.
Chynkojo brought humiliation;
He proved a skilled horseman,
And now imposed great oppression.
The time to slaughter one another has come;
The hour of massacre has come.
The low gray hills have come;
Khoja's enemy host has stormed in.
A mass bullets cannot pierce has come;
A grim-faced army has come.
Unless you yourself are my mighty champion,
Mighty Toltoy has come
To overturn the mountain pass.
Tölöbay, son of Tölök,
Has engulfed us from below.
Chynkojo, son of Shygai,
The khoja whose deeds were bold—
Let us not belittle the hero,
The valiant Toltoy who has come.
If we do not give Aychürök as tribute,
The time has come for him to bring slaughter.
