Do you think he did not know them?!
The sable cap upon one's head—
Who has not worn it at a jaunty tilt,
Who, while fortune was theirs,
Did not stride about roaring?
I have told many of its secrets;
Such is the nature of Beijing!
My heroic son, understand,
And heed me without making me wail!
Beijing is no easy place;
Those who go there to die
Are not a people it restores!
In that Beijing,
Of the great men of old,
None went and exacted judgment;
Whoever attacked the people
Of Kakan-Chin did not survive.
Solomon did not exact judgment there;
That is formidable Beijing!
Unfolding its nature from the root,
I have told it to you!
What he knew and perceived,
Hero Bakai told thus;
What he had seen and knew,
Hero Bakai told openly.
The young sultan, hero Semey,
Your hero who did not fear death,
Drew his arm back,
Your lion remaining unmoved,
His gray-wolf spirit roused, he cried out.
When he heard hero Bakai's words,
He summoned his two mothers...
Below Tört-Kül,
In the garden prince Manas planted,
Facing Great Talas,
At the edge of Great Ak-Chiy,
Within the walled stronghold,
In Chachykei's palace,
Hero Semey, son of the prince,
Your hero aided by spirits,
Külchoro, son of Almambet—
The ready expert in shooting;
Kanchoro, son of Chubak—
The strong master of the whip;
Argyn and Kyrgyz together,
Hearing Semetey would go to Beijing,
Poor heads were thrown into turmoil.
From the banks of Üch-Koshoy,
Sarykan, khan of the Argyn,
Heard the news and came;
A teeming multitude came.
Bölökbai, keeper of the treasury,
Leading his forty Kalchas,
(With his father, hero Manas,
He had been a friend unto doomsday),
He came raising a battle cry.
All the khans gathered,
The people came and assembled;
The khans wept in torrents,
The people grieved with a roar.
