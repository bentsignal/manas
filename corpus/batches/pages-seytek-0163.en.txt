Along the banks of Üch-Koshoy,
Where it meets the Talas,
On the flank lies Kan-Jailoo—
At the edge of Kan-Jailoo.
There is the dark gorge of Acha-Sai.
My son Seytek, listen well,
I have many a sorrow!
When the Argyn and Kyrgyz grew restless
And rode past in streaming ranks,
When strength, courage, and valor
Were being put to the test,
My elder Semetey
Would catch and slaughter a barren mare,
And among the broad-trousered, broad-chested,
Among the many broad-breasted men,
Among the comely champions,
He would test their strength.
Sixty barren mares and forty fillies
Would be staked as the prize.
A bull would be slaughtered,
And among the many men of steel,
The champions who entered battle,
Those skilled and ready with the spear,
The mighty men who knew the enemy’s wiles,
Those resourceful in counsel,
Who said, “Let my entrusted life be sacrificed,”
And offered up their lives,
Charging against the foe,
Guarding their people from the enemy—
My uncle would test them all.
From among the many stern-hearted men,
From champions whom the people tested,
From true gray wolves,
From those who stood beneath the banner,
From falcons who, for the common people,
Would sacrifice their lives,
My noble uncle Semetey
Would choose the finest.
When those consummately skilled with the spear,
Born superior to other men,
Across the whole wide world
Were raising a mighty tumult;
When the red Zulfikar sword
Was gripped and swung by its hilt,
Kicking through gorges and twisting them,
Felling every adversary,
Kicking ice and shattering it,
Striking down all who broke ranks,
And pressing ever onward;
When the whole wide world
Was held beneath our sway;
When our spirits surged at their height,
When we stood but a hair’s breadth from hell,
We would carve the haunch and feast,
And ask, “Where is any punishment?”
When Zulfikar hung
At our bare wrists;
When we subdued the whole world
And drove it before us;
When we bore away those who escaped
And kept them in our sights;
When the whole wide world
Was held beneath our sway;
When sprawling Kara-Shaar
Was held beneath our sway;
When we subdued those who grappled with us
And kept them in our sights;
When we shattered the front and left it bereft,
Subdued every enemy we met,
Shattered the flanks and left them bereft,
Subdued all whom we pursued,
And held them beneath our sway.
In the hollow of Jylgynduu,
Along the broad Talas,
In the palace with gates,
In the fortress of Üch-Koshoy,
At the dark eye of the spring,
Right beneath the plane tree,
On the flank lies Kötörmö,
In the hollow of Kötörmö,
