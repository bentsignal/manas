The wretched soul formed like a tiger—
It seems I have overthrown him.
The dear soul formed like a boar—
It seems I have cut him down.
Buurul, whom they would not give when asked—
I shall cast a cold lasso over him;
Chürök, whom I have long desired,
I shall take for myself alone.
Bringing humiliation upon Afghanistan,
I shall gather khojas and mullahs;
Chürök, daughter of Akun,
I shall take as wife for myself.
I shall not accept what they say;
To the accursed Toltoy,
I shall not give Aychürök.
My wrath rose and I stiffened;
The orphan son of Manas—
I shall seize his treasury.
Burning their goods,
I shall seize them and gouge out their eyes;
The slaves left from the companions—
I shall bind and take Bakai.
Dragging him along the road,
I shall slaughter him as a sacrifice.
The accursed Akun,
The infidel who begrudged his daughter—
I shall do with him as I please,
And slaughter all his people.
I shall leave them like a deserted camp;
Thus shall I reach their root,
And slaughter them without leaving one.
Taylak, Bakai's wretched son,
Taylak, the worst dog of all—
I shall cut his hamstrings
And make him a horse herder.
Kalyk, son of Sary Khan—
I shall put a bridle around his neck;
Saying, ‘He is my captured slave,’
I shall sell him to the Kyzylbash people.
I shall destroy and seize them all;
Now that the mighty gray wolf is dead,
The strength has left them all.
Bloodthirsty Semetey has died;
My own eyes behold his death.
Taybuurul came out with neck stretched,
Distraught, dragging his reins;
Your bloodthirsty hero's ak-olpok armor
Hung askew from the saddle bow.
Saying, ‘My eyes have seen his death,’
Saying, ‘His horse has fled back,’
Saying, ‘God has granted my wish.’
Shouting, ‘Baabedin!’
Chynkojo flailed his arms,
And raised the battle cry of Shygai.
Raging, he spurred ahead;
“Ah, God has granted it!” he cried,
And all his weapons
He hurled toward Taybuurul.
Circling on the black horse,
He now came within half a target's range.
Its fastenings were nine;
He undid the fastenings.
Its buttons were nine;
He undid the buttons.
The khoja set his mind
On quickly dismounting,
To seize as plunder
The Buurul horse that had emerged frisking.
Girding on his Arkesken sword,
Chynkojo, rejoicing,
Came drawing near.
Circling within half a target's range,
The clumsy khoja lurched,
His two coat-tails flapping.
When the khoja came close,
The companion's heart was stirred;
