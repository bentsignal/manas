# The 100,000-line discrepancy in Sayakbay Karalaev’s corpus

Prepared 2026-09-15.

## Finding

The evidence supports two separate conclusions:

1. **500,553 is a real, long-established aggregate claim**, demonstrably printed by the 1995 *Manas* encyclopedia and repeated in the 2010 complete-edition introduction. It covers five generations: *Manas*, *Semetey*, *Seytek*, Kenen, and Alymsaryk/Kulansaryk.
2. **316,157 cannot presently be reconciled with the manuscript sequence to which the same encyclopedia assigns it.** The live catalogue’s old inventories 925–953 contain 198,100 explicitly counted lines in 27 records. The only two blank records, 925 and 926, total 603 manuscript pages. To reach 316,157 they would need 118,057 lines, about 196 lines per page. The neighboring manuscripts run around 20–50 lines per page. To reach **216,157**, they need 18,057 lines, about 30 lines per page.

My best assessment is therefore that **316,157 is a one-digit, +100,000 error in the 1995 encyclopedia’s component table**, while **500,553 was inherited independently from an archive-wide aggregate or older institutional count**. Replacing 316,157 with 216,157 makes the frequently quoted modern component sum 400,553, proving that those modern components cannot simultaneously explain the traditional aggregate. This is a bookkeeping contradiction, not evidence that 100,000 recoverable *Semetey* lines are absent from the 2013 edition.

Confidence:

- **High** that the printed 1995 page itself says 316157; this is not OCR confusion.
- **High** that old 925–953 map to current 104–132 and that their 27 known counts sum to 198,100.
- **High** that 925–926 cannot plausibly supply 118,057 lines.
- **Moderate-to-high** that 316,157 is erroneous.
- **Moderate** that the intended value was exactly 216,157.
- **Low** that the provenance of 500,553 can be reconstructed earlier than 1995 from public sources alone.

## Earliest located scholarly attestations

The earliest primary scholarly source located is volume 2 of *Манас энциклопедиясы* (Bishkek: Кыргыз энциклопедиясынын Башкы редакциясы / “Мурас,” **1995**), preserved locally as sources/raw/manas-encyclopedia-2-comparison.pdf. Its printed page 380, in the entry **“СЕМЕТЕЙ ЭПОСУ,”** visibly reads “С. Каралаев, 925—953-инв. 316157 ыр сап.” A direct rendering confirms a clear typeset **3**, so OCR did not turn a 2 into a 3.

The same encyclopedia separately states that Karalaev’s whole text is **500553** lines and gives the *Manas* section as **83830** lines. It cites B. M. Yunusaliev’s 1967 essay for the uniqueness and completeness of the recording, but the quoted passage does not supply the total. No pre-1995 source with the exact total was located.

The 2010 complete *Manas* edition repeats 500,553 and says recording continued through 25 April 1947: <https://new.bizdin.kg/media/books/Manas-eposu-1-tom.pdf>. Later repetitions include the [National Library](https://nlkr.gov.kg/news/manas-kyrgyz-ruhunun-ochpos-zhyldyzy/), [Ministry of Education](https://edu.gov.kg/announcements/56/), a [2021 university journal](https://mnu.kg/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/vestnik-2021-1.pdf), and a [2022 article](https://bulletennauki.ru/gallery/92_66.pdf). These are downstream repetitions, not independent recounts.

## Arithmetic and competing decompositions

| Scheme | Manas | Semetey | Seytek | Continuations | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Common modern | 84,513 | 316,157 | 84,697 | 15,186 | 500,553 |
| Corrected Semetey candidate | 84,513 | 216,157 | 84,697 | 15,186 | 400,553 |
| Alternate popular | 84,830 | 397,775 | 17,948 | — | 500,553 |

The alternate decomposition appears in a [public teaching/repost source](https://ok.ru/group/53243993456790/topic/66989514530966). A related [music textbook](https://new.bizdin.kg/media/books/Muzyka-5-okuu-kitebi.pdf) repeats 397,775 and 17,948 but uses 84,513 and adds 15,186, producing inconsistent arithmetic.

These incompatible allocations preserve the headline total while moving tens of thousands of lines between sections. They show that later component tables are not stable archival measurements. The 1995 encyclopedia itself adds another *Manas* value, 83,830.

## Could the 100,000 be outside old 925–953?

No public catalogue evidence supports that interpretation. The encyclopedia explicitly attaches 316,157 to **925–953**, not to additional inventory ranges. The current catalogue maps that exact consecutive range to 104–132. All 29 records are Sayakbay *Semetey* records and none is labelled a copy explaining an extra duplicate count.

The catalogue contains later recordings and mixed materials elsewhere, but no located source assigns a further 100,000 *Semetey* lines from those items to the 925–953 total. The detailed crosswalk and every record/sample URL are in research/SAYAKBAY-SEMETEY-MANUSCRIPT-CROSSWALK-2026-09-15.md and its matching JSON.

## Practical consequence

The project should use the 2013 edition’s **216,333 extracted source rows** as a near-complete representation of the catalogued sequence unless manuscript comparison demonstrates omissions. The 176-row difference from the hypothesized 216,157 is compatible with headings, prose, segmentation, or a small count variation. It is not evidence of a 100,000-line gap.

The strongest way to settle the uncertainty is to obtain official line totals for inventories 925/current 104 and 926/current 105 or full scans of those units. If they sum near 18,057, the 216,157 reconstruction becomes compelling. No institution was contacted and no corpus or release file was changed.
