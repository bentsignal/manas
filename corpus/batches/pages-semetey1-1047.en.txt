Your sister-in-law lost awareness;
When four handspans had entered,
She groaned like a camel calf.
When six handspans had entered,
She cried, "Oh, my great-hearted one!"
When nine handspans had entered,
Like one suffering labor pains,
She bleated like a goat and kid.
When ten handspans had entered,
Moaning overtook her and she collapsed.
When twelve handspans had entered,
"My destroyer, you have consumed me!" she cried,
And stretched out like a hair rope.
Your hero's great instrument—
She lay whimpering as he used it,
Extending it into her precious place,
And stilling the precious woman's soul.
Such were Aychürök's words,
After burning for so many years;
That was where the princely leopard Semetey
Found contentment in his heart.
Within the white palace,
When bored, they played ordo;
They drank kumis and enjoyed maidens,
And when not bored, lay together.
In pleasures of countless kinds,
The gray wolves immersed themselves like this.
Morning and evening, twice a day,
They remained without boredom in the palace.
Semetey's guests,
Tümönbay, son of Tülkü,
And Azhybay, son of Azhy—
Now let me tell
The tale of these two.
Of surpassing good will and noble thought,
These were sons of brothers,
And the finest men to come from Afghanistan.
Azhybay, son of Azhy,
Sharpened his black tongue,
And sang out before the khan.
Broad-jawed and flat-tongued,
He was a formidable eloquent servant;
When he accomplished a task without force,
Among all people upon the earth
He was a son beyond price.
Both eloquent and keen in argument,
He was one to rely on for stratagems.
Tümönbay, son of Tülkü,
Proud-hearted and stern-tempered,
Was a thundering hero;
His speech was venomous.
Toward a man who displeased him,
He bore an anger that would not heal.
He had these words to say:
"Give me your eldest daughter," he demanded,
And had come to besiege
The city of Khan Akun.
Toltoy of the Zhediger
Had troops in numbers that bent the earth.
"I shall seize Aychürök,
And inflict harsh suffering," was
Chynkozho's intent.
The Zhediger had an army
That would overrun Afghanistan.
To Chynkozho and Toltoy,
"By force I shall seize Chürök
And deliver her into your hands," he said,
