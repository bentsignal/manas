My eyes like blackcurrants—
You are going about having them gouged out.
Sending me broadside
Against the bloodthirsty hero's orphan son,
You are going about having me slaughtered.
Saying I would tell you my grievance,
I came to see you—do you see?
Wounded and suffering,
I came to die—do you see?
Blessed gray wolf Manas—
You provoked him;
Refusing to go peacefully and reeling crookedly,
Accursed Khoja,
You made your enmity known.
For the great black Jediger,
You have incurred their blood-guilt;
All the people,
Ill-starred Chynkojo,
You truly caused to be slaughtered.
You first desired corruption,
Accursed Chynkojo;
Saying, ‘Take his wife,’
You drove us toward death.
You have become deranged—
Why desire corruption so greatly?
The one who charged and attacked me
Was Semetey himself;
For all the peoples of the age,
His time to bring slaughter had come.
Aychürök, daughter of Akun,
Had worn her horse's flanks lean,
Traveling through the whole world;
The time had come for her
To bring merciless turmoil.
You foolish colt, you do not know it;
Her husband was mighty Semetey,
And the time had come for her
To lead him here.
You meant to strip off my coat,
Accursed fugitive slave;
Listen to my answer.
You meant to show my eyes
A wonder fit for men to see.
The son of Manas proved a lion;
Semetey of terrible intent
Had come to us this year—
His time to bring slaughter had come.
While among your own people,
You were hero Khoja;
Stirring conflict over everything and nothing,
You were infidel Khoja.
At Jeti-Özön, the Jediger—
You reached and gathered them;
Inciting this people,
You caused them to be slaughtered.
At Kökötöy's memorial feast,
You will not let me tell what I saw.
You bore black thoughts toward your kin;
Indeed, Khoja, you will never prosper!
Your senses have left you, hero Khoja;
You understand nothing.
Father and mother—
You curse them and make them a mockery!”
As Toltoy stood speaking thus,
Dear Khoja grew heated.
Now Chynkojo came forth,
Speaking to hero Toltoy:
“In pursuing a bride,
You proved hero Toltoy;
In cursing father and mother,
You proved infidel Toltoy.
You do not know your own brutality;
Father and mother—
You curse them and make them a mockery.
Crying, ‘A boy speared me,’
You come shouting.
My blustering Toltoy,
If you continue thus, you will die!
