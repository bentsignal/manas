Now reached and leaned against.
He turned the piebald horse's head
And the renowned poor hero
Raised his spyglass.
A blue haze appeared against the sky,
Clouded and angular,
Pointed like a cape.
Across the churned sand,
Dust rose in bursts.
When he looked at the surging dust,
It was Akkula's dust.
He had lowered his head to the ground;
Your padishah's Akkula
Had taken a remarkable stride.
He had lowered his neck to the ground;
Your padishah's Akkula
Ran with an impossible stride.
When he came to a descent,
His forelegs thrust like a roe deer's;
When he leaned into an ascent,
Mighty racehorse Akkula's
Mane and tail streamed out.
When he reached open steppe,
His hooves thundered on the ground.
When he champed
The black-steel bit,
It clattered in his throat.
His forelock parted toward the sky,
And his tulpar form appeared.
He flattened his ears like a kulan
And swept the earth like a whirlwind.
When he came into view,
Valiant noble Almam
Watched and marked him carefully.
Stones burst beneath his tread;
Hero Joloy's Achbuudan
Came flying with a roar.
He was behind Akkula.
When young Aydar of the flowing forelock
Drove away the horses,
Kokotoy's Bokmurun
Had used much cunning.
Heeding his words, young Aydar
Tied Maaniker firmly
And slept for three days.
After three days, young Aydar
Woke in alarm from sleep,
Turned onto his right side,
Dipped his hands in water
And performed ablution for his hands;
He dipped his feet in water
And performed ablution for his feet.
He hurriedly recited namaz,
Saddled Maaniker,
Opened his eyes wide as grain mash,
And all sleep scattered away.
When he stood high and looked,
There was no raven croaking,
No crow cawing.
Nothing caught his eye;
He could not tell where
The people beside him had gone.
Then young Aydar
Struck the whip hard
Against a tulpar like Maaniker,
Upon the black thigh never struck by a whip.
Its sheep-like flesh divided,
And its back straightened like a hare's;
Its foal-like flesh stood out,
And its coat smoothed like a sighthound's.
Its cavernous mouth opened;
Its contracted hooves
Spread as broad as great platters.
White foam mixed with blood
Splashed upon its chest.
Beneath the clouded sky,
Over the rippling grass,
It now flew away with a roar.
