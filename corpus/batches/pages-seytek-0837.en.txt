Whenever he saw a commotion, he raised his banner;
Against all who challenged him, he raised an uproar.
Titans and giants grieved to face him;
Before that evil, venomous being,
Mortals despaired of life.
Unable to withstand his wrath,
Whole peoples died in combat;
Every person who challenged him died.
No one could endure him.
They grappled and clashed,
Fought and spilled blood.
War horns and trumpets sounded;
That frenzied champion, eager for strife,
Had the muskets fire in need.
A vast, rending tumult arose,
And his monstrous excess became plain.
He donned his bulletproof armor.
“He is an unbearably mighty champion,” they said;
“He has become death itself,” they said;
“Those who go against him never return,” they said.
His people lamented and the nation cried out,
Despairing of their own lives.
The strange wrath of that swine
Revealed his monstrous savagery.
Such was Sarybai’s nature.
When roused, he went berserk,
Like the long ridge of Kaptal-Too.
When wrathfully enraged,
He was like a vast expanse of Kök-Ap.
When they saw Sarybai,
Warriors despaired of life.
He bore an iron spear,
And a steel mace was fastened upon him.
Sparks blazed from his mace;
When he opened his mouth, fire burned—
He seemed to blaze unnaturally.
When one considers God the Creator,
He fashioned every manner of living soul.
His saddle’s forebow hung down
Like a grain merchant’s sack.
The breath issuing from his mouth
Was like flame from a forge.
His brow was high, his venom fierce;
Such was his appearance to the eye.
He drove his splendid army onward,
Driving the whole host together.
Anyone who watched carefully
Despaired of his entrusted life.
Misery seethed across the wide world;
A red haze of dust rose.
The land spread wondrously before the eye.
That infidel, son of a demon swine,
Raised seven sacred banners
And stirred seventy separate tumults.
Dark-bladed spears swayed,
And black-iron Zulfikar swords
Flashed in every hand.
Their patterns divided into countless ranks;
They looked like giants and demons.
Mounted on camels, they carried spears—
What kind of beings were these, cursed to strike from above?
He truly had a champion army,
A fearsome force assembled.
It was massed like blue steel.
How could this stone-hearted one’s aspect
Be so imposing?
He sated the bold with battle,
Dividing all this host without remainder.
Thick-armed and stone-hearted,
Look upon the army he commanded:
Sturdy-limbed, hulking brutes.
At the word “Go!” they closed in;
Every soul they hunted submitted.
Their forms were unlike those of men.
When a mortal dared confront them,
Heedless of person or people,
They blazed their Venus-bright eyes,
And made human misery seethe.
They had their hour of drinking blood—
A truly formidable host had assembled.
