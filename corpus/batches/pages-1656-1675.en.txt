The slaughtered people were beyond counting.
To shout and stand by him,
His precious Syrgak was not here.
Who had stirred the stars in the sky,
Who had sworn friendship into the next world,
His devastating hero Chubak was absent;
To rise towering and fire,
His devastating lion Manas was absent!
Finding no mountain for a crow to circle,
To stand by him as support,
Finding no honored sultan, no hero,
Without counting the hours of the day,
Without taking rest by day,
Fighting day and night,
He could find no peace for his life.
After seven days of fighting through,
Wounded in seventy places,
He had fled away.
Argynkan's unfortunate son,
Fought on alone;
Azizkan's young hawk,
Fortunate when he entered a firefight,
The noble one's desire was not sated.
Making Sarala prance,
Making whoever he met crawl,
Smashing seven thousand of them,
Thrusting his spear wherever he reached,
“That consummate Kojozhash,
Where is that swine?” he cried.
Bloody battle, a great field of war—
Blood flowed through the broad ravine!
