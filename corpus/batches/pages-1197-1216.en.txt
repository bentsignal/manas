“Against the fugitive I knew,
I shall turn and wage battle,” he said.
“Parted from the countless horses,
How can I remain alive?” he said.
“If my death has come, let me die.
If my strength suffices, the horses,
Let me raid them back.
At the very least, half of them,
Let me split them off with a thunder.”
Thinking this, Zhangybay
Cried “Go!” behind them, making ready.
The herdsmen behind him,
All of them forty men,
At your Alma who raided the horses,
Shot arrows like rain,
Fired bullets like hail.
They came up in a crowd,
Forty-one men galloping.
Zhangybay, chief herdsman,
Spurred on the right and left,
Whipped on the right and left.
The lacquered spear in his hand,
