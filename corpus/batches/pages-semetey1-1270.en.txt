Can there be such a fool,
Contesting a lion's wife?
To see the spectacle together,
The ancestral spirit struck that people
To die by your gray-maned hero.
When the hero sultan, son of Manas,
Suddenly came out of the horde,
His eyes blazed like a lion's;
Those who saw him lost all strength.
Bloodlust seized
The young gray-maned lord.
The garden planted in broad Afghanistan,
The golden throne mounted as a bridegroom—
Now was the time to clash
With Toltoy who had come himself.
Raising sky-high
The red banner with golden finial,
The Opol-Too-like leopard
Now advanced
Toward Chynkojo.
Let us leave the valiant man
Who had set his course,
And begin the tale
Of Kulchoro, Almambet's son.
The swaggering gray-maned hero
Was twelve years old,
A glowing red ember.
God Almighty in His power
Creates every kind of soul;
None like him existed in the age.
The Creator performs every deed;
No mortal like him existed.
The brightest and fairest of boys,
His might was exceptional;
When he mounted to attack an enemy,
His strength was exceptional.
Thick-armed and stone-hearted,
Kulchoro was no Jediger;
He could bring slaughter upon the age
And raid the Chinese.
He looked to both sides;
Descendant of a blood-drinker,
He licked his lips and nose.
Early words became late;
At the thought of confronting
Toltoy of the Jediger once,
His two eyes became four.
He did not look around;
Your stripling left as a gift
By ocean-like gray-maned Almam
Did not think, "I will die."
When Kulchoro put on
The bulletproof armor
Your dear mother Kanykey had made,
It began above his navel.
When they saw the enormous power
Of the twelve-year-old boy,
The Afghan people lost heart.
Wearing his bulletproof coat,
Without drawing brow or frowning,
As though going to his in-laws,
He smiled openly in joy.
With the honor-cutting sword at his side,
Like a cloud about to rain again,
Wrath spread across his face.
He moved apart in anger;
The direction he looked caught fire.
At six years old,
He had a child's thoughts;
At twelve,
He slaughtered whoever faced him.
His fortune was complete;
He was a lion supported by the Shah.
Except for his bad name,
Kulchoro under Semetey
Was a hero of a spear's cast.
