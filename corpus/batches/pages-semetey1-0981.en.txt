Almambet’s only son
Was broad across the right shoulder,
A lion who came from Ogoi,
Thick-armed and stone-hearted,
Exceedingly handsome and also a lion,
A sturdy man of every limb.
He hung his blue sword at his side;
If you had seen Kulchoro,
He raged like a blue tiger.
The sight of him divided one’s thoughts;
Now he plainly reached
The utterly spoiled beauty.
Before that excitable wretch,
That exceedingly pampered woman,
He came crying and ready,
Swaying as though struck from this side.
Before the stubborn, spoiled wretch,
Before the boy could speak to her face,
Your grieving, delicate sister-in-law
Pranced like a spirited horse,
And began speaking first,
Voicing her words slowly and firmly:
“Your unruly elder Semetey—
Is that wild man safe and well?
Is the tulpar beneath him
Safe and sound,
And the falcon upon his hand?”
When your sister-in-law said this,
The choro stood and replied:
“Noble sister-in-law Chachykey!
Your hardship has passed,
And your fortune has overflowed.
Tell where the sword fits its sheath;
Sister blessed by Kydyr,
This time tell the truth!
Think and tell of every kind
Of suffering and death.
If you possess knowledge, sister-in-law,
Leave nothing out when you speak.
We opened tireless Buurul’s stride;
Along the shore of Ala-Köl
We lost Akshunkar!
We heard words from men of counsel:
Unless you refuse out of obstinacy,
We heard that you would know.
My elder Semetey,
If you tell us of the falcon,
He will say neither this nor that,
Will not raise his whip against you,
And will not take another wife after you.
Made an extraordinary promise;
Tell me, sister-in-law, what does this mean?”
When the boy had said this,
Semetey’s wayward, pampered wife
Burst into a frenzy:
“Shame, shame upon your face!
You have spoken cold, ugly words.
The man in the field
Has business with his bird;
The woman inside the house
Has business with her food.
As for the falcon that was lost,
What business could
A woman sitting at home have with it?
Vanish from my sight;
Do not come near me!
You who speak with a hiss,
Who mention the time I was mocked—
For these words you have spoken,
By your father’s grave, filthy slave,
Vanish from my sight!
I shall slaughter you now;
By your father’s grave, devil-slave,
I shall gouge out your eyes!
Remember what I have said:
You and your bloodthirsty companion together—
Go down into the black earth!”
She raged and swelled with fury,
