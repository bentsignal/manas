Every person they met was slaughtered,
Sarybay’s countless host,
When their khan gave the command,
Swept over the land that day.
The maiden Kuyaly, lion-hearted girl,
While they swarmed like ants,
They surged up and surrounded her,
Thrusting spears and shouting,
Each of them a grave menace,
Raising the cry of “Sarybay!”
When their numbers overwhelmed her,
The earth was churned into dust,
As they closed in and encircled her,
The ground sagged beneath the massed host,
The maiden Kuyaly, the champion,
As they were about to trap her in their midst,
A roar arose upon their flank,
A clamor splitting the sky,
His warriors and lions,
His champions and heroes,
Those most skillful with the spear,
Those strongest at devising stratagems,
Brave Seytek, the hero’s son,
Was coming forth with his chosen men,
The swiftest fierce as ogres,
Swooping like hawks upon their targets,
Brave Seytek, the hero’s son,
Was driving them forward.
Stretching out all who faced him,
He came steadily onward.
“If fate has come, I shall die,” he said,
“For the champion maiden Kuyaly,
I shall stand as her protector,” he said,
Brave Seytek, son of the lion,
Drove back all who crowded upon him,
Spearing heroes on the slope,
Piercing many in succession,
His spears split the army apart.
By the time the hero’s son arrived,
Broad in spirit as Talas,
In stature the image of Manas,
In judgment the equal of Koshoy,
Charging like a wild boar,
The way he cleaved through their ranks
Was like a winter blizzard.
Terrified at the sight of this,
Sarybay, son of an ogre,
Found no deep grave in which to hide,
No friendly host to flee toward,
As the dark army poured in,
That was how they faced one another.
Standing face-to-face,
Firing guns and drawing bows,
They fought with wet, crushing blows,
Clashing their spears,
Raising a fearsome tumult,
The armies met and uproar rose,
Host crashed against host,
They hammered one another,
May God never show us such a sight,
The tasseled standards seethed aloft,
Their battle cries became a mighty roar,
Such was the dreadful struggle,
When the armies confronted each other,
And waged battle without yielding,
Baring their great teeth,
Horses died until mountains were blocked,
Mustaches bristled,
Corpses of heroes rose like hills,
Many mortals there beheld
The journey from which none return,
Drenched in crimson blood,
Many mortals lay dying.
It was a grievous, mighty conflict,
The people were slaughtered without mercy.
Spears shattered and lashings fell,
Corpses of heroes formed mountains,
An immeasurable slaughter took place.
The hollow filled with crimson blood,
Deep ravines filled with corpses.
