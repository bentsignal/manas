I, being Ur's daughter,
We shall plunge into merriment,
If the words spoken are true.
Sharing meat from one cauldron,
Sharing one delight,
Easing our aching longing,
Walking arm in arm,
We shall lie in each other's embrace.
In great delight and merriment
We shall immerse ourselves in joy.
Our destination is Jelpiniş;
Wearying horses, ranging the land,
Unleashing hounds and flying birds,
Binding tassels to our steel spears,
And farther on, warring with foes,
Sarybai is the one I will drive off—
Such is the foe I shall grapple with…
The Creator restored my senses,
And brought me to you, gray-maned wolf;
We lodged together as guests,
And shared the name of warrior.
In this mortal world
We steeped ourselves in pleasure,
And lay upon one bed.
Our bodies entwined, our blood mingled,
We turned to each other and joined our souls.
My noble one, born for me,
My companion until Judgment Day,
My heart yearns for my people,
For the people called wretched.
My people once found gold,
Yet spirits would ravage them.
What has become of my dear homeland?
Without broad expanses there is no land;
No man is brave who misses not his people,
And there must be no fewer than fifty men.
Why should I crave too deeply
The pleasures of this two-day world?
Let me reach my dear people.
Forgive my unfulfilled longing,
And relinquish your claim upon me,”—
So Kuyaly spoke,
Biting her finger and shaking her head;
They clasped hands in farewell,
Making the face of the earth resound,
And galloped toward Jelpiniş.
She found what she desired
And encountered the bloodthirsty hero;
Kuyaly of Kaiypchal
Now set out upon the champion’s road.
Semetey led the five doomed heroes—
“Our journey has grown long;
The time to die has come,
And this world has grown narrow.
Why should we remain any longer?
Let us enter a place
Unknown to man or nation,”—
Said Bakai and Kanykei,
Külchoro and Semetey,
And your moonlike sister-in-law Aichürök.
The five vanished from mortal sight,
Preparing to depart upon their journey.
Our khan has vanished,
And we shall never meet him again.
With brave Semetey at their head,
The five set forth upon their journey.
They have no village where their tents are pitched,
And no tidings of their travels;
Seen today, gone tomorrow—
Such, alas, is this wretched world!
When the people heard of their journey,
The nation wailed and the people wept;
The white-bearded elders wept,
And all the mighty nation wept.
Bereft of their renowned ones,
The people donned mourning and grieved:
“When brave Semetey was among us,
We carved and ate rich horsemeat,
And asked, ‘Where could calamity be?’
We wore white armor with golden collars and copper sleeves,
