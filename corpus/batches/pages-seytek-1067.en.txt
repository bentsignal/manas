I took it in hand and wore it down.
I bound it firmly at my right side,
And kept it overnight upon my perch.
I witnessed such a wondrous sight.
I saw such a mighty bird of prey.
What could this mean,
Who will interpret this dream of mine?”
Thus Akbermet
Finished recounting her dream.
Then, affirming it as truth, she began to speak,
And uttered these words:
“For the valiant hero Seytek,
My royal head has been brought low,
And great calamity has befallen me.
By day I have no laughter,
By night I have no sleep.
Only a spoonful of blood remains in me,
My flesh is but a bruised, raw wound.
If I roam through
All eighteen thousand worlds,
Will I find my gray-maned hero,
Or, never seeing the hero Seytek,
Must I remain forever wakeful?
In the dream I dreamed last night,
My heart surged and my blood boiled;
I dreamed a most joyful dream.
Thinking, ‘The panther will fall into my hands,
The one I desire will come himself,’
My soul-companion Külayım,
I stand here greatly rejoicing.”
Then Külayım,
In a voice filled with singular sorrow,
With a face like a flower in bloom,
Spoke softly yet with conviction,
And began, asking leave to speak:
“Unfortunate Perizada,
We have endured much suffering.
All our wisdom has been spent,
And every recourse exhausted;
For six months we have traveled without rest,
Journeying far upon your quest,
From the most precious realm in the world,
For the fierce gray-maned panther,
For the sake of dear Seytek.
All the mounts have grown saddle-sore,
And all our hearts have grown heavy.
The dream you saw is a sign of fortune;
God has set your affairs aright.
Divine power will bring before you
The person you saw in your dream.
In the dream you dreamed asleep,
The sharp Albar sword you girded
Is a gift bestowed by God.
According to the traditions,
And if you ask the wise,
It is the heirloom handed down
As a gift from his forefather, gray-maned Manas,
That mighty and sagacious hero,
Among all sons born of humankind,
The one unmatched champion.
His given name is Akjoltoy;
Alone, he is worth a host.
His breadth is like a sprawling valley,
His keenness is like steel;
God has granted your desire.
The answer to your longing,
Joltoy, has come bearing news.
If a swift steed was tied at your post,
You mounted your destined fortune.
The keen Albar sword that cleaves stone
Is your kinsman Joltoy himself;
It means the panther’s hour of coming has arrived.
If a falcon alighted at your ford,
It means your destined road lies there.
God has opened the way to your fortune,
And the enemy who opposed you has fled.
Sister, if you dream while sleeping
That your fortune opened in the marketplace,
Your sustenance, luck, and prosperity
Have spread across the face of the earth.
