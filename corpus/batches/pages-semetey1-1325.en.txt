In the midst of that war,
All forty companions were there.
From the dark and lowering Beijing,
When they went straight in and wrought slaughter,
They were steeped in the dust of chaos;
Without fleeing before the Kakan,
They fought unceasingly for six months.
When they went to that Beijing,
When they exacted judgment
From the Kakan whom none could touch,
As they withdrew from Beijing,
Your father had even Beijing,
Untouched by Chalyyar, struck down.
The reason he had it struck was this:
Muzburchak and Khan Kökchö died,
All who met the foe died.
The courageous gray-wolf giant died,
All who grappled with them died.
Only sons of their fathers died,
Many died with their hopes unfulfilled.
Precious lives beyond recovery died;
Before the seething unbelievers,
Before hero Khan Kongurbai,
All who fought died.
Those who met him did not return,
Those who encountered him did not escape alive.
When unbeliever and Muslim
Stood clashing together,
Such was the nature of that struggle.
Beijing is no easy place,
China is no easy people;
Your tiger father, the lion,
Entered Great Beijing and returned,
Having lost his tigers.
Your lion father exchanged fire,
Clashing with Beijing;
Your lion father grieved,
And while waging holy war against the Kakan,
He lost Khan Chubak,
Who had sat as his support.
Your father Manas, lionhearted hero,
Saw Akkula struck down by a bullet,
And Almambet suffer martyrdom;
He fought and returned,
And then your lion father
Lost those who had been his support.
Can Beijing be an easy place?
My heroic son, listen!
It has a desert forty days across,
And, as one ear has heard,
A people no man would wish to see.
Beijing is no easy place;
Your father Manas would not listen,
And led the armies of the world,
Leaving the village of sixty sons
And widowed women desolate!
Your wretched mother speaks—know this;
Oh, my only son, heed my words!
In teeming China,
There are the peoples of forty tribes;
Whoever attacks it, that Beijing
By custom drinks their blood.
Beijing is no easy place;
The Chinese are no people to toy with!
Mighty China is a powerful nation;
My dear son, know this:
When you see their multitude, they are black beetles!
If you wage holy war against the unbelievers,
My darling foal,
Hero Khan Kongurbai
Has a fortress beside the road;
The Kalcha whose road is barred
Has a lion who will answer you.
Do not dig my grave,
Forever refusing to listen;
Do not make my sorrows boil.
Forever refusing to heed
Your fathers and forefathers,
