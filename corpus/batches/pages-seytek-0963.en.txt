Life fled, wheeling away,
Gnawing at whoever he met,
Sarybay tormented every soul.
Against that swine whose soul is departing,
Let us all fall upon him,
Let us attack together!
Against that infidel who boasts of his strength,
With an uproar that splits the sky,
Let us charge from every side,
Let us shout and level our spears,
Let us make the battle fierce!
Clashing and grappling,
Thrashing one another like autumn wool,
Becoming like hungry lions,
Spilling blood with our bullets,
Reckless of our lives,
Fighting in fury,
Let us unleash boundless havoc.
Let grim calamity become our sport;
With that dog who ravaged the people,
Let us grapple once!
Let us tear at each other without tiring,
And drink our fill of blood.
Whoever faces us,
Let us hew them down with axes,
Thrusting and stabbing with spears,
Let us fulfill every desire,
Let us meet them face-to-face.
Shouting and fighting,
Let us unleash a furious assault.
Let every spear be stained with blood;
If death comes and our days are done,
Let it be at Sarybay’s hands.
Let countless warriors be slain;
Let us behold the worst ordeal.
Sarybay has gone mad with rage;
Let us test ourselves against him.
Shouting from every side,
Let every battle cry unite in one place,
Let us invoke Manas as our war cry,
And level our spears from every side.
Let us not stand idle—let battle begin;
Let us see how great his strength is,
And die while locked in combat.
Fighting with no thought for life,
If the Almighty so wills,
Let us surrender our precious lives.
This Sarybay seems to have gone mad;
Let me try my strength against him!”
The swine’s torment had gone too far;
Claiming everything as his own,
Rallying their fiercest courage for battle,
Let us unleash a mighty conflict.
Not merely one of us, but all of us—
All of us, beyond counting—
Against the hideous swine,
Against the wrath-filled infidel,
Against the finest warrior who meets us,
Against the burning ember of hell,
Against death’s unerring shaft,
Against his hero’s girded waist,
Against this savage himself,
Let us reach him with battle cries,
And pass amid resounding blows.
Let us unleash a battle;
If death comes, let us die,
And behold a wondrous spectacle.
Sarybay is the enemy we face;
Let us exchange battle cries with him
And see what comes.
The people summoned their courage and surged,
And now they waged a savage battle;
Sarybay was the one they grappled with,
And the melee roared around him.
With fierce courage and great valor,
Every warrior fought without holding back;
The uproar split the sky,
A vast tumult of countless war cries.
Most wondrous was their fierce courage,
And the death of Sarybay.
