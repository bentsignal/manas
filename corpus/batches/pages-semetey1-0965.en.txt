We have honored Oroy's memory!
Do not snarl, elder sister;
We have long known your shamelessness.
The reason I remained unmarried
Was that I honored the spirit
Of your father-in-law who went to the next world.
Yesterday, at Khan Kökötöy's feast,
Beside the Karkyra,
Before the gathered people,
With dear Koshoy at their head,
There were multitudes and nations there;
Among infidels and Muslims
Were giants no horse could carry.
Calling out like a young goose,
My elder Koshoy the old man,
His white beard shining,
Whose blessing enriched the people,
Was a soul resembling a prophet.
Koshoy gave his blessing;
Infidels and Muslims alike
Raised their hands with a clamor
And joined in giving the blessing.
My husband Semetey
Was a soul conceived through that blessing.
For my poor father Akun,
My heroic late father-in-law
Had arranged a pledged marriage.
Driving livestock as marriage kin,
Bowing in homage and chewing fat,
Setting all the giants apart,
He drove six hundred gray horses;
He displayed at the horse's forelock
A silk bundle as large as a horse's head.
This is the reasoning I am telling you:
Semetey is my own husband!”
So Aychürök provoked her, saying:
“Ill-mannered senior wife,
If I do not use a greater stratagem,
If I do not seize and carry away
Your lord's White Falcon,
If I do not make sport of this,
And make you a cast-off wife,
May I bow the hair down my back
And marry my father, Khan Akun!”
Having spoken thus, Aychürök,
The unfortunate woman skilled
Through studying the Arabic language,
Scooped a handful of dust from the ground.
She rapidly recited backward
A difficult sorcerer's spell;
In the blink of an eye,
Before anyone could say a word,
She recited swiftly without restraint
And cast a chilling spell.
The dust in her hand
She consecrated with a rite
And struck her with it.
The pampered wife Chachykey
Screamed from her bellowing,
Calling upon her dead ancestors;
She staggered in a seizure.
The forty young women beside her
Were left utterly astonished.
Chachykey suffered a convulsion;
Six or seven supported her,
And, two abreast, carried her
Into Chachykey's fortress.
When she remained in this condition,
Semetey gathered everyone:
Khojas in white turbans,
Mullahs learned in scripture,
The wisest of the good men,
And shamans experienced in divination.
For six days he had them recite over her;
Bound as though insane,
Chachykey recovered her form,
And when the seventh day came,
Chachykey regained her speech.
