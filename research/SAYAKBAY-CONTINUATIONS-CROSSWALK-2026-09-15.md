## Episode and citation crosswalk

A. Zhainakova's *Manas Encyclopedia* article **КЕНЕН** summarizes the continuation from Kenen's conception and birth through his marriage and rule, the birth of twins Kulansaryk and Alymsaryk, Chyngtemir's invasion, Kenen's death, and Zhelzhetpes's escape with the twins to Eki-Aral. The article attributes the narrative to Sayakbay Karalaev.

- Online article: https://tamgasoft.kg/dict/index.php?lang=en&lfrom=kg&word=%D0%BA%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B5%D0%BD
- It cites Manuscript Fund inventory **960**, page **145**, for the pregnancy passage and cites inventory **960** generally at the article's end.

The separate encyclopedia article **КУЛАНСАРЫК** quotes the twins' birth and cites Sayakbay Karalaev, Manuscript Fund inventory **146**, page **401**:

- https://el-sozduk.kg/kulansaryk/

Inventory 146 is the second unit in the exact 15,186-line pair above. The page-401 citation also demonstrates that its physical extent exceeds the five-page public sample even though the live catalogue omits a page count.

The inventory-960 citation is unresolved. In the catalogue's old-number sequence, 960 is current inventory 139, *Seytek*, part VII, recorded in 1943, 8,179 lines; the encyclopedia bibliography assigns it overall pages 1683–2022. A page 145 within that volume is possible as local foliation, and the Kenen episode may have been recorded there as an overlapping or earlier telling. It cannot replace the exact 145/146 crosswalk because 8,179 does not equal 15,186 and the article itself later cites 146 for the twins. The public evidence supports **overlap or mixed shelfmark usage**, not a claim that inventory 960 alone is the counted continuation.

The 1991 edition preface supplies the narrative relationship: Sayakbay extended *Seytek* through Kenen, Alymsaryk, and Kulansaryk, following the tradition of his teacher Choyuke. It describes these later poems as less developed and less widely known than the trilogy proper.

## Relationship to the 1990/1991 printed witness

Its continuation begins on PDF page 315 / printed page 314 under **КЕНЕН** and **КЕНЕНДИН ТӨРӨЛҮШҮ**, then covers Kenen's birth and rule, Kulansaryk and Alymsaryk's birth, Chyngtemir's invasion, Kenen's death, and the twins' escape with Zhelzhetpes. Verse ends on PDF page 348 / printed page 347, followed by a parenthesized prose synopsis of the escape and hiding between two rivers. This episode sequence matches Zhainakova's encyclopedia synopsis and therefore overlaps the 145/146 manuscript pair.

The corpus currently contains **3,154 unique source rows** from this printed continuation, spanning `p0315` through the verse block on `p0348`. The underlying OCR has 3,192 nonempty display lines, but the extra rows include headings/page furniture and the prose synopsis; neither number is a certified printed verse count.

The printed witness is demonstrably abridged. Its preface states:

> Кайталоолор, көркөмдүк сапаты начар саптар гана кыскартылып

That is, repetitions and lines judged artistically weak were shortened/cut while the composition was retained. Its terminal prose synopsis replaces narrative verse. The edition therefore preserves only a selected overlap with the **15,186 reported manuscript lines** and cannot certify full continuation coverage. A raw comparison of 3,154 corpus rows to 15,186 reported lines is about **20.77%**, leaving a numerical difference of **12,032**, but this is only a scale indicator because OCR/source rows, headings, and archival verse-line counting are not guaranteed to be one-to-one.
