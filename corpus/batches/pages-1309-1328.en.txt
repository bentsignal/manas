Not one of you died in battle!
And while Manas was there,
You ate slices of horse-rump meat;
‘If my sovereign should suffer,
I will crush the governor-general,’ you said.
In the hollow of Zhylgynduu,
Along broad Talas,
While the multitude lay in happiness,
Would you lie there rejoicing,
All of you boasting together,
‘If there is a formidable, deep-rooted enemy,
The steeds we ride today
We shall make rear and leap,’ you would say,
While magnanimous Manas was there,
