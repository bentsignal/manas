To devise a means for him,
Neither Sary Khan nor Bakay was there.
The peerless lion stood utterly alone;
The poor hero grew angry,
Towering apart from other men,
His terrible bearing held high.
As though sixty rivers converged
And he were formed from their torrents;
As though he were formed
From every spur of twelve mountains;
As though he were formed from the lair
Of your hungry lion;
As though he were formed from the supports
Of heaven and earth.
Raging and champing for battle,
Leopard-born Semetey
Set out toward the Jediger.
Young Lord Semetey
Mounted Buurul,
Making him spirited then,
Making him heave as though his master had died.
Semetey emerged
From the treasury horde;
The direction he lunged became red fire,
And all who saw him lost heart.
Nine guardian spirits supported him;
A black-striped leopard
Strained at his side.
One of the forty chiltens
Supported him unseen;
A stunted gray-maned lion
Made ready alongside him.
The forty chiltens and Khizr Ilyas
All joined in supporting him.
A giant black eagle, a zymyr bird,
As though about to carry him away,
Stretched its feet down from the sky.
A black-striped dragon
Crawled beneath him
As though drawing him onward.
The orphaned son of Manas,
Your rooster-like lion—
Manas, whose spirit lived,
Supported him above all.
Shimmering dark blue,
The direction he faced became red fire.
At the place where he spent a night,
A deep-rooted shrine arose;
Khizr supported from his birth
The descendant of the inexhaustible one.
At the place he chose to visit,
A shrine with a city arose.
His sacred power was extraordinary;
No enemy who challenged him prospered.
When he entered facing battle,
Bullets crawled like snakes.
The valiant man's descendant—
No one who confronted him survived.
As he stood now,
He could conquer the whole world.
He was no Chynkojo or Toltoy;
He could plainly bring slaughter
Across the entire world.
Bullet-defying Arbuurul
Was his exceptionally swift steed.
He was no Chynkojo or Toltoy;
He was of an age to besiege
Beijing in Kakanchyn,
And had strength to engulf and slaughter.
What he wore upon him
Was gold-collared kureoko armor.
The Jediger coveted it;
They were about to fall into turmoil.
Driving after him,
The Jediger would meet slaughter
Because of desire for Aychurok.
