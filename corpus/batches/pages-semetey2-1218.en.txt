Can a hero behave as you do?
Will anyone prosper who calls you brave?
I came to your Seven Valleys,
Thinking yours was a worthy land;
Oh, accursed Jediger,
I came thinking you were a people!
If I lose all faith in you, Jediger,
I shall readily move back to Talas;
Every day you have set my heart aflame.
Jediger, you have burned me within!
You chattering Jediger,
If you act so upon seeing his child,
What will you do when you see his champion?
If you act so upon seeing his warrior,
What will you do when you see the great man himself?
You vast host of Jediger,
There is no boat upon the dark water;
While your Kanchoro stands watching,
None of you dares even raise an eyebrow!
Make this slaughter truly glorious;
Plunder all his possessions,
Leaving not so much as a thread!
You lanky, swarthy Jediger,
Show what you can do in battle;
Divide Semetey’s countless riches
Precisely by the measure of the whip!
Bring ruin upon his feast,
Strike without wavering!
Surround on every side
The bloodthirsty man left horseless!
Look how much ancestral fortune
He inherited from his forefathers!
Mighty on horseback but helpless in war,
At the forked mountain pass
The bloodthirsty man stands gaping;
Those of you skilled with the spear,
Charge shouting and thrust your spears!
All your champions and elders,
As though overtaken by slaughter,
Go and perish together!
All your strongmen and lions,
Overwhelm the bloodthirsty son of Manas;
Every one of you, swarm upon him!
Thrusting spears and striking axes,
All of you charge together!
You vast host of Jediger,
Heed these words of mine;
You snarling, wretched people,
Or you will be annihilated!
If you do not heed my words,
Along the Seven Valleys,
In the hollow of Jerge-Tal,
I shall plant my army’s standard.
If you stir against me, Jediger,
I shall have every one of you slaughtered!
In the hollow of Opol-Too,
Down toward Andijan and Tarasha,
Across the broad plain,
I shall summon a mighty army
And have it massacre your people,
Leaving none untouched by slaughter!
I shall bind the valiant Külchoro,
My heroic kinsman, to my waist,
And, setting myself against you, Jediger,
Utterly wipe out your lineage!
I shall level every hollow and hill;
Do not trifle with me, Jediger!
If I fail to bind your chiefs,
If I fail to slaughter you all,
May I die unworthy of the name Kanchoro,
May I sink barren, as though never born!
You Jediger who bar my way,
You wretched people kindling your own deaths,
Go on, try to flee—let me see!
I shall raise an army from the Argyn,
And muster a vast host of Kyrgyz;
You sprawling Jediger,
I shall track your yellow trail,
