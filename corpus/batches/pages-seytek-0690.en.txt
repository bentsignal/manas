Then I am not hero Sarybai.
Dust rises beneath my tread,
And souls depart at my thunder.
I am the black eye of the Antichrist,
The monster himself.
Give me the slightest chance and I shall slaughter you;
Neither Semetey nor Choro—
Today I shall not be sated
By all eighteen thousand worlds.
When I charge in fury,
I become an irresistible flood.
Khans die beneath your sweeping blow;
Everyone you meet dies.
I am mighty, I am the strongest,
So formidable am I.
I am the black eye of the Antichrist,
Sarybai himself.
To anyone who takes my measure,
I am a fortress walled in steel.
Known as Sarybai’s son,
I am famed among many peoples.
I am calamity itself,
The black eye of the Antichrist.
I am doom to those who walk at ease,
A true taker of souls.
I strike down the majestic;
When angered, I devour the world
Like a dragon.
My terrible fury has seized me;
I shall slaughter you
Without leaving a single hair.”
Uttering many dreadful words,
He stood there shouting.
Now perfect God struck
The infidel, son of a monster.
The mad brute made the earth sag;
Under the infidel’s might
The ground seemed to split apart.
When he beheld the monster’s son,
Armed with his great musket,
Such was his appearance when measured.
He could not keep from raging;
Sarybai ground his formidable teeth
Again and again.
His lashes blazed and turned to embers;
Among men there was none
Like the ill-fated infidel.
“I shall defeat your nobles
And swallow your whole clan.
I shall utterly slaughter your people;
If I do not break your back,
Strip away your strength,
And make your blood flow like water,
Leaving not even your written trace,
I shall grind you all to dust.
You accursed wretch, you will never prosper;
Never, from one age to the next,
Will you abandon your defiance.
Abandon all hope for your life;
What use is your rage?
Not one hair of you will remain;
You will be massacred.
I shall inflict hell upon you all
And engulf your village in ruin.
Yesterday I stiffened in fury
And slaughtered many giants.
Without sparing a single one,
I put them all to the sword.
Of those who dared oppose me,
On the blood of many giants
I, Khan Sarybai, have feasted.
Whole peoples died by provoking my wrath,
And many formidable champions died.
I mow down all who cross my path;
I am a bolt of black steel,
Without equal in the world.
I gnaw apart all whom I meet;
Broad-chested and wide-breasted,
I am a towering hero.
I have made the whole world my slave,
