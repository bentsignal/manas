God knows whether it was true:
“When I looked to his right,
A black-mottled leopard,
As though about to rend its prey,
Lay there crouching.
When I looked to his left,
A black-eared lion
Was poised and straining to spring,
More wondrous than all the rest.
When I looked before him,
He bore the aspect of a giant black eagle;
Seytek alone possessed
The awe of a thousand champions.
A striped tiger was upon his face;
Valiant Seytek the leopard—
No mortal could ever be like him.
He held his sword in his hand,
Its spine jutting stiffly upward,
As though he were about to slaughter all,
Glaring fiercely, eyes wide,
With some forty companions beside him.
Sixty of them bore spears,
Enough to guard the whole world.
If they became enraged,
Let alone devouring mere men,
They seemed ready to drink the very blood of lions.
If they grew wrathful, the black earth
Seemed ready to smoke through its depths.
If they poured their fury over the world,
They would leave it utterly desolate.
My thoughts stood scattered;
In my dream I saw this fearsome sight,
And witnessed such inexhaustible might.
His eyes were burning embers;
