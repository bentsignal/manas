Before Esen Khan,
The fugitives reported
That he had slain two thousand wrestlers.
Hearing this news, Esen Khan
Gave him the name "Bloodthirsty."
The noble lion had humiliated
Their merchants
And oppressed those who came.
His deeds are far too many to name;
Shall I go on recounting
More deeds like those?
It is well known that
Lord Manas the lion was a hero.
Shielding the Kyrgyz even from the wind,
It is well known that mighty Lord Manas
Was their fortress.
Would no male child be born from Manas?
If he is truly his son,
Will he not pursue his father's blood-price!
Does a son not die for his father?
If he is truly a hero,
Will Semetey, the hero's son,
Not go to Beijing and return!
My golden spring in the valley,
My young champion, son of lion Manas,
Whom I beheld late in life.
For your father Manas the lion,
For the hero gone to the other world,
Break the enemy ranks with your spear;
Let them call you "the doomed man's son,"
And if death comes, fall to a bullet!
If you have the means, my only one,
Seize Kongurbai, khan of the monks,
That swaggering scoundrel,
And bring him back from the ford;
Will your vengeance not then be sated?
Bring him and offer him as a sacrifice!
Drag him along the road
And have the Dangyts tear him apart!
Cut off his head and bind it on,
So the sorrow within me may ease;
Bring it to Sarykan!
The traveler takes a migration;
The tiger among mortals takes revenge.
When he sets out, he brings good fortune,
And against the foe is like a countless host.
May God guide your road;
May the spirit of your tiger father,
My orphan, uphold you!
May Kongurbai, the foe you will grapple with,
My orphan, be weaker than you!
When you level your spear toward him,
When you make holy war upon China,
May Almambet, Chubak, and hero Syrgak,
Those lions, be your companions!
Lean forward and spear the warriors;
If your heroic gray-wolf father supports you,
Spear fifty of them in a single rank!
Turn your spear-fight into autumn harvest;
Raze to the ground the city
Of the wandering Black Chinese!
My young champion, son of Manas,
Now is your time to show heroism
Against the Black Chinese you confront!
My young champion, son of the Bloodthirsty One,
Now is your time to wreak havoc
Upon the hostile Black Chinese!
You brave youth born of Manas,
Go and bring a great upheaval
Upon the Black Chinese you confront!"
Sarykan spoke many words;
Then Sarykan ceased.
