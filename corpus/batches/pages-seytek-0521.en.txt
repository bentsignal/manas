He was a lone-born wolf indeed.
He was an ocean, a worthy equal,
A true gray-wolf hero.
“Ever since I became Sarybai,
Ever since Sarybai mounted a horse,
No desert has remained untrodden by me,
No people unseen by me.
I wandered through many lands,
Conquering this multitude,
Making people scream.
Those who claimed to be warriors and giants,
Who called themselves masters of the earth—
I slaughtered many such champions.
Never among all those have I seen his like;
He is truly mighty, truly a hero!”
So saying, Sarybai
Tested his valor
And brought down a mountain’s weight.
The spear blazed and became an ember;
When Külchoro thrust his spear,
Sarybai stood fast and endured it.
Then Külchoro said:
“If God delivers the slave to me,”
And, holding this thought,
Külchoro aimed without withdrawing his spear,
And at that infidel Sarybai
He thrust sixty times.
Sarybai rang but paid no heed,
Valuing sixty spear thrusts
No more than the hem of his robe,
Then found a desperate stratagem.
He struck Külchoro’s spear
Across the middle of its shaft.
The spear broke and was rendered useless;
Splintering across the middle,
Külchoro’s ornamented spear
Whirled shrieking into the sky.
That gray wolf Külchoro
Had his weapon and his beloved;
Such strength did the gray wolf possess.
Its point had been forged in steel
That it might pierce wherever it struck,
And rip apart wherever it was thrust;
Should it chance to hit its mark,
It would cut through to the far side.
Thus had it been fashioned.
Its point had been forged in steel,
And tempered sixty times
In a dragon’s venom.
Death is common to all—this was its fire;
Such was the nature of the spear.
He aimed at the measured spot,
And valiant hero Külchoro
Thrust and stabbed Sarybai.
Choro began his furious assault;
With both coat fronts flapping,
Sarybai nearly tumbled
From Zhelmaian.
Zhelmaian staggered,
And Sarybai stood pale,
Marvelling at Külchoro:
“He is renowned, a mountain indeed,
A renowned gray-wolf hero.
Thick-armed and stone-hearted,
A broad fellow with an elephant’s frame.
Against any rival or enemy
He can unleash great havoc.
He is a warrior and a hero,
A singularly born champion.
His weight is heavy and his venom strong;
He surpasses the whole world.”
He began a fierce assault,
But when Sarybai struck mightily,
He nearly fell away.
“What thick-muscled creature is this?
No such man appears in an age.
I barely stopped myself with a cry;
My soul nearly leapt from my breast.
