Facing toward the west,
Manas strode with ringing steps;
The dragon fled in a flash.
Almambet and Chubak
Stirred and got to their feet.
Their eyes opened clear again;
The two lions who had lain numb
Bowed their heads toward Manas.
The pillar of the companions,
Manas was the warrior's support.
When the journey's road was long,
When there were countless hosts of troops,
Hero Manas was the people's prop.
His world was complete.
Ocean-like leopard Manas—
His heart was wider than a lake,
