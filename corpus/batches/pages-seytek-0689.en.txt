“I shall slaughter you all.
If I do not slay every last champion,
Leave your people destitute,
And wage the fiercest battle;
If I do not break the backs
Of your men who call themselves heroes;
If I do not massacre your people,
Slaughtering every last soul,
Kill your khans,
And leave your women in black mourning;
If I do not spread slaughter to your borders,
Seize your maidens and women as plunder,
And take every single one;
I bristle in my anger,
And swell as vast as a mountain.
My strength seethes and surges;
Today I roar transformed.
I shall crush every one of your villages,
And load all your gold and silver
For transport toward Jelpiniş.
I am enraged and stand rigid;
If I do not spill your blood like water,
Drink your blood like water,
And quench my thirst;
May I strike down your lower demons
And seize all four kinds of livestock.
If I do not cast a chilling lasso
Over Buurul, whom you would never surrender if asked;
If I do not take as plunder
Your maidens who boast of their beauty,
May I die and cease to be Sarybai,
May I sink into darkness as though never born!
This accursed breed
Does not know it is about to die;
We cannot leave it alive;
That swine can never be redeemed.”
When the infidel, son of a monster,
Saw the hero Choro,
His shout split heads,
And his bellow split stones.
With that thick-muscled dog,
He came forth to confront him.
The one-armed gray-maned lion—
The spirit of Manas appeared openly,
Struck alongside him as a companion,
And openly lent him aid.
They rushed to face each other,
Preparing to meet in combat,
To turn each other’s horses’ heads,
Roaring threateningly,
To charge each other head-on
And test a hero’s courage.
If the true God so willed,
Külchoro would surrender his fly-like soul.
Fierce Külchoro
Unleashed his full courage.
Thinking his poor soul already dead,
He bound it to a hair and cast it away.
The one he faced was Sarybai;
He raised the Syr spear
And rode straight toward him.
Sarybai was a mad brute,
Standing unmoved by its humming flight
And reckoning it no higher than his heel.
Sarybai stood and said:
“Come not as one, but as a thousand;
Come six at once to grapple with me.
When I charge in fury,
My witless Külchoro,
Your eyes will be gouged out!
With Khan Semetey at their head,
Your Seytek too will be slaughtered.
If I do not gouge out your warriors’ eyes
And butcher every one of you;
If I do not fell your plane trees,
Topple and demolish your houses,
And lay waste your encampment;
If I do not herd your khans away captive,
Leaving not a hair’s worth of life;
If I do not destroy every last one of you,
