Bringing calamity upon mankind,
Sarybay drove us here.
When he carried out his massacre,
Saying, ‘Do as I command,’
Sarybay brought us as his chosen force.
The black halter of the fugitives
Sarybay brought concealed.
Our precious heads into calamity
Sarybay brought entangled.
While the people lived in freedom,
Hunting deer when they grew weary,
And when they were not weary, the dear people
Delighting in great festivities,
Sharing joy together,
While many peoples lived happily,
He brought such strife upon the nation,
And revealed horrors never seen by man.
The warriors’ misery boiled over,
As though struck by a plague of turmoil,
He drove souls onward without rest.
The infidel son of an ogre
Was seized by his thirst for blood.
He ruined the nation and corrupted the people,
Starting conflict and breaking the army,
Humiliating the entire nation
And oppressing all the people,
Destroying all that had been completed,
And leaving even resourceful men
Helplessly ruined.
For every hundred men, one man
Was appointed chief and drove them on.
Thus the dear people were oppressed,
And their misery boiled over.
Please hear the people’s grievance;
We have no inheritance left to divide.
Restrain your anger.
You fought the desperate Sarybay
And struck him down,
Laying him flat upon his back.
This tiny host has committed no crime,
And has no strength left to fight.
Will you take us as your own people?
Lion son of the bloodstained warrior,
Will you heed these rightful words?
Do not needlessly massacre
The multitude who have done no wrong;
Spare their spoonful of blood.
If by chance you find them guilty,
Then, hero, slaughter them if you must.
But if the rumor spoken is true,
On the dreadful Day of Judgment
We shall make the ogre’s infidel son
A scapegoat, saying, ‘He slaughtered the innocent,
And committed this outrage.’
So shall we bear witness against him.
Strike us fiercely if you will;
Without count and without recourse,
We have fallen into your hands today.
Take us as your own people.
Do not descend to Sarybay’s level
And inflict grievous conflict upon us.
Do not slaughter all together
The dear people who bear no guilt.
You will pass beyond a bloodstained hand,
And reach the farthest bounds of the nation;
It would betray supreme fairness and justice,
Hero, if you failed to show them now.
When your noble bearing leads us,
Reveal the mark of kingship.
Great trials run in your blood;
Spare the spoonful of blood
Of these guiltless people, hero.
Because of what has happened,
We gathered as a people,
Driven here by force,
Bound without cord or rope,
Our eyes darting in bewilderment.
For every thousand men, one
Was appointed as chief.
