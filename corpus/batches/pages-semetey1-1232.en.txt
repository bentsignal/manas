Had leveled his spear.
He roared in anger,
Came blazing from the ridge,
And called the battle cry "Manas!"
He was a boy of twelve.
"I will block his road,
Offer him as a sacrifice,
Lead his horse away as plunder,
And take it as booty.
Now I will find my chance
And cut him with my sword.
I will bring the axe down
Upon this impudent boy
And spill his blood like an albasty."
He turned Surkoen's head;
The leopard Sultan Toltoy
Stood waiting for the boy.
Shouting, "Manas! Manas!"
Calling the battle cry "Manas,"
He came crying, "Manas! Manas!"
The battle cry spoken in Manas's name
Deafened ears and noses.
When Toltoy looked again,
Where he stood became a deep ravine;
His own eyelashes became enemies.
Toltoy of the Jediger
Lost heart,
And in hero Toltoy's eyes
Mountain-like Manas appeared.
When Toltoy looked carefully,
As the boy cried "Manas!"
Hundreds led by Manas
Streamed out of the marsh,
And thousands led by Bakay
Surged out behind them.
Wearing a coat of yellow gold,
Mounted on a piebald horse,
With a bare sword at his side,
He circled in fury.
The sight made hearts waver;
He showed the form of a taker of souls.
Whipping the piebald horse,
“Seize him, seize him, seize him!”
“If you seize him, kill him!”
“Bind both his hands,
Gouge out both his eyes,
And only then kill him!”
Almambet raised the battle cry.
With hungry shout and fierce battle cry,
Whom no wrestler could overpower,
And no lion's tooth could pierce,
Wearing a blue rapys coat,
Mounted on a mottled blue horse,
Chubak of Akbaltay
Raised the cry behind him
And became his support.
Hero Toltoy of the Jediger
Lost heart;
It seemed as though the usual lion Khan Manas
Had appeared alive.
"They will bring me to my end,
Destroy us all,
And bind boastful Kojo
And carry him away alive!
It is leopard Manas himself;
How could he spare a life?
It is valiant Bakay himself;
This means he will bring torment.
Can one who challenges him live?
Can one who confronts him survive?
Will the living leopard
Bring us to our end like this,
Take us alive,
Drag us along the road,
And offer us as sacrifices?
Will I stand and match him,
Let myself be sacrificed,
And be dragged along the road?"
