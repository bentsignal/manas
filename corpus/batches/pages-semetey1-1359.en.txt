Foremost among women in white kerchiefs.
The one you oppose is Kakan-Chyn,
O mighty Bakai, my support!
Without turning from the land of Beijing,
You have set off in pursuit;
Without turning, beginning a holy war,
You have set off seeking blood vengeance.
May the enemy who attacks you be overcome!
May my Lord prosper your undertaking;
May Khizir support your road!
May your tiger father charge forth
And appear on your road to support you!
I have this precious thing;
My blessed son, listen well!
Buttoning its front,
Covering his broad chest,
So my son might wear it when breaking the host,
Roaring and taking the risk,
So he might strike the foe without stopping,
Break apart the Chinese host,
And drive them as far as the khagan,
I made four articles for you four;
Prince Bakai, the work was hard!
That you would bring turmoil
Upon Kakan-Chyn, I knew long ago.
That you would lead your boys
Against the teeming Chinese,
I knew long ago;
That you would unleash calamity and horror,
I knew long ago.
So he might wear it against his body,
And face and strike the enemy,
I have a short coat of plate armor.
When I tell it, it holds such wonders.
Whoever wears it need have little regret;
If the gray wolves put it on
And take it to China, there will be great conflict.
This barren, slippery world—
If they should raid the horses,
And fight one-on-one
With mighty hero Kongurbai,
I feared a spear thrust might pass through.
I, black-faced Kanykey,
Would have labored in vain if I had not foreseen it;
Long ago I prepared
This bulletproof armor.
Across the front of the shoulder,
Along the edge of the ribs,
I had fitted and fashioned
Iron reinforcing bands.
It has nine buttons;
So he might wear it buttoned,
And go without stopping to strike
Enemies who roll against him;
So upon the foe he faces
He might firmly bring hardship;
So my orphan might enter boldly
And avenge his father Manas,
I surpassed myself in the making.
To wear into battle,
For you four I made four coats of armor,
Prince, uncle Bakai,
Fitted to each of you!
So the heroes might wear these,
And face and strike the enemy.
I have coats I made with care;
I give them as gifts—put them on,
And face and strike the enemy!
Make war upon Beijing;
Recall the works
Your unfortunate valiant woman
Prepared for you!
And now, my son, listen well!
Again I pondered, for the task was hard;
Again, by my will, I made
Four articles for you four!
To obtain sharp iron,
I opened all my livestock stores,
