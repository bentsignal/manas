Swearing an oath unto death,
And licked the edges of their swords.
Roaring in fury,
He summoned all the people without exception,
And made the vast host of Jediger
Pledge their word in this manner:
“From now on, wretched people,
You have seen what befalls the one who flees;
When I rise in wrath,
You will die before your appointed hour!
Come, all you gray-bearded elders;
Captains of fifty and captains of a thousand,
Let no captain of a hundred remain behind!
Come, all my people, leaving none behind;
Come calmly and keep your wits!
Do not halt, Jediger,
Do not bustle about or raise an uproar,
Do not let the ninety tribes stir strife;
Come surging forward, all my people!
Let your wise and capable men come,
Your resourceful masters of stratagem come;
Let your axe-wielding lions come,
Bull-necked and camel-thighed,
Men who crush all who grapple with them,
Your towering heroes;
Do not falter, Jediger—
Come here now!
Let your thick-armed lions come;
I have something to say, Jediger—
Let the multitude come, leaving none behind.
Let your broad-chested, iron-hearted,
Stately men of Jediger come;
Let your generous heroes come—
Though a thousand stay behind, let your best one come!
Let those who stand beneath the banner come,
Those who, when outright conflict erupts,
At once level their spears!
In the time it takes to turn around,
In the blink of an eye,
Let those whose shouts can split stone,
Who charge in fury and shatter heads,
Come strike where death awaits!
Let your broad-chested lions come,
Who never retreat from their purpose
And never turn back before death;
Let your iron-hearted heroes come,
Who never withdraw from a contest!
When fierce battle-cries arise,
When the tumult no soul can bear erupts,
When the uproar rends the sky,
Let those who wheel their chargers come!
When tremendous turmoil descends,
And their fly-like lives are imperiled,
Let those who never shrink from death,
Who rejoice at the word ‘death’
And never turn back even when dying,
Come—your wolves, your champions against the foe!
Let those who unleash volleys of gunfire come,
Those who take moon-beautiful women as spoils
And, soaring like white swans,
Seize the heathens who would fly to the heavens;
Let those men come!
Let those who pursue the enemy to the last come,
Firing muskets and drawing bows,
And plunging into the bloody clash!
With fierce cries and ringing cheers,
With dappled banners and brass-tipped spears,
Let your fully armed, steadfast heroes come!
Mark these words of mine:
Though you grope about amid slaughter,
Semetey, the wild son of Manas,
Will not be destroyed.
While Semetey and Bakay remain,
Your cause can never prosper.
If Semetey now regains his strength,
Külchoro, the champion at his side,
Will launch Semetey into battle;
He will bind his belt firmly—
