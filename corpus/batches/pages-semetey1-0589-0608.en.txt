Your father's grave! The world is shit!
The six vile ones would not greet me,

“Seize bloodthirsty Bakai,

The reason they behaved this way:
Khan Tekechi and Khan Shygai
Laid a claim against my poor soul.
They stood at Üyrülmö,

They readied spears and gathered shot;
The six vile ones came out to fight,
And battled your uncle, Khan Bakai.
The forty companions left by Manas,
With Kyrgylchal, the dog of them all—
Like stars streaming from the sky,
They joined those six vile ones.
They drew their bows like falling rain,
And fired their shots like hail.
When the dogs came to besiege me,
My wide possessions were divided,
My treasury was left strewn out.
My ribs came loose and split apart;
My strength was torn in all directions.
Utterly alone, a solitary pine,
With no one to support me—
I, the childless leopard, was alone.
Surviving without dying,
For forty days and nights
I fought those vile men.
Cursed be loneliness!
No stepmother on whom I can rely,

No place in which to hide,

No great Kyrgyz nation at my side.

My poor soul fought those six vile ones,

I earned the name of troublemaker,

Drinking blood, I hardened myself.
