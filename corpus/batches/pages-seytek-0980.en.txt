Pouring out his anger, Sarybay,
Such was his ferocity.
He leveled his spear straight at her,
No mortal could withstand it.
The great host pressed in around them,
Leaving not a hair’s breadth of respite.
Sarybay, fierce in anger,
Fought in this fashion.
“If this spear of mine truly strikes,
It will strike at the elbow,” he thought.
This rabid bitch,
“This is where that mad bitch will truly die,” he thought.
Spurring himself on with this thought,
He swaggered about.
“If I thrust my spear with all my strength,
Will my spear not strike home?
However great a hero she may be,
Will her head not sink to the ground?”
Thinking such thoughts to himself,
His mind roaming at ease—
Thus Sarybay pondered,
Proud in his own breast,
And turned Tookyzyl’s head,
His deep-blue steel spear
He thrust forward with a roar.
“This is where you die,” he said,
And Sarybay the hero, as you see,
Struck with a resounding blow,
Driving his thrust home.
The spear bent and its tip blunted,
Kayypchal’s lioness of a daughter
Showed her wrestler’s strength.
When the blue spear struck,
It shattered into pieces.
Kuyaly of Kayipchal,
Was protected by the Forty Chiltens.
When the mighty spear struck her,
All of the Forty Chiltens
Caught her by the hem and held her up.
The spirit of valiant Manas
Stood beside her and supported her.
The poor girl with the strong back
Her coat now bore her up;
The spirit of mountain-like Manas
Now lifted her high.
Good fortune and blessings
Turned their gaze upon her now.
The precious Forty Chiltens
Propped her up from every side.
Her nine guardians
Came to her aid;
That lioness, that wrestler girl,
Who had crossed so much ground at a gallop,
Who had pleased so many people,
Whom Father Khizr had led,
Whom he had watched over across many lands,
Without swaying, without stirring,
Without a twitch, without a stir,
When the kazayyl was leveled,
Praying to the Creator,
Without flinching or heeding it,
She took Sarybay’s thrust;
See how heavily it landed.
She held it in utter contempt,
Without looking up or down,
The wrestler girl stood there.
To that infidel Sarybay
She spoke these words:
“You stretched out and thrust your spear;
You took my turn.
You drew your bow and rained down arrows,
You fired shots like hail,
And stirred up an ugly battle.
You even fired your shots in haste;
What you did was shameful.
Keep your promise.
Do not murmur or go back on your word;
Do not swerve or flinch;
Agree to what I have said.
