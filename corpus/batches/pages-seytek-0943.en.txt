And filled his sword’s scabbard with blood.
Until destruction had run its course,
A dense and tangled conflict raged.
“I am the hero Külchoro;
Many tens of thousands of warriors came,
Yet this swine Sarybai
Attacked my caravan from the hills,
And still I have not taken my revenge.”
“I am the hero Seytek;
If fate has come, let me die.
This vile swine has run mad;
Let me test myself against him.
Unwavering, I shall bring disaster upon him;
If my hand can reach him, for my honor
I shall take my long-awaited revenge.
Against this pestilent swine
I shall ride out in single combat.
Enraged against the infidel,
To break his mighty strength,
I shall certainly go alone.
I shall strike hard with my spear;
Let me clash with him and see.
Ten thousand troops have slipped from his hand;
Let me see the extent of his strength.
I shall rain shots upon him
And leave such a mark
For the ruined nation to remember.
He is Sarybai and I am Seytek;
We shall clash with battle cries,
Unleash calamity upon each other,
Struggle without sparing our lives,
And fight to the finish;
I shall make a wondrous slaughter.
Not sparing my thread-thin life,
I shall fight him to the end.
I have longed to meet him face-to-face
And kindle another battle;
My early word has grown late,
And my two eyes have become four with waiting—
To thrust my spear with all my might,
To stand against him face-to-face,
To learn the fate written on my brow,
And see it all fulfilled;
If God should put me to the test,
To surrender my precious life;
To observe and discover
His courage and heroism.
If he is a champion and a hero,
If the fiend Sarybai is a valiant man,
If such is the destiny of a brave lion,
And if I am the hero Seytek,
I shall call every champion who comes against me
To account for it all.
I shall not flee from the wrathful infidel,
But meet him in battle.
If the Creator grants me honor,
I shall perform a wondrous deed,
Beat down his long-held fury,
And stand face-to-face with him
To unleash a great commotion.
Not a single thread-thin life shall remain;
We shall slaughter one another to the last.
I have composed my account;
I have sought the road to Sarybai.
We shall ride out and joust in single combat,
Until a great uproar reaches its height.
Prepared for that very thing,
We shall unleash a battle.
We shall advance in force and see,
For we ourselves burn with desire.
We bow to the will of God;
We long to enter single combat
And strike him down.
We stand consumed by that desire.
Against this Sarybai on the plain,
Who gives no quarter to anyone with a jaw,
And yields to no rider on a bareback horse,
Whose strength cannot be matched in wrestling,
Whom we cannot overcome in combat,
All our saplings have been blown down,
