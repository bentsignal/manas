Unless it drank its fill of blood,
It did not return to the sheath.
When swung, it lengthened;
Its edge reddened like an ember.
It did not recoil from stone,
And when struck in fury,
It did not turn back from iron.
When Manas held dominion
Across the surface of the earth,
Whoever stirred his anger,
Manas did not let escape before him.
While ravenous Manas lived,
From blessed gray-wolf Manas
It hardened, sated with blood,
And became renowned as Zulpukor.
Its edge was steel, its sheath keen—
That single treasure inherited
From his father, gray-wolf Manas,
To draw while facing
Khojo who challenged him;
The lion, if strength allowed,
Would hew down with the sword
Khojo who came to confront him,
Slaughter his people,
And unleash mighty conflict,
Taking undying vengeance
Upon Khojo, who attacked first.
At his courage, the Afghans
Fled in a clattering rout.
The sight stirred the heart;
That gray wolf’s only son
Revealed the aspect of a lion.
High-browed and stone-hearted,
He brought the Jediger great turmoil.
Akkelte at his shoulder—
When the blessed weapon was slung on,
It was a mirror upon the shoulder;
When fired into clashing battle,
It was comfort for the entrusted life.
In sport it was Ochogor,
In earnest it was Koichagyr;
When he turned and fired, it broke ranks.
Akkelte had a nature all its own.
That was the joined iron weapon,
The treasure left as a gift
By valiant Sultan Manas.
Its voice thundered unlike any other;
No mortal faced it and came on.
When he heard Akkelte’s voice,
Kongurbai of Kakan scattered all his wealth
And fled all the way
To Beijing.
Wrapped with agyinek,
When there was a foe to fight,
It was fitted and destined for him.
Almabash, surpassingly keen iron,
Made no distinction between far and near;
In the hardest battle,
It did not fail for sixty days.
Yesterday, while bloodthirsty Manas lived,
Before lion Bakai,
Your uncle Lord Bakai—
Behold his lordly nature—
On a plain flat as a blade, without mountains,
At the great Russian city,
Your uncle lion Bakai
Was imposing his tribute.
The Russians caused immense devastation
And stole from Bakai
His treasury.
Hero Manas, whom he had brought,
And dear, distinguished Bakai
Surrounded the Russians.
Saying, “Submit to what we demand;
You have taken the treasury.
Without conflict or quarrel,
Give us Akkelte.
