# Rank-cap formula correction

Lexical correction: таш сайынган means a Chinese/Kalmyk official wearing a distinguishing ball/button on the cap, Yudakhin таш I.5 https://el-sozduk.kg/таш/. In this formula таза means finest, not bald/shaven: Yudakhin таза3 https://el-sozduk.kg/ТАЗ/. Raw source unchanged.

All 32 occurrences in the published source range were read alongside their English and corrected. This changes neither source IDs nor verse order.
