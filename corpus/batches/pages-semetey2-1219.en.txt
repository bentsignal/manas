Hounding and slaughtering you till you turn pale—
Or may I and all my people die unborn!
You cringing Jediger,
Try to flee without leaving one behind!
Let me see how you escape!”
Roaring in fury,
He drove the vast host of Jediger
Back again in confusion.
The illustrious Kanchoro’s
Eyes boiled like a boar’s;
His broad, gate-like teeth
Clashed as he ground them.
The people clamored and tumult arose;
The warrior unleashed his wrath.
The Jediger wholly renounced
Their fly-like lives on the spot.
How capable Kanchoro was:
He drove the Jediger forward,
As though he might devour them,
And shouted at brave Kyyaz.
Making his mountain-bay horse surge,
The mountain-like Kyyaz approached;
And the seething Kanchoro
Saw the fullness of his rage.
“Damn you, Jediger!
I thought Kyyaz was a hero,
But when the enemy appears, he flees—
You have proved a woman, Kyyaz!
The man you must fight is Semetey,
Yet in your panic you are poised
To scatter your own possessions!
You panic-stricken Jediger,
It is shameful beyond measure:
You have come only to flee!
The dream we saw has turned ill;
Has there ever been, in all the ages,
A people who fled from the field of battle?
All your vaunted elders,
As though struck by slaughter,
Go and perish together!
Has the bloodthirsty son of Manas
Recited some spell from scripture
And mounted it as his steed?
His dapple-gray horse was his wings;
Dark-flanked and blue-backed,
That fiery bloodthirsty warrior
Turned back after besieging Kakan,
But the legacy of that entrusted bread—
His Buurul—has been taken from him.
Blood has flooded his breast;
Deprived of Buurul,
His good fortune has deserted him.
Leaning upon Akkelte,
He has been left on foot;
Your shambling bloodthirsty foe
Is fated to die a foul death this year!
Among all the horses God created,
What champion steed remains
To lift and carry that man?
That reckless bloodthirsty warrior
Is fated to die this year.
The reason he must die is this:
The forty calamities have overtaken him;
His Buurul has vanished from his grasp
And all his blessings have scattered.
The forty holy spirits who sustained him
Have withdrawn far from his body.
At the forked, arching mountain pass
He has been left looming there on foot;
Disaster, death, and dread misfortune
Have closed around the hero.
Damn you, Jediger,
Swarm upon him and wage war!
Take your vengeance upon Semetey;
Raid Keng-Kol and seize its herds!
Stately hero Kyyaz,
Take your revenge before departing;
Devastate Talas and seize its herds!
