The formidable men stood at the flanks,
Spearmen formed the front;
Their lions stood behind,
Their renowned men in front,
Their champions at the flanks.
Their moon-crested standard became their banner,
And their shouts became a clamor.
Hill and valley were all uproar;
Everyone in the lowlands
Cried “Kiyaz!” as their battle cry.
On the hills were six hosts of horses;
The Jediger raised a shout
And raided them as they passed.
The countless black host rose as one
And reached the herd together.
In the mountains were ninety hosts of horses;
With piercing cries and clamor,
They raided them as they passed.
The countless black host rose as one
And reached the herd together.
The honored mighty warrior was dead.
The Jediger held a feast over
The warrior's countless black herds
And divided them by the measure of a whip.
Countless treasures and myriad goods—
The Jediger now swarmed the valley.
The Jediger filling Jeti-Özön
Feasted to the full
On the warrior-khan's wealth.
Every hero who had strength
Took a powerful, fleet horse;
Every wretched weakling
Took a shaggy, stormy colt.
The many Kyrgyz and Argyn of Talas
Now looked bitterly upon the pillage.
The Jediger cast catch-ropes
And separated out the Kyrgyz of Talas.
They inflicted unheard-of humiliation,
Extraordinary calamity and strife.
The Jediger swarmed in and took the livestock.
The foolish Jediger
Killed the men and took their lives.
The valley cried out and heaved;
Among the mingled Argyn and Kyrgyz,
The turmoil became devastation.
The teeming Jediger
Inflicted cruel suffering.
Saying, “This is for Chynkojo and Toltoy,”
They sacked them yet again.
The Jediger came in, sparing no soul,
Not one bay colt with a mane,
Not one lamb with a tail to provide meat,
Not one long-legged beast.
Of those able to wage war,
They spared no soul who could swing a club,
No hero who could lead a people,
No people capable of courage.
Among broad-rumped women with ornate hair pendants,
They spared no skilled young wife;
Among thimble-mouthed, camel-calf-eyed,
Rolling black-eyed girls
In crimson dresses with crescent waists,
They spared not one shimmering maiden.
They carried out a dreadful pillage,
And leaving them nothing at all,
They stood plundering everything.
At that time the Jediger
Went roaring and swaggering,
Their noses bulging like leather flasks.
“Our hostile warrior is dead,” they said;
“The accursed warrior has reached
The true place from which none returns.
Our grievous affliction and fetters are loosed;
God has granted this to us.
Now our season of fortune has come!”
They broke open the treasury;
They gorged themselves beyond measure on plunder.
