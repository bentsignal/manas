You girded your appointed belt,
And raised turmoil against all who faced you.
You besieged the Khagan and took his lands,
And conquered the populous Chinese realm
That even the Prophet could not have entered.
You reached it, broke it, and took the land;
O hero, you shattered Jerken and attacked it.
While you were in your prime,
You raided Jet-Kait and took booty.
You routed the myriad host and leveled it.
Kara-Shaar and Kambyl
You besieged, subdued, and opened as a road.
Solon, Shive, and Manchu
You beat like wool and brought low.
You leveled them with your inlaid spear,
And while your lion lived,
You made the people suffer and ruled them.
Your might became known.
One of your names was inscribed
In the records of Beijing.
While your noble prince lived,
You drove white deer and made them cattle.
You stirred turmoil throughout the world,
And made bullets rain from your cartridge pouch.
While your hero lived,
You crushed every foe you faced.
The six keen blades fallen from heaven,
Calling them your paternal inheritance,
You bound them at your slender waist
And subdued the world alone.
You were a lion, but became a wanderer;
You were parted from your valiant one.
You burned and grieved until you became thus.
Parted from the noble hero,
You became a herdsman’s slave.
You were a mountain pass, but became a road;
Parted from the renowned lion,
You fell into abject disgrace.
Today speak your truth without wavering.
Will the divinely favored lion
Stand by without raising battle?
Will God strike Choro down
Before he fights and takes revenge?
The gold-jawed, cast-iron-headed mace—
When you see its venom, flee far away.
Gripping it and swinging it in his hand,
He raised a black stone as large as a house
And hurled it with both hands.
Sparks flew from his mace,
Flames blazed and crackled,
And fire sprang from the black stone.
Dust rose from his blow,
And those who saw it nearly died.
Choro began to show his strength,
And his many sworn enemies
Renounced not only their livestock
But even their lives entirely.
It was then that brave Seytek,
Questioning his uncle,
Entered the mountain fastness
And became embroiled in a great
And incurable tumult.
He entered a calamity that would not subside,
And became embroiled in a dispute.
“My precious uncle, bloodthirsty lion,
Sing and tell your song;
Tell your truth as solemnly as your creed.
Uncle, reveal your secret.
While your fortune was unharmed,
While your gray-wolf uncle lived,
You reached Afghanistan and raided it.
If your army did not strike it,
How could Jet-Kait have remained intact?
You went and shattered Afghanistan;
If you did not crush and pulverize it,
If you did not subdue it,
How could Bakünchö have remained intact?
While the hulking gray wolf lived,
You leveled men with your keen steel
And ruled every land you encountered.
