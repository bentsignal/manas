A black day fallen on their heads,
Had their hearts sorely troubled.
Those men who worried like Asan the Sorrowful,
“I had only one horse!” they said;
More than these were languishing.
All the lion-like heroes said,
“We have had our pleasure exchanging shots;
If death comes, we die!”
More than these were rejoicing.
The rash and senseless,
More than these were panicking.
The weaklings without courage,
Penned in the middle,
More than these were fleeing.
Their steeds would not stand still;
More than these were rearing and plunging.
The heroes who had courage,
