Without quarrel or strife,
Let us turn back—heed me;
Let us preserve this vast people from slaughter
And remain safe and sound.
My dear comrade, listen to me;
Let us return safely to Jeti-Özön
And lead the bay horse
Before my uncle Sary Khan.
Let us make peace as equals,
And each depart safely to his own land.”
So brave Kyyaz spoke,
Terrified of Sary Khan,
Addressing Chubak’s son Choro
With these words;
Before Kanchoro
Kyyaz made his plea.
As though stabbed in a tender gland,
Kanchoro flared up on hearing this
And sprang down:
“Damn him—Sary Khan
Is a bald charcoal burner.
His greatness and his khanly rank
Are scarcely lower than mine.
Rather than fear Sary Khan,
What if you should behold him, Jediger?
What if you are slaughtered
To the last man, Jediger?
Jeti-Özön is your land,
Or so you keep claiming.
May the earth swallow you, Kyyaz!
I had thought you were a warrior.
I will say, ‘I was mistaken; I submit,’
And return to the bloodthirsty one.
Just watch, Jediger,
And see how I bring about your ruin.
I shall capture you, mount you on a horse,
And take you as a gift
To the fierce wolf, the son of Manas;
By one means or another I shall make peace,
And reconcile myself with him.
After that, Jediger,
I shall decide what to do with you.
You will not rally when I cheer you on;
You Jediger swine will never prosper.
You will not rise when I give you courage;
No Jediger will ever prosper.
You call Sary Khan formidable
And fear a bald woodcutter.
You were a marksman and a warrior—
Now prove your marksmanship.
Make your utmost effort, brave Kyyaz;
There is a broad stone by the lone road—
Hide behind that broad stone.
Once hidden behind it,
He has words no man can fathom,
And many things he could say;
Between his two eyes
He has a red mole as large as a bowl.
Aim at that red mole and fire;
Shoot the cur dead!
For if you do not kill him that way,
Spears cannot pierce him,
Heavy muskets cannot punch through him,
And rifle balls cannot penetrate him—
His invulnerability is indeed real.
That he advanced through Chin-Machin, Kakan, and Beijing
Is also true.
That the accursed man never yielded
His companion’s life is also true.
That bald Sary struck down
The spearmen is also true;
That the firebrand stood
At the foot of the banner is also true.
This Sary Khan will not simply vanish;
Unless Sary Khan is gone,
Jediger’s cause will never prosper.
This hero Sary Khan
Has every cunning of an infidel,
