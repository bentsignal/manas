Had he not seized it?
Before, fighting six times,
He had caused much strife in Afghanistan.
The peoples of the world gathered;
Many of the palmful of Afghans
Were slaughtered.
Akun's army stood apart.
What could dear Akun do?
He wrapped his belt as a turban
And implored the Almighty:
"If death comes, I will die;
What God has written,
If it falls upon my neck, I will see.
I will become support
For the orphaned son of Manas.
If the Jediger falls into my hands,
I will offer them as sacrifices!"
Khan Akun came out straining.
What could dear Akun do?
He prepared for war.
His spear was truly pointed,
His sword exceptionally sharp.
The hero skilled with the battle-axe,
Tumenbay, son of Tulku,
Was the valiant Afghan.
If an army engulfed them,
If a dug pit lay before them,
If a hazy fire struck,
If an unbroken bullet struck,
He was the hero who would ward it off.
"I will endure the Jediger's humiliation
Through hardship;
I will become support
For the young lord and tiger."
The valiant man's mount
Was a black-blazed courser.
Surging upon the courser,
He drove the great common host
And spoke to the Afghans:
"Do not lose your wits;
Damn you, Afghans,
Do not flee as before!
You have a ridge of refuge,
And heroes girded for battle.
The great Jediger host
Engulfed Afghanistan
And often raided your migrations;
Leopard Khan Semetey
Will take your revenge.
Support has come to you, Afghans;
A lion to make a host has come!"
Shouting, "Baabedin!"
Calling the Afghan battle cry,
They became a vast, accomplished army,
So numerous the earth bent.
All mounted tulpar horses
And tightly knotted their hair tails.
The earth filled with their numbers;
They were a people spread across the land.
His courser thundered;
He slung the alengir bow
And patterned arrows,
Roaring, "Advance!"
They streamed like ants.
When his army suddenly emerged
Upon the earthen ridge,
The Jediger on the far side
Were coming in a surging mass.
Spearpoints struck one another,
And human heads collided.
They drew bows like rain
And fired bullets like hail.
Closing in toward the city,
Prepared for mass battle,
The remaining Jediger
Drew bows like rain
And fired bullets like hail.
Without tiring, the people engulfed them,
