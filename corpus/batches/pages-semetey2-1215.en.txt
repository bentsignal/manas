He sharpened and trained me for war.
Almambet, who departed for the other world,
Guarded me better than my father.
When I reached the age of six,
He had an amulet hung around my neck.
Wishing me to become a peerless gray wolf,
He raised me with tender affection,
Led me with him and made me a warrior,
And made me equal to his own heroic son.
He made me raid true Kakan and Beijing
Without the least mercy.
How could I let myself be parted
From my dark gray-wolf lion?
How could I abandon
My noble gray-wolf hero,
My fearsome, blood-spilling uncle?
The Jediger are ten thousand and I am alone,
Yet for seven months without rest
I shall fight them to mutual destruction.
Against the massed Jediger
I shall stand and level my spear;
For ninety days without rest I shall fight,
And unleash hellish wrath.
I shall rescue
My valiant lionhearted uncle;
I shall drive the strong before me and slaughter them.
For this peerless gray wolf
I shall fall as a sacrifice at his feet!”
Thus spoke Külchoro:
“I shall drive away their strongest.
Damn you, Jediger—
He who spares an enemy will be wounded;
He who spares his horse is left afield.
I shall strike down even the blameless
And make a slaughter of them.
I shall drive the boastful Jediger
Toward Jeti-Özön,
Make them cry, ‘Jakey, mercy!’
And force them truly to taste torment.
Those who survive the dead
I shall make cry, ‘Akebai, mercy!’
I shall make a glorious massacre.
Between Kyyaz and Kanchoro,
I shall take the head of one.
Before my uncle
I shall offer it in sacrifice.
While my uncle’s eyes still see,
I shall wage a battle,
Make their blood flow like water,
And exact my long-carried vengeance.
While his eyes are still alive,
For my lord, my gray-wolf uncle,
I shall become a sacrifice.
Making them cry, ‘Poor Choro!’
I shall unleash a mighty exchange of fire.”
Thus spoke Külchoro,
Thick-armed and stonehearted,
The broad-limbed, formidable warrior.
He buckled on his scabbardless sword;
The tiger-born lion Choro
Seemed about to become a storm,
Ready to slaughter whoever crossed his path.
Wrath spread across his face,
And he strained like a lion.
The formidable gray wolf
Found his battle cunning,
And thundered in anger.
No soul could withstand his awesome presence;
All men trembled.
He thundered in fury,
No soul able to withstand his might;
All who saw him quaked.
Behold what your Choro was like:
The bold and dashing lion Choro,
Peerless in swiftness,
The clashing hero Choro.
