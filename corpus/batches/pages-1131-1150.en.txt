Had ready.
Bakay gathered the pious elders,
Had verses recited from the Quran,
And at noon performed the marriage rite.
Kanykey on my right,
Khan Bakay on my left,
Seating me between them,
They asked me for an account.
The turmoil from the beginning,
I told them then,
Lion Manas, my blue-maned one,
The story I have told you here.
When she heard the story,
My friend's fiery wife Kanykey
Stood wailing and sobbing.
“Do not tell Manas,” she said,
Weeping and crying out,
