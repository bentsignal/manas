Such was their giant nature:
They were still but half-fed.
Clasping hands,
The wolf-maned ones, bustling with joy,
Made their pledge.
Holding hands and speaking,
They declared their pledge firmly:
‘If we begin a holy war against unbelievers,
And one of us leaves another
Without giving him support,
May the oath sworn before God strike us!
May the sacred writing knotted in our belts strike us!
May Akkelte's bullet strike us!
May the blue matchcord's ember strike us!
Whoever turns back from wise counsel,
Whoever withdraws from the pledge,
May the shaggy-breasted earth strike him!
May the open-crowned sky strike him!
If Allah issues His decree,
If it falls upon our heads, let us face it.
If one of us happens to fall to a bullet,
Let us all die together!
Let us bundle our spears together,
Let us face the gunfire together,
Let us scout together,
Let us raise turmoil together.
Let our sheep share a fold,
