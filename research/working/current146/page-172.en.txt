The warrior Odukan appeared immense,
He hastened with immeasurable strength,
He had burning fury and flame,
An enemy life was fixed in his thoughts,
His rising rage scorched living souls,
Now it was Ebegey above all,
They prayed he might find a stratagem,
Saying that death is kin to every mortal,
One who could restore a life nearly severed,
When they hewed at one another squarely,
He could scatter four thousand at the pass,
They said the warrior was left like an old man,
Toward the Kerik standing ready,
They mounted Zhelzhetpes upon it,
Rousing his spirit and setting him upright,
His death-defying comrades stood with him,
Showing the victory of a hero,
He fell into hands filled with misfortune,
The warrior Zhelzhetpes burned with anguish,
From among the multitude of warriors,
A swelling conflict began,
Kögöy’s precious life hung by a thread,
Kenen entrusted the fallen one to them,
His life, tiny as a fly, grew faint,
His eyes burned as he faced the true world beyond,
The wrathful enemy formation was broken,
The great and numerous Kyrgyz people,
Precious Kenen and Ebegey,
The four stood and greeted one another,
Infidel and Muslim alike,
Do not forget the turmoil if you seek it,
Rage stirred up the rebellion,
They waged a fierce conflict,
Mounted on Kerik, he raced through the dust,
Taking the road toward safety,
He escaped out of the turmoil.
