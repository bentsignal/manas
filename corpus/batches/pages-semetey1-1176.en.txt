To Khan Manas’s encampment
Dear Agysh came.
They dressed him in badana armor;
Badana was the name of the trousers.
They made him run up and down;
Those badana trousers were exceptional,
Fashioned to fit him
By your poor, fair sister-in-law.
Silk within and cotton cloth without,
Double-layered badana with lark-eye links:
Fire would not pass through if tinder burned,
Nor a bullet if a musket fired.
Now was the time to wear them.
They were half a kez thick.
From a treasury saddlebag,
The unfortunate khan’s wife
Displayed her careful provision;
Upon Agysh, as was fitting,
She put the badana armor.
When they saw her provision,
Infidel and Muslim,
All the assembled people,
And khans as huge as mountains,
Said, “She is truly a lord’s wife,”
And every khan was pleased.
Her chest held riches beyond count;
Kanysh, wife of Manas,
Had everything one could name.
Against the mountain-sized champion,
Elbow-sized brave Agysh,
All sinew from head to foot,
Stepped forward then,
Going to wrestle him.
The giants Koshoi and Töshtük
Raised their hands and cried out;
All of them gave their blessing,
Including old hero Koshoi.
Brave Agysh stepped forward
And spoke these words to Manas:
“Against that swaggering swine,
I will grapple and wrestle fiercely,
Tearing at whoever stands before me,
Butting like a blue bull;
I will wrestle him openly.
Take the black-jawed rawhide whip,
Grasp it and swing it:
‘Lift him, Agysh, lift him!’
‘Take him, Agysh, take him!’
Across the fish-like flesh of my fat rump,
Strike and split it open;
Let my flesh sting,
Let my blood scatter.
From that swine who plays the hero,
I will seize him by the shins.
Jerking him up and down,
I will use my ancient strength.
If you do not strike, it brings shame;
Your blow is entrusted to us.
When anger rises, I will prevail;
If my anger does not rise,
I may merely keep laughing.
When I see your fierce wrath,
Then I will put forth my strength.”
Having said this,
Agysh strode toward the champion,
Going to wrestle him.
Tükübai of the Kalmyks
Never released a man he grappled;
From the flesh of a single swine,
One meal could not fill him.
Who has seen a hero like this?
He braced himself and charged.
If Agysh fell into his hands,
He looked ready to mow him down.
Without even stirring from his place,
He looked ready to suck him in whole.
The great Hairy-Sole champion
Charged and reached him.
