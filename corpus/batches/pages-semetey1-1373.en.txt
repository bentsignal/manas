Ah, wondrous Issyk-Köl—
Is it not the rump of the earth?
Would anyone dwelling here ever leave?!
It has its Küngöi and its Teskei,
With mountains rising into view;
When one looks toward their foothills,
The human heart is overcome.
On its slopes pines grow thick in heaps,
And crystal-clear springs pour from the hollows.
Its bounty is complete;
God willing
It's a memorable place!
Are its summer pastures not nearby?
Would the Kyrgyz not settle there for summer!
Are its winter quarters not nearby?
Would the Kyrgyz not winter there!
Roe deer cry from the slopes,
And owls call from the rugged ground.
It's a blessed place,
Its hillsides bear fescue and wormwood.
The great Kyrgyz people would choose it
It was a place to stay.
With kumis by day and girls by night,
It's a place to be.
Ah, wondrous Issyk-Köl—
Is it not the rump of the earth?
Would any mortal dwelling there ever leave?!
Looking toward the Küngöi side—
Haze lies over Chaar-Tal;
Looking at the opposite side -
Munaryktap Kara-Tal.
Between these two
Are many kinds of unseen creatures.
There is a deer, there is a tiger,
Wherever one turns are countless wonders.
Rich in crops, spacious for cattle,
It has countless splendid places.
There are ibex on its terraces,
Argali on its hills,
There's a deer on the bend,
Marshes in patch after patch,
And separate swans and geese.
There is a forest, there is a willow,
Along this Issyk-Köl
Every living creature is found.
It has demoiselle cranes, ducks, and geese,
And many kinds of marshland.
There is a fox on the plain,
For those who dwell there, Issyk-Köl
Has wealth waiting to be found.
Its deep ravines hold many foxes,
And its abundance brings much laughter.
Its thousand valleys are each distinct;
Its delights are famed among the people.
Gold seems poured among its stones,
And the Kyrgyz would settle there content;
Almighty Allah
Made every kind of land.
Could it be a land too small
To pitch a royal camp and hold games?
Could it be a land where one would not shoot
The maral in its marshes?!
O yawning gorge,
Uncle Bakai, O gray wolf,
I have many laments to tell!
The spring flows,
The nightingale sang in wonder,
The listener is moved to tears;
Such an interesting place!
Uncle Bakai, bringer of good fortune,
Look at the forest!
It has noisy wild beasts;
Look at the mountain side
There is snow upon its summit!
Your Almighty God
Truly created this land
In surpassing beauty!
It is a lake that opens one's grief,
